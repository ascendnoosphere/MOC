# MOC agenda replay and proof work, 25 November 2026

This ledger covers both supplied fifteen-path reports (Cycles 01 and
02). The numbering below follows Cycle 02; the reports reorder several
paths. The baseline is the repository after the checked $2n-1$ contact
and $2n-2$ functional-rank reductions, not the older revision cited by
the reports. A bounded search is not a proof. A failed stronger claim
is not a counterexample to MOC.
