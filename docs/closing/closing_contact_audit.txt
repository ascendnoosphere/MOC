INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE STRENGTHENED HIGH-CONTACT THEOREM
2026-10-17. Reviewer: variational track.

Audited source: closing_high_contact_standalone.txt (literature track).
Result: the theorem and proof pass this independent audit. This is a
counterexample localization theorem, not a proof of the MOC conjecture.

1. Exact strengthened conclusion.

For n>=4, a hypothetical strict MOC violation can be replaced by a
violation with the same relative unitary U, the same multiplier eta,
and exactly the same positive gap, while all n! matching products are
nonzero and pairwise distinct and at least 2n-2 of them are exposed on
one supporting line. The proof may preserve any chosen dense conic
Zariski-open set of actual finite-spectral matching vectors.

2. Points independently checked.

The matching-product image of the rank<=2 matrix variety has complex
dimension 2n-2. On the nonzero-entry rank2 locus, equality of every
matching product recovers every 2-by-2 crossratio, so the complete fiber
is precisely row/column rescaling with total multiplier one. Its
dimension is 2n-2 after quotienting the scalar stabilizer.

Generic GL2 basis choice puts each rank2 factorization into additive
spectral form, up to row and column factors. Their total scalar is
absorbed by a common nth-root scaling of a and b. Chevalley then gives
a nonempty open set of actual finite-spectral points. Its conic hull is
still open and still consists of actual points. This avoids requiring
surjectivity onto every exceptional point of the closure.

The exact determinant linear functional is

 d_U(z) = sum_pi [sgn(pi) product_i U_(i,pi(i))/det(U)] z_pi.

Its coefficients need not be real for this proof. It is enough that
det(D_a U+U D_b)=det(U) det(D_a+U D_b U*).

At k active labels, fixing d_U-z_pi0 and the k-1 active complex
differences imposes k complex equations. Every local component of the
fiber through a closed point has dimension at least (2n-2)-k. When this
is positive one can choose a nonconstant irreducible algebraic curve
through the point, including when the ambient fiber is singular.

On the smooth compact normalization, all coordinates are meromorphic.
The complement of the prescribed open set and the points at infinity
remove only finitely many points. The remainder is connected. If the
feasible positive-gap component had no boundary there, every deficit
would be nonnegative on this punctured compact surface. A pole of a
meromorphic function has real part of both signs in every punctured
neighborhood, so every coordinate difference would have no poles and
would be constant.

Only common translation could remain. A fixed multiplicative quartet
z00*z11=z10*z01 with Gamma=z00-z10-z01+z11 nonzero excludes it, because
adding c to all four coordinates changes the quadratic identity by
c*Gamma. The curve would therefore be constant, a contradiction.

Hence a finite new contact exists inside the prescribed open set. At
that point the complex gap, hence its positive real part, is unchanged.
Iterating stops after finitely many steps with at least 2n-2 contacts.

3. Two scope boundaries that must remain explicit.

First, the argument applies just as well to an arbitrary complex linear
functional d(z). In particular it does not use unitarity after defining
d_U. Such arbitrary functionals can violate the permutation hull, for
example d(z)=2*z_pi-z_sigma at a point where pi is uniquely exposed and
sigma is strictly below it. The theorem consequently cannot itself
make its terminal configurations harmless. A terminal positivity lemma
must use a further property of actual unitary determinant coefficients.

Second, the complex tie equations are unavoidable for a holomorphic
deformation preserving support: the real part of each active complex
difference is zero, so the open mapping theorem makes that difference
constant. At k=2n-2 the tie equations together with the fixed complex
gap generically exhaust the complex dimension. Continuing the same
method requires a new algebraic dependence, or a separate argument on
the terminal strata.

The exact verifier closing_contact_tangent_verify.py proves that the
terminal six-contact locus for n=4 contains finite spectra with all 24
products distinct and nonzero. Thus a claim that every terminal point
must have product collisions or obvious spectral degeneracy is false.

4. Relation to the earlier cycle proof.

The fixed-complex-gap proof is simpler and stronger for the unrestricted
MOC localization than the earlier bounded-subharmonic gluing argument.
It requires no capacity continuity, capacity minimizer, or behavior of
capacity at infinity. The earlier cycle-capacity theorem remains valid
as a separate statement, but neither version proves positivity at the
terminal locus.

No unrestricted MOC proof, cycle-capacity proof, or claim of literature
novelty follows from this audit.
