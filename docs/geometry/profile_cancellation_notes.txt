1. Audit of the proposed n2 profile argument.

Write A=diag(a1,a2), B=U diag(b1,b2) U*, and p=|u11|^2.
Unitarity gives |u22|^2=p, |u12|^2=|u21|^2=1-p and
u11 conjugate(u21)+u12 conjugate(u22)=0. Therefore, exactly,

 B11=p b1+(1-p)b2,       B22=(1-p)b1+p b2,
 B12=(b1-b2)u11 conjugate(u21),
 B21=(b1-b2)u21 conjugate(u11).

For complex b1,b2, B21 is generally NOT conjugate(B12). Their product is

 B12 B21=p(1-p)(b1-b2)^2,                                  (1)

with the complex square, not an absolute square. Consequently

 det(A+B)
  =(a1+p b1+(1-p)b2)(a2+(1-p)b1+p b2)
        -p(1-p)(b1-b2)^2
  =p(a1+b1)(a2+b2)+(1-p)(a1+b2)(a2+b1).                    (2)

All p^2 terms cancel. Equation (2) holds for arbitrary complex a and b.
It proves the full n2 MOC statement, with weights p and 1-p, directly.
Every real support profile is affine in p, not a genuinely concave
quadratic. No additional planar argument is needed for complex n2.

2. The cancellation that does extend to every dimension.

Fix normal A,B, with A diagonal, and vary A only in the coordinate plane
spanned by ei,ej. Let A0 agree with A off this plane and have both plane
eigenvalues equal to aj. Put C0=A0+B, Delta=ai-aj. Every such two-plane
orbit has the form

 A(v)=A0+Delta vv*,  ||v||=1, v supported on {i,j}.

The rank-one determinant identity, valid also for singular C0, gives

 det(A(v)+B)=det(C0)+Delta v* adj(C0) v.                    (3)

For a fixed support multiplier eta, let H be the Hermitian part of the
two-by-two compression of eta Delta adj(C0), and alpha=Re(eta det C0).
If p=|vi|^2, optimization over the relative phase gives the exact profile

 alpha+H11 p+H22(1-p)+2|H12|sqrt(p(1-p)).                  (4)

This is concave, but it generally has a strict interior maximum. The
maximum over the whole plane is alpha+lambda_max(H). Thus rank-one
affinity survives in all dimensions; endpoint maximization does not.
The off-diagonal cofactor term is an actual coupled contribution that
cannot be discarded or replaced by a free phase interval.

5. Why the exact tangent certificate controls the whole analytic curve.

Let V be the real vector space of permutation profiles of all squared
minors, including the constant profile. Exact row reduction gives
dim V=14. Evaluation on the six retained contacts has rank6, so

 L={f in V: f vanishes at those six contacts}

has dimension8. The ten profiles F0,M0,...,M8 all belong to L and span
an eight-dimensional space. They therefore span all of L.

For completeness, identities between these profiles on permutations
hold on every unitary. Indeed the exact coefficients

 c_pi(U)=sgn(pi) product_i u_(i,pi(i))/det U

satisfy, for every equal-size I,J,

 |det U_{I,J}|^2=sum_{pi:pi(I)=J} c_pi(U).

This follows either from the complementary-minor identity or by comparing
coefficients in

 det(Da+U Db U*)=sum_pi c_pi(U) product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)).

Consequently a linear relation vanishing on all permutation profiles
vanishes identically on U(4). This also shows that every Ft has a profile
in V, by the squared-minor expansion of the determinant.

Because all six ties are retained, Ft belongs to L. Choose a fixed linear
right inverse from L to the ten generator coefficients. Apply it only to

 Ft-F0-t F'_0=O(t^2).

Combining with (5) gives exact functions epsilon_j(t)=O(t^2) such that

 Ft=[1-(401443/528)t+epsilon_9(t)]F0
       +sum_(j=0)^8 [t lambda_j+epsilon_j(t)]Mj.             (6)

For every sufficiently small positive t, all ten coefficients in (6)
are strictly positive. In particular Ft(U)>=0 simultaneously for every
U. This proves the retained six-contact support inequality.

7.4 Exact absorption and the full local conclusion.

For each edge the actual analytic profile belongs to its fixed final-
contact space. The generators span that space. After including the
correction just discussed, the profile remainder after its constant and
linear terms is O(t^2). A fixed linear right inverse assigns an O(t^2)
coefficient correction. Thus the exact edge profile has coefficients

 b_l+t d_l+O(t^2).

They are positive for sufficiently small positive t: if b_l>0 this is
continuity; if b_l=0 it follows from d_l>0. Because there are finitely
many coefficients and five edges, one common positive parameter interval
works for all edges and every U. All five support inequalities therefore
hold simultaneously for the determinant. Section 7.1 proved that their
intersection is exactly the permutation-product hull. This proves the
full MOC theorem stated at the start of Section 7.

This is a local family theorem in dimension four. It uses the previously
proved tetrahedral base inequality; it neither proves positivity on every
terminal family nor repairs the failed universal safe-cone assertion.
The all-edge completion supersedes the single-direction limitation only
for the particular curve specified here, after the additional checks in
this section have been performed.

8. Uniform first-order positivity in the same six-contact stratum.

The large chosen spectral tangent is not necessary for the retained
support direction. There is an exact five-dimensional tangent statement.
Keep the same base point, eta, and six retained contacts. Let D be the
24-by-16 Jacobian of the deficits, with h equal to the retained reference
value, and let E be the five tie-equation rows. Exact row reduction gives
rank(D)=10 and rank(E)=5. Since ker D is contained in ker E, the deficit
image of the allowed spectral tangent space ker E has dimension5.

The four released labels are17,18,19,22. Define the following profiles:

 R17=M3+M8-F0/10,
 R18=M2+M5-F0/10,
 R19=(M5+M7+M8)/2+M6-F0/4,
 R22=M4+M8-F0/10.

Each Rj belongs to that actual tangent image, has value0 at permutation
index0, and takes values1 at its named released label and0 at the other
three. The scale profile F0 belongs to the image, vanishes at all four
released labels, and has value10 at index0. These five profiles are
independent and hence form a basis of the entire allowed tangent image.
Thus every actual fixed-six-contact spectral tangent is exactly

 alpha F0+a R17+b R18+c R19+d R22,

where a,b,c,d are its four released deficit slopes. For a transverse
departure in which all four old ties become strict, a,b,c,d are positive.

Two exact relations among the generators are

 -M0+M1+M2-M3-M5+M6=0,
 2M1-2M3-2M4-4M5-4M7-2M8+F0=0.

Subtract epsilon times the first relation and add epsilon times the
second to the preceding tangent decomposition. Its nine minor
coefficients become

 (epsilon, epsilon, b-epsilon, a-epsilon, d-2epsilon,
  b+c/2-3epsilon, c-epsilon, c/2-4epsilon,
  a+c/2+d-2epsilon).

Taking epsilon=min(a,b,c,d)/16 makes every one strictly positive; in
fact each is at least epsilon. The F0 coefficient is unrestricted at
first order because its zeroth-order coefficient is1. The full-space
span and O(t^2) absorption argument from Section5 now applies to every
such transverse analytic spectral curve. Therefore the retained support
inequality is locally safe throughout this transverse component of the
six-contact stratum, not just along the single tangent used in Section4.

The exact checker profile_terminal_tangent_verify.py verifies both ranks,
