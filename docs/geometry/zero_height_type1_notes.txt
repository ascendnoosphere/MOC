ZERO-HEIGHT SIX-CONTACT TYPE1: EXACT SAME-RAY CLOSURE
2026-10-17. Proves its MOC support inequality by rotation to an external
supporting line; it does not claim that arbitrary h=0 profiles have this form.

THEOREM. Assume all24 order-four matching products are nonzero and pairwise
distinct. Suppose the full active set at a support line through the origin
is S={0,1,3,6,7,8}, using zero-based lexicographic permutation labels.
This is type1 in zero_height_circle_saturation.json. Then the six active
products lie on the same open ray from the origin. Consequently their
MOC support inequality follows from the known external-vertex theorem
by a small rotation of the support direction and continuity.

1. Exact projective chart from the four circle constraints.
Write beta_j=-b_j, so products use a_i-beta_pi(i). Every matching uses
all eight labelled points exactly once. A common Mobius transformation
therefore multiplies all24 matching products by one common nonzero
scalar (using homogeneous brackets when a point is infinity).

The active transposition ratios give the four generalized circles
 {a1,a2,beta0,beta2}, {a2,a3,beta2,beta3},
 {a1,a3,beta1,beta2}, {a0,a1,beta0,beta1}.
Normalize beta2=infinity,a1=0,a2=1 and write a3=t. The first three
circles give beta0=x, beta1=yt, beta3=1+z(t-1), with x,y,z real.
The fourth circle gives a real cross ratio lambda and
 a0=lambda*x*y*t/[x(lambda-1)+yt].
Here lambda=a0(yt-x)/[x(yt-a0)].

Nonzero matching products exclude every cross collision a_i=beta_j.
Pairwise-distinct matching products exclude repeated a's or repeated
beta's. Thus all eight points are pairwise distinct; every denominator
used below is nonzero and x,y,z,lambda avoid0 and1. Also t is nonreal:
if t were real, all eight transformed points would lie on the real line,
all matching products would have one common real phase, and the active
nonzero product would force all24 labels active at a zero-height line.
That contradicts the stated full active set of six labels.

2. Normalize product0 and list the active ratios.
Let q_pi=z_pi/z_0. Then the six active ratios are respectively
 q0=1,
 q1=z/(z-1),
 q3=z(y-1)/[y(z-1)],
 q6=-1/(lambda-1),
 q7=-z/[(lambda-1)(z-1)],
 q8=-(x-1)/[x(lambda-1)].
They are real, as required. Because z0 is a nonzero active product, the
original support functional on all products is a nonzero real multiple
of Im q_pi. All inactive imaginary parts therefore have the same strict
sign. Every ratio of two inactive imaginary parts is strictly positive.

3. Five exact inactive-gap ratios and their signs.
Write G_i=Im q_i; the following ratios are orientation independent:
 G5/G2=-(y-1)|t|^2/[(z-1)|t-1|^2];
 G10/G9=(x-1)/[z|t-1|^2];
 G13/G9=lambda*x^2(y-1)/|x-yt|^2;
 G14/G10=lambda*x*y(z-1)|t-1|^2/|x-yt|^2;
 G9/G2=y*z|t|^2/[x(lambda-1)(z-1)].
The involved labels2,5,9,10,13,14 are all inactive. All ratios are
positive and all squared absolute values are strictly positive.
Consequently
 (y-1)(z-1)<0,
 lambda(y-1)>0,
 lambda*x*y(z-1)>0,
 z(x-1)>0,
 y*z/[x(lambda-1)(z-1)]>0.
The first two relations imply lambda(z-1)<0; the third then gives xy<0.

If y>0, then x<0, hence z<0 from z(x-1)>0. Thus z-1<0,
y>1, lambda>0. The final sign relation forces lambda<1.
This is chamber A: x<0,y>1,z<0,0<lambda<1.

If y<0, then x>0. The first relation gives z>1, the second lambda<0,
and z(x-1)>0 gives x>1.
This is chamber B: x>1,y<0,z>1,lambda<0.
These exhaust the possibilities because y cannot be zero. Substitution
of either chamber's signs in the six displayed active ratios shows that
all six ratios are strictly POSITIVE. Hence all active products lie on
the same open ray; opposite-ray contact cannot occur in this type.

4. Rotation and the external-vertex theorem.
Let the original supported products be w_pi=eta*z_pi, Re w_pi<=0,
with equality exactly on S. Write w0=i*kappa, kappa real nonzero.
For active labels w_pi=i*kappa*q_pi and q_pi>0. For epsilon>0 small,
replace eta by eta_epsilon=exp(i*epsilon*sign(kappa))*eta. Then
 Re(eta_epsilon*z_pi)=-|kappa|*q_pi*sin(epsilon)<0 for active labels.
All finitely many inactive products already have strictly negative real
part, so they remain strictly negative for sufficiently small epsilon.
Thus h_epsilon=max Re(eta_epsilon*z_pi)<0.

The established external-vertex theorem gives the MOC supporting
inequality at eta_epsilon. Its two sides are continuous in epsilon;
letting epsilon decrease to0 proves the original zero-height support
inequality for every unitary mixer. This argument uses only direction
rotation at the fixed spectra, not an unproved inward spectral deformation.

If the chosen version of the external theorem supplies an ordinary-minor
cone decomposition, closedness of that finite generated cone also yields
ordinary-minor membership of the zero-height limit. The support inequality
itself needs only the usual external-vertex conclusion, so no stronger
cone-membership claim is assumed here.
