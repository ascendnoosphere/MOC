EXACT REAL-LIFT EXCLUSION OF A NINE-CONTACT MOC FACE
2026-10-17

Scope and result
---------------
This is a proved exclusion of one full terminal face orbit, not a proof of
4x4 MOC. The face has ray indices (3,19,21,26,52) in the canonical82-ray
ordering and orbit size1152 in the audited nine-contact census. Every actual
spectral support gap in its positive mixed interior forces two matching
products to be equal. Thus it cannot be a terminal counterexample with all24
matching products pairwise distinct. The b=0 boundary has10 contacts and is
covered by the separate, already-proved ten-contact theorem. A zero mixed
coefficient leaves an ordinary nonnegative-minor combination.

This argument uses the full real spectral-lift equations. The necessary
projected degree18 equation by itself does not exclude this face.

Notation and imported algebraic facts
------------------------------------
Permutations are lexicographically ordered, zero-based. Let z_pi be the24
matching products, eta a nonzero supporting direction, and
  g_pi=h-Re(eta*z_pi)>=0.
The14 selected matching values are coordinates on the common-minor space;
E_i evaluates the i-th permutation. Let e=(1,...,1). The exact cubic F and
its Hessian H are defined and checked in closing_moc4_model_verify.py and
closing_moc4_model_certificate.json. Write Q=D_e F.

The prior model proof establishes:
  grad F(-eta*z)=0,
  F(g)=h Q(g),
  N(g)=0,
where N is a homogeneous polynomial of degree18 satisfying, for Q!=0,
  N(g)=det(Q(g)H(g)-F(g)H(e))/Q(g)^12.
The degree18 lemma and the Q=0 branch are proved in closing_moc4_notes.txt;
no unverified degree truncation is used here.

Writing -eta*z=x+i y, with real x,y, yields
  H(x)y=0,                 grad F(y)=grad F(x).             (1)
This follows directly by polarizing the quadratic gradient.

The82 rays and their completeness are separately certified by
cone82_completeness_verify.py and its exact certificate. Both new verifiers
regenerate the rays and compare their ordering to the shared census data.
The face-orbit size and its role in the full contact classification use
terminal_profile_nine_census_verify.py. The present proof works on this
specific face without relying on the orbit count.

The nonic equation and parameter coverage
----------------------------------------
Normalize the mixed coefficient to1 and write
  g=a*r3+b*r19+c*r21+d*r26+f52,   a,b,c,d>=0.
Its nine common zeros are [0,1,3,5,6,10,11,12,13]. Exact identities are
  F(g)=(a+1)(b+1),
  Q(g)=1+b+cd-bd,
  N(g)=-4[(a+1)^2(b+1)(c+1)R]^2,
  R=(a+1)(b+1)(d+1)+bd^2-cd(b+d+1).
The pure-standard-library checker global_moc4_nine_nonic_verify.py proves
these identities using all7315 nodes (2i,2j,2k,2l), i+j+k+l<=18. Q is odd,
hence nonzero, at every node. The already-proved degree18 bound makes this
an exact unisolvent polynomial certificate, not numerical evidence.

For an actual spectral gap, F>0 implies Q!=0. Also R=0 and d=0 is impossible.
Put
  S=b+d+1,
  L=(a+2)(d+1)+b(1-d).
Every remaining nonnegative solution is parametrized by
  c=((a+1)(b+1)(d+1)+bd^2)/(d S),
  Q=(b+1)L/S,
  h=(a+1)S/L.
Thus d>0, c>0, and L!=0. If b=0, removing r19 enlarges the common zero set
from9 to10 labels, which the checker verifies exactly. Hence it remains to
treat a>=0, b>0, d>0, L!=0.

A kernel basis valid on the whole remaining domain
-------------------------------------------------
Let x=g-h e. The certificate stores two explicit rational14-vectors u,v.
Their only denominator factors are
  a+1, b, d, d+1, a+d+1, S, L,
all nonzero on the stated domain. Define covectors
  p=e_0^*-e_5^*,
  r=E_0-E_12=e_0^*-e_10^*.
The verifier checks coefficientwise
  H(x)u=H(x)v=0,
  p(u)=1, r(u)=0, p(v)=0, r(v)=1.                           (2)
Furthermore, as an exact rational identity,
  det(H(x)+p p^T+r r^T)
   =4b^2(a+1)^4(b+1)^2(d+1)^2(a+d+1)^2/L^2 >0.             (3)
Here p,r on the left are their coordinate columns. Thus H(x) has rank12
and its entire kernel is span(u,v), everywhere on this domain.

For transparency, the determinant certificate computes one exact12-minor
and the complementary2x2 wedges of u,v. The general rank12 complementary-
minor formula, together with the normalization in(2), gives(3) on a dense
open set. Both sides are rational functions, so the identity extends through
zeros of that chosen minor or wedge. The final expression has no such
exceptional denominator. This is why temporary Gaussian-elimination pivots
do not exclude any parameter points.

The two-dimensional quadratic-pencil signature
-----------------------------------------------
Let G=grad F(x), J=H(u)v. The certificate verifies
  G_13=b+1>0,
  grad F(u)=kappa G for some finite real kappa,
  grad F(v)=-C G,     C=S^2/[4b^2(a+d+1)^2]>0.              (4)
No formula or sign for kappa is required. Proportionality in(4) is checked
without dividing by a parameter-dependent polynomial: all identities
  (grad F(u))_i G_13=(grad F(u))_13 G_i
are verified exactly.

The following two exact2x2 determinants prove G and J independent:
  G_12 J_13-G_13 J_12
    =(b+1)S(bd-b-d-1)/(bL),
  G_11 J_13-G_13 J_11
    =(b+1)^2(d+1)(b(a+d)-d-1)/(bdL).
Their bracketed numerators differ by b(a+1)>0, so they cannot both vanish.

Now let y be any real solution of(1). By(2)-(3), y=s u+t v for real s,t.
Polarization and(4) give
  grad F(y)=(kappa s^2-Ct^2)G+st J=G.
Independence implies
  st=0,     kappa s^2-Ct^2=1.
If t!=0, then s=0 and -Ct^2=1, impossible over the reals. Consequently t=0,
and r(y)=0. Since g_0=g_12=0, also r(x)=0. Thus
  z_0=z_12.
This excludes the mixed interior whenever all24 matching products are
pairwise distinct. Formally, the discarded noncollision branch would have
  (r(y))^2=-4b^2(a+d+1)^2/(b+d+1)^2.

General reusable lemma
----------------------
The same argument applies to any real cubic spectral model: if H(x) has a
2-dimensional kernel with basis u,v, grad F(u) is parallel to grad F(x),
grad F(v) is a strictly negative multiple of grad F(x), and H(u)v is
independent of grad F(x), every real imaginary lift lies along u. This gives
a finite exact way to exploit the real structure after a projected-secant
or Hessian test has stopped eliminating a cost face.
