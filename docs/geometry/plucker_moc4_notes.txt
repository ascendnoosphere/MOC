1. A universal positive budget in order four

Let a,b be arbitrary complex four-tuples, eta a complex support functional,
z_pi=product_i(a_i+b_{pi(i)}), h=max_pi Re(eta z_pi), and
    g_pi=h-Re(eta z_pi) >=0.
Use zero-based labels temporarily. Let P have independently selected swaps
(01),(23), and Q have independently selected swaps (02),(13). For each
s=(s1,s2,s3,s4) in {0,1}^4, put pi_s=Q_s o P_s and delta_s=g_{pi_s}.
The complementary outcome is bar(s)=(1-s1,...,1-s4). Define
    K=sum_s (-1)^(s1+s2+s3+s4) delta_s,
    B=2 sum_{s1=0} sqrt(delta_s delta_bar(s)),
    m=max_pi g_pi.
The choice s1=0 simply selects one member of each complementary pair.

THEOREM. For all these actual spectral data,
    B m^17 >= 2^(-144) |K|^18.                         (1)
This form includes m=0. In particular K!=0 forces B>0, quantitatively.
Every relabeling of the two matching layers has the same result.

This is stronger than the previously proved B=0 => K=0, but considerably
weaker than the desired linear estimate B>=|K|. The proof also applies to
all nonnegative common-minor costs lying on N=0, where N is the homogeneous
degree-18 spectral obstruction polynomial defined below.

3. Removing a small amount of cost to reach a zero-budget face

Write g=sum_r lambda_r r, lambda_r>=0, in the 82-ray cone. All ray entries
are integers in {0,1,2}; their alternating cycle values satisfy |K(r)|<=2.
In each of the eight complementary pairs, choose an endpoint with the
smaller delta. Delete every ray having a positive entry at any chosen
endpoint. Let h0 be the sum of deleted rays and g0=g-h0.

All costs remain pointwise nonnegative, g0 vanishes at the chosen endpoints,
and therefore B(g0)=0. Each deleted ray is at least1 at a chosen endpoint,
so, without needing the rays' supports to be disjoint,
    sum_deleted lambda_r <= sum_chosen delta_s <= B(g)/2.
Consequently
    0<=h0_pi<=B(g),          |K(h0)|<=B(g),
    |K(g0)| >= |K(g)|-B(g).                            (2)
This is why a zero-budget face theorem can give a positive quantitative
estimate on the entire spectral cost set.

4. Uniform lower bound for N on every zero-budget face

The earlier certificate has 96 faces containing a nonzero-K ray, in two
symmetry orbits of sizes64 and32. Their two positive factorizations imply
    |N(g0)| >= |K(g0)|^18 / 2^16.                      (3)
For clarity, on a first representative cone, with all coefficients
nonnegative and mixed coefficient alpha,
 -N/4=[alpha^2(alpha+a)(alpha+b)(alpha+e)
                   (alpha+a+d)^2(alpha+b+c)^2]^2.
On a second representative subcone,
 -N/4=[alpha^3(alpha+a+c)^2(alpha+b+d)^2
                   ((alpha+a)(alpha+b)+e(alpha+a+b))]^2.
Each bracket is at least alpha^9, and |K|=2alpha. A seven-ray second-type
face first cancels its opposite mixed rays; their sum is a positive sum of
ordinary rays, giving one of the two equivalent six-ray subcones.

The exact N, not merely its zero set, is invariant under the row/column
relabelings and inversion used in the census. Indeed their coordinate
matrices T are integral and unimodular, Te=e, and F(Tx)=epsilon F(x),
epsilon=+/-1. With Q=D_eF and H the Hessian of F,

The determinant is unchanged and Q^12 is unchanged. Polynomial extension
covers Q=0. The new verifier checks the signed-cubic and unimodular

which is homogeneous of degree18 and vanishes on every actual spectral

6. Completing the proof

All14 coordinates of the segment from g0 to g are in[0,m]. Since N is
homogeneous of degree18, the sum of coefficient norms of its first
partials is18||N||_1. Equations (2),(4) and N(g)=0 give
    |N(g0)| <=18*2^105*m^17*B.                         (5)
If B<|K|/2, (2),(3) give |N(g0)|>=|K|^18/2^34.
Combining with (5), and using18<=2^5, proves(1).
If B>=|K|/2, use |K|<=8m, since the alternating sum has eight positive and
eight negative terms in[0,m]. Then
    |K|/2 >=2^(-52)|K|^18/m^17,
which is stronger than(1). If m=0, every gap and K vanish.

The exact biased-cycle capacity
 Cap(delta)=inf_x sum_s delta_s exp(sum_j(-1)^s_j x_j)
satisfies Cap>=B by complementary-pair AM--GM. Hence(1) holds with Cap in
place of B. It does NOT imply Cap>=|K|, the needed all-phase cycle estimate.
