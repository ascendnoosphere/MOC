FINITE PROPAGATION OF THE UNIVERSAL WEIGHTED-MINOR INEQUALITY
2026-10-17

These results close additional coefficient faces of the order4 MOC problem.
They do not prove unrestricted MOC4. All positivity conclusions below hold
for every complex unitary matrix, without a distinct-product assumption.

Input theorem
-------------
The independently audited theorem in global_weighted_minor_notes.txt gives,
for every tau>0 and every U in U(4),
 f(U)+tau*rho(23,34)(U)+tau^{-1}*rho(13,13)(U)>=0,
where f=rho(3,3)+rho(2,4)+rho(12,13)-rho(13,23).
Row/column relabelings and transposition give the same theorem for48 mixed
rays. Each mixed ray has three compensating pairs, the edges of a triangle
on three ordinary-minor rays. The exact witnesses used here are checked
against all24 permutation costs; the common-minor model identifies these
costs with the corresponding unitary functionals.

The ray ordering is the canonical82-ray ordering from
cone82_completeness_verify.py, and is regenerated in both new verifiers.
Write r_j(U) for the squared-minor functional of an ordinary ray j<34 and
f_j(U) for a mixed ray. Every r_j(U)>=0.

1. New full face (3,12,21,26,78)
--------------------------------
Let g=a*r3+b*r12+c*r21+d*r26+f78, a,b,c,d>=0. Put A=a+1,B=b+1.
The exact degree18 identity is N(g)=-4P(g)^2, where
 P=AB(AB-cd)[AB(c+d+1)+cd(a+b+1)].
The second bracket is strictly positive and AB>=1. Thus an actual spectral
support cost, which satisfies N(g)=0, has cd=AB>=1. In particular c,d>0.

The weighted theorem relabels to
 f78+tau*r21+tau^{-1}*r26>=0.
One explicit relabeling is transposition followed by
 pi -> pi^{-1} o (3,0,1,2), with zero-based labels. The checker verifies it
on every permutation. Taking tau=c and using d>=1/c gives
 g(U)>=f78(U)+c*r21(U)+(1/c)*r26(U)>=0.
Thus this full coefficient face is safe for all complex unitary matrices.
Its symmetry orbit contains288 nine-contact faces.

Verification: global_moc4_weighted_propagation_verify.py checks the exact
relabeling and all7315 lower-grid nodes for N=-4P^2, using the previously
proved total degree18 bound. Q=1+cd>0 on the grid. It uses standard-library
integer arithmetic, with no approximate fitting or numerical tolerances.

2. The prior lift face also has a shorter positivity proof
----------------------------------------------------------
For g=a*r3+b*r19+c*r21+d*r26+f52, the independently certified nonic forces
 cd=[(a+1)(b+1)(d+1)+bd^2]/(b+d+1).
Therefore
 cd-1=(d+1)[a(b+1)+bd]/(b+d+1)>=0.
The compensation pair for f52 is(r21,r26), so the same weighted argument
proves g(U)>=0 for every U. This strengthens the earlier imaginary-lift
exclusion by removing its distinct-product assumption. It is the SAME
face orbit as the lift theorem and must not be counted twice.

The two previously constrained faces (3,19,21,24,46) and
(3,20,21,27,53) similarly force cd=a+1 and bd=a+1, respectively, and are
already covered by the preceding baseline. The other f78 face
(3,12,21,30,78) is proved separately in global_weighted_minor_notes.txt.

3. New triangle-compensator face (3,21,26,30,52)
------------------------------------------------
Let g=a*r3+b*r21+c*r26+d*r30+f52 with nonnegative coefficients. Let P be
the explicit nonic stored in global_moc4_triangle_certificate.json.
The verifier establishes the following exact facts:
  F(g)=1+a+bcd, Q(g)=1+bc+bd+cd>0,
  N(g)=-4P(a,b,c,d)^2,
  P is symmetric in b,c,d.
The N identity is checked at all7315 unisolvent degree18 nodes.

Two coefficient-sign certificates locate every nonnegative zero of P.
First, substitute
 b=x/(1+x), c=y/(1+y), d=z/(1+z),  x,y,z>=0,
and multiply by(1+x)^2(1+y)^2(1+z)^2. The resulting polynomial in a,x,y,z
has100 nonzero coefficients, all strictly positive, including its constant.
Consequently P>0 whenever b,c,d<1.

Second, substitute
 b=1+x,
 c=y/[b(1+y+z)], d=z/[b(1+y+z)], x,y,z>=0,
and multiply by b^4(1+y+z)^4. The resulting polynomial has345 nonzero
coefficients, all strictly positive, including its constant. These
parameters cover exactly b>=1 and b(c+d)<1: the inverse is
 x=b-1, y=bc/[1-b(c+d)], z=bd/[1-b(c+d)].
Consequently P>0 throughout that region.

Thus at any nonnegative zero of P, choose a largest member of b,c,d and
call it b by symmetry. Necessarily
 b>=1 and b(c+d)>=1.                                      (*)
In particular c+d>0.

All three weighted pairs of f52 are certified:
 (r21,r26), (r21,r30), (r26,r30).
Choose the two pairs incident to the largest coefficient. Convex-combine
 f52+b*r21+(1/b)*r26>=0,
 f52+b*r21+(1/b)*r30>=0
with weights c/(c+d) and d/(c+d). This gives
 f52+b*r21+[c/(b(c+d))]*r26+[d/(b(c+d))]*r30>=0.
By(*), both displayed ordinary coefficients are no larger than c,d.
Adding the nonnegative remainders and a*r3 proves g(U)>=0 for every U.
The same argument applies if either of the other coefficients is largest,
using the corresponding two certified pairs. No unproved symmetry of the
unitary functional is being assumed.

The face's symmetry orbit contains192 nine-contact faces. The new files
are global_moc4_triangle_certificate.json and global_moc4_triangle_verify.py.
The checker is standard-library only and verifies all three relabeling
witnesses, the two polynomial-sign expansions, and the nonic identity.

4. Counts and a precise limitation of this propagation method
------------------------------------------------------------
Relative to the previously audited19 of29 nine-contact orbit types, the
new lift/weighted face52 adds1, the two f78 faces add2, and the triangle
face adds1. The total is therefore23 of29, with no overlap counted twice.
The six remaining types contain3552 faces; the23 treated types contain

conditional terminal classification under that census's assumptions; it
is not an unrestricted proof of MOC4.
