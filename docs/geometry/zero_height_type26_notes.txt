ZERO-HEIGHT TYPE 26: EXACT ORDINARY-MINOR POSITIVITY

Status: proved, with exact verification. The original facet/cubic proof was
independently checked by the metaproof agent. The final direct decomposition
below removes any facet-enumeration dependence from the local positivity proof.

Conventions and scope
---------------------
Permutation coordinates are the 24 permutations of (0,1,2,3) in lexicographic
order. Ray indices refer to the verified 82-ray cone of nonnegative profiles,
as regenerated in zero_height_type26_verify.py. The first 34 rays are ordinary
minor indicators; their evaluation on a unitary is a squared minor and is
therefore nonnegative. The symbol f74 denotes mixed ray 74.

The six-contact face has zero coordinates (0,3,5,9,11,12) and rays
    (3,8,14,21,22,27,29,31,32,74).
Its symmetry orbit has 576 faces in the zero-height census. Write
    g = sum_{j in O} a_j r_j + alpha f74,
    O = (3,8,14,21,22,27,29,31,32), a_j >= 0, alpha >= 0.

LOCAL THEOREM. If g belongs to this face and F(g)=0 for the verified skew
cubic F, then g is a nonnegative combination of ordinary minor indicators.
This theorem does not require an unproved converse from F(g)=0 to spectral
realizability. Only the necessary cubic identity is used.

Proof of the coefficient bound
------------------------------
Suppose alpha > a21+a31, and put u=alpha-a21-a31 > 0. Substitute
    alpha = a21+a31+u
into the cubic. Its exact expansion has 101 nonzero monomials, all with
strictly positive integer coefficients, and contains u^3 with coefficient 1.
Every variable in this expansion is nonnegative. Hence F(g) >= u^3 > 0,
contrary to the hypothesis. Consequently
    alpha <= a21+a31.

This expansion is reconstructed directly from the 105 matching linear forms
of the verified cubic model:
    F(x) = (1/6) sum_M epsilon_M (L_M x)^3.
The checker substitutes the generators r21+f74 and r31+f74 for a21 and a31,
f74 for u, and the other ordinary generators unchanged. It verifies all 101
coefficients exactly and compares them with zero_height_type26_outside.json.
There is no interpolation or floating-point arithmetic in this calculation.

Direct positive decomposition
-----------------------------
The following identities hold in all 24 permutation coordinates:
    f74+r21 = r3+r8+r29,
    f74+r31 = r8+r14+r27.
Choose s=min(alpha,a21) and t=alpha-s. The coefficient bound gives
    s,t >= 0, s <= a21, t <= a31, s+t=alpha.
Then
    g = (a3+s) r3 + (a8+s+t) r8 + (a14+t) r14
        + (a21-s) r21 + a22 r22 + (a27+t) r27
        + (a29+s) r29 + (a31-t) r31 + a32 r32.
Every coefficient is nonnegative. This proves the local theorem directly.

Application to zero-height spectral profiles
-------------------------------------------
For an actual support deficit g=h e-Re(eta z), the previously verified
spectral cubic identity is F(g)=h Q(g). At h=0 this gives F(g)=0. Therefore
every actual profile in the type-26 face is ordinary-minor positive, and its
unitary evaluation is nonnegative. All row/column relabelings and transpose
images have the same conclusion.

An independent geometric check
-----------------------------
The nine ordinary rays have rank eight. Exact enumeration gives 12 facets of
their cone. On the full ten-ray face only one additional inequality is needed:
    L(g)=g_2+g_6-g_8+g_16=a21+a31-alpha >= 0.
This agrees with the cubic bound and the direct decomposition. The proof
above, however, does not need completeness of this facet enumeration.
