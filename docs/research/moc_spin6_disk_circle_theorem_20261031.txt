SPIN6 DISK: CIRCLE THEOREM AND THE REMAINING INTERIOR SCHUR GAP
31 October 2026. Unrestricted complex MOC4 is not proved.

The canonical disk of docs/research/moc_global_spin6_bloch_messiah_disk_20261031.txt
eliminates the radial SO6 coordinate. This note proves that every such
circle lies under the matching maximum, and isolates the precise scalar
inequality that is still open.

1. Traceless Weyl identification

For X,Y in C^3 let a,b be the traceless spinor spectra of
moc_global_spin6_hessian.tracezero_sources, and let A,B,constant be its
coefficient matrices. For each of the 24 audited block maps
(sigma, signs, pi) in build/moc_global_spin6_hessian.json,

  constant + sum_i (A[i,sigma(i)] + signs_i B[i,sigma(i)])
      = prod_k (a_k + b_{pi(k)})

as a polynomial identity in the six coordinates of X and Y. The checker
expands all 24 differences and gets zero. The six maps with all signs
positive are exactly the six permutation assignments of C = A+B.

Replay:
  PYTHONPATH=code/research python3 -B code/research/moc_unrestricted_disk_gap.py
The identity step prints traceless_weyl_identities = 24 before any search.
A separate 30-source float check of the six positive vertices against the
permutation products has error at most 6e-15.

2. Circle theorem

Fix a phase eta and write c0 = Re(eta*constant), C = Re(eta*(A+B)),
D = Re(eta*(A-B)). On a canonical disk,

  F(z) = F0 + 2 Re(z S) + d |z|^2,    |z| <= 1,

with the audited formulas for F0, S, d. At |z| = 1 the Bogoliubov partner
Y vanishes and X = V diag(z,z,1) W* is unitary, so

  F(z) = c0 + sum_ij C_ij |X_ij|^2.

The matrix (|X_ij|^2) is doubly stochastic. Birkhoff's theorem gives

  F(z) <= max over 6 permutations of (c0 + sum_i C[i,sigma(i)]).

The right-hand side is the maximum M6 of the six all-positive Weyl
vertices, hence at most the full matching maximum M. Therefore every
circle maximum obeys

  F0 + d + 2|S| <= M6 <= M.

The identity F(e^{it}) = c0 + <C, |X|^2> was checked on 200 Haar frames
and 8 phases, with error at most 2e-14. No source disk in the 2500-frame
census had its circle above M6.

3. Interior reduction

The quadratic in z has circle maximum F0+d+2r, r = |S|. If d >= -r the
disk maximum is that circle value, so section 2 closes the disk. If
d < -r the maximum is interior,

  max F = F0 + r^2/(-d) = (F0+d+2r) + (r+d)^2/(-d),

and the second term is the exact stick-up above the circle. The Schur
certificate

  lambda >= 0 and [[M-F0-lambda, -S], [-conj(S), lambda-d]] positive semidefinite

exists if and only if M >= max F. Consequently, inside the canonical
chart, the support inequality holds for every disk if and only if

  (M - F0)(-d) >= |S|^2    whenever d < -|S|.

The chart-covering argument that every SO6 matrix lies on some disk is
the one already written in the Bloch-Messiah note. This file does not
reprove that covering. With it, the displayed scalar inequality is the
remaining unrestricted MOC4 statement for traceless sources, hence, by
the Mobius normalization in moc_global_tracezero_spinor_normal_form_20261031.txt,
for all disjoint simple spectra.

4. The six positive vertices do not control the interior

Source coupling is necessary, and the eighteen signed vertices are
necessary as well.

An uncoupled real pair (C,D), drawn from seed 7 of
uncoupled_obstruction in the same script, has an interior peak 0.345
above its own Birkhoff maximum M6. Arbitrary block costs are not enough.

For actual traceless sources the same gap occurs. Of 2500 normalized
Haar samples (seed 20261031), 1012 disks were in the regime d < -r.
In 341 samples the interior peak exceeded M6, while every sample stayed
under the full matching maximum M. The smallest slack M - max F in the
whole cloud is 8.0e-4, and it sits on a circle rather than in the
interior. Among the 1012 interior disks the smallest slack is 5.8e-3
and the median slack of all 2500 disks is 4.1e-2. The figure
  build/moc_unrestricted_20261031/disk/interior_gap.png
shows only the interior regime. Points below the diagonal are exactly
the open inequality stick-up <= circle slack.

5. Numerical sharpness, and what the near-equality is

Minimizing the slack with no interior constraint (36 BFGS starts, seed
2026103112) produced no negative value. The smallest slack was 2.1e-6,
on a circle. The smallest interior slack in that run was 2.7e-4.

Forcing d+r <= -1e-4 (8 SLSQP starts, seed 2026103119) drives the
interior slack down to 1.1e-6. The best point is not a counterexample
and not a new equality stratum. Its two leading spinor coordinates of X
agree through 1e-6, so rows 0 and 1 of both Re(eta A) and Re(eta B)
agree. The top two Weyl vertices,

  sigma (1,2,0) and (2,1,0), signs (-1,-1,+1),

differ by 3e-12 and both equal M. The block masses at the disk center
are a fractional matrix at Frobenius distance 0.645 from the nearest
single vertex, with the same value, because that face is flat. At the
same point M6 = -2.1e-4 while M = 2.46e-2, so the signed vertices are
what meet the interior peak. Data:
  build/moc_unrestricted_20261031/disk/interior_geometry.json
  build/moc_unrestricted_20261031/disk/gap.json
