Reusable full matching-SOS contact oracle in the homogeneous cap gauge
31 October 2026

The new oracle minimizes a fixed cost over a full matching-SOS contact-face
dual, using y.f34=-1. It retains all14 matching features before removing
directions forced to vanish by contacts. There is no8-feature restriction,
trace normalization, or imposed cap-strength cut.

Public Python interface

 from moc_full_contact_sos_oracle import FullContactSosOracle
 oracle=FullContactSosOracle((9,))
 y24,info=oracle.solve(g24,warm_start=None,mu_final=2e-7,certify=False)

Here g24 is a full24-vector and must vanish at the specified contacts.
y24 has zero weights at contacts, y24.f34=-1, and positive contact-null
moment. A previous full24-vector may be passed as warm_start.

Exact certificate replay is optional:

 y24,info=oracle.solve(g24,certify=True)
 completed24,completion=oracle.complete_dual(y24,g24,certify=True)

solve(...,certify=True) converts the binary floating weights to exact
rationals, enforces the cap gauge exactly, and verifies a positive rational
LDL factorization of the contact-null moment. The exact rational weights
are in info['exact_dual']['weight_rationals']. For cap-zero contacts such
as index9, complete_dual adds contact masses and verifies a FULL14 by14
positive moment in exact rational arithmetic. This preserves the pairing
with every cost that vanishes at those contacts and preserves y.f34=-1.
The completion's exact rational weights are in completion['weight_rationals'].

These exact checks concern the supplied binary-rational costs and weights.
For a newly discovered actual spectral obstruction, its spectral products
must still be replayed in exact arithmetic; float-array input alone is not
promoted to an exact actual spectral cost.

Optimization model and implementation

Let B be a 24 by14 integer matching-feature basis, C a fixed contact set,
and E an integer basis of ker(B_C). Set F=BE. The moment condition is

 H_y=F^T diag(y) F >=0,
 y_i=0 for i in C,
 y.f34=-1.

Every PSD Gram reproducing a cost zero on C annihilates the contact feature
vectors, so E removes forced directions without weakening the full SOS test.
For C={9}, F has13 independent columns and the moment map has rank23 on
the23 remaining weights. The dual includes every direction in this space.

The existing independently exact positive f34 dual supplies a strictly
feasible start after restriction to the contact kernel. Its positive moment
is whitened to the identity. SVD makes the affine moment directions
orthonormal in the Frobenius inner product. A log-determinant central path
then uses damped Newton steps with PSD-preserving Cholesky backtracking.
Only moment-map kernel directions are removed; the cap and cost must lie
in the retained linear image, otherwise the API raises an explicit error.

The oracle stops immediately at a sufficiently negative feasible pairing
when stop_at_negative=True. Such a point is already a valid separating dual
and an upper bound on the optimization infimum; it need not be a minimizer.
This also handles unbounded negative sections without falsely reporting
that failed convergence certifies nonmembership.

The result includes the actual pairing, cap-gauge residual, moment eigenvalue
margins, a conservative floating arithmetic-radius diagnostic, central-path
gap, KKT residual and a repaired primal candidate. A positive feasible dual
pairing by itself is NOT a proof of a positive optimum. In particular,
repaired_primal_bound_candidate is not certified when the repaired primal
Gram is indefinite. The boolean repaired_primal_numerically_psd and its
minimum eigenvalue make that distinction explicit. Negative early stops
have no reported central-path gap or primal-bound candidate.

The actual finite countercost with a separately proved positive13-dimensional
Gram was normalized by its positive cap coefficient t. The oracle returned
pairing approximately21.5290355051, cap pairing exactly-1 to floating output,
and contact-null moment minimum eigenvalue approximately2.7408e-8. Exact
rational LDL replay of that returned dual passed. The repaired primal at
this control has a small negative eigenvalue approximately-1.81e-8, which is
reported as indefinite rather than used as a rigorous lower bound.

For the planted non-SOS cost f34, the initial feasible dual has pairing-1.
Exact rational contact-moment LDL passed. Adding contact mass9 gives a
FULL14-dimensional exact positive moment, with minimum LDL pivot about
.0006752383 and exact pairing-1 with f34. The homogeneous normalization
therefore detects the intended non-SOS control without an external cut.

Ten nearby perturbed costs also passed warm-start solves, with positive
moment margins. On this machine they took roughly.016seconds each; this
is an implementation timing, not a guarantee. A cold control solve took
roughly.013seconds, excluding optional exact certificate serialization.
