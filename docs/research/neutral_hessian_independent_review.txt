Independent review: neutral Hessian for actual conjugate secants
2026-10-30

Verdict. The proof in session_actual_neutral_hessian.txt is valid, using the
imported irreducibility, dimension, contraction and critical-ideal facts
recorded in CORANK_TWO_LIFT_EXACTNESS.md. It proves that an actual rank-12
spectral midpoint has inertia (6,6,2); it is not a proof of unrestricted MOC.
The sample and the explicit point below have been checked exactly, while the
HMSV source inputs and their coordinate bridge are imported from the existing
independent audit rather than reproved here.

Density. The finite spectral-product map has irreducible closure of dimension
six (closing_high_contact_standalone.txt). The verified gradient identities
put that image in the irreducible six-dimensional critical cone X, so its
closure is X. Two independent spectral inputs dominate X x X, and addition
therefore dominates S. The locus of smooth S and transverse endpoint tangent
spaces is a nonempty Zariski open condition on the pair space. Nonemptiness
of the tangent rank condition is checked by neutral_verify.py; smoothness is
obtained by dominance of addition and density of the smooth locus, not merely
from the single tangent-rank calculation. If a complex polynomial in the two
spectral parameter copies vanishes for (z,conj z), writing z=u+iv and
complexifying u,v makes that polynomial vanish identically. Thus the conjugate
pairs meet every nonempty pair-space open condition and their good locus is
Euclidean dense in the original real spectral-parameter space.

At good secants, the mixed Hessian block on T_w X plus T_conj(w) X is zero:
H_w annihilates T_w X, and H_conj(w) annihilates T_conj(w) X. Its real
restriction has equal positive/negative indices and even nullity. The
contraction gives rank(H_x|W)<=10; since rank H_x=12, ker H_x is contained
in W. Also x lies in W and in the radical of the restriction, but not in the
full kernel: if H_x x=0 then grad F(x)=0, and the critical ideal gives a
kernel of dimension at least six. The restricted radical has dimension at
least 3, hence at least 4, and at most 4 because W has codimension 2 and the
full radical has dimension 2. It is exactly 4, with inertia (4,4,4). The
two radical directions outside the full kernel pair nondegenerately with
the complement, giving two hyperbolic planes and full inertia (6,6,2).

All actual points belong to S and the contraction implies rank H_x<=12
throughout S, including singular points. At any actual rank-12 point,
approximating by good actual points is legitimate; a nonzero 12-minor
keeps rank at least 12 nearby, so the nearby ranks are exactly 12 and their
inertia is locally constant. This establishes the extension even if the
limiting secant is singular or a spectral degeneracy is present.

A genuine unbalanced complex secant. For the coefficient point
 (a,b,c,d)=(1,8,2,3) on rays (3,16,21,30,53), the independent portable
 code/research/verify_neutral_hessian_face16.py
recomputes the coordinate vector, F=12, Q=19, h=12/19, and exact Hessian
inertia (5,7,2), using exact characteristic-polynomial root counts.
It also proves secant membership directly: with K a rational kernel basis,
y=K(u,v), the equations grad F(y)=grad F(x) have Groebner basis
  u^2-(48/43)uv-9808/16967,  v^2+12943/16967.
These have complex solutions; H_x y=0 and the cubic identities then show
x+i y and x-i y are critical, so x is their midpoint in S. The second
polynomial also shows directly that no real y exists at this point. Thus the
point is a genuine real point of the complex secant excluded as an actual
conjugate secant. The inertia theorem is a global necessary test, not a
claim that this point could evade other exact lift tests.

independently: real tangent rank 12, Hessian rank 12, mixed block zero, and
