Exact failure of the power-minus-one-half ellipsoid Gram section
31 October 2026

The proposed explicit ellipsoid section fit(g,V,power=-1/2) is not always
positive semidefinite, even on an actual complex spectral supporting cost
in canonical nonordinary cap34. The same exact cost admits a different,
very sparse positive Gram. Thus this is a failure of one formula, not an
obstruction to matching-SOS membership or Marcus--de Oliveira.

1. An exact finite spectral supporting cost

Use zero-based lexicographic permutation indices in S4, and define
z_pi=product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)). The following finite complex spectra suffice:

 a=(-1/2, -1, 0,
    -30538743519/74444818948 + 26929415425 i/223334456844),
 b=(1250/3327 - 1100 i/3327,
    17500/34981 + 6600 i/34981,
    8150/13829 - 1650 i/13829,
    1/3).

Take
 eta=22414464370674 + (61527553321050/11)i,
 h=124288402500,
 g_pi=h-Re(eta z_pi).

The exact nonnegative gap vector is

 [496902338325,   6663585087, 246256222500, 496442849412,
  254543282500, 994968662650,   3728652075,    697107587,
             0, 247925065662, 242610327500, 493566937650,
             0, 244853194337, 246917463750,   2301888087,
  491789352500,   2320582500,   5966477500, 253851216325,
             0, 245623177575,   2261561250,            0].

Thus h is an actual supporting height, with exactly four contacts
8,12,20,23. All 24 products and exact gap identities are replayed by the
verifier; floating convex-hull output is unnecessary for this claim.

The discovery used the projective cofactor cloud a=(0,1,infinity,x), with
 beta=(1/2-33i/25, 1/4+33i/50, 37/100-33i/100, -1).
For each permutation, write
 u_pi=-beta_pi(0)(1-beta_pi(1)),  v_pi=-u_pi beta_pi(3).
The four-real cloud has rows (Re u,Im u,Re v,Im v). Its facet at those
four contacts has exact normal
 (13392170,-9451330,-19211093,-13202213)
and height 546868971/50. Set
 alpha=13392170+9451330i,
 eta0=-19211093+13202213i,
 x=alpha/eta0.
The common Mobius transformation z -> 1/(z-2), applied in the difference
formulation, gives the finite spectra above. Explicitly,
 a'=(-1/2,-1,0,1/(x-2)), b'_j=-1/(beta_j-2).
The product denominator is
 K=2(x-2) product_j(beta_j-2),
so z'_pi=(u_pi x+v_pi)/K. Multiplying eta0 K and the supporting height
by the same positive rational scaling gives the displayed eta,h,g.

2. Rational replay of the ellipsoid section

Let B be a 24 by 14 rational basis for the real matching-feature space:
columns span the indicators 1[pi(I)=J] with |I|=|J|=1 or 2. The verifier
constructs B directly and confirms its rank. Let E be a rational basis for
ker B_C, where C is the four-contact set. Let R=B_K E, where K is the
twenty-positive-gap set. Normalize the positive diagonal matrix D by
D_ii=g_i/max(g). Then

 S=R^T D^(-1)R,
 T=R S^(-1)R^T,
 P=D^(-1/2) T D^(-1/2).

P is precisely the orthogonal projector onto the weighted contact-kernel
feature space used in fit with power=-1/2. In particular,

 M=P Hadamard-squared = D^(-1)(T Hadamard-squared)D^(-1)

is rational. The fit section solves M lambda=1 and returns
Z=Q^T diag(lambda)Q for an orthonormal basis Q of that space. The rational
matrix

 H=R^T D^(-1)diag(lambda)R

is congruent to Z. Therefore a negative rational quadratic form of H proves
the fitted section is not PSD without computing any algebraic square roots
or trusting floating eigenvalue signs.

The verifier solves the 20 by 20 system in exact rational arithmetic,
checks M lambda=1 exactly, and constructs H. The integer vector

 w=(6,5,3,2,3,-1,-11,-5,-11,7)^T

satisfies w^T H w<0 exactly. Its value is approximately -101.323810905758.
The full rational numerator/denominator and every entry of B,E,lambda,H
are retained in exact_obstruction.json.

For comparison, direct floating execution of the original formula gives

 power       minimum eigenvalue   maximum eigenvalue   diagonal residual
 -0.5         -0.2273820159          8.725906249          4.01e-14
 -0.75         0.1226348371         37.78915582           1.09e-14
 -1            0.1785832814       1427.298944             3.73e-13

The weighted linear system for power=-0.5 has condition approximately
915.63. Its failure is not a roundoff-sized negative eigenvalue. Positivity
of the other powers is reported only as a numerical check on this cost.

3. An alternative exact positive Gram for the same cost

Use the repository's exact canonical ray indexing and let

 features=(f34,r0,r7,r10,r13,r17,r18,r20,r22,r23,r27,r28,r30,r31).

The verifier checks g=sum_j c_j feature_j, with coefficients

 c=(239592637413,6663585087,2320582500,3728652075,2261561250,
    0,5966477500,0,0,246917463750,2301888087,247925065662,0,697107587).

These are all nonnegative. Write delta for the indicator of permutation
index5. Exact Boolean coordinate identities give

 f34^2=f34+2 delta,  r23 r28=delta,  r_j^2=r_j.

Consequently the matrix with diagonal c and only two nonzero offdiagonal
entries G_9,11=G_11,9=-c0 reproduces g at all 24 matching points. Its only
nontrivial principal block is

 [246917463750  -239592637413]
 [-239592637413  247925065662].

Its determinant is exactly 3812396510795967419931>0. The other diagonal
entries are nonnegative, so G is PSD. This proves exact matching-SOS
membership for this same cost while refuting the specific ellipsoid section.
The normalized cap34 coefficient is 239592637413/994968662650>0.
