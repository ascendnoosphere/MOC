Independent Choi and Kraus audit of the alternating CP completion
31 October2026

Input theorem. Grace/Hodge proved the real-jet identity for an arbitrary
real orthogonal4x4 mixer. Choose beta(w)=(0,1,-1,w), write its real jet
J=[F0 F1], and let c be the signed determinant amplitudes. The universal
Clifford decomposition gives
 H(c)=J^Tdiag(c)J=P+Q^PT, P=h h^T, Q=r r^T,
where h has7 columns and r one. The real PT-odd corner is one-dimensional,
and for the explicitly determined scalar lambda,
 Q^PT-Q=-lambda H(sign),
 H(c+lambda sign)=h h^T+r r^T>=0.                         (1)
This note independently audits the transition from (1) to a quantum
channel and its constants at two exact mixers. It does not substitute
example checks for the universal real-jet theorem.

Choi congruence

Let P_pi act on four qubits by permuting tensor factors. The24 vectors
vec(P_pi) span14 dimensions. Their permutation-function space is the
same14-dimensional space as the columns of the jet J. Therefore there
is an injective linear map L on range(J^T) satisfying
 L j_pi=vec(P_pi).
Consequently
 Choi(Phi_q)=sum_pi q_pi vec(P_pi)vec(P_pi)^T=L H(q)L^T,
 Phi_q(X)=sum_pi q_pi P_pi X P_pi^T.
This is a congruence, not an assertion that the original signed twirl
was completely positive. For q=c+lambda sign, (1) gives eight real
Kraus vectors Lh_1,...,Lh_7,Lr.

The alternating coherent sum vanishes exactly:
 sum_pi sign(pi)P_pi=0.
Indeed there is no fully alternating tensor in (C^2)^tensor4. Also
sum_pi sign(pi)=0. Thus the corrected channel is unital and trace
preserving, and the coherent operator is unchanged:
 T_c=sum c_pi P_pi=sum q_pi P_pi.

The first Kraus operator is T_c itself. To see this without relying on
an example, every P_pi fixes the fully symmetric subspace Sym^4(C^2).
The scalar Clifford column has coefficient1 there; the other six
holomorphic channels and the pseudoscalar channel have coefficient0.
Choose any invariant unit vector v. Comparing the channel on |x><v|
gives K0 x<Tv|=T_c x<v|, hence K0=T_c. Therefore
 Phi_q(X)=T_c X T_c^T+sum_{alpha=1}^7 K_alpha X K_alpha^T,
 T_c^T T_c+sum_{alpha=1}^7 K_alpha^T K_alpha=I,
 T_c T_c^T+sum_{alpha=1}^7 K_alpha K_alpha^T=I.             (2)
The seven error channels annihilate the five-dimensional fully
symmetric sector. Formula (2) is an exact operator sum-of-squares
completion of the fermionic compression contraction.

No claim is made about minimizing over other invisible gauge families.

At both mixers the displayed lambda is the UNIQUE scalar on the
alternating line for which the Choi matrix is PSD. The verifier
restricts H(sign) to the six-dimensional kernel of the rank-eight
completed matrix and exports exact rational vectors giving positive
and negative quadratic values. Any nonzero change of lambda makes
that restricted form indefinite. This example uniqueness statement
is distinct from a universal uniqueness theorem.

Negative q coefficients are compatible with complete positivity. This
is not a classical common-minor probability law. The original common
profiles and all actual MOC product averages remain unchanged because
the alternating vector is invisible to them.

The correction cannot be linear in c on the whole amplitude family.
For a signed permutation mixer, c is a coordinate unit vector and
r=0, so lambda=0. Those24 vectors spanR24. A linear functional with
these values would vanish identically, contradicting the exact Q59
value above. Orthogonality-related denominator cancellations therefore
do not establish linearity in the signed amplitude coordinates.
