EXACT CLOSURE OF THE MOC4 FACE (3,21,27,32,53)
2026-10-30

Scope: this treats the entire displayed coefficient face and its coefficient
boundaries. It does not prove unrestricted complex MOC4. The spectral cubic,
the n=4 external-half-plane theorem, and the established identification of
permutation profiles with their unitary minor functions are imported inputs.
The standalone verifier is code/research/verify_residual_face_32.py. It reads
no generated build data and runs its model, pencil, rank, and reference checks
once. Its rank subroutine is code/research/verify_residual_face_32_rank.py.

Use one-based indices and rho(I,J)(U)=|det U[I,J]|^2. The canonical labels are

 r3=rho(1,4),       r21=rho(12,34),
 r27=rho(13,34),    r32=rho(23,13),
 f53=rho(2,2)+rho(3,3)+rho(12,34)-rho(13,13).

Let z_pi=product_i(alpha_i+beta_pi(i)), eta!=0, and
h=max_pi Re(eta z_pi). Suppose the permutation support gap has the form

              g=a r3+b r21+c r27+d r32+f53,   a,b,c,d>=0.          (1)

The positive mixed coefficient has been normalized to one by positive
scaling of eta,h and all coefficients. The result is that no such actual
spectral gap has h>=0. For h<0, the imported external-half-plane theorem
proves the support inequality. If the mixed coefficient is zero, the
support gap is already an ordinary nonnegative squared-minor sum.

1. Necessary equations for actual spectra
-----------------------------------------
The exact model code/geometry/closing_moc4_model_verify.py constructs the
homogeneous cubic F in 14 selected matching coordinates and verifies
coefficientwise that grad F(z)=0 for every actual complex spectral matching
vector. Write e=(1,...,1), H_v=H_F(v), and

                  Q(v)=D_e F(v)=sum_i partial_i F(v).

The model also checks H_e e=0. Set x=g-he and let y be the real
imaginary-part vector of eta z. Expanding grad F(-x+iy)=0 gives

              H_x y=0,       grad F(y)=grad F(x).                 (2)

Euler's identity and cubic polarization give

 3F(x)=x.grad F(x)=x.grad F(y)=y^T H_x y/2=0.

Consequently

                   F(g)=hQ(g),        Q(x)=Q(g).                  (3)

On (1), direct exact evaluation yields

 F(g)=-(a+bd+b+d+1)<0,
 Q(g)=cd-(b+1)(d+1).                                             (4)

Thus h=0 is impossible, Q!=0, and h=F/Q. For h>0, necessarily Q<0.
No simple-spectrum or distinct-product assumption enters these identities.

H_x must be singular. If y!=0 this follows from (2). If y=0, then
H_x x=2 grad F(x)=0. Here x!=0: otherwise g=he and F(g)=h^3F(e)=0,
contrary to (4). This handles a zero imaginary part without a genericity
assumption.

2. The entire determinant branch and its connected domain
----------------------------------------------------------
Put

 N=(a+1)(c+1)(a+b+1)>0,
 T=-a^2 c+ac^2+a+c^2+c+1+b(a^2-ac+a+c^2+c+1),
 P=N+dT.

The exact determinant identity is

           det(QH_g-F(g)H_e)=-4F(g)^2 Q^12 P^2.                  (5)

Singularity and Q!=0 therefore force P=0. Since N>0, every nonnegative
coefficient point on this branch has d>0,T<0 and

                             d=-N/T.                            (6)

In particular there is no omitted T=0 component.

The coefficient of b in T is strictly positive, since

 2(a^2-ac+a+c^2+c+1)=(a-c)^2+a^2+c^2+2a+2c+2.

Moreover T at b=c equals (c+1)(a+c^2+c+1)>0. Thus T<0 requires
k=c-b>0. Substitute c=b+k, and define

 L=bk+k^2+b+1>0,       M=(b+1)(c^2+c+1)>0.

Then

 T=-k a^2+L a+M,
 d=(c+1)[a^2+(b+2)a+b+1]/[k a^2-L a-M].                        (7)

For fixed b>=0,k>0, T has exactly one positive root a_T(b,k), and T<0
is equivalent to a>a_T. In particular a,c,d are all strictly positive.
The derivative of d on that ray is

 -(c+1)[(L+k(b+2))a^2+2(M+k(b+1))a
           +M(b+2)-L(b+1)]/[k a^2-L a-M]^2 <0.                 (8)

The constant term in the bracket is positive because

 M(b+2)-L(b+1)=(b+1)[b^3+2b^2k+3b^2+bk^2+4bk+2b+k^2+2k+1].

Its limits are infinity at a_T and (c+1)/k at infinity. Also

 Q=(k-1)d-b-1,
 lim_(a->infinity) Q=(k^2-k-b-1)/k.                             (9)

If k<=1, Q<0 throughout the ray a>a_T. If k>1, Q<0 is a nonempty
upper ray precisely when b>k^2-k-1. Hence the projection of the whole
positive-height branch onto (b,k) is

                    b>=0,  k>0,  b>k^2-k-1.                   (10)

Its b>0 part is a path-connected epigraph. Above each point, the allowed
a form an upper ray with a continuous threshold: a_T for k<=1, and the
unique Q=0 root for k>1. The latter threshold tends to a_T as k decreases
to one. To see this, every fixed a>a_T has Q<0 at k=1 and remains
admissible in a neighborhood, while every threshold is at least a_T.
The strict upper-ray description over this connected base proves path
connectedness of the b>0 branch. Every admissible b=0 point is a limit
of b>0 points: keep k,a fixed and use the strict inequalities T<0,Q<0
and the continuous formula (6). There are no a=0,c=0,d=0 points.

Equations (7)--(9) and their derivative and factor identities are checked
symbolically by the standalone verifier. Connectedness itself follows from
the argument above, rather than from a parameter grid.

3. Corank two and a nonsingular kernel quadratic
-----------------------------------------------
Let p(t)=det(H_g-tH_e). Its degree is at most three, independently of any
interpolation. Indeed rank H_e=4 gives degree at most four. The vector e
lies in ker H_e, and H_g e=H_e g. Therefore, for every u in ker H_e,

                    e^T H_g u=g^T H_e u=0.

Thus e is a nonzero radical vector of H_g restricted to ker H_e; the
determinant of that restriction vanishes. This is, up to a fixed nonzero
factor, the degree-four coefficient. The verifier constructs an explicit
basis of ker H_e and checks this radical identity exactly. Four exact
pencil evaluations at 0,1,-1,2 then determine every coefficient.

Besides (5), those calculations establish p(0)=0, p(h)=p'(h)=0 on (6),
and

 p'(0)=-4cd(ab-ac-bc-c)(a+bd+b+d+1)
             (abd+ac+ad+a+bc+bd+b+c+d+1)>0.                    (11)

The sign holds on the entire nonnegative branch T<0: c,d>0 and
ab-ac-bc-c=-ak-c(b+1)<0, while the other factors are positive.
Since h>0, a cubic with roots 0 and the double root h satisfies

                   [z^2]det(H_x+zH_e)=p'(0)/h>0.              (12)

Separately, the verifier proves rank H_x<=12 identically on the rational
graph (6), h=F/Q. Exact elimination of ten variables leaves a 4x4 system
in the original coordinates numbered (1,6,7,8), using zero-based indices.
A 2x2 principal block in positions (0,2) is invertible at the exact seed

              (a,b,c,d,h)=(7,1,2,216/7,495/14).

All four entries of its remaining 2x2 Schur complement vanish identically
after (6) and h=F/Q. The ten initial pivots at this seed are

 495/14,495/14,1,1,216/7,216/7,-495/14,1,-495/14,-55,

and the final 2x2 minor is 1150362889/485149500!=0. These nonzero values
certify a nonempty rational chart. Every 13x13 minor is therefore the zero
rational function of (a,b,c), and polynomial continuation extends rank<=12
to every point with T<0,Q<0, including points where a chart pivot vanishes.
No fixed elimination chart is assumed to work everywhere.

A matrix of corank at least three would make its pencil determinant vanish
to order at least three in z. Thus (12), together with rank<=12, gives
rank H_x=12 everywhere on this branch. In a kernel/complement orthonormal
decomposition, the coefficient in (12) is the determinant of the invertible
complementary Hessian block times det(H_e restricted to ker H_x).
Therefore Q(y)=y^T H_e y/2 is nonsingular on this two-dimensional kernel.
The nonzero coefficient (12) alone would not prove corank two; the separate
Schur certificate is essential.

4. The real lift is impossible at positive height
------------------------------------------------
At the displayed rational seed, exact arithmetic gives Q(g)=-2, rank H_x=12,
and, in the verifier's nullspace basis,

 (Q|ker H_x)00=15739047339848/11203238751694609>0,
 det(Q|ker H_x)=16429754880/11203238751694609>0.

The restricted quadratic is positive definite there. Constant rank makes
its kernel vary continuously, and its nonsingularity prevents its inertia
from changing on the path-connected b>0 branch. It remains positive definite
at admissible b=0 points by continuity and nonsingularity.

For the actual real vector y in (2), this gives Q(y)>=0. But summing the
gradient identity in (2) gives Q(y)=Q(x)=Q(g)<0. This contradiction excludes
every actual positive-height gap (1).

5. Other heights, coefficient boundaries, and scope
---------------------------------------------------
The h=0 case was already excluded by F(g)<0 and F(g)=hQ(g).
For h<0, use S. W. Drury, The external vertices conjecture in case n=4,
Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra 11 (2004), 180--191, Theorem 1.1 and
the half-plane assertion in the proof of Corollary 1.2, page 181:
 https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/263/263/263

That assertion states that a closed half-plane excluding zero and containing
all permutation vertices also contains the determinant of the normal sum
(with the paper's subtraction convention absorbed into the second spectrum).
Here H={w:Re(eta w)<=h} excludes zero because h<0 and contains all vertices
by the definition of h. It therefore contains det(A+UBU*) for every unitary U.
Equivalently, one may rotate and scale to Re(-eta z/(-h))>=1 and apply the
paper's normalized assertion. This is an imported theorem, not a consequence
of the positive-height inertia argument.

If the f53 coefficient is zero, the four remaining rays are the ordinary
squared minors listed at the start, so their nonnegative combination is
nonnegative on every U(4). If it is positive, positive normalization reduces
to (1) and preserves the sign of h. This proves the support inequality on
the entire face and its coefficient boundaries, at every height.

The conclusion covers the row/column/inversion images of this canonical
nine-contact face. It does not by itself settle other face types, gaps with
fewer contacts, or unrestricted complex MOC4. The result is conditional on
the imported exact spectral model and Drury theorem; the coefficient signs,
determinant identities, generic-corank certificate, degree bound, ordinary
ray labels, and reference positivity are recomputed by the portable verifier.
