Exact real-lift obstruction on a five-ray MOC4 face

The calculation uses the conventions and coordinate ordering of
build/session_lift/closing_moc4_model_verify.py. The 24-dimensional point is
  g_full = 20 r_3 + (3486/1109) r_21 + (1/4) r_27 + r_30 + r_53,
with rays from data/certificates/geometry/global_terminal_face_census.json.
Its 14 selected coordinates are
  g = (2,1,1,0,0,1,0,0,0,0,0,1/4,3486/1109,20).
Set e=(1,...,1), Q(g)=sum_i partial_i F(g), h=F(g)/Q(g)=21420/1109,
and x=g-he. We use the necessary real-lift equations
  H_F(x)y=0,  grad F(y)=grad F(x),  y real.
Indices below are zero-based. The following calculation rules these equations
out, without solving a quartic or relying on numerical approximations.

Let M be the leading 12 by 12 submatrix of H_F(x). It has determinant
  107866750572714281903469225 / 7441307787809619364 > 0.
Define the 14 by 2 matrix
  N = [ -M^(-1) H_F(x)[0:12,12:14] ; I_2 ].
Every y in the Hessian kernel is N(a,b)^T, with a=y_12, b=y_13.
Let K=Hessian(partial_1 F)/2 and T=N^T K N. Since partial_1 F is a
homogeneous quadratic, partial_1 F(y)=(a,b) T (a,b)^T. Exact computation gives
  T_00 = -1855151797543943572862816 / 9783832251493358902809 < 0,
  det T = 77593729460021913332405 / 13045109668657811870412 > 0,
  partial_1 F(x) = 91256324 / 1229881 > 0.
Thus T is negative definite, so partial_1 F(y)<=0 for real y in the
kernel, in contradiction to the requested positive gradient coordinate.
The full Hessian has rank exactly 12; the proof only needs the displayed
minor, so it remains valid if the full rank increases at nearby points.

Coordinate-free mechanism and Schur complement. For any homogeneous cubic
F, any x and any direction v, the identity
  v dot grad F(y) = (1/2) y^T H_F(v) y
holds. Consequently, if H_F(v) is negative semidefinite on ker H_F(x) and
v dot grad F(x)>0, the necessary real-lift equations are inconsistent. The
criterion is unchanged by a change of basis of the kernel, since that acts
on the restricted form by congruence and preserves its inertia. The signs
can also be reversed. This works at any Hessian rank, including rank 12.

Suppose H_F(x)=[M B; B^T C] with M invertible, and set
  S(x)=C-B^T M^(-1) B,   N(x)=[-M^(-1)B; I].
Differentiating the Schur complement gives the intrinsic identity
  D_v S(x) = N(x)^T H_F(v) N(x).
If H_F(x) has rank equal to the size of M, then S(x)=0 and the columns of N
span the full kernel. More generally, every Hessian-kernel vector is Nw,
even if S is nonzero. Thus negative semidefiniteness of D_v S on all w is
a valid sufficient obstruction without requiring the rank to stay fixed.
For v=e_1 in the example, T=(1/2) D_{e_1} S. The script evaluates this
using the block derivative formula, directly from the Hessian and M inverse.
This also gives an exact approach for testing rank-12 families: evaluate the
2 by 2 Schur derivative and the signed gradient along the family; a sign
change or a zero minor requires a separate chart or certificate.

Family. For arbitrary positive coefficients c=(c3,c21,c27,c30,c53), form g,
h=F(g)/Q(g), x, M, N, T as above. The same obstruction holds whenever
  Q(g) != 0, det M != 0, partial_1 F(x)>0, T_00<0, det T>0.
These are explicit rational inequalities in the five coefficients and define
an open subset of the positive coefficient orthant containing the displayed
point. They give an exact membership test, not an asserted explicit box or
a classification of the whole face. For example (20,3487/1109,1/4,1,1)
also satisfies them, but its full Hessian has rank 14, so its kernel is
trivial. The open family therefore does not by itself establish a family of
rank-12 residual points. A second exact rank-12 point is
  (20, 1090193958/346521001, 251/1000, 1, 1);
it also passes the same sign test. These coefficients lie on the proposed
parameter curve b=42(21+20c-c^2)/(c^2-42c+357), with c=251/1000.
The family statement only concerns the necessary lift equations; no claim is
made that every nearby point satisfies additional candidate equalities.

Reproduce with exact rational arithmetic from the repository root:
  python3 build/lift_audit/separation.py
  python3 build/lift_audit/separation.py 20 3487/1109 1/4 1 1
  python3 build/lift_audit/separation.py 20 1090193958/346521001 251/1000 1 1
The script verifies the rank and full kernel at the displayed point, derives
Q and h from F, and checks the signs. On other inputs, failure of its sign
test means only that this particular certificate does not decide the point.
