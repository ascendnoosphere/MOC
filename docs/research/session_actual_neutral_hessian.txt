Neutral Hessian inertia for actual conjugate secants

This is a structural consequence of the audited HMSV contraction, not
a solution of MOC. At any actual input whose real secant midpoint x
has Hessian rank 12, H_x has inertia (6 positive, 6 negative, 2 zero).

Proof on a generic actual input. Write x=(w+conj(w))/2 with w in the
complex critical cone X, and let W be the real 12-dimensional tangent
space to its secant cone S at x. The complexification of W is the
direct sum T_w X plus T_conj(w) X. Since H_w kills T_w X and
H_conj(w) kills T_conj(w) X, the mixed block of H_x between those
two complex spaces is zero. Therefore the real restriction H_x|W
is the realification of a complex symmetric six-dimensional form:
its positive and negative indices are equal and its radical has even
dimension.

The audited HMSV contraction says dim grad(S)<=10. Thus the
derivative H_x restricted to the smooth 12-dimensional tangent W
has kernel dimension at least 2. Since full H_x has rank 12, that
kernel is exactly ker H_x, and ker H_x is contained in W. The cubic
F vanishes on S: it vanishes on every secant of critical points by
polarization. Since W is tangent to S, H_x x=2 grad F(x) is normal
to W. The cone point x belongs to W, is in the radical of H_x|W,
and is not in ker H_x; if it were, grad F(x)=0 and the critical
locus tangent would force Hessian rank at most 8. Hence the radical
of the restricted real form has dimension at least 3, and therefore
at least 4 by its evenness. It has dimension at most 4, because W
has codimension 2 and the full radical has dimension 2. Thus the
restriction has inertia (4,4,4). The two radical directions outside
ker H_x pair with the 2-dimensional complement, contributing two
hyperbolic planes. The full inertia is (6,6,2).

The generic conditions hold on a dense open subset of the real
spectral-parameter space: exact computation at a specific point shows
that the tangent sum has dimension 12 and H_x rank 12. The ambient
secant has dimension 12 and the tangent sum is contained in its
tangent. Conjugate-pair parameters are Zariski dense in the complex
pair space: a polynomial vanishing after writing w=u+iv and its
conjugate u-iv, with real u,v, vanishes identically after complexifying
u,v. Therefore generic such secants meet the smooth locus. Every actual
point of Hessian rank 12 is a limit of these generic points; rank
remains 12 locally because all actual points have rank at most 12,
so the signature is locally constant and the result extends.

The exact tangent and Hessian sample is in
code/research/verify_neutral_hessian_generic.py. In the chosen coordinates the
Jacobian has complex rank 6; its real and imaginary columns have
real rank 12; H_x has rank 12; the mixed tangent block is zero and
the pure block has complex rank 4.

This condition is strictly stronger than complex secant membership.
The exact face-16 example checked in
code/research/verify_neutral_hessian_face16.py has a complex lift and
Hessian inertia (5,7,2), so it is not an actual conjugate secant.
The independent audit is in docs/research/neutral_hessian_independent_review.txt.

A polynomial consequence holds at every actual point, including
higher-corank points: the 12th compound matrix C_12(H_x) is positive
semidefinite. At rank 12 its sole nonzero eigenvalue is the product
of the 12 nonzero eigenvalues and is strictly positive; at smaller
rank the compound matrix is zero. In particular, every principal
12 by 12 minor is nonnegative, and E12, their sum, is nonnegative.
This is a necessary condition on actual points, not a proof of the
missing global nonnegative-support statement.
