Eight-spin singlets and a nonperturbative polynomial-state test for MOC
31 October 2026

The unrestricted conjecture remains the target. These calculations do not
prove it. They eliminate a stronger proposed degree-one positive-state
construction by an exact witness, repair that witness with a degree-two
positive state, and identify the precise eight-spin invariant represented by
unitary minor profiles. No historical priority is claimed.

1. A concrete quantum-state construction

For a fixed unitary U and actual matching products z_pi, put
 c_pi=Re[sgn(pi) product_i U_i,pi(i)/det U].
These signed numbers sum to one and reproduce the determinant d. Retain
only positive c_pi and normalize them to q_pi. Let f_pi be any finite real
polynomial feature vector of the product coordinates x_pi=Re z_pi,
y_pi=Im z_pi. Define three real symmetric matrices
 G=sum q_pi f_pi f_pi^T,
 Hx=sum q_pi x_pi f_pi f_pi^T,
 Hy=sum q_pi y_pi f_pi f_pi^T.

If a real symmetric PSD matrix S satisfies
 tr(G S)=1, tr(Hx S)=Re d, tr(Hy S)=Im d,
then
 w_pi=q_pi f_pi^T S f_pi
are nonnegative, sum to one, and reproduce d. Thus this is an explicit
positive certificate with a finite linear moment problem. The matrix S may
be given as a sum of rational rank-one outer products. No numerical SDP
solver needs to be trusted for the final certificate.

For G positive definite, changing features by G^{-1/2} identifies this
condition with membership in the numerical range of the compressed normal
observable diag(z_pi) on the feature subspace. Complex pure states suffice
by Toeplitz--Hausdorff; real PSD S packages real and imaginary components.
The elementary weight proof above needs neither that theorem nor a square
root. The same statement works on the support of a singular G.

This is a finite, nonperturbative state construction. It permits increases
above the initial positive amplitudes, unlike the exact capacity-only
recipe refuted in moc_amplitude_capacity_obstruction.py. The full
positive-support assertion is still unproved, and a fixed low feature
degree is a genuinely stronger conjecture rather than a consequence.

2. Exact obstruction to every degree-one real-affine state

Take
 a=(2-i,-3-i,2+2i,-1+3i),
 b=(-2-2i,-2-3i,-2-2i,2-2i),
 R=(1/3701)[ 999  2010 -2230  1920
             3090   849   660 -1730
             1230 -2860 -2001    30
             1280  -870  2070  2649 ].
Exactly R^T R=I and det R=1. The determinant is
 d=(-92278800-131758800 i)/13697401.
The positive real-amplitude permutation indices, zero-based lex S4, are
 [2,4,5,6,7,8,10,11,12,14,17,18,19,20,21,22].

Use f=(1,x,y) and the support direction ell(z)=-30 Re z+13 Im z.
The rational 3 by 3 localizer
 L=sum q_pi[ell(z_pi)-ell(d)] f_pi f_pi^T
is strictly negative definite. All three leading principal determinants
have signs -,+,-, checked exactly. Consequently every nonzero affine
polynomial l(x,y), and every nontrivial sum of squared absolute values of
such polynomials, has strictly negative localizer expectation. No
normalized degree-one polynomial-state law can reproduce d.
More quantitatively, -L-G is strictly positive definite by three exact
positive principal minors. Thus every normalized affine-SOS tilt has
ell(d)-ell(sum w_pi z_pi)>1 in the stated rational support direction.

This disproves a universal three-feature quantum-tilt criterion. It is not
a MOC counterexample: the exact positive law at permutation indices 2,4,7
has weights (592507395,845222009,27892503)/1465621907 and reproduces d.
It also does not disprove the positive-amplitude-support candidate: those
three indices all lie in the displayed positive support.

The rational mixer came from Cayley rounding of a fresh random real
orthogonal input. The initial robust failure was found among 2999
nonflat inputs, independent of the earlier 4095-record replay. It had
minimum three-feature support eigenvalue about -0.00357695 in whitened
coordinates; the exact witness uses a different nearby sign cell and is
validated independently of that floating calculation.

4. The eight-qubit invariant is explicit

For general n let |V_pi> be the normalized tensor product of bipartite
singlets (|01>-|10>)/sqrt(2), paired from the first n spins to the second n
according to pi. Their Gram matrix is exactly
 <V_pi|V_sigma>=2^{cycles(pi^{-1}sigma)-n}.
For n=4 its rank is the Catalan number14, agreeing with the actual
fourteen-dimensional matching-invariant space. This is a concrete
SU(2)-singlet realization of that space, not a new unexplained object.

Use the complex, un-real-parted Leibniz amplitudes
 a_pi=sgn(pi) product_i U_i,pi(i)/det U,
and form |t_U>=sum a_pi|V_pi>. At the computational basis vector whose
first-half occupied set is I and second-half occupied set is J^c, its
coefficient is exactly
 2^{-n/2}(-1)^{|I|}|det U[I,J]|^2,   |I|=|J|.
Every other occupation coefficient is zero. This follows from grouping
Leibniz terms by pi(I)=J and using complementary unitary minors. Thus the
physical singlet vector has a fixed Marshall sign pattern even though
its valence-bond coefficients can be negative or complex.

The norm gives a direct link to random-matrix/exterior-minor discrepancy:
 ||t_U||^2=2^{-n} sum_k ||M_{U,k}||_F^2
          =2^{-n}[n+1+sum_k delta_k(U)],
where M_{U,k}=(|det U[I,J]|^2) and
delta_k=||M_{U,k}-J/binom(n,k)||_F^2. For n=4 this is
 ||t_U||^2=(5+2 delta_1+delta_2)/16.
The script verifies every576 Gram entry, rank14, all70 nonzero occupation
coefficients for four exact mixers, and this norm identity.

Marshall sign positivity by itself does not solve MOC. In fact R59 from
the existing common-minor obstruction gives a Marshall-positive singlet
that admits no nonnegative normalized bipartite valence-bond expansion.
Such an expansion would reproduce every minor, contradicting the exact
f34=-55/3481 witness. A quantum positivity proof must exploit the actual
product-spinor observable or additional unitary structure. It cannot
replace that requirement by entrywise positivity of the singlet vector.

Similarly, balanced moment-map equations at a putative compact negative
minimum do not alone yield a contradiction. A single valence-bond
amplitude already has a negative real global product-spinor minimum and
balanced one-spin moment map. The maximizing-permutation contact and
unitary-stationarity equations are essential extra structure.

There is a sharper elementary obstruction to a proposed Marshall
dominance proof. For any normalized valence-bond mixture V_lambda, the
Marshall-corrected difference t_U-V_lambda has, in each fixed grade k,
coefficient matrix (M_U,k-sum lambda M_perm,k)/4. Both matrices are
doubly stochastic. Hence every row of the difference sums to zero, and
entrywise nonnegativity forces every entry to vanish. Componentwise
Marshall dominance is therefore exactly equality of all common-minor
profiles; it is not a weaker sufficient positivity property. The Q59
obstruction rules it out for every lambda there. A proof deriving this
condition from hypothetical negative-minimum stationarity would be
substantive, but no Perron--Frobenius shortcut establishes it.

5. Boundary-conditioned continuation and the spin Casimir

The generic search normalization a=(0,1,i,a4) is legitimate under the
already-proved common complex Mobius symmetry, not under affine changes
alone. A Mobius map takes any three distinct a values to 0,1,i. Every
matching product and d then acquire the same nonzero scalar factor kappa,
while c_pi and its positive support are unchanged. Multiplication of the
planar cloud by a nonzero complex scalar preserves the entire real
polynomial feature space of degree at most two. Generic pole-free charts
are therefore sufficient for discovery; collisions, poles and rank-drop
feature boundaries still require explicit treatment in a universal proof.

New joint searches varied six SO4 Cayley parameters, ten normalized real
spectral parameters and one support angle. The 400-generation run made
54536 objective evaluations and found no negative six-feature support
margin (minimum observed 0.0802672). A near-identity 18-parameter variant
made 57744 evaluations and reached positive margin 0.0000707774. A wider
fixed-R3701 spectral search made 44088 evaluations, minimum 0.2116275.
These optimizers do not prove bounds. In the near-identity computation,
the numerical positive-amplitude cutoff omits very small positive terms;
its reported support has seven permutations. This narrower numerical
state space and the singular-value rank cutoff must be retained in any
interpretation; exact arithmetic is required before calling a negative
margin a counterexample. All raw settings and best points are saved in
 build/moc_qft_polynomial_tilt/boundary_search_*.json.
Two additional constrained single-zero-pair searches used
a=(0,1,i,a4), b=(0,b1,b2,b3), fixed mixers Q59 and R3701, and nine real
search parameters including the support angle. Each made 43272 objective
evaluations in 600 generations, with no negative margin; minimum
observed margins were 0.139118 and 0.134505. These exact zero-pair inputs
are already known to satisfy MOC, but the stronger polynomial-state
candidate need not follow from that theorem. Their explicit inclusion
closes a coverage omission in generic continuous sampling only.

The eight-spin bridge gives an additional exact connection, communicated
to the combined variational lane. Let
 L_same=sum_{i<j, both labels in the same half}(I-Swap_ij).
On total-spin-zero eight-qubit states,
 L_same=12I-2 J_A^2,
where J_A is the total angular momentum of the first four spins. Its
eigenvalues on the fourteen-dimensional invariant space are
 0 with multiplicity1, 8 with multiplicity9, 12 with multiplicity4.
The exact script independently checks that characteristic polynomial by
constructing the 256-by-24 valence-bond matrix. These are precisely the
constant, standard and two-dimensional matching Fourier sectors. Their
halved values 0,4,6 coincide with the n4 unitary-heat rates. The full
eight-label transposition Casimir is 20I; thus the opposite-half operator
has eigenvalues20,12,8 on these sectors.

For Q=P_matching-t_U the constant sector vanishes because sum c_pi=1.
Only the eigenvalues8 and12 remain. At an actual unitary support maximum,
the heat-Laplacian necessary condition gives Re(L_same d)>=0. The matching
support comparisons also give Re(L_same P_matching)>=0. Their difference
has no determined sign from these two facts. Consequently the Casimir
connection explains the spectral and heat decompositions but does not
produce the missing sign. Mixed spinor--mixer curvature and the active
contact conditions must supply further information.
