PT-symmetric feedback does not repair the star ARE embedding
31 October 2026

Use the complete star block interpolation operator C=C0+L(B) and the
homogeneous scalar constraint

                     ell(B)=1_6^T B k,  k=(1,-1,0,1)^T,

from moc_star_block_gram_affine_20261031.txt.  The earlier unrestricted
feedback test is in moc_star_feedback_are_obstruction_20261031.txt.

THEOREM.  Suppose U is6-by4 and A is4-by4, and for EVERY symmetric X
with X_UT=X_RS one has

                  L(U X)=A^T X+X A,  ell(U X)=0.             (1)

Then U=0 and A=0.

The auxiliary source corner being PT-invariant therefore does not
rescue this natural four-state feedback construction.  The result is
independent of the fixed primary block D and source cost.  It does
not exclude general nonlinear constructions, more general linear
operators X -> B, state/auxiliary changes of identification, or larger
state-space Riccati/Bezoutian formulations.

1. Direct nine-direction certificate

The PT-symmetric subspace of Sym4 has dimension9.  Use the ten upper
triangular entries, identifying X_03 with X_12.  Equating the ten
symmetric entries of the first equation in(1), plus its scalar second
equation, for each of the nine basis matrices produces99 homogeneous
linear equations in the24 entries of U and16 entries of A.

The exact99-by40 coefficient matrix has a40-by40 INTEGER minor with
determinant256.  It therefore has rank40 and zero nullspace.  The
checker exports every coefficient, the chosen40 rows, their equation
labels, and the determinant.  This proves(1) exactly; no optimizer or
rank tolerance is involved.

2. Equivalent normal-direction formulation

Let J_Q be the symmetric Hessian of UT-RS:

                          [0  0  0  1]
                          [0  0 -1  0]
                    J_Q = [0 -1  0  0].
                          [1  0  0  0]

Then tr(J_Q X)=2(X_UT-X_RS), and J_Q has rank4.  Vanishing on the
nine-dimensional kernel of this linear functional is equivalent to
the existence of a symmetric K and scalar tau such that for ALL
symmetric X,

           L(U X)-A^T X-X A=tr(J_Q X) K,
                    ell(U X)=tau tr(J_Q X).                (2)

The checker independently forms this110-by51 linear system.  It too
has full column rank: a51-by51 integer minor has determinant-524288.
Thus (2) only has U=A=K=tau=0.  This independently audits the choice
of the nine-dimensional PT subspace and its normal direction.

3. Why the scalar constraint is not relaxed in practice

Set v^T=1_6^T U.  If v^T X k vanishes on the PT-symmetric subspace,
its representing symmetric matrix (k v^T+v k^T)/2 is a scalar
multiple of J_Q.  The former has rank at most2, while J_Q has rank4,
so that scalar is zero.  Since k is nonzero, sym(k v^T)=0 implies v=0.
Consequently ell(U X)=0 on this subspace already forces1_6^T U=0,
and ell(U X)=0 for all symmetric X.  The obstruction comes from the
full interpolation operator rather than a missed scalar normal term.
