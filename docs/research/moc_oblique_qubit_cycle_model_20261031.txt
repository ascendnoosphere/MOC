SPECTRAL PRODUCT CLOUDS AS CYCLE TRACES OF OBLIQUE QUBIT PROJECTORS
31 October 2026

This is an exact source model, valid in every dimension. It is not a
positivity theorem: the projectors below are generally not Hermitian.

1. General n identity

For finite spectra a_i,b_i, assume c_i=a_i+b_i is nonzero. Put

 v_i=(1,b_i)^T, u_i=(a_i,1)/c_i,
 F_i=v_i u_i.

Then u_i v_i=1, so F_i^2=F_i, trF_i=1 and detF_i=0. Each F_i is a rank1
idempotent in M2(C), with two independent pre/post vectors rather than
a positive density operator. The normalized source matrix is

 C_ij=u_i v_j=(a_i+b_j)/c_i.

For a cycle(i1 i2 ... ik), direct multiplication gives

 tr(F_i1 F_i2 ... F_ik)
   =C_i1,i2 C_i2,i3 ... C_ik,i1.

Consequently, for every permutation pi,

 product_i(a_i+b_pi(i))
   =(product_i c_i) product_(cycles of pi) tr(F_i1 ... F_ik).      (1)

The identity extends across c_i=0 after clearing denominators. It can also
be expressed as a trace against a permutation operator on n qubits, with
the permutation convention chosen consistently with(1). No Hermitian
conjugation is used or silently inserted.

Thus the rank-two product structure is exactly a two-dimensional trace
algebra. Replacing these sources by arbitrary positive/noncommutative
inputs would lose information; earlier generic stable-input and coherent
contraction shortcuts were too broad.

2. Central star normalization

For n=4 take a2=infinity,b2=0 projectively, with leaves0,1,3. Write

 c_i=a_i+b_i, t_i=b_i/c_i=i*r_i/kappa, r_i real.

The three central exchange contacts are precisely Re(kappa*t_i)=0.
Normalize the central row/column to obtain

 F2=[[1,0],[0,0]],
 Fi=[[1-t_i,1/c_i],[b_i(1-t_i),t_i]].

The directed center-three-cycle gaps are governed by

 Kij=r_i*r_j/kappa-i*r_j*a_j/c_i,
 Kii=-i*r_i,
 rank K<=2.

Indeed

 kappa*(1-tr(F2 Fi Fj))=Kij+i*(r_i+r_j).

The last term is pure imaginary, so ReKij is the corresponding primary
support gap. Exact index table, in peripheral leaf order(0,1,3):

 K01=A=g8, K10=E=g12,
 K30=B=g15, K03=D=g20,
 K13=C=g4, K31=F=g3.

The first directions(A,B,C) already form the oriented triangle0→1→3→0.
An earlier informal statement reversing B/D was corrected; the observed
primary-pair reflection(1,1,-1) is not derived from that informal argument.

The universal2-by-2 trace identity

 tr(XYZ)+tr(XZY)=tr(XY)trZ+tr(XZ)trY+tr(YZ)trX-trX trY trZ

then explains the three pair-sum identities after taking the supporting
real phase. These become the compulsory primary Gram orthogonality
relations A·E=B·D=C·F=0. Higher cycle traces retain the cross-module terms
that separated Jones/Markov constructions failed to capture.

3. A useful complex-analysis parameterization

For three distinct real r_i and three distinct c_i there is a unique
complex Mobius map c(r)=(alpha*r+beta)/(gamma*r+delta) taking r_i to c_i.
Up to an irrelevant common scale of the c_i, write

 c(r)=(r-z)/(r-p).

Then the basic source divided difference is explicitly

 r_j*c_j-r_i*c_i
   =(r_j-r_i)*[r_i*r_j-p(r_i+r_j)+z*p] /
     [(r_i-p)(r_j-p)],

up to the same common scale. This supplies a two-state rational realization
for a possible source-dependent positive-real/Hardy completion. It does
not prove that the associated ordinary Pick or Loewner kernels are PSD.

In fact the natural Hermitian divided kernel

 H_ij=(Kij+conj(Kji))/(i(r_i-r_j))

with the continuous diagonal limit is indefinite on the exact actual
control: its exact LDL pivot signs are(-,-,+), inertia(1+,2-). A plain
Loewner-positivity argument therefore fails and would require a genuine
indefinite realization/dilation step, not a relabeling of the kernel.
