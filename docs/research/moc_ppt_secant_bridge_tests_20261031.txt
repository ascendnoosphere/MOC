Testing a stronger full-cap bridge for the reduced auxiliary PPT cone
31 October 2026

The unrestricted MOC target is not proved. These are formal-cost tests of
the new universal sufficient cone, explicitly separated from actual spectra
and from exact feasibility certificates.

The sufficient cone used here is the new14+4 cone in Section5 of
moc_qft_auxiliary_ppt_jet_20261031.txt: a14-by14 PSD matrix on the jet image,
with only its parameter2-by-feature2 exceptional corner required PPT,
plus arbitrary nonnegative ordinary-minor ray coefficients. Both row/column
orientations are allowed. The larger full32PPT constraint was NOT used for
the reported successful runs.

Candidate global bridge under investigation

For a nonnegative canonical cap cost g=f34+sum v_j r_j, with Q(g)!=0,
put x=g-F(g)e/Q(g). Is N9(g)=0 alone enough to place g in the reduced
PPT-plus-ordinary cone? This is stronger than containment only for actual
real spectral costs: N9=0 gives a complex secant, and does not supply a
real conjugate-critical-point lift. The candidate is unproved.

This is a concrete alternative to real-lift exclusions. It can be refuted
by a formal nonnegative complex-secant cost with an exact dual separator;
a failed numerical projection is not such a refutation.

Exact full-cap inverse search followed by numerical cone tests

The degree theorem in moc_full_cap_nonic_degree_20261031.txt makes N9
quadratic in v17 with the other12coefficients fixed. For each chosen
rational fiber, five exact determinant values determine N9^2 (degree<=4),
and exact rational factorization extracts its quadratic factor. Numerical
positive roots are then used only to propose formal cone inputs.

Twelve rational fibers with coefficients of order one had no positive
v17 roots. Twelve further fibers with six small off-face coefficients
had two positive roots; this is a biased finite scan, not a distribution
or a coverage claim.

Thus the proposed numerical certificate has strict margins in all three

Independent direct negative-unitary search

Fifty BFGS starts on SO4 for each old formal control found minima near
zero and no negative cost. A separate150-start analytic-gradient SLSQP
search at the rational Q59 mixer tested split-real secants

 x=z(a,b)-z(c,d), all four spectra real,

for L_Q59(x)>max_pi x_pi. It found no strict violation. This finite
nonconvex search neither proves positivity of the formal controls nor
proves the split-real secant assertion.

What the tests change

The old exact nonactual/neutral/pencil obstructions do not block the new
sufficient cone: all tested ones are numerically covered. Hence there is
reason to test a uniform complex-secant containment theorem rather than
requiring a case-by-case exclusion of every nonactual real-form chamber.
No uniform containment or complete MOC proof follows from these tests.
