Exact algebraic theorem.
Let i run through any finite type, and let x_i,y_i,p_i,q_i,mu_i be complex
numbers. Assume

 normSq(x_i)+normSq(y_i)=1,
 p_i=mu_i conjugate(x_i), q_i=mu_i conjugate(y_i),
 x_i p_i+y_i q_i=m,
 sum_i(x_i p_i-y_i q_i)=0,
 sum_i x_i q_i=0,
 m!=0.

Then every mu_i equals m, and

 sum_i [x_i;y_i][conjugate(x_i),conjugate(y_i)]
   =(cardinality/2) I_2.

For index type Fin(2*n), the right-hand side is exactly n I_2. The module
proves both the separate lemmas and their direct composition
balanced_from_euler. There is no hidden restriction on repeated labels,
zero individual coordinates, finite cardinality, or complex phase of m.
The algebra works for any nonzero complex m, stronger than the real negative
m supplied by the contemplated minimax extraction.

Only the diagonal sl2-generator identity and one nilpotent-generator
identity are needed. The second off-diagonal moment vanishes by complex
conjugation, rather than a separate assumption.

The matrix moment is defined entrywise as the finite sum of outer-product
entries. Thus the final statement is an actual 2-by-2 complex matrix
identity, not a verbal use of 'balance'.

Scope and remaining bridge.
The arrays p,q are arbitrary algebraic arrays in the Lean statement. Their
identification with holomorphic derivatives, the existence of a compact
negative minimax witness, active-contact subgradients, the Euler identities
for actual matching determinants, and the derivation of the two sl2
identities are NOT formalized in this file. No such extraction theorem is
assumed as an axiom. The algebraic lemma is a verified client for any future
proof of that analytic extraction. Balance alone also gives no sign theorem
for the support gap and does not prove unrestricted MOC.
