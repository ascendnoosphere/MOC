THE INVERSE BALANCED-SOURCE DETERMINANT HIERARCHY
31 October 2026

Scope. This generalizes the elementary n=4 rank-four source factorization.
It supplies an explicit necessary algebraic structure for actual spectral
profiles in every dimension. It does not establish their convex-hull
containment, a positive SOS completion, or unrestricted MOC.

1. A direct identity valid for every n>=2

Choose real L of size n-by-(n-1) with L^T L=I and L^T 1=0. Put

 e0=1/sqrt(n), E=[e0,L], S=L K L^T,
 A=diag(a), B=diag(b),
 A0=L^T A L, B0=L^T B L,
 u=L^T A e0, v=L^T B e0.

For an actual matching profile z_pi=product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)),

 q_z(K)=sum_pi sgn(pi) z_pi product_i S_i,pi(i)
       =det(A S+S B).

The last determinant, conjugated by the orthogonal E, becomes

 det [[0,u^T K],[K v,A0 K+K B0]].

If K is invertible and H=K^(-T), multiplication on both sides by
diag(1,K^(-1)) gives the exact identity

 P_z(H):=q_z(K)/(det K)^2
        =-u^T adj(H^T A0+B0 H^T) v.                 (1)

This is homogeneous of degree n-2 in the(n-1)^2 independent entries of H.
If u or v is zero it is identically zero. Otherwise choose fixed complex
invertible P,Q with P v=e_last and u^T Q=e_last^T. The adjugate transformation
formula turns(1) into a nonzero scalar times the top-left(n-2)-by-(n-2)
determinant of P(H^T A0+B0 H^T)Q. Thus actual source polynomials are small
determinants of complex linear forms.

For n=2 this is a scalar; n=3 a linear form; n=4 a determinant2 quadratic;
n=5 a determinant3 cubic; n=6 a determinant4 quartic. The n=4 rank bound
four and real inertia bounds(4,4) are the first quadratic instance.

2. Why the inverse expression is polynomial on matching functions

For an ordinary subset indicator r_{I,J}, |I|=|J|=k, the signed pairing is

 q_r(K)=(-1)^(sum I+sum J) det S[I,J] det S[Ic,Jc].

Cauchy-Binet expresses each S minor in K minors with constant L coefficients.
Jacobi transforms a k-minor of K into detK times an(n-1-k)-minor of H,
and an(n-k)-minor into detK times a(k-1)-minor. Their product divided by
(detK)^2 is therefore polynomial of total degree n-2. For k=0,n the
expression is detS=0. Consequently the inverse polynomial map is linear
on the span of ordinary matching functions and kills constants.

All rank-two spectral product functions lie in this span: in the expansion
of product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)), choose which rows contribute b factors and sum
over their image subsets. This also proves the polynomial identity for
actual profiles without invoking any projective duality or nonic equation.

3. A fermionic and complex-analysis interpretation that is computational

The determinant in(1) is a Slater amplitude of n-2 linear modes. The source
restriction therefore retains exterior-power structure that is absent from
an arbitrary nonnegative algebraic nonic cost. For n=4 its real part is a
difference of at most four positive and four negative squares; the exact
new formal past cost outside full matching SOS violates this bound.

In the actual n=4 star face, a different sparse3-by-3 Fricke matrix M has
rank2 and Lorentz signature. After the six ray-trade reductions, all its
entries and diagonal entries are positive. This gives three real rapidities
theta_i and a positive kernel

 Kij=sech(theta_i-theta_j)=2sqrt(x_i*x_j)/(x_i+x_j), x_i=e^(2theta_i)>0.

The normalized exponential functions f_i(t)=sqrt(2x_i)exp(-x_i t) in L2(R+)
have Gram K. The fermionic wedge f_1 wedge ... wedge f_m has squared norm

 detK=product_(i<j)((x_i-x_j)/(x_i+x_j))^2
     =product_(i<j)tanh(theta_i-theta_j)^2.

This is an explicit positive determinant and not a claimed physical proof
of MOC. It gives a canonical nonlinear positive covariance, and explains
why rank3 rather than a single complex square can be natural.

Derivatives provide a positive Cauchy-jet extension:

 <t^k f_i,t^l f_j>
   =2sqrt(x_i*x_j)(k+l)!/(x_i+x_j)^(k+l+1).

These blocks retain nonzero cross terms and can be used for an analytic
Hardy-space interpolation/completion problem. For the star auxiliary corner,
functions U=ab,R=ad,S=cb,T=cd satisfy UT=RS pointwise, so their Gram obeys
C_UT=C_RS, exactly the forced two-qubit partial-transpose invariance.
Positive Cauchy-jet completion PLUS the twenty cost equations remains an
unproved interpolation problem; positivity alone does not solve it.
