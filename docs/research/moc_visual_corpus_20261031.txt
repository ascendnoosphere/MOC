Corpus visual analysis, 31 October 2026

Seven figures use the frozen unified file containing the same 20,127 original
records. They do not create additional observations. Every final PNG was opened
with view_image. Inspection led to more footer space, higher-contrast matrix
labels, explicit symlog thresholds, and a narrower optimizer roundoff zoom.
Code: code/research/moc_visual_corpus.py.
Figures, metrics and inspection hashes: build/moc_visual_atlas_20261031/corpus/.

01_sampling_and_scope.png
All integer dimensions 1 through 1000 occur, but the generators and tests change
with dimension. The 3,189 optimizer endpoints reuse 532 spectral cases. Large
records mostly use low-rank Householder products and selected inner hulls;
small records have complete permutation hulls. Source-by-family counts make
these differences visible. The classifications are original producer outputs:
7,478 numeric inner-hull witnesses, 7,218 numeric complete-hull interior cases,
3,189 members at optimizer tolerance, 2,004 degenerate-within-tolerance cases,
192 complete-hull boundary cases, 44 initially unresolved degenerate cases,
and two separately labelled exact/control records. These categories are not
independent unrestricted tests or exact certificates unless so indicated.

02_analysis_reach.png
This shows coverage of all eleven earlier roles and six new per-record roles.
Missing diagnostics are not failures. The seven research approaches produced
six per-record diagnostic roles because several contributions are exact family
theorems or cone certificates, not corpus-level measurements. New exact probes
are separately inventoried and do not inflate the original row count.

03_conditioning_and_family.png
The two upper boxplots use the SAME guarded n4 Haar cases. Their Gaussian to
anisotropic median raw-clearance ratio is 41.31, while the whitened ratio is
1.136. This isolates coordinate normalization within a retained sample.
The approximately 23,000 ratio in the earlier report used the broader raw
population; it also includes the effect of excluding numerically thin hulls.
Neither comparison should be substituted for the other.

Only 264/625 anisotropic and 444/625 clustered Haar cases pass the primary
shape guards, versus every Gaussian and disk case. Selection is therefore
part of the result. Clustered median whitened clearance is still only 0.4683,
against Gaussian 0.9281. The Gaussian dimension curve rises rapidly, while
the clustered curve is almost flat. Counts at n8 and n9 are only 15 and five
per family, so the right-hand ends cannot support precise asymptotic claims.
The boxplots omit individual outlier markers for legibility; full distributions
and extremes are retained in the scalar appendix and machine-readable data.

04_near_permutation_geometry.png
Among 857 eligible near-permutation cases, 277 have the identity matching
product on the numerical boundary and 580 have it in the interior. Their
clearance distributions differ by orders of magnitude, despite similar
mixing-versus-displacement trends. This follows the elementary Lipschitz
bound between the edge clearances of d and z_id; it is not a new conjecture.

The retrospective holdout illustrates the danger of pooled prediction.
Mixing-only AUC is 0.918 overall, 0.635 within near-permutation cases, and
0.587 within Haar cases. Adding identity-product clearance gives near-
permutation AUC 0.913. There are only nine positive Haar labels. The label
means normalized boundary proximity, not failure of MOC. The original trained
models and split were reused; no model was refitted to improve these plots.

05_weak_patterns_and_negative_control.png
This intentionally retains weak associations. Within Haar data the rank
correlation of mixing with clearance is 0.182; near a permutation it is 0.267.
Identity-product clearance correlates 0.245 with Haar clearance but 0.932
with near-permutation clearance. Correlations involving target radius and
clearance can partly reflect definitions and should not be treated as causal.

For 626 Gaussian Haar n4 cases, determinant phase versus whitened clearance
has rank correlation -0.0431. Its absolute value is exceeded in about 30% of
1,000 seeded random-label permutations. This exploratory comparison was not
a prespecified significance test; the numerical scatter is the relevant point.

There is a simple exact reason to expect this null relation in the circular
Gaussian model. Simultaneously rotating both spectra by exp(i theta) leaves
their joint law unchanged, rotates every product and d by exp(i n theta),
and leaves affine-normalized clearance unchanged. For d nonzero, every nonzero
Fourier coefficient of its phase conditional on any rotation-invariant
statistic must therefore vanish. Thus phase is uniform and independent of
that statistic under this rotationally invariant ensemble. This argument
does not apply to arbitrary fixed spectra or anisotropic sampling, and says
nothing about MOC validity. It explains why absolute phase is a useful
negative control rather than a promising search coordinate here.

06_optimizer_noise_and_reuse.png
The largest recorded positive support excess is 1.166e-15. The zoom shows the
floating noise near zero instead of hiding it beneath the broad negative
tail. Of 3,189 starts, 1,629 lie within +/-1e-12 of zero. Gradient convergence
alone neither proves a global maximum nor certifies an exact boundary point.
The figure separates real orthogonal and complex unitary searches; the real
subgroup is insufficient for the unrestricted target.

07_cross_method_failure_sets.png
This is a new join across the approaches. It uses 3,178 records satisfying
the primary shape guard and the topology edge guard, with the required
diagnostics present. It is a smaller denominator than each individual lane.

The covariance-formula failure set (weight below -1e-9) has nine elements;
the whitened initial heat-expansion set has 21; the winding-miss set has 78.
These three sets are pairwise disjoint in this retained sample. All 78 winding
misses are clustered; 59 also defeat diagonal pinching. None of the nine
covariance failures defeats pinching, and only one is within the descriptive
0.05 boundary threshold. Thus a single observed notion of numerical difficulty
does not explain these three failed approaches.

The explicit covariance formula or the winding test succeeds for every one
of these 3,178 records. A nonzero winding supplies hull membership for the
tested point as well as the separate attained-region interpretation. This
suggests complementary constructive certificates. It does not prove that
the union covers arbitrary spectra or mixers. In particular these ordinary-
support campaign records do not adequately sample the remaining nonordinary
support families.

Every retained record has a negative entry in the canonical heat law. That
law's sign therefore cannot distinguish these successful and unsuccessful
methods. The fixed law's failure is a constraint on the representation, not
an MOC counterexample. Six of the nine covariance failures are inconclusive
for Gram; three receive a numerical Gram certificate. All 78 winding misses
are inconclusive for Gram, which is another reason not to equate missing a
certificate with leaving the permutation hull.

The reports and atlas preserve raw IDs for follow-up. The findings are
descriptive except for the separately stated symmetry argument. No full
unrestricted proof follows from these plots.
