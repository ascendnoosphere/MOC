A nonordinary spectral family closed by exact support certificates
31 October 2026

The variational obstruction has a positive resolution for its entire spectral
family. For every real r>=1, every U in U(4), and

  a=(1,-1,-ir,ir),       b=(-r,r,-i,i),

MOC holds: det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*) belongs to the convex hull of the 24
matching products. The same conclusion holds on the full pole-free complex
Möbius orbit of these spectra, with independent row/column relabelings. This
is a family theorem, not a proof of unrestricted MOC4. Its new ingredient is
an explicit all-support certificate for these spectra. It imports the
repository's previously proved universal weighted-minor inequality.

This family is not ordinary-minor positive: for every r>1 exactly two of its
four hull-edge costs have a strictly positive mixed-ray coefficient. It is
also the family containing the dense Walsh stationary point with a negative
semidefinite Hessian and cubic ascent. Thus the result closes a concrete
nonordinary family that defeats the first and second variation tests.

1. The permutation hull

Put Y=2r(r^2-1), L=(r+1)^4 and

  X=max(4r^2, 2r(r-1)^2).

Direct expansion of the 24 products gives the following nine distinct
expressions before exceptional coincidences:

  +/- iY,
  -2r(r+1)^2, -4r^2, -(r^2-1)^2, -(r-1)^4, -(r+1)^4,
  2r(r-1)^2, 4r^2.

For r>1 their convex hull is the quadrilateral with vertices -L, X, iY,-iY.
Every real expression is between -L and X. The rightmost vertex switches when
Delta=r^2-4r+1 changes sign, at r=2+sqrt(3). The r=1 case follows by continuity.
It suffices to prove the four supporting-edge inequalities below.

2. The only imported positivity theorem

Write rho(I,J)=|det U[I,J]|^2, using one-based indices. Ordinary rays r_j are
single squared minors in the repository's canonical 82-ray numbering. The
mixed ray f34 is

  f34=rho(3,3)+rho(2,4)+rho(12,13)-rho(13,23).

The already proved weighted-minor theorem states, for every complex unitary U
and every tau>0,

  f34 + tau*rho(23,34) + tau^(-1)*rho(13,13) >= 0.

Here rho(23,34)=r28 and rho(13,13)=r23, so tau=1 gives

  f34+r23+r28 >=0.                                           (W)

The theorem and its analytic proof are in
  docs/geometry/global_weighted_minor_notes.txt.
Its proof on SO(4), followed by the proved convexification of common-minor
profiles, is needed for arbitrary complex U. This report does not claim a new
proof of that imported theorem. All ordinary rays used below are nonnegative.

The determinant's minor expansion and unitary identities identify its support
cost with the common-minor functional having the same 24 permutation costs.
The exact 14-dimensional identification is already used by the repository's
cone certificates. The new verifier checks every cost identity as a polynomial
in r on all 24 permutations. Thus it proves identities of the corresponding
unitary functionals; it does not infer a unitary inequality merely from values
at the permutations. A separate dimension/rank check in the verifier explains
this identification from complementary two-minors and their row/column norms.
Explicitly, complementary two-minors give 18 coordinates q. Their row and
column norm sums impose five independent affine equations. The resulting
constant-plus-q space has dimension 14; its evaluation on the 24 permutation
matrices also has rank 14. The singleton identity

  2|U_ij|^2 = sum_{I contains i, J contains j} rho(I,J) - 1

places first- and third-order minors in the same space. It follows by expanding
the two-minors and using row/column orthogonality. Thus the exact rank check
identifies the evaluation kernel with identities already valid for all U.

3. Both nonordinary right edges

For either sign, take the edge from X to +/-iY. Row and column relabeling
transports its cost to the canonical f34 cone. The explicit reindexings are
stored in cross_spectral_family_exact.json; the two signs give identical
coefficient formulas after reindexing.

When Delta<=0, use the unnormalized support inequality

  (r^2-1) Re d +/- 2r Im d <= 4r^2(r^2-1).

Its deficit, after the stated relabeling, is exactly

  (r^2-1) [4r(r-1)^2 f34
    -2r Delta (r17+r20+r30+r31)
    +P r18 + 8r^2(r23+r28)],
  P=r^4-4r^3+10r^2-4r+1=(r-1)^4+4r^2.

Replace the first and last terms by

  4r(r-1)^2 (f34+r23+r28) -4r Delta (r23+r28).

Every coefficient is nonnegative for r>=1 and Delta<=0. Equation (W) therefore
proves both right-edge inequalities in this branch.

When Delta>=0, use

  (r+1) Re d +/- (r-1) Im d <= 2r(r-1)^2(r+1).

Its deficit is exactly

  (r+1) [2r(r^2+1)(f34+r23+r28)
    +(r-1)^2(r^2+1) r18 +2r Delta(r22+r27)].

Again every coefficient is nonnegative. This proves both right edges for the
second branch. At Delta=0 both descriptions are valid. No optimization or
stationarity assumption on U enters this proof.

For r>1 the mixed coefficient is respectively

  4r(r-1)^3(r+1),       or       2r(r+1)(r^2+1),

and is strictly positive. The canonical capacity dual evaluates this mixed
coefficient with a minus sign and annihilates the 13 accompanying ordinary
rays. Hence these two actual hull edges are outside the ordinary-minor cone.
The mixed inequality is essential to this certificate.

4. Both left edges

The left-edge inequalities are

  -Y Re d +/- L Im d <= YL.

For the minus sign, the exact deficit is

  A(r17+r20+r22+r27) + B r21 + C(r24+r29) + D(r30+r31),

where

  A=2r(r-1)(r+1)^3(r^2+1),
  B=2r(r-1)(r+1)(r^2+1)^2,
  C=8r^2(r-1)(r+1)(r^2+1),
  D=8r^2(r-1)(r+1)^3.

For the plus sign, replace the four parenthesized groups by, respectively,

  (r18+r19+r28+r33), r21, (r24+r29), (r23+r26).

All coefficients are nonnegative for r>=1. The certificate checks both
identities exactly. Together with the two right edges this proves the stated
MOC theorem for every U, including every support maximizer. It does not claim
that all maximizers are monomial.

5. Möbius propagation

Let f(z)=(alpha*z+beta)/(gamma*z+delta), with nonzero
D=alpha*delta-beta*gamma and no poles at any a_i or -b_j. Set

  a'_i=f(a_i),       b'_j=-f(-b_j).

For C=-U diag(b) U*, the ordered matrix identity is

  (gamma*A+delta*I)[f(A)-f(C)](gamma*C+delta*I)=D(A-C).

Expanding the two ordered products cancels the AC terms without requiring
A and C to commute. Normality is preserved by rational functional calculus.
Taking determinants proves that both the target determinant and every matching
product are multiplied by the same nonzero scalar

  K=D^4/[ product_i(gamma*a_i+delta)
           product_j(delta-gamma*b_j) ].

Multiplication by K preserves real convex combinations. The family theorem
therefore extends to its entire finite pole-free Möbius orbit. This covariance
is an existing lemma; see docs/research/mobius_universal_b_lemmas.md. It does
not mean that a Möbius map itself preserves planar convex hulls. Permuting either
spectrum and applying limits where all transformed spectral entries stay finite
also preserve the conclusion.

6. What the higher variations say

At the real Walsh mixer W/2 from the earlier obstruction, put d0=r(r^2-1)>0.
The support F(U)=Re(i det(A+U diag(b) U*)) has a Hessian of rank six on the
twelve real off-diagonal tangent coordinates. A basis of its kernel is

  X1=K12+K34,             Y1=i(E12+E21+E34+E43),
  X2=K13-K24,             Y2=i(E13+E31+E24+E42),
  X3=K14+K23,             Y3=i(E14+E41+E23+E32),

where Kij=Eij-Eji. The three Y directions commute with the conjugated B; they
are right-phase stabilizers. In kernel coordinates
Z=x1 X1+y1 Y1+x2 X2+y2 Y2+x3 X3+y3 Y3, the full third directional derivative is

  D^3 F[Z,Z,Z] = 96 d0 x1 x2 x3.

The exact r=3 expansion is in kernel_cubic.json. Its extension to all r follows
because the imaginary support functional divided by r(r^2-1) is independent
of r: its 24 permutation values are the same constants 0,+2,-2, and the
common-minor identification applies. Thus the physical Hessian kernel after
removing the phase stabilizers has three dimensions, with a product cubic.
Each coordinate null flow is flat, while any direction with x1*x2*x3 nonzero
has one sign of ascent. This identifies the higher-order escape mechanism for
the entire stationary family. The global edge certificates above go further:
they bound every mixer, rather than only excluding this stationary point.

Their only axioms are propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; there is no sorry or

with explicit hypotheses on the ray values. The matrix identities, universal
weighted-minor theorem and complete MOC family theorem are not yet formalized
in this module. This distinction is intentional and must be preserved.

The polynomial certificate has no floating-point proof step. A linear program
was used to discover the left-edge ordinary-ray combination, after which exact
rational reconstruction and symbolic identities checked it. No new observations
were added to the frozen 20,127-record campaign. Checks on explicit r=2,3,5
inputs and their Möbius images are supplementary validation, not the proof.

A literature check found Mulero-Martínez, A variational framework for
determinantal inequalities of normal matrices: Successes and obstructions,
Linear Algebra and its Applications 740 (2026), 19-38,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2026.03.019.
The publisher abstract explicitly allows nonmonomial support maximizers and
retains unrestricted MOC as open. The full text returned HTTP403 here, so its
proofs and exact examples were not inspected. No historical novelty claim is
made for this family closure or the variational calculations.

The remaining gap is precise: these cross-spectral configurations and their
Möbius orbits occupy a restricted spectral locus. General nonordinary six-,
seven- and eight-contact supports need not have the same two equal compensation
budgets. The proof supplies a complete new family certificate, not a reduction
asserting that all spectra lie in this family.
