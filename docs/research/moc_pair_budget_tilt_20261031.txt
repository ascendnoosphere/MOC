Direct exact multiplication gives

 P^T C P=diag(1,1,2,1)/16,

 [a,b]^T R[a,b]=diag(0,-1)/16.

Set delta=epsilon/(1+epsilon), so0<delta<1 and delta<epsilon, and put

                         U_delta=[P,a-delta b].

Then the two crucial compressions are exactly

 U_delta^T C U_delta=diag(1,1,2,1,2delta)/16 >=delta I/16,

 U_delta^T(R+epsilon C)U_delta
   =diag(epsilon,epsilon,2epsilon,1+epsilon,
                                  delta(2epsilon-delta))/16 >0.       (1)

This is a rational moving plane. Its last cap-positive direction becomes
isotropic as epsilon approaches zero; no fixed nondegenerate limit is
being asserted.

                  g=t f34+sum_j nu_j r_j,

with nu_j=g(pi_j)>=0 whenever g>=0. For any of the planes above, with
A=U^T C U>0 and X_j=U^T[M(r_j)+beta_j C]U>0, one has the exact identity

                U^T M(g)U+y_epsilon(g) A=sum_j g(pi_j)X_j.
