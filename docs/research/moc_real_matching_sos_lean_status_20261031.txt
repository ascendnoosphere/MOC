The four matrix indices are Fin2 times Fin2, in the order
 (0,0), (0,1), (1,0), (1,1).
The partial transpose exchanges the first qubit indices. K is the
actual tensor product of J2=[[0,1],[-1,0]] with itself.

What Lean proves

1. For any commutative ring and any four-vector u, the rank-one matrix
Q_ij=u_i u_j obeys
 Q^PT-Q=-det(mat2(u)) K.
The scalar determinant is also identified with the actual Fin2 matrix
determinant. All16 entries of the four-by-four matrix identity are
proved by finite index elimination and ring normalization.

2. For an arbitrary real symmetric four-by-four matrix A,
 A-A^PT=(A_03-A_12)K,
 sum_ij K_ij A_ij=2(A_03-A_12).
Consequently zero pairing with K forces A^PT=A. These are finite
matrix identities, with no positivity or numerical assumptions.

3. For any finite embedding matrix E, the rank-one PT defect pushes
forward exactly, and
 E Q^PT E^T+det(mat2(u)) E K E^T=E Q E^T.
The proof includes finite bilinear pushforward linearity and the exact
identity E(uu^T)E^T=(Eu)(Eu)^T.

4. Real Gram quadratic forms expand into finite sums of squares. Thus
if H=Gram(W)+E Q^PT E^T and S=E K E^T, then
 H+det(mat2(u))S
has a nonnegative quadratic form. This is theorem
real_clifford_gauge_nonnegative. It is conditional on the displayed
decomposition; the file does not postulate that decomposition for O4.

5. For arbitrary finite signed weights c, signed balancing weights s,
and feature values f_p,i, Lean proves the moment/quadratic identity
 a^T moment(c,f) a=sum_p c_p (sum_i a_i f_p,i)^2,
and the exact identity for adding a scalar multiple of s to c.

Theorem sos_expectation_of_gauge then proves:
 - the shifted moment matrix has nonnegative quadratic form;
 - g is explicitly a finite sum of squared linear feature combinations;
 - sum_p s_p g_p=0;
 ==> sum_p c_p g_p>=0.
No assumption c_p>=0 is made or needed.

6. The final theorem support_bound_of_real_clifford_sos composes the
Clifford matrix decomposition, the sign-kernel identity, and the SOS
condition. With total mass sum c=1 and a specified score identity
sum c_p score_p=determinantScore, it proves
 determinantScore<=height.
This is an actual support-certificate client with all required
mathematical inputs explicit.
