M01: a universal inequality beyond the existing weighted-minor cone
==================================================================

STATUS: proved auxiliary positivity theorem. This does not prove MOC4.
Its proof works directly for all complex unitary 4x4 matrices.

Use zero-based matrix entries in this note. Let r_j denote the canonical
ordinary squared-minor rays, f=f34=r7+r10+r17-r25, and

 H=r0+r7+r10+r13+r17+r20+r22+r27+r30+r31.

THEOREM. Every U in U(4) satisfies

 f +(1615/196)r0 +(501/196)r7 +(4835/588)r10 +(697/196)r13
   +(403/56)r17 +(459/56)(r20+r22+r27+r30+r31) >= 0.       (1)

In particular f+(33/4)H >=0. The largest unequal coefficient in (1)
is 1615/196, which is slightly smaller than 33/4. No spectral assumptions are needed.

Proof. Put B_ij=|U_ij|^2, a=B00,b=B13,c=B22,d=B31, S=a+b+c+d,
x=B01,y=B02,z=B03, and

 T=y(B10+B23)+z(B11+B30)+x(B20+B32).

The following inequality holds for every doubly stochastic B:

                  y B21 <= (3/2)T+S.                          (2)

Here is a finite exact proof of (2), not a numerical certificate. By
Birkhoff's theorem write B=sum_sigma alpha_sigma P_sigma, alpha>=0,
sum alpha=1, in lexicographic S4 order. Let F(B)=(3/2)T-yB21 and define

 A_sigma,tau=F(P_sigma+P_tau)-F(P_sigma)-F(P_tau)
             +S(P_sigma)+S(P_tau).

Then F(B)+S(B)=alpha^T A alpha/2. The exact rational matrix A has just two
negative entries, A_12,23=A_23,12=-1. If v=e12-e23, the matrix
N=A-vv^T is entrywise nonnegative (all 576 entries are checked exactly; 2N is integral).
Thus alpha^T A alpha=(alpha12-alpha23)^2+alpha^T N alpha>=0.

Let M=r17+r20+r22+r27+r30+r31. The six products occurring in T are

 U02 U10, U03 U11, U01 U20, U02 U23, U03 U30, U01 U32.

Each is, up to sign, the corresponding 2-minor plus respectively

 U00 U12, U01 U13, U00 U21, U03 U22, U00 U33, U02 U31.

The sum of the squared moduli of these six error products is at most
3a+b+c+d, because each |U_ij|<=1. Young's inequality with eta=7/2
therefore gives

 T <= (9/2)M +(9/7)(3a+b+c+d).

Combining with (2),

 y B21 <= (27/4)M +(95/14)a +(41/14)(b+c+d).

Apply Young's inequality again, this time with tau=14/3, to
r25=|U01 U22-U02 U21|^2:

 r25 <= (17/3)c+(17/14)yB21
      <= (459/56)M +(1615/196)a +(697/196)b
                        +(5423/588)c +(697/196)d.

Finally f=b+c+r17-r25. Rearrangement proves (1). QED.

Why this is a new certificate relative to our established family:
The cap has compensator coordinates (r18,r23,r28). They are all zero
in f+(33/4)H. Its mixed coefficient is one. The exact maximal weighted
closure theorem (moonshots_04_06.txt) proves that no conic combination
of ANY of the 48 mixed rays' previously established weighted laws,
together with ordinary nonnegative rays, can certify this cost.
Thus (1) strictly enlarges that certificate cone.

Limitation: at least six contacts leave at most seven ordinary cap
coordinates; H uses ten. The displayed theorem does not by itself close
the localized six-contact cases. It supplies a genuinely different
positive atom and may be propagated or adapted to sparser budgets.
