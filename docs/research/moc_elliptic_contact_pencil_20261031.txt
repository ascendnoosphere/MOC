COMPUTABLE ELLIPTIC CONTACT PENCILS FROM ACTUAL SOURCES
31 October 2026

This gives a concrete map from a generic five-dimensional conormal-cost
space to a real pencil of4-by-4 quadrics. It does NOT construct the missing
uniform matching-SOS Gram or prove unrestricted MOC.

Let V be the14-dimensional matching-function space and let n be a smooth
real N8' conormal with n.e=0. Biduality identifies its source-contact space
B=normal(T_n N8') as a four-dimensional vector space in V. The corresponding
projective critical-source curve is an elliptic normal quartic E in P(B).
Conormal costs have the form g=h e+B x.

DIRECT SOURCE CONSTRUCTION

When an actual source p_pi=product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)) is given, n can be recovered
without differentiating a degree-nine polynomial. Solve the real linear
equations annihilating e, Re/Im p, and all eight complex spectral derivatives,
within the14-dimensional matching dual. The normal is generically unique.

Let f_n(a,b)=sum_pi n_pi p_pi(a,b). At a generic critical source its complex
8-by-8 Hessian has rank4. Its kernel maps under the source differential to
the two-dimensional complex span of p and a tangent p' to the critical
elliptic curve. Hence

 B=span_R(Re p,Im p,Re p',Im p').

This recovers B directly from source derivatives and avoids inverting the
SL4/Gale invariant quotient. It is a generic construction: rank drops must
be detected rather than silently treated as a four-dimensional tangent.

Let q_a be the14 HMSV critical quadrics, represented by symmetric quadratic
matrices T_a in the selected product coordinates. Restrict them to B:

 Q_a=B_selected^T T_a B_selected.

Generically these14 symmetric4-by-4 matrices span exactly TWO independent
quadrics Q0,Q1. Their intersection is E. The binary quartic det(Q0+z Q1)
records the singular quadrics of the pencil. This is the CONTACT-curve
pencil, not automatically the original pencil through eight SL4 labels;
the two elliptic curves are related through the ruling/Picard construction.

A RATIONAL RULING TEST FOR ACTUALITY

Given x not on E, put a0=x^TQ0x, a1=x^TQ1x and

 Qx=a1 Q0-a0 Q1.

This is the unique member of the pencil containing x. If x is the real
part of a nonreal conjugate critical source, its conjugate secant line is
real and lies on Qx. Thus a nonsingular Qx must have inertia(2,2).

In that split chart, take two quotient directions u,v in the tangent
hyperplane x^perp_Qx modulo x. Write their null-direction polynomial as

 a+2b t+c t^2, c!=0, b^2-ac>0.

The two roots give the two ruling lines span(x,u+t v). Restrict either
Q0 or Q1 which does not vanish at x to that line. Its intersection with E
has a quadratic discriminant delta(t). Negative discriminant gives a
nonreal conjugate source pair; positive discriminant gives two real source
points instead. Reducing delta(t) modulo the null-direction polynomial gives
r0+r1 t, so the trace and norm of the two ruling discriminants are rational:

 trace =2r0-2b r1/c,
 norm  =r0^2-2b r0r1/c+a r1^2/c.

Away from tangent and critical-point degeneracies, a conjugate secant exists
iff norm<0 OR trace<0. Negative norm means exactly one conjugate secant;
positive norm and negative trace mean two. This is the quadric-ruling
description of the earlier2-by-2 moment-line future test, not an additional
positivity theorem. Degenerate Qx and points on E need separate charts.

EXACT CHECKS

code/research/moc_actual_contact_elliptic_pencil.py reconstructs the complete
contact space and pencil with exact arithmetic. The original nonordinary
actual seed and the direct Gaussian-integer triangle counterexample both
give source-Hessian rank4, critical-quadric restriction span2, and Qx inertia
(2,2). The known conjugate source line is exactly isotropic for Qx.

code/research/moc_elliptic_ruling_future.py checks the rational ruling test.
Both anchors have negative discriminant norm and exactly one conjugate
secant, agreeing with their definite moment-line chamber. Matrices B,Q0,Q1,
binary-quartic coefficients, and conormal cost coordinates are saved under
build/moc_qft_polynomial_tilt/actual_contact_elliptic_pencil.json and
elliptic_ruling_future.json.

The sharp eleven-contact equality anchor is genuinely exceptional. Its
source-critical Hessian has rank3 and its real normal singleton matrix is

  [[6,-6,-6,6],[6,-6,-6,6],[-6,6,6,-6],[-6,6,6,-6]].

This has rank1 and Kirchhoff cofactor tau=0, precisely the both-label-groups-
dependent chart in the N8' audit. Its known two-square certificate remains
valid, but the generic tangent reconstruction is not used there.

WHAT REMAINS

The desired next step is a constructive full matching-SOS Gram on each
cost space h e+B x, using either this pencil geometry or the stronger
all-real-N9 face approach. Merely recognizing the future branch does not
give such a Gram. Cross terms involving all ordinary modules and all ten
nonmatching cancellations must remain available; small source-plane and
coherent-block compressions have already been shown insufficient.
