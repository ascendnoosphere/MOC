Connectedness of the positive-height determinant branch for the eight-contact child
2026-10-30

Consider g=f34+a r0+b r7+c r10+d r17+e r28, all coefficients nonnegative,
with
 F=-(b+1)(c+1)(a+d+1), Q=de-d-1,
 P=(a+d+1)[(b+1)(c+1)+e(b+c+1-d)]+de(d+e+1).
The determinant condition P=0 and h=F/Q>0 imply Q<0 and
 U=(b+1)(c+1)+e(b+c+1-d)<0. Indeed, P=0 implies U<=0, but U=0 would force
de(d+e+1)=0; then U=(b+1)(c+1)+e(b+c+1)>0, a contradiction. Thus d,e>0.
Put
 D0=b+c+1+(b+1)(c+1)/e,
 a=[(d+1){(b+1)(c+1)+e(b+c+1)}+de^2]/[de-(b+1)(c+1)-e(b+c+1)].
The numerator and denominator are strictly positive. The branch is the graph
of this unique positive a over
 b,c>=0, e>0, d>D0, and, when e>1, d<1/(e-1).

The graph's base is path connected, including b=0 and c=0. For fixed b,c,
the base e values are (0,E(b,c)), where E>1 is the unique solution of
 (e-1)D0(e)=1. Existence and uniqueness follow because this expression is 0
at e=1, strictly increasing for e>1, and tends to infinity. E is continuous.
For e<=1 no upper d bound is needed. For e>1, the inequality e<E makes the
open d interval nonempty. Thus (b,c,e) can first be moved linearly in e to
1/2, then b,c can be varied, then e moved to any target value; all paths
remain in the base because E(b,c)>1 everywhere.

The d fibers are nonempty connected open intervals and possess the continuous
section
 m=max(e-1,0),    d_*=D0+(1-m D0)/(1+m).
For e<=1 this is D0+1. For e>1, mD0<1, so d_*>D0 and m d_*<1.
Connect each endpoint to its section value inside its fixed d interval and
follow the section along the above base path. The uniquely determined a then
varies continuously. This explicitly proves path connectedness of the full
positive-height branch, with no interior-only caveat on b,c.

If a separate certificate proves rank(H_x)<=12 throughout this branch and
the Hessian-pencil z^2 coefficient is strictly positive there, then the
rank is exactly 12 and H_e restricted to ker H_x is nondegenerate everywhere.
Its inertia is constant by the connectedness just proved. The exact seed
(a,b,c,d,e)=(71,1,1,8,1) gives F=-320, Q=-1, h=320 and
 Q|ker(H_x) = [[9/2164820,-31/86592800],
                [-31/86592800,60561/13854848000]],
whose leading entry and determinant 1/55419392000 are positive. Consequently
that certificate would make the restriction positive definite everywhere,
contradicting Q(y)=Q(x)=Q<0 for an actual real lift. This note does not
claim the required rank and pencil certificates have already been completed.
