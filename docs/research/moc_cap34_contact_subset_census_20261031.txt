5. PRECISE CONTACT-RANK HYPOTHESES KEPT SEPARATE

Grace's second-order theorem is documented in
  moc_actual_nonic_second_order_contact_rank_20261031.txt.
At a normalized negative local minimum of a linear physical objective,
assume rank8 neutral M(g), face-CQ DN9|ker C nonzero, actual-source image
locally open within the face-restricted real nonic, and a nonzero nonic
multiplier. A sufficient openness condition involves Terracini rank13.

Let B(d)=M(d)h with h in ker M(g), t=rank B, r=rank(C restricted to ker B),
and k=rank C. The necessary second-order inequality is
  k-r>=t-5,
equivalently rank(B restricted to ker C)<=5. When t=9 this is k-r>=4.
Since constant costs lie in ker B and their contact values are all one,
r>=1, so k>=5. Generic r=4 would require k>=8. On our canonical zero
rows k=count exactly, as independently proved above.

For k>=8, the contact costspace has dimension<=6 and its radial g is in
the restricted kernel, making the rank<=5 dimension obstruction
automatic. This explains why the high-contact families are the sharp
next finite targets; it does not discard the k5..7 rank-deficient cases.
Rank7 pencils, source/future boundary, CQ failure, lower psi rank and
multiplier exceptions remain separate. No global reduction is claimed.
