M04. Exact maximal closure of the established weighted-law library
-----------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: PROVED SHARP OBSTRUCTION, with explicit primal and dual recovery.

Let W be the conic hull of all ordinary rays and all previously established
continuous weighted laws

       f_i+t r_p+t^(-1)r_q,  t>0,

for all48 mixed labels and their three compensator pairs. For a cap cost

 g=f+A r18+B r23+C r28+sum_(j in J) b_j r_j,  b_j>=0,

membership in W depends only on (A,B,C). Put M=max(A,B,C), S=A+B+C.
The exact condition is

 M<=1 and S>=2,      or      M>=1 and M(S-M)>=1.             (M04.1)

No choice of the other ten ordinary coefficients can improve this test.

Proof of sufficiency and exact primal recovery. Let c=(A,B,C). If
sum min(c_i,1)>=2, choose v_i<=min(c_i,1) with sum v_i=2. The three
unit-parameter weighted laws with weights lambda_ij=1-v_k, k not in{i,j},
have total mixed coefficient one and ordinary coefficients v_i. Add the
nonnegative remainders c_i-v_i.
Otherwise (M04.1) forces a unique largest coefficient M>1, with D=S-M<1
and MD>=1. For each other coefficient c_j use weight c_j/D on

                 f+(1/D)r_M+D r_j.

Their sum uses coefficient1/D<=M on r_M and exactly c_j on the others.
Add (M-1/D)r_M. All operations are rational for rational input.

Proof of necessity against the entire library. Prescribe a linear functional
ell on the14 independent cap coordinates by

 ell(f)=-1, ell(r18)=X, ell(r23)=Y, ell(r28)=Z,
 ell(r_j)=0 for j in J.

For X,Y,Z>=0, exact cap-basis evaluation gives ell(r_k)>=0 for every
ordinary ray. For every other mixed label i!=34, ell(f_i) has constant
term T(34,i)>=1 and nonnegative X,Y,Z coefficients. This also follows
from the ordinary absorption identities and the maximal-ratio identities
T(34,i)f+f_i=nonnegative capordinary. Thus all weighted laws for i!=34
have strictly positive ell value. The only remaining conditions are

                   XY>=1/4, XZ>=1/4, YZ>=1/4.

They make ell nonnegative on all three continuous weighted laws for f.
If M<=1 and S<2, choose X=Y=Z=1/2. Then ell(g)=S/2-1<0.
If M>=1 and M(S-M)<1, put 1/(2M) at the largest coefficient and M/2 at
the other two coordinates. The three product conditions hold, and
ell(g)=[M(S-M)-1]/2<0. These are exact rational separating functionals.

M05. New universal inequalities from doubly stochastic quadratic bounds
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: PROVED NEW POSITIVE FAMILY, including a seven-contact family.
A numerical sharp-constant search remains exploratory.

The dense ten-coordinate inequality has been strengthened to

                         f+(33/4)sum_(j in J) r_j>=0           (M05.1)

for all complex U in U(4). The stronger unequal coefficients and their
proof are in cap_other_ten_bound.txt. The exact verifier is
code/research/verify_cap_other_ten_bound.py. It uses a Birkhoff quadratic
certificate with coefficient3/2 and exact Young parameters7/2,14/3.
The contact set of this dense atom has only three permutations, so conic
combinations containing it cannot produce a cost with six contacts.

A sparse refinement removes that limitation. For all U in U(4),

             f+12r0+5r7+r10+5r17+4r20+4r30>=0.               (M05.2)

Its permutation contact set is exactly

                        {7,8,9,14,15,18,22}.

It has seven contacts, with only six ordinary coordinates. All three old
compensator coefficients are zero. By M04, neither (M05.1) nor (M05.2)
belongs to the conic hull of any of the48 previous weighted families.

Proof of the sparse bound. Write B_ij=|U_ij|^2, and use zero-based entries.
Every doubly stochastic4x4 matrix satisfies

 B02 B21 <= (3/2)B02 B10+B03 B11+B03 B30+B00+B13.             (M05.3)

For an exact proof, write B=sum alpha_sigma P_sigma by Birkhoff's theorem.
Let

 F(B)=(3/2)B02 B10+B03 B11+B03 B30-B02 B21,
 S(B)=B00+B13,
 A_sigma,tau=F(P_sigma+P_tau)-F(P_sigma)-F(P_tau)
                                      +S(P_sigma)+S(P_tau).

In lexicographic S4 order put v=e12-e23 and w=e13-e23. The verifier checks
all576 entries of N=A-vv^T-ww^T and proves N is entrywise nonnegative.
Therefore

 F(B)+S(B)=[(alpha12-alpha23)^2+(alpha13-alpha23)^2
                                             +alpha^T N alpha]/2>=0.

Now use

 B02 B10 <=2r17+2B00,
 B03 B11 <=2r20+2B13,
 B03 B30 <=2r30+2B00.

Each follows from the corresponding determinant and |a+b|^2<=2|a|^2+2|b|^2,
using |U_ij|<=1 on the remaining factor. Inserting these into (M05.3) gives

 B02 B21 <=3r17+2r20+2r30+6B00+3B13.

Finally

 r25=|U01 U22-U02 U21|^2 <=2B22+2B02 B21,
 f=B13+B22+r17-r25.

Rearrangement gives (M05.2), directly on every complex unitary matrix.

There is a four-parameter family. For eta17,eta20,eta30,tau>0, put F=1+1/tau
and define

 c0 =F[1+(3/2)(1+1/eta17)+(1+1/eta30)],
 c7 =F(2+1/eta20)-1,       c10=tau,
 c17=(3/2)F(1+eta17)-1,
 c20=F(1+eta20),           c30=F(1+eta30).

Then f+sum c_j r_j>=0. This follows from the same proof with independent
Young parameters. Setting all four parameters to one recovers (M05.2). The parameters
(5/2,2,10/3,21/4) give the simpler uniform consequence

                 f+(21/4)(r0+r7+r10+r17+r20+r30)>=0.
For a proposed budget b on these six labels, the maximal permissible
parameters are explicit:

 tau=b10, F=1+1/tau,
 eta17=(b17+1)/[(3/2)F]-1,
 eta20=b20/F-1, eta30=b30/F-1.

Positive values and the remaining c0<=b0,c7<=b7 conditions are necessary
and sufficient for membership in this parameter family plus ordinary
remainders. Increasing any eta reduces its singleton budget, and choosing
tau=b10 minimizes all other required budgets, proving this recovery rule.
The verifier implements it as family_membership.

M06. Ternary or recursive pure-mixed capacity operations
-------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: PROVED OBSTRUCTION TO THIS EXTENSION; no new ternary atoms.
The nonlinear unitary SPN method in M05 does escape the M04 obstruction.

For a triple of mixed labels with capacities a=T12,b=T13,c=T23, exact
ordinary membership of nonnegative coefficients x,y,z is characterized by

 x<=ay+bz,       y<=ax+cz,       z<=bx+cy.

This follows from the common duals and constructive weighted allocation in
mixed_cancellation_graph.txt. Each capacity is at least one. A cone extreme
ray cannot have three positive coordinates: two distinct balance equalities
would imply, for example, x>=y+z and y>=x+z, contrary to z>0. Its extreme
rays are therefore the already certified binary endpoint ratios

 (a,1,0),(1,a,0),(b,0,1),(1,0,b),(0,c,1),(0,1,c),

with duplicates when a capacity is one. The verifier enumerates exact
intersections of every pair of the six bounding planes.

All17296 triples fall into these eight unordered capacity types:

 (1,1,1):2352; (1,1,2):5760; (1,1,3):432; (1,2,2):4608;
 (1,2,3):2304; (1,3,3):144; (2,2,2):1408; (2,2,3):288.

Execution: code/research/verify_mixed_ternary_redundancy.py passed. It

Thus ternary ordinary cancellations cannot enlarge the capacity cone.
Repeated generation and cancellation of pure mixed rays is subject to the
same common duals and gives no new unitary positivity law. A nonlinear
certificate must exploit additional unitary geometry. M05's Birkhoff SPN
identities and determinant Young bounds do so, with an explicit new
seven-contact positive family rather than a conjectural graph extension.
