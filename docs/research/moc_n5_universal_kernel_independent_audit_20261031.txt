Independent mathematical audit of the universal n5 kernel theorem
31 October 2026

Conclusion and scope

No gap was found in the structural passage from the14 formal base
identities to the full1024-entry Gram identity. The Hodge convention,
permutation equivariance, common complementary column transformation,
and singular-spectrum limit are consistent. Combined with the separately
replayed30-variable base certificates, these steps prove PSD and rank at
most16 for every real orthogonal5 by5 mixer and every complex spectrum.

This is a theorem about the signed fixed-spectrum product kernel. It is
not MOC, not a theorem for arbitrary complex unitary mixers, and not an
all-dimensional statement. Fixed rational examples are not premises of
the universal argument. This audit does not claim that the complete
universal theorem has itself been encoded in Lean.

1. The row-determinant bridge has the correct orientation

Write M for the matrix whose row i is the row of O multiplied entrywise
by1, bar(b), b, or |b|^2 according to membership in the two subsets.
Then the desired signed permutation sum is det(M)/det(O).
Multiplication by O^T gives exactly the four row types I,C,B,BC used
in the proof. Since O is real orthogonal, det(O)^2=1 and

 det(M O^T)=det(M) det(O)=det(M)/det(O).

This covers det(O)=-1 as well as det(O)=1. Deleting an identity row
and its matching column has positive cofactor sign, so restriction to
the subset union introduces no extra sign.

2. The Hodge/cofactor correction has the stated sign

For a three-subset I and missing index m in I, the grade-four coordinate
is the minor with columns I^c union {m}. Move m to the final column and
expand that determinant. Combining the Clifford sign, Hodge sign(-1)^m,
and column-reordering sign gives(-1)^sum(I), independently of m.
The ordinary final-column cofactor sign contributes(-1)^a for the
a-th row of I. This is exactly the stated

 r_ia=(-1)^(sum(I)+a) det C[I without ia,I^c].

For m outside I, the untwisted grade-four coefficient is zero. The
right-hand side r C is also zero, because the cofactor cross product
annihilates each of the two columns C[I,I^c]. Consequently

 v_C,I,grade4 H = r_C,I C

holds without assuming invertibility, normality, or commutation. The
independent structural verifier checks all50 entries as polynomial
identities in15 independent symmetric entries.

For invertible B and C=bar(B), commutation gives

 bar(v_B,I,grade4) H B C^-1 H^T = r_C,I B H^T.

This proves that the division-free correction used in the theorem
really is the proposed polar correction, with the correct side of
matrix multiplication. No phase choice or square root is introduced.

3. Permutation equivariance discards no row-dependent sign

Let P send generator e_i to e_p(i). If sorting p(I) has sign s_I,
the transformed input blade and its transformed exterior image both
acquire s_I. The two signs multiply to1. Thus the untwisted Clifford
row transforms by one signed orthogonal blade-coordinate map, the
same map for every subset row.

More explicitly, if S=I symmetric_difference J, the required sign
identity is

 sigma(p(I),p(J)) s_I s_J = sigma(I,J) s_S,

with all image sets sorted. The independent audit exhausts all120
permutations and all252 equal-cardinality minor pairs:30240 checks.
It also checks that the resulting16 by16 coordinate maps are orthogonal.

The Hodge map obeys T4=det(P) H P H^T for column coordinates. This rule
is verified for all120 permutations, including the60 odd permutations.
It implies r_(P C P^T),p(I)=det(P) r_C,I P^T. One way to see this is
to use the already proved identity r C=v4 H for invertible C, cancel C,
then extend the resulting polynomial identity to singular C.

Therefore r B H^T transforms by exactly the same grade-four coordinate
map as every other corrected row. Complex conjugation commutes with
that real map. All grades together have one common real orthogonal
column action. Hermitian exchange is legitimate for the actual kernel
because the signed weights are real; the proposed Gram matrix is
Hermitian by construction.

4. Complementation has one common scaled unitary column map

Jacobi's identity is

 det B[I,J]=(-1)^(sum I+sum J) det(B) det B^-1[J^c,I^c].

Symmetry of B^-1 is essential: it transposes the last minor to the
ordering used by the Clifford formula. The sign identity

 sigma(I,J)(-1)^(sum I+sum J)=sigma(I^c,J^c)

then yields v_B,I=d v_(B^-1),I^c. The independent audit checks all252
equal-cardinality pairs, including the empty and full sets. Its parity
proof is valid because the exponent difference is
n(n-1)-2nk+k(k+1), an even integer.

Let M_B=H B bar(B)^-1 H^T. For symmetric normal B, M_B is unitary and
M_(B^-1)=M_B^-1. For subset size2 the complementary corrected row has
size3, so undoing its polar factor produces M_B. For subset size3 the
same M_B is already present. Hence EVERY row has the same transformation

 w_B,I=w_(B^-1),I^c diag(d I11,bar(d) M_B).

The column matrix times its adjoint is |d|^2 I16. For the stated Gram
convention, bar(w_I) w_K^T, its conjugate times its transpose is also
|d|^2 I16. Thus the Gram entries transform with exactly that scalar.
There is no subset-dependent unitary left inside the inner product.

The row determinant transforms identically. Right multiplication by
(BC)^-1 converts its four row types to

 (BC)^-1, B^-1, C^-1, I,

which are precisely the complementary row types for B^-1,C^-1. The
determinant multiplier is det(BC)=|d|^2. Both sides of the proposed
identity therefore share the same complement rule.

5. All1024 entries are covered

After complementing pairs with total size above5 and, if necessary,
exchanging the two subsets, every nonempty pair has sizes

 (1,l), l=1,2,3,4, with intersection0 or1;
 (2,l), l=2,3, with intersection0,1,2.

These are exactly14 orbit types. A permutation is determined by matching
the four cells I intersection K, I without K, K without I, and the
outside of their union. This gives an explicit relabelling to each
representative. Empty cases are immediate scalar-coordinate identities.

The first implementation of the coverage loop mutated its outer-loop
subset while complementing or exchanging a pair. Its count1024 alone
therefore did not certify enumeration of the original1024 pairs. The
author independently identified and corrected that implementation issue.
It does not alter the mathematical orbit classification.

The independent audit starts from fresh immutable left/right subsets
for each pair and saves an actual five-index permutation witness for
every one of the1024 original ordered pairs. Thus coverage no longer
depends only on an iteration count or on cardinality labels.

The contained-pair compact commutator formula was also checked by hand:
for A=BC, symmetry gives CB=A^T. Expanding the difference between the
two3 by3 determinants along their common final row gives

 Q01(B02 A12-B12 A20)
 +Q02(B12 A10-B02 A11)
 +Q12(B02 A10-B12 A00).

This independently confirms the telescoping step used for the most
substantial two/three contained orbit.

6. Singular and repeated spectra require no additional assumption

The inverse is used only in the proof of the complement rule on the
open class where every b_j is nonzero. The asserted factor itself is
polynomial: its exceptional weight-three rows are r_bar(B),I B H^T,
and all other rows are ordinary minors or conjugated minors.

For fixed real O, approximate any b by vectors with no zero entry.
The approximating B and bar(B) remain symmetric and commuting and
share the same real orthogonal diagonalizer. Every entry of both the
kernel and its16-column factor converges polynomially. Thus the full
Gram identity, PSD, and the rank bound extend to all zero spectra.
Repeated nonzero values already lie in the invertible class; no
spectral-separation denominator appears anywhere. The polynomial factor
also gives a direct value at all degenerate spectra without choosing
a unitary polar phase there.
