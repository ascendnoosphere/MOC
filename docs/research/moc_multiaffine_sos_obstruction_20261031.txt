An actual spectral support outside the fixed-b multiaffine SOS cone
31 October 2026

The fixed-b multiaffine positive-kernel route cannot by itself prove every
actual MOC support inequality. This note gives an exact separating
certificate for a concrete actual spectral support, not merely a failed
numerical SDP or an arbitrary formal cost. It does not refute MOC.

Use the known nonordinary spectra

  a=(0,100,-214+146i,269-294i),
  b=(-207-148i,-28-84i,-59+82i,83+104i).

Let permutations have zero-based lexicographic indices. Set

  z_pi=product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)),
  h_pi=Re[(-25+i)z_pi],
  g_pi=max_sigma h_sigma-h_pi.

All g_pi are nonnegative. The only zero is g_1=0. The conjecture requires
L_U(g)>=0 for every unitary U, where L_U denotes the real signed determinant
amplitude functional. Positivity of a separate fixed-b moment kernel can
certify this if the cost itself is a sum of squared absolute values of
multiaffine polynomials in the assigned b-values.

Define the 24 by 16 exact evaluation matrix

  F_pi,I=product_{i in I}(b_pi(i)/512), I subset {0,1,2,3}.

Scaling by512 does not change the function space. The tested cone is

  g_pi=sum_l |(F v_l)_pi|^2, v_l arbitrary complex vectors.    (1)

Equivalently, g is the diagonal evaluation of a Hermitian PSD Gram matrix
on the full sixteen multiaffine features. The coefficients may depend on
the stated spectra arbitrarily. The exact result is that (1) is impossible.

The certificate is stored in
data/certificates/moc_multiaffine_sos_obstruction.json. It contains a rational
real vector y on the24 permutations and a rational complex congruence
matrix R. Its two decisive properties are

  sum_pi y_pi g_pi=4336227117440549/2500000>0,                (2)

and strict negativity of the y-weighted square form on every multiaffine
function that vanishes at permutation1.

Why the restricted square form covers every possible SOS

Let N be an exact basis of ker(F_1), and let T=F N. Any summand in(1) must
vanish at permutation1, because g_1=0 and every summand is nonnegative.
Thus every possible summand belongs to the evaluation image of T.

The four elementary-symmetric identities

  sum_{|I|=k} F_pi,I=e_k(b/512), k=1,2,3,4,

give four independent column relations after subtracting the corresponding
constant columns. Hence rank F<=12. Its function space contains the
constant function, whose value at permutation1 is1, so the contact-vanishing
evaluation image has dimension at most11.

The certificate selects11 columns of T, forming T0. It supplies a rational
complex matrix R and defines E=T0 R. Exact rational multiplication verifies

  K=E* diag(y) E=K*.

For every row i, the exact rational number

  -K_ii-sum_{j!=i}(|Re K_ij|+|Im K_ij|)

is strictly positive. The ordinary absolute value of K_ij is no larger than
the displayed real-plus-imaginary bound. Gershgorin's theorem therefore
makes K strictly negative definite. In particular E has11 independent
columns. Its columns vanish at permutation1, so they span the entire
contact-vanishing image established above.

For every nonzero possible SOS summand f in this image,

  sum_pi y_pi |f_pi|^2<0.

Summing gives sum y_pi g_pi<=0, contradicting(2). This proves the claimed
nonexistence without a numerical semidefinite decision or a rank tolerance.

Discovery and verification are distinct

The certificate was found by column generation. An LP approximates the SOS
cone with sampled rank-one squares. Its dual supplies y. The largest
eigenvector of the dual feature localizer adds an omitted square. A small
negative constant shift of the resulting dual makes the feature localizer
strictly negative while retaining positive pairing with g. The final dual
and a diagonalizing congruence are rounded to rational numbers, after which
all proof obligations above are rechecked exactly. The LP, floating
eigenvalues, and their stopping tolerances are not used in the proof.

Reproduction of the proof, requiring SymPy but no optimizer:

  python code/research/moc_multiaffine_sos_obstruction.py

Optional reconstruction of a certificate, using SciPy for discovery:

  python code/research/moc_multiaffine_sos_obstruction.py --discover

The exact output, including all24 support gaps and all11 strict Gershgorin
margins, is build/moc_amplitude_capacity/multiaffine_sos_exact_report.json.

The result leaves the full MOC target intact. A sum of row-side and
column-side kernel certificates, additional ordinary-minor rays, or a
different spectral argument is a larger class than(1), and is not excluded
by this single-sided certificate.

The combined row-and-column extension also fails exactly

A second exact certificate excludes that larger combined class for the
same actual spectra and support direction. Define

  F_b(pi,I)=product_{i in I}(b_pi(i)/512),
  F_a(pi,I)=product_{i in I}(a_pi^(-1)(i)/512).

The proposed larger cone consists of arbitrary sums of squared absolute
values from the F_b feature space, arbitrary such sums from F_a, and
nonnegative combinations of all ordinary singleton and two-subset matching
indicator rays. These are precisely the usual34 distinct ordinary rays.

The new rational separator y satisfies

  y.g=52996832583341777/12500000>0.

For each of the two contact-vanishing feature spaces, the certificate gives
an eleven-column rational evaluation basis and a rational complex
congruence. Each of the resulting11-by-11 y-localizers is strictly negative
definite, certified by11 strict rational Gershgorin margins. All27 ordinary
rays vanishing at the unique contact have strictly negative y scores. The
other seven ordinary rays have value1 at the contact and cannot have a
positive coefficient in any nonnegative representation of g, because
g_contact=0.

Thus every nonzero element of the combined cone that vanishes at the
contact pairs negatively with y, whereas this actual gap pairs positively.
It follows that g is outside the sum of both fixed-spectral SOS cones and
the complete ordinary-ray cone. This quantifies the obstruction after
allowing both row and column kernel positivity; it is not a failure of the
kernel PSD theorem or of MOC.

The proof is reproduced with rational arithmetic by

  python code/research/moc_two_sided_sos_obstruction.py

The certificate input is
data/certificates/moc_two_sided_sos_obstruction.json. All22 strict localizer
margins, the27 ordinary-ray scores and the exact pairing are recorded in
build/moc_amplitude_capacity/two_sided_sos_exact_report.json. Optional
--discover runs the two-block LP/eigenvector oracle and then the exact
verification; the optimizer is used only to propose the rational proof.

This does not exclude auxiliary spectral kernels: the fixed-b PSD theorem
may be invoked at an arbitrary auxiliary tuple b' unrelated to the actual
b. It also does not exclude the larger PPT jet cone derived from varying
an auxiliary cross ratio. Those are separate closure obligations.
