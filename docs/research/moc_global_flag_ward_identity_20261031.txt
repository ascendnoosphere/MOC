Global flag Ward identities and the remaining weighted-Hessian problem
=====================================================================

For f(U)=Re(eta det(Da+U Db U*)), use the12 right-flag generators
Kij=Eij-Eji and Fij=i(Eij+Eji). The Hessian is the symmetric exponential-
coordinate Hessian, including all mixed entries. The result below is
global, uses the actual determinant source, and assumes no contact family.

For any traceless diagonal anti-Hermitian D, write

    v=U*DU=v_off+v_diag.

At every U, including nonstationary U,

    H_flag(K,v_off)
       = df([K,v_off]/2+[K,v_diag]).

Thus in12 real coordinates

    H V=B_D gradient,

where V is the off-diagonal coordinate vector of U*DU and B_D is the
skew-symmetric matrix whose row K contains the coordinates of
[K,v_off]/2+[K,v_diag]. There are three such identities, one per D.

Proof of the connection terms
----------------------------
Left diagonal phases commute with Da, so left multiplication by exp(tD)
leaves f invariant. In right coordinates the symmetry vector is U*DU.
BCH, or the bi-invariant connection, gives

    H(K,v)=df([K,v])/2.

Right diagonal phases commute with Db and likewise leave f invariant,
but their vector field has the opposite connection term:

    H(K,v_diag)=-df([K,v_diag])/2.

Subtracting proves the displayed flag identity. The verifier also
checks the underlying trace/Jacobi formulas in the free associative
word algebra, without using an example as their proof.

At stationarity the gradient vanishes, so all three phase vectors are
in the flag Hessian kernel. More precisely, let c be the number of
connected components of the bipartite nonzero-entry support graph of U.
Then the phase-vector map has rank4-c, and

    rank H_flag <= 8+c.

Indeed off(U*DU)=0 means DU=UD' for some diagonal D'. Every nonzero
Uij forces d_i=d'_j. The phase is constant on each graph component;
the trace condition removes one of those c parameters. Therefore the
map from the3D traceless left torus has kernel dimensionc-1 and rank4-c.
For connected support this gives rank H_flag<=9, without a generic-
nonzero-entry assumption. At a permutation c=4 and no false rank9
assertion is made.

Two further global checks
--------------------------
In the Spin6 block coordinates, write the supported function as

    f=f_constant+f_symmetric+f_skew,
    f_symmetric=Re eta sum Aij Qij,
    f_skew=Re eta sum Bij Pij,
    mean_symmetric=Re eta sum Aij/3.

The full flag Hessian trace obeys the universal Casimir identity

    tr H=-24(f_symmetric-mean_symmetric)-16 f_skew.

The six block-diagonal right-source basis matrices are checked exactly
under sum_K ad_K^2. Their symmetric trace-free parts have eigenvalue-24,
their skew parts eigenvalue-16, and the scalar part eigenvalue0. This
proves the trace formula for all actual source coefficients, rather
than merely checking it on a numerical source.

Every individual root-plane2x2 Hessian block also has the form lambda*Id:
H(Kij,Kij)=H(Fij,Fij) and H(Kij,Fij)=0 at every flag. A generic Schur
complement reduces that pair orbit to det(S+V diag(bi,bj)V*), a2x2
determinant affine in the SU2 Bloch vector. Polynomial continuation
covers singular Schur charts. This identity holds in every dimension;
it does not by itself exploit the special4x4 source relations.

What remains unproved
---------------------
At a local maximum, the Casimir identity only implies

    24(f_symmetric-mean_symmetric)+16 f_skew >= 0.

That inequality is not a permutation support bound. The Ward nullity
and the scalar pair blocks likewise do not alone contradict a strict
above-permutation value. A cost-dependent positive Hessian weighting,
using the actual source cross-block relations, is still missing. No
MOC conclusion or no-spurious-local-maxima theorem is claimed here.
