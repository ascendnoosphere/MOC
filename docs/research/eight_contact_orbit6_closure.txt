Eight-contact orbit 6: full five-parameter support closure

Let

 g=f34+a r0+b r7+c r17+d r18+e r28,    a,b,c,d,e>=0.

The verified cubic gives

 F=-(b+1)(a+c+1)<0,   Q=ce-c-de-1.

An actual gap obeys F=hQ. Therefore Q=0 and h=0 are impossible. If
h<0, the previously proved Drury external-half-plane theorem covers
the support inequality; see section 6 of docs/geometry/MOC4_Verified_Results.md
and https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/263/263/263.
If de>=1, the established weighted-minor pair (r18,r28) covers support.
It remains to exclude actual gaps with h>0 and de<1.

Let p(s)=det(H_g-s H_1). The exact full pencil calculation gives
p(0)=0, degree(p)<=3, and the height-substituted determinant is a
nonzero prefactor times P^2. The explicit P is in the verifier; it is
linear in b with

 dP/db=(e+1)(a+c+1)(a+d+1)>0.

Moreover Q^2 p'(F/Q) is divisible by P. Thus on the actual branch,
where Q!=0 and P=0, both p(h)=0 and p'(h)=0. The coefficient k1 of s
in p(s) is

  4e(b+1)^3(d+1)(a+c+1) K R,

 K=c[(a+1)e-d]-d(a+1)(e+1),
 R=ac+ade+ad+ae+a+cd+c+de+d+e+1>0.

If e=0, the unsafe branch has
 P=(a+c+1)(ab+ad+a+bd+b+d+1)>0, impossible.
For e>0 and de<1 put u=1-de in (0,1]. The exact identity

 e(P|b=0+aK) = a^2 e(e+1)
   +(a+1)c e[e^2+(2-u)e+1]+c u(1-u)
   +a[e^2(u+1)+e(1+u(2-u))+u(1-u)]
   +e^2 u+e u(2-u)+u(1-u)

has nonnegative summands and a strictly positive e^2 u term. Since
P=0 and b>=0 give P|b=0<=0, this forces aK>0 and k1>0.
The three roots p(0)=p(h)=p'(h)=0 imply

  [t^2] det(H_(g-h1)+t H_1)=k1/h>0.

This contradicts the audited universal actual-data inequality that
the quadratic coefficient is <=0; see
docs/research/actual_pencil_sign.txt. Hence no actual positive-height
gap lies in the unsafe region. Together with the negative-height
theorem and weighted safe region, this proves support on the entire
family, including boundary parameter values and without a genericity
assumption. The zero mixed-coefficient case is an ordinary-minor sum;
positive mixed coefficients are normalized to one by scaling.
