Data and exact identities

The generator places all34 ordinary24-entry rays, the24-entry mixed
ray f34, and the lexicographic sign vector in static Lean integer tables.
Python supplies table data only. The following identities are checked
by Lean's kernel with decide, then lifted exactly from integers to reals:

 r_j(p)^2=r_j(p), for all34 ordinary rays and24 indices;
 f34(p)^2=f34(p)+2*delta_5(p);
 r18*r23=r18*r28=r23*r28=delta_5;
 f34=r7+r10+r17-r25;
 sum_p sign(p)r_j(p)=0 for all34 ordinary rays;
 sum_p sign(p)f34(p)=0.

The compiler does not trust a Python equality or a numerical tolerance.
The static indices correspond to the stored face-census enumeration.
Identification of that enumeration with the full original permutation
matrix problem is external to this finite module.

Gram certificate

Let t be real, v_j be34 real ordinary coefficients, and define
 g_p=t*f34(p)+sum_j v_j*r_j(p).
Let W be any finite family of real three-vectors, and let
 B_ij=sum_s W_s,i W_s,j.
The three coordinates refer to ordinary rays18,23,28.

The theorem cap_gram_identity assumes explicitly
 B_00=v18, B_11=v23, B_22=v28,
 B_01+B_02+B_12=-t,
and proves the exact24-point identity

 g=t*f34^2+sum_s(W_s,0*r18+W_s,1*r23+W_s,2*r28)^2
     +sum_{j notin{18,23,28}}v_j*r_j^2.

The factorization B=W^T W supplies positive semidefiniteness directly;
there is no opaque PSD assumption or numerical optimizer.

Under t>=0 and every v_j>=0, theorem cap_sos_identity constructs explicit
SOS coefficients using sqrt(t), the supplied W, and sqrt(v_j). Its
35 matching features are f34 followed by the34 ordinary rays. The
factor-index set is Option(kappa) plus Fin34; unused triangle ordinary
terms have zero coefficients. It proves every cost identity exactly.

The finite sign identities also give cap_alternating_zero for every
real t,v, without any separate sign-balance assumption.

Support bound client

The theorem cap_support_bound invokes the previously accepted
MOC.RealMatchingSos.support_bound_of_real_clifford_sos.
Its explicit inputs are:
 - the supplied triangle factor W and its three diagonal/one offsum identities;
 - t>=0 and v_j>=0;
 - a Clifford moment decomposition and the sign-kernel identity for the35 features;
 - height-score_p=g_p at all24 indices;
 - total signed mass1 and the signed determinant-score identity.
It concludes determinantScore<=height.

Thus the Boolean certificate algebra is connected to the accepted
Clifford positivity client, rather than merely checking a table identity.

Scope

Existence of the triangle factor W for every actual spectral cap cost
is NOT proved. In particular the proposed scalar criterion involving
three square-root lengths is not assumed to hold for arbitrary spectra.
The universal orthogonal Clifford decomposition, the unitary profile
bridge, and the spectral identification of a supplied cap decomposition
remain separate mathematical inputs. This module does not prove MOC4.
