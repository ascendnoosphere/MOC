Moonshots 13 and 14: new single-mixed eight- and six-contact families

These are exact family exclusions, not closures of the entire 8-contact or
6-contact cones and not a proof of MOC4. Ray numbering and the 14-coordinate
cubic F are those verified in build/session_lift/closing_moc4_model_verify.py.
A real spectral gap has g=x+he, H_F(x)y=0 and grad F(y)=grad F(x); in
particular F(g)=h Q(g), where Q=D_e F. Indeed x dot grad F(y) equals
y^T H_F(x)y/2=0; using grad F(y)=grad F(x) and Euler homogeneity
gives F(x)=0. Since H_F(e)e=0, Q(x+he)=Q(x) and the cubic expansion
gives F(x+he)=F(x)+hQ(x). When Q(g)!=0, h=F(g)/Q(g).
If y=0, grad F(x)=0 implies H_F(x)x=0 by homogeneity. Both families below
have a common zero coordinate among the 14 selected permutations; x=0 at
nonzero h would give g=he and contradict that common zero.

M13. Let
  g=r0+r7+c r10+d r18+e r22+r34,  c,d,e>=0.
For c,d,e>0 the common zero set in the 24 permutations is exactly
[7,9,12,14,15,20,22,23]. The ordinary generators contain none of the
known f34 compensation pairs (18,23),(18,28),(23,28). Direct exact calculation
shows
  F(g)=-(c+1)(e+4),  Q(g)=de-e-1,
  det H_F(g-(F/Q)e_ones)
   = -4(c+1)^2(ce+4c+4)^2 T^2/Q^2,
  T=3cde+12cd+4ce+16c+4d^2e+12d+4e+16.
The numerator is strictly negative for nonnegative parameters. If Q=0,
F<0 contradicts F=hQ; if Q!=0, the Hessian is nonsingular, so no real
spectral lift exists. The exact polynomial pencil and the positivity and
contact checks are in code/research/verify_eight_contact_slice_0710.py.
The full 8-contact cap has two additional ordinary coefficients a,b free;
its exact full pencil has now been computed, but is not closed by the above.

M14. The above cap lies in the maximal 6-contact cap with ordinary rays
[0,7,10,18,20,22,30] and mixed ray 34. A concrete enlargement, with all
ordinary coefficients in the 8-contact base fixed to 1, is
  g=r0+r7+r10+r18+r22+r34+t r20+u r30,  t,u>=0.
For t,u>0 its common zero set is exactly [7,9,12,14,15,22], and again no
compensation pair is present. Exact calculation gives
  F=-(tu+3t+3u+10), Q=-(tu+2t+2u+1),
  det H_F(g-(F/Q)e_ones) = -4 B^2/Q^2,
  B=9t^2u^2+54t^2u+85t^2+54tu^2+377tu+669t
    +85u^2+669u+1278.
All coefficients of B are positive, so Q<0 and the determinant is strictly
negative for all t,u>=0. Thus this entire 2-parameter family has no real
spectral lift. The exact calculation is checked by
code/research/verify_six_contact_slice_0710.py.

At t=u=0 this six-contact normal form specializes to a point of M13, but
simply zeroing t,u from an arbitrary six-contact actual gap does not establish
that its projection remains an actual gap. The argument above directly tests
the six-contact family and does not rely on that unproved bridge.

Further full-cap M13 calculation. With free ordinary weights a,b,c,d,e,
F=-(c+1)(ab+a+b+e+1), Q=de-e-1. Exact sparse elimination factors the
14-by-14 Hessian pencil into one linear and one quadratic factor in h;
after h=F/Q the determinant is -4(c+1)^2 P^2/Q^2. The polynomial P has
individual degrees (3,3,2,2,2) in (a,b,c,d,e) and has mixed signs. Its
exact expression and its verification are in
code/research/verify_eight_contact_cap_0710_pencil.py.
The positive-height spectral-singular branch is genuinely nonempty: for
(a,b,c,d,e)=(2,2,1,1,9), F=-36, Q=-1 and h=36. At that point the Hessian
has rank 12, and Q on its kernel has matrix
  [[140/8427, -70/2809],[-70/2809,105/2809]],
which is positive semidefinite of rank one, excluding Q(y)=Q(x)=-1.
This point is checked by code/research/verify_eight_contact_branch_point_0710.py.
The whole Hessian pencil is singular at this point, so a proof based solely
on a nonzero z^2 coefficient cannot cover this branch. Numerical probes of
other positive-height branch points found positive-definite Q on the kernel,
but this is not a uniform semidefinite proof. The unequal-weight part of
the cap remains open.

For the larger equal-weight subfamily a=b=t, the full-cap spectral
polynomial factors as P=J B, where
 J=(t+1)^2(c+1)+e(c+1-t^2),
 B=t^4(c+1)+2t^3(c+1)(d+2)
   +t^2[5cd+ce+6c+e(d^2-d+1)+5d+6]
   +2t[cde+2cd+ce+2c+d^2e+2d+e+2]
   +cde+cd+ce+c+d^2e+de+d+e+1.
B is strictly positive for nonnegative parameters, since d^2-d+1>0 and
its constant term is 1. Consequently this four-parameter equal-weight
subfamily is excluded whenever J!=0, including all t^2<=c+1. The J=0
branch has t^2>c+1 and e=(t+1)^2(c+1)/(t^2-c-1); the sample
(2,2,1,1,9) above lies on it. The factor and positivity check is
code/research/verify_eight_contact_equal_0710.py. The entire J=0 branch has
since been excluded by the reduced-pencil and connectedness proof in
docs/research/eight0710_equal_closure.txt, so all equal-weight cases are
closed. The unequal-weight P=0 branch remains open.
