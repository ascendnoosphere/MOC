The signed-amplitude cancellation strategy needs genuine increases
2026-10-31

The natural monotone repair of the real Leibniz amplitudes is false, already
for a real orthogonal matrix of order four. This eliminates one possible
closure of the surviving positive-amplitude-support proposal. It does not
refute that proposal or MOC, and no unrestricted proof is claimed.

Let permutations be indexed from zero in lexicographic order in S4. Set

  Q=(1/59)[-25  46  16  22
           -46 -31  20  -2
           -16  20 -11 -52
            22   2  52 -17],

  a=(-2,1+2i,-1+i,1), b=(0,i,2+2i,-1-i).

Direct rational arithmetic gives Q^T Q=I, det Q=1, and

  d=det(D_a+Q D_b Q^T)=(29760+106340i)/3481.

Define c_pi=sgn(pi) product_i Q_i,pi(i), and z_pi=product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)).
Then sum c=1 and d=sum c_pi z_pi. The only negative c indices are
2,10,13,21, with numerators -170000,-76544,-76544,-165044, respectively,
over D=59^4=12117361. Thus their total negative mass is 488132/D.

One tempting repair removes this negative mass by subtracting a positive
submeasure from c^+. Such a repair would give weights

  sum w_pi=1, 0<=w_pi<=c_pi^+, sum w_pi z_pi=d.                (1)

For this example (1) is impossible. Put

  h(z)=-18 Re(z)+20 Im(z), lambda=-296.

For every nonnegative w with sum w=1 and w<=c^+,

  sum w_pi h(z_pi)
    = lambda+sum w_pi(h(z_pi)-lambda)
    <= lambda+sum c_pi^+ max(h(z_pi)-lambda,0)
    = 5538488640/D.                                          (2)

On the other hand,

  h(d)=1591120/3481,
  h(d)-5538488640/D=200080/D>0.                              (3)

The upper bound in (2) is exact: assign w_pi=c_pi to every positive c_pi
with h(z_pi)>-296, zero to every positive c_pi with h(z_pi)<-296, and
w_15=295156/D. These weights sum to one and attain (2). All signs and
identities are checked by the standalone rational verifier below.

MOC and positive-amplitude support do hold for these inputs. The exact law

  d=(285161 z_3+39662 z_17+145112 z_18)/469935                (4)

has strictly positive coefficients summing to one. Each selected c_pi is
strictly positive. Thus allowing the final positive weights to exceed the
initial positive amplitudes is essential. The example does not imply that
every successful law increases each individual positive amplitude; it says
that no law can satisfy all upper bounds in (1) simultaneously.

The same input also rules out a simpler geometric prerequisite. For the
negative-amplitude permutation 21, z_21=30-10i, hence h(z_21)=-740, while
min_{c_pi>0} h(z_pi)=-530. Thus a negative-amplitude product need not belong
to the convex hull of the positive-amplitude products. In particular one
cannot first represent each individual negative product by positive-support
products and then try to cancel its weight.

This is stronger than a failure of the previously tested affine covariance
formula: it excludes every positive moment correction satisfying the stated
capacity bounds, regardless of its dependence on the spectra.

A second exact obstruction: a single holomorphic affine squared tilt

Allowing weights to increase removes the first obstruction, but the simple
positive formula

  w_pi=c_pi^+ |alpha+beta z_pi|^2
       /sum_sigma c_sigma^+ |alpha+beta z_sigma|^2             (5)

does not always represent d either. At the same Q, take

  a=(-1,3,i,-1+3i), b=(2-2i,-3-3i,3+3i,1+2i).

Now d=(1152029-375238i)/3481. Let h(z)=Re z+2 Im z and f_pi=(1,z_pi)^T.
The Hermitian matrix

  K=sum_{c_pi>0} c_pi (h(d)-h(z_pi)) conjugate(f_pi) f_pi^T

is exactly

  (1/42180533641)
  [720501298460               170393190885776-240685785143152i
   170393190885776+240685785143152i   142783480810534560].

Its leading diagonal entry is strictly positive, and

  det K=15912196652416618402093160320/1779197418239532716881>0.

Hence K is positive definite. For every nonzero coefficient vector t=(alpha,beta),

  sum c_pi^+ |alpha+beta z_pi|^2 (h(d)-h(z_pi))=t* K t>0.

After dividing by the strictly positive denominator in (5), every such law
has support expectation strictly below h(d). No choice of alpha,beta, even
spectral-dependent, fixes this. Positive mixtures of these laws also fail.
This is a finite exact exclusion of a specified positive tilt family; it
does not exclude general positive-support weights.

A computable larger positive family

For comparison, let q_pi be any positive probability weights on any finite
planar point set, and suppose the q covariance is positive definite. Whiten
the points to vectors v_pi with E_q v=0 and E_q vv^T=I2. Let delta be the
whitened target and m=E_q[v|v|^2]. For any real t define

  p_t(v)=1-2t+(delta-t m).v+t|v|^2,
  w_pi=q_pi p_t(v_pi).

Then sum w=1 and sum w v=delta. If t>0, completing the square shows p_t>=0
on all of R2 exactly when

  (8+|m|^2)t^2-(4+2 delta.m)t+|delta|^2<=0.                  (6)

At t=0, global nonnegativity holds exactly when delta=0. Thus the scalar
quadratic test (6) yields a concrete sufficient positive-weight formula,
with no LP solver. These weights may exceed q and are quadratic in both
z and conjugate z. This calculation is elementary and is not claimed to
prove that (6) holds for every MOC input.

The still larger family w_pi proportional to q_pi|alpha+beta Re z_pi+
gamma Im z_pi|^2 is the numerical range of the 3 by 3 compression of
diag(z_pi) to the q-weighted span of 1,Re z,Im z. This is a useful bounded
positive-operator formulation, but its universal validity requires a new
theorem. A numerical scan of the existing 4095 shape-qualified n4 records
in 256 directions found no support separation above 1e-7; the largest was
7.43e-9 in a near-permutation case. This scan is not a membership proof.
The separate QFT lane found a fresh robust failure of this larger family,
so it is not promoted to a universal criterion here. Its exactification
is recorded by that lane rather than duplicated.

The scalar formula (6) was tested separately on all 14 prior covariance-
affine failures: it repaired nine, including all five robust failures whose
old minimum weight was below -1e-6. The other five are numerically delicate
near-permutation cases; their failed floating scalar test alone is not an
exact obstruction. Full values are in
build/moc_amplitude_capacity/isotropic_prior_failures.json.

The QFT lane's fresh rational counterexample does rigorously exclude universal
validity of (6). It uses

  O=(1/3701)[999 2010 -2230 1920
            3090 849 660 -1730
            1230 -2860 -2001 30
            1280 -870 2070 2649],
  a=(2-i,-3-i,2+2i,-1+3i),
  b=(-2-2i,-2-3i,-2-2i,2-2i).

This O is exactly special orthogonal. For q=c^+/sum c^+, let r=z-mu be raw
planar centered coordinates, Sigma=E_q rr^T, delta=d-mu, and
k=E_q[r(r^T Sigma^(-1)r)]. The whitened coefficients in (6) can be computed
without radicals as

  A=8+k^T Sigma^(-1)k,
  B=4+2 delta^T Sigma^(-1)k,
  C=delta^T Sigma^(-1)delta.

Independent exact rational arithmetic gives

  A>397, 0<B<9/5, C>37/10000,
  B^2-4AC < -6589/2500 <0.

Thus no real t satisfies (6). Numerically A=397.74212865, B=1.77925539,
C=.00371933085, and the discriminant is -2.75158855. The exact verifier
recomputes the matrix, products, determinant, covariance, third moment and
all four rational inequalities; it does not rely on the numerical data.
QFT's stronger negative-definite 3 by 3 localizer excludes every real-affine
squared tilt at this input, and is stored separately in
build/moc_qft_polynomial_tilt/affine_exact_obstruction.json.

The signed scalar-variance theorem cannot simply be iterated

The literature lane proves that L_c|z|^2-|L_c z|^2>=0 for real orthogonal
mixers, with L_c g=sum c_pi g_pi. This gives a positive Hermitian moment
matrix on {1,z}; it does not give the analogous theorem on {1,z,z^2}.
An exact counterexample to that extension uses the original Q/59 and

  a=(3,-1,2,3+3i), b=(3+i,-1,1+3i,-3-i).

Direct exact calculation gives

  L_c z^2=(-338962240-766999480i)/205379,
  L_c |z|^4=200832621006224/12117361,
  L_c |z|^4-|L_c z^2|^2
    =-4085248743422256/42180533641<0.

Thus the signed Hermitian moment matrix on {1,z,z^2} is indefinite. The
positive-part six-feature localizer remains a different, unproved candidate;
this witness does not refute it. It does prevent deriving its feasibility
by blindly iterating the real-orthogonal scalar-variance argument through
entrywise powers of the rank-two matrix (a_i+b_j).

Primary-paper relation

Bebiano, de Providencia and Kovacec, On the corners of certain determinantal
ranges, Coimbra preprint 05-11 (2005), page 3, already state the real
orthogonal version of MOC as

  det(M Hadamard Q) in conv{product_i M_i,pi(i)}, rank M=2.

https://www.mat.uc.pt/preprints/ps/p0511.pdf

Their Theorem 11 concerns local corners under a uniqueness-of-preimage
hypothesis. It is not a positive global determinant-ratio theorem. The
matrix M_ij=a_i+b_j in the exact witness has rank at most two, so the
capacity obstruction applies within precisely that established formulation.
The paper was inspected directly with the web PDF tool; no novelty claim
is made for the rank-two Hadamard reformulation.

The invoked arXiv sources remain Lyons, Determinantal probability measures,
math/0204325v4, and Olshanski, Determinantal point processes and fermion
quasifree states, 2002.10723v2. They provide the relevant positive-state and
coupling framework, while the exact witness above shows that a repair of
the signed amplitudes must permit redistribution beyond monotone mass
removal. Neither source asserts the missing MOC matching law.
