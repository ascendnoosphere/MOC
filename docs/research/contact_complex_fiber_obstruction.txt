A six-contact obstruction to extending the fixed-complex-fiber method
2026-10-30

Result. There are finite spectral points with exactly six active matching
products, all 24 products nonzero and distinct, and a fixed unitary U such
that the six complex quantities used in the existing contact-transfer step
are local holomorphic coordinates on the six-dimensional matching variety.
Consequently, near those points there is no nonconstant curve of matching
products preserving all five complex active differences and the complex
quantity det_U-z_ref. Thus that specific deformation mechanism can stop at
six even though many real degrees of freedom remain.

This does NOT disprove a different transfer theorem for strict counterexamples.
The example is a safe point: its support violation for the chosen U tends to
-1. Real perturbations preserving only the real parts are not ruled out.

Exact certificate. Put J=i sqrt(3),
  a=(0,1/2,(1+J)/2,(1-J)/2),
  b=(-1,1,(-2+J)/7,(-2-J)/7),
  eta=14/3-(14/9)J,   U=I-(1/2)11^T.
The lexicographic zero-based active labels at this degenerate base include
  S=(4,8,9,10,13,15).
The pre-existing exact real-IFT certificate
 code/geometry/closing_contact_tangent_verify.py
shows that for sufficiently small positive parameter there are finite spectra
with precisely S active, all products distinct and nonzero, and eta fixed.
The base has ten contacts, but this nearby six-contact assertion is exact,
not a numerical sampling claim.

Use reference label 4 and define the six holomorphic quantities
  (z_8-z_4,z_9-z_4,z_10-z_4,z_13-z_4,z_15-z_4,det_U-z_4).
At the base point the determinant of their Jacobian with respect to
(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,b_0,b_1) is
  -(729+243 J)/470596 != 0.
The standalone exact script code/research/contact_complex_fiber_obstruction.py
checks this value from grouped Leibniz coefficients, checks unitarity of U,
and runs the exact IFT certificate. The Jacobian remains nonsingular at the
nearby six-contact points. The base support height is 1 and the support
violation Re(eta det_U)-max Re(eta z_pi) is -1; by continuity the nearby
points also have strictly negative violation.

Why the nonzero determinant rules out the complex-fiber step. The analytic
map from eight spectral variables to all 24 matching products has complex
rank at most six, as proved in closing_high_contact_standalone.txt. The six
observables above are fixed linear combinations of those products and have
rank six at these points. Hence the full matching map has rank six, with the
same tangent kernel as the observable map. On a sufficiently small
neighborhood the observable map is a submersion and its local fibers are
connected; the derivative of the full matching map vanishes along them.
Therefore all 24 matching coordinates are constant on those fibers. There is
no nonconstant holomorphic matching curve satisfying the six complex
constraints. This is stronger than counting equations: the exact minor and
IFT give actual distinct six-contact points where the obstruction occurs.

Potential remaining route. A new escalation argument would have to exploit
the weaker real constraints, change the fixed complex value of det_U-z_4,
use the strict positive-violation hypothesis, or establish additional
structure at genuinely hypothetical counterexamples. This result alone makes
no claim about whether such an argument can reach nine contacts.
