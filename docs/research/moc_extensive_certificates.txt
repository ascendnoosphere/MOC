SPARSE SPECTRAL CERTIFICATES: COMPLETE SMALL-DIMENSION DATA AUDIT
31 October 2026

The main result is an exact counterexample to a highly successful sparse
selection rule. In a generic rational 4x4 example, every convex representation
of the determinant by matching products must use a permutation at
transposition distance three from the unique maximum-product assignment of
|U_ij|^2. All eighteen closer products fail to contain the determinant.
The determinant nevertheless has a strictly positive three-product
representation in the full MOC hull. This refutes the selection rule, not MOC.

This lane reconstructed all 9,364 complete-hull small-case records, including
7,765 noncontrol draws. It does not independently analyze the other campaign
records; the parent integration and other research lanes cover those.

Selection rules and computation

Let pi0 maximize product_i |U_i,pi0(i)|^2, computed by Hungarian assignment
on -log |U_ij|^2. Retain either:

  R1: pi0 and all one-transposition neighbors;
  R2: all permutations at transposition distance at most two from pi0;
  B:  the support of a deterministic greedy Birkhoff decomposition of |U|^2;
  R1+B and R2+B: unions of those sets.

R2 has O(n^4) products and B has O(n^2); these rules avoid enumerating n!
products for selection. The audit enumerates the full hull independently
to measure coverage and extract full-hull witnesses. The two union rules
were added after examining the first results and are exploratory.

For each record, regenerate U from its recorded seed, reconstruct every
matching product at the original common logarithmic scale, and use the full
product covariance to condition planar hull tests. The tolerance in these
coordinates is 2e-7. Point/segment hulls are handled separately. This is
floating-point evidence, not a universal or interval-certified inclusion.
The maximum residual in the greedy Birkhoff decomposition was 1.00e-11.

Exact rational radius-two obstruction

Use the following complex spectra, with indices 0,...,3:

  a=((1+2i)/2, (2+i)/2, (-1-i)/2, 0),
  b=((-2-i)/2, (1+i)/2, (-1-2i)/2, 0)
       + ((1+i)/1000, (2+4i)/1000,
          (3+9i)/1000, (4+16i)/1000).

Let H=(R+iJ)/5, where

  R=[[-5,9,0,1], [9,-11,-3,-3],
     [0,-3,-3,-1], [1,-3,-1,-3]],
  J=[[0,1,-3,4], [-1,0,6,-3],
     [3,-6,0,-1], [-4,3,1,0]],

and U=(I+iH)(I-iH)^(-1). Exact rational arithmetic verifies H=H*, U*U=I,
and every entry of U is nonzero. The two spectra are nonconcyclic: the
determinants with rows (|z|^2,Re z,Im z,1) are respectively -1 and
-24675996703/25000000000. All 24 matching products are nonzero and distinct.

The unique maximizer of product_i |U_i,pi(i)|^2 is

  pi0=(2,0,1,3).

Its exact score gap over every competitor is positive (approximately
0.00639169); the exact rational minimum is in the certificate. Let C be
the eighteen permutations with distance(pi,pi0)<=2. For the linear functional

  ell(z)=161291766 Re(z)+702475427 Im(z),

the exact inequalities are

  ell(z_pi) <= 493616552242196103/12500000000  for every pi in C,

  ell(det(D_a+UD_bU*)) - max_{pi in C} ell(z_pi)
      =178036711804373127596957/9982244500000000 > 0.

The supporting edge uses permutations (0,2,3,1) and (1,3,2,0). Thus no
nonnegative weights supported on C can represent the determinant.

For comparison, the following full-hull triangle represents the SAME
determinant exactly:

  permutations: (0,2,3,1), (1,2,3,0), (1,3,0,2);
  distances from pi0: 2,3,3;
  weights:
    3293919714886997182337359/4210724210044312849409540,
    532129419370971507233379/4210724210044312849409540,
    192337537893172079919401/2105362105022156424704770.

All three weights are strictly positive and sum to one; their approximate
values are 0.78226917, 0.12637480, and 0.09135604. Their weighted matching
product sum equals the determinant exactly. The determinant itself is

  -41192044863319167/249556112500000000
       + i*14911309975062341/124778056250000000.

Consequently every convex representation must include at least one relative
four-cycle (distance three). The simple idea that a dominant assignment and
its one/two exchanges provide enough products is false even on an open set:
assignment uniqueness, strict separation, strict triangle inclusion and
product distinctness all persist under sufficiently small perturbations.
This is not an isolated repeated-product or zero-product exception.

What the barycentric data says

In each complete polygon, a fan from its first hull vertex produces a
three-product witness whenever the numerical point falls in a fan triangle.
Of the 4167 noncontrol n4 cases, 4165 returned such a witness; the two failures
are the already independently audited near-boundary roundoff cases
small_n04_03237 and small_n04_04076. The median minimum barycentric weight is
0.11720; the 10th and 90th percentiles are 0.003462 and 0.24534. These are
the best weights among the chosen fan triangles, not among all triples.

Their maximum cycle distances from pi0 are 1 for 118 witnesses, 2 for 1762,
and 3 for 2285. Those counts depend on the fan and do NOT establish that
four-cycles are necessary in 2285 examples. Only the strict separating-line
certificate above proves necessity for its example.

Once three products z0,z1,z2 are selected, the weights have the exact formula

  w0=orient(z1,z2,d)/orient(z0,z1,z2),
  w1=orient(z2,z0,d)/orient(z0,z1,z2),
  w2=orient(z0,z1,d)/orient(z0,z1,z2),

where orient(x,y,z)=Im(conj(y-x)(z-x)). The hard step is choosing products
so every weight is nonnegative for all spectra and mixers. Caratheodory's
three-point bound does not solve selection. A strictly interior polygon
point need not have a strictly positive three-vertex representation (the
center of a square is the simplest example); a four-product enclosure can
be preferable for a robust interior certificate. The present exact example
already has a strictly positive triangle, so no fourth product is needed.

Before perturbation, the failed example has b equal to a permutation of -a.
Then products with a cancelling match vanish, leaving derangement cycle
products. This explains why near-cancellation inputs stress assignment-
local selection: a few long cycles can carry essential hull directions.
The generic perturbation above shows this mechanism survives away from the
exact cancellation locus. It supplies an obstruction to a proof strategy,
not a proof or counterexample for MOC.

  python code/research/moc_extensive_certificates.py --summarize

The --exact mode is portable and reads no generated seed or empirical data:
it constructs the rational example directly and checks all stated equalities
and inequalities. Other modes read the saved small-case campaign records.

Outputs in build/moc_extensive_20261031:
  certificate_rows.jsonl          all 9364 per-record audits and witnesses;
  certificates_summary.json      raw groups and representative failures;
  certificate_analysis.json      aggregate tables and precision correction;
  exact_radius2_obstruction.json  exact rational proof data;
  precision_radius2.json         100-digit replay and input interpretation.

No universal sparse selector, full n4 proof, or unrestricted-n proof has
been obtained from these computations. A next selector must accommodate
essential long cycles; simply increasing a local rule's sample success
rate does not close the missing quantified statement.
