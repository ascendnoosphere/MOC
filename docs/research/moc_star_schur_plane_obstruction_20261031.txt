EXACT OBSTRUCTIONS TO TWO NATURAL STAR-SCHUR GRAM TRANSLATIONS
31 October2026

The actual star cost with contacts C=[0,1,2,14] in
build/moc_star_actual_nonordinary_counterexample/exact.json has a full
matching-SOS certificate. This note obstructs specified smaller source
maps, not SOS membership or MOC.

Use the ten star matching coordinates
 (a,b,c,d,e,f,u,r,s,t)
and the exact evaluation matrix B in schur_psd.json. The source Hessian
has the exact block form

 H_x ~ [[A,L],[L^T,0]],
 A=H_(abc) directsum H_(def) directsum (-h H_(ut-rs)),
 h=(abc+def)/(ut-rs), W=A^-1 L, S=-L^T W.

At this actual control S is positive semidefinite of rank2. Exact data
from moc_star_schur_exact_control.py include S,W, a range factor of S,
and the actual imaginary source, which lies in the kernel plane.

Two candidate face-factor planes are natural:
 E_image=range(W*range(S)), E_kernel=range(W*ker(S)).
The second includes the face components of the full source Hessian
kernel; a PSD Hodge-complement metric on ker(S) does not change this
plane. We tested ENTIRE metric families, not merely one chosen metric.

For each plane choose an integer10x2 basis Z. A Gram with both factors
in this plane has the form G=Z M Z^T with M symmetric2x2. Its24 costs
are linear combinations of the three columns

 ((BZ)_1^2, 2(BZ)_1(BZ)_2, (BZ)_2^2).

For BOTH planes, a4x4 minor formed by adjoining the target cost column
is nonzero over QQ. Therefore the target is not in this3-dimensional
cost span. No symmetric metric M, even indefinite, interpolates the
cost; in particular no positive metric can do so.

A stronger obstruction permits an arbitrary real diagonal feature map.
The exact star gap rows include unit evaluations a,b,c,d,e,f and the
three pair sums a+e,b+d,c+f. Every interpolating Gram MUST therefore
satisfy

 G_ae=G_bd=G_cf=0.

For a row-scaled plane G=D Z M Z^T D, all six corresponding entries of D
must be nonzero because the six target unit costs are strictly positive.
Hence these three conditions reduce to
 z_a^T M z_e=z_b^T M z_d=z_c^T M z_f=0.
They are a homogeneous3x3 linear system in (M00,M01,M11), with rows
 (zi0*zj0, zi0*zj1+zi1*zj0, zi1*zj1).

Its determinant is nonzero for EACH of the two source planes. Thus M=0,
contradicting the positive unit costs. This rules out every real
diagonal reweighting of either natural plane as well. It does not rule
out more general mixing of feature rows, complex row rotations, or a
different nonlinear source transformation.

The no-go extends to the FULL four-dimensional source pullback spaces,
not just the two distinguished planes. Let Z be an integer basis of
range(W), or of range(L). Form the24x10 quadratic evaluation matrix Q_Z
whose columns are all products z_i*z_j, with factor2 on offdiagonal
terms. Restrict [B,Q_Z] to the20 noncontact rows. This20x20 integer
matrix is invertible in BOTH cases: modulo the prime1000003 its
determinants are respectively489764 and658578. Therefore the quadratic
cost space of either full pullback intersects V_star only in{0}.

This allows ALL symmetric4x4 metrics, including arbitrary cross terms
between the Schur image and kernel. A nonzero such pullback cannot be
repaired by adding any already-matching cost, because that addition does
not change its nonmatching component. In particular ordinary-cone slack
plus positive pullbacks of either source space cannot generate the
nonordinary control. Cancellation between distinct nonmatching source
spaces, or an embedding outside these four-dimensional spaces, is not
excluded.

Separate numerical two-square probe, with no source-plane restriction:

The six unit rows and three orthogonal pairs admit the complete planar
parameterization
 A=sqrt(a), E=i sqrt(e),
 B=sqrt(b)exp(i theta), D=epsilon_D i sqrt(d)exp(i theta),
 C=sqrt(c)exp(i phi), F=epsilon_F i sqrt(f)exp(i phi),
with four arbitrary complex auxiliary coefficients U,R,S,T. Reflection
allows the sign of E to be fixed positive; epsilon_D,epsilon_F enumerate
all four remaining sign branches. Thus there are ten real unknowns and
the remaining eleven cost equations, with no missing rank2 Gram branch
when all six unit costs are positive.

moc_star_rank_two_sos_probe.py checks its analytic Jacobian and runs16
local starts per sign branch on the exact control. The smallest maximum
residual is9.363713698864361e-4 after normalizing max(g)=1. This is NOT
an impossibility proof: isolated solutions or other basins may remain.
The symbolic lane is deriving exact equations and the rank2 cost-image
eliminant to resolve that question. The exact plane obstructions above
do not depend on numerical optimization.
