2. C0 is a closed convex cone

Choose a full-column-rank24-by14 evaluation table B for V. An SOS cost
has the form g=diag(B T B^T), T>=0. If a sequence g_k in C0 converges,
then its coordinate sums are bounded, and

 sum_pi g_k(pi)=tr(B^T B T_k)
             >=lambda_min(B^T B) tr(T_k).

The PSD Gram matrices T_k are bounded. A convergent subsequence gives a
PSD limiting Gram with the limiting cost. Hence C0 is closed. Convexity
and positive homogeneity follow directly from addition/scaling of Grams.

This closure argument includes costs having additional contacts and
singular Gram matrices; no strict positivity is required in the limit.
