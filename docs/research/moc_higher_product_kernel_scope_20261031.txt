Higher-dimensional fixed-spectrum kernel: bounded scope answer
31 October 2026

Present conclusion

No reliable negative kernel was found in1000 dimension-five cases and100
dimension-six cases. Every stably whitened generic case had PSD signature
and rank16 or32 respectively. Eight fixed rational examples were proved
PSD exactly, including every Gaussian-integer case that produced a
suspicious negative after numerical whitening.

These results support the possibility of an all-dimensional theorem, but
do not prove universal positivity in dimension five, dimension six, or
all dimensions. A new dimension-five Clifford gluing candidate now passes
one complete1024-entry exact Gram check. Its universal proof is a concrete
next target. The unrestricted MOC support-representation step is separate.

Definition and numerical method

For real orthogonal O and complex b, let

 c_pi=sgn(pi) product_i O_i,pi(i)/det O,
 F_pi,I=product_(i in I) b_pi(i),
 G=F^* diag(c) F.

All2^n subset features are retained. The tests used Haar SO matrices,
rational and Gaussian Cayley matrices, and matrices near identity. Spectra
included distinct small Gaussian integers, complex Gaussian vectors, unit
circles, real vectors, nearly repeated values, and widely scaled values.

After invertible column normalization of F, set
 A=diag(sqrt(abs(c)/sum abs(c))) F_normalized.
For A=U Sigma V^*, remove its numerical evaluation-nullspace. The kernel
on the remaining space is congruent to

 H=U^* diag(sign(c)) U.

This compares against the absolute-weight Gram matrix and avoids declaring
the structural zero eigenvalues of G to be a negative direction. The
program records both the unweighted feature rank and the weighted feature
rank. It separately reconstructs the proposed polynomial direction and
checks its signed quadratic quotient in the original features.

Whitening was classed stable only when both evaluation ranks were the
generic observed2^n-n and the smallest retained weighted singular value
was larger than1e-6 times the largest. This is a conditioning guard, not a
mathematical generic-rank theorem. All inputs remain saved, including the
intentionally ill-conditioned cases.

Ill-conditioned whitening can generate appreciable false signs. In
particular, rational Cayley matrices can have exact zero entries, making
many permutation weights zero. Floating-point roundoff perturbs those
zero weights, and aggressive whitening amplifies the resulting spurious
directions. Nearly repeated spectra create another source of unstable
feature directions. Raw whitened minima alone are therefore not reported
as kernel counterexamples.

Exact rational checks

For dimension five, cases0,360,696,804,852,936 were rebuilt with exact
rational Cayley matrices and Gaussian-integer spectra. Cases360,696,804,
852,936 include all five Gaussian-integer apparent whitening negatives.
For dimension six, cases0 and84 were rebuilt;84 was its only apparent
Gaussian-integer negative.

The verifier constructs an integer-scaled Hermitian Gram matrix using
Python arbitrary-precision integers, then performs exact Gaussian-rational
Schur elimination. Every nonzero pivot is strictly positive and the final
Schur residual is exactly zero. Exact ranks are16 in dimension five,
except the degenerate case360 with rank8, and32 in both dimension-six
examples. The integer Gram matrices, denominators, rational orthogonals,
spectra, and positive pivots are saved. These establish only the listed
fixed examples.

Why the simple dimension-four formula is special

Write

 v_I=sum_(|J|=|I|) det B[I,J] reverse(e_I)e_J,
 B=O diag(b) O^T,

in the Euclidean Clifford algebra. In dimension four, the grade-four
coordinate occurs only on subset rows of cardinality two. Conjugating
that coordinate therefore changes no relative spectral degree between
different occupied row weights. This is the already proved formula.

In dimension five, grade-four coordinates occur on rows of cardinality
two AND three. Conjugating both gives a cross term with homogeneous
degrees b^2 conjugate(b)^3, while the actual kernel requires
b^3 conjugate(b)^2. The naive formula has an exact nonzero error on the
weight-two/weight-three block. That failure alone says nothing negative
about the PSD property.

A new dimension-five unitary gluing candidate

Identify grade-four coordinates with the standard five-dimensional space
by H[S,j]=(-1)^j when S={0,1,2,3,4}\{j}, and zero otherwise. Suppose B is
invertible and put

 M4=H B conjugate(B)^(-1) H^T.

Normality makes M4 unitary. Start with the naive conjugated grade-four
factor. Leave its cardinality-two rows unchanged. Right-multiply the
grade-four part of each cardinality-three row by M4. All other Clifford
coordinates remain unchanged.

The extra factor has exactly the missing scalar homogeneity t/conjugate(t)
under b -> t b. It preserves the same-weight Gram blocks by unitarity.
Three generic numerical full-kernel checks passed. An independent dense
rational example now passes all1024 Gram entries exactly over QQ(i), and
the5 by5 gluing matrix is independently verified unitary exactly. The
corrected factor has32 rows and16 columns and is saved in full.

There is a polynomial route around the displayed inverse. For a fixed
three-subset I, the Hodge image of the grade-four row for a symmetric
matrix C is r_C C, where r_C is supported on I and consists, up to fixed
orientation signs, of the two-minors of C[I,I^c]. This is the ordinary
three-dimensional cofactor/cross-product identity. With C=conjugate(B)
and BC=CB, the corrected Hodge row becomes

 r_C C B C^(-1)=r_C B.

Thus the proposed corrected row is polynomial of degree one in B and two
in conjugate(B). A universal identity, once proved, would extend to
singular spectra by continuity. The present artifact verifies a fixed
example rather than supplying that universal polynomial proof.

Complementation also remains compatible with this candidate: M4(B^-1)
equals M4(B)^-1. Jacobi complementation therefore introduces the same
unitary column transformation, times a determinant scalar, for both
cardinality-two and cardinality-three rows. This suggests a finite
normality-commutator proof analogous to the dimension-four argument.

Dimension six remains open here

Grade four is occupied at subset weights two, three, and four; grade six
is also present. A natural attempt to interpolate the gluing by exterior
squares of the unitary polar phase B|B|^-1 fails numerical full-kernel
checks, with relative errors around0.003--0.007. Conjugating or preserving
the grade-six coordinate does not repair it. A candidate using phases of
pairwise spectral differences also failed. These are failures of proposed
factor formulas, not counterexamples to PSD.

The observed rank2^(n-1) equals the dimension of the even Clifford
coefficient space. A general factor may require a change of complex
structure mixing the higher Clifford channels, rather than independent
phase corrections to each grade. No uncomputed object is asserted as a
theorem or used to infer positivity.

Primary-source connection, with precise remaining obligation

Sergey Bravyi, Lagrangian representation for fermionic linear optics,
arXiv:quant-ph/0404180v2, Theorem2 in SectionVII, characterizes completely
positive fermionic Gaussian maps through a real contraction condition
on their block correlation matrix and a nonnegative scalar. This gives
a concrete target for a possible positive-map derivation. Identifying
our signed product kernel with such a map has not been done; contraction
of a different fermionic compression would not establish this claim.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0404180v2

Soichi Okada, Pfaffian Formulas and Schur Q-Function Identities,
arXiv:1706.01029v1, Theorems3.2 and3.4, gives Pfaffian Cauchy--Binet and
minor-summation identities. These provide explicit algebraic tools for
trying to convert the Clifford minor sums into a genuine Gram identity.
No specialization to the kernel above is currently proved.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.01029v1

Masao Ishikawa and Masato Wakayama, Applications of Minor Summation
Formula III, Plucker Relations, Lattice Paths and Pfaffian Identities,
arXiv:math/0312358v3, is another primary minor-summation reference.
Its abstract was checked; it is not cited as proving our positivity.
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0312358v3
