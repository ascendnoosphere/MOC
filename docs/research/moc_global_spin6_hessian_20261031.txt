Unrestricted 4x4 source: quadratic SO6 model and complete flag Hessian
====================================================================

Status and scope
----------------
This is a global algebraic reformulation and derivative calculation for
arbitrary complex spectra and arbitrary 4x4 unitary matrices. It is not
a partial spectral/contact family. It does not yet prove the required
global rearrangement inequality, and it is not a proof of MOC4.

Exact APIs and replay:
    code/research/moc_global_spin6_hessian.py
    python code/research/moc_global_spin6_hessian.py
Data:
    build/moc_global_spin6_hessian.json

The replay checks universal symbolic trace identities, all six-variable
centroid-zero coefficient identities, the full su4->so6 derivative rank,
and a dense genuinely complex SU4 source. For that source it independently
checks all 12 flag-gradient entries and all 78 independent mixed-Hessian
entries against determinant Frechet derivatives. Replay is about 4s.

1. Global Hodge-real representation
-----------------------------------
Index wedge^2 C4 by 01,02,03,12,13,23. Use representatives I=01,02,03,
complements Ic=23,13,12, and Hodge signs h=+1,-1,+1. An orthonormal
real basis of the Hodge-antilinear fixed space consists, interleaved, of

    (eI+h eIc)/sqrt2,  i(eI-h eIc)/sqrt2.

Let S be this basis matrix. After a harmless left diagonal-unitary gauge
we may assume detU=1: such a gauge commutes with Da and preserves the
determinant under study. Then

    R=S* (wedge^2 U) S

is real orthogonal with determinant 1. This map SU4->SO6 is onto: its
derivative maps the 15-dimensional su4 injectively onto so6, as verified
by exact rank 15. The image is consequently open and closed in the
connected group SO6. The kernel is {+I,-I}. Thus optimization over R
does not enlarge the actual unitary source domain.

2. Exact quadratic objective
----------------------------
For x in C4 write x#_i=product_{k!=i} xk. Define

    Kx=S* diag(xi+xj-sum(x)/2)_{ij} S,
    La=S* diag(product_{k notin I} ak)_I S,
    Lb=S* diag(product_{k in I} bk)_I S.

All these source matrices have three commuting normal 2x2 Cartan blocks.
No inverses or nonzero-spectrum assumptions occur. Cauchy--Binet and
the additive-wedge trace identity give, exactly,

    det(Da+U Db U*) = c + tr(La R Lb R^T)
       + tr(K_a# R K_b R^T)/2
       + tr(K_a R K_b# R^T)/2,                         (1)

    c=product(a)+product(b)
       +sum(a#)sum(b)/4+sum(a)sum(b#)/4.

For arbitrary 4x4 matrices X,Y, the identity used here is

    tr(KX KY)=2tr(XY)-tr(X)tr(Y)/2.

It follows directly from
tr(dLambda2(X)dLambda2(Y))=2tr(XY)+tr(X)tr(Y). The replay proves the
displayed identity symbolically for 32 independent matrix variables.

Equation (1) reduces the full determinant objective to a quadratic
assignment functional of a real 6x6 orthogonal matrix. It retains all
complex spectra and all unitary phases.

3. Concrete 3x3 block variables
--------------------------------
Partition R into 2x2 blocks and define

    Qij=||Rij||_F^2/2,     Pij=det(Rij).

Q is doubly stochastic and |Pij|<=Qij. Write

    sa,I=aI+aIc,    da,I=aIc-aI,
    lambda_x,I=xi+xj-sum(x)/2,

where aI denotes a pair product. Then

    determinant = c+sum_ij (Aij Qij+Bij Pij),            (2)

    Aij=sa,i sb,j/2,
    Bij=da,i (bJ-bJc)/2
        +lambda_a#,i lambda_b,j+lambda_a,i lambda_b#,j.

The elementary block identity behind (2) is

    tr((alpha I+beta J)M(gamma I+delta J)M^T)
      =alpha gamma ||M||_F^2-2 beta delta detM,
    J=[0 1;-1 0].

This is checked symbolically with all eight scalar/block variables
independent. For a real support function use Re(eta*c), Re(eta*A), and
Re(eta*B).

At a permutation endpoint, Q is a 3x3 permutation matrix and
P_i,sigma(i)=epsilon_i, with epsilon_i=+/-1 and product epsilon=+1.
There are 6*4=24 such Weyl D3 endpoints. The replay identifies each
of them with its original 4x4 permutation and checks the actual product
value, not merely equality of maxima.

The constraints Q doubly stochastic and |P|<=Q are insufficient alone.
The source coefficients A,B are coupled polynomially; exploiting that
coupling is the unproved global inequality, rather than an assumed
universal convexity theorem for arbitrary A,B.

4. Centroid-zero global coordinates
------------------------------------
The parent's common-Mobius normalization reduces generic disjoint sources
to sum(a)=sum(b)=0; continuity is needed only after a uniform proof.
The coefficient formulas themselves are polynomial and need no generic
assumption. Put

    X=(a0+a1,a0+a2,a0+a3),
    Y=(b0+b1,b0+b2,b0+b3),
    sa,i=Xi^2-(X0^2+X1^2+X2^2)/2,
    sb,j=Yj^2-(Y0^2+Y1^2+Y2^2)/2,
    X#i=product_{k!=i}Xk,  Y#j=product_{l!=j}Yl.

Then (2) simplifies to

    c=product(a)+product(b),
    Aij=sa,i sb,j/2,
    Bij=[Xi Yj(sa,i+sb,j)-X#i Y#j]/2.                 (3)

The API tracezero_sources(X,Y) exports (3) and the corresponding spectra.
Every coefficient equality with (2) is checked symbolically for six
arbitrary complex variables. These compact equations are the actual
source constraints for a possible global SO6 rearrangement theorem.

5. Exact stationarity condition
--------------------------------
Let X=Da+B, B=U Db U*, C=eta adj(X). Along a skew-Hermitian direction K,

    df(K)=Re tr(C[K,B])=Re tr([B,C]K).

Therefore stationarity on the unitary flag is precisely

    [B,eta adj(X)] is Hermitian.                       (4)

Since adj(X) commutes with X, [B,C]=-[Da,C]. In the B-eigenbasis,
replace X by U*DaU+Db and B by Db. For the 12 real root generators
Eij-Eji and i(Eij+Eji), the replay verifies the quantitative identity

    sum_root (df)^2 = ||stress-stress*||_F^2/2,
    stress=[Db,eta adj(X)].

Thus (4) is an exact real-form condition. Replacing it by the stronger
complex commutation condition [B,C]=0 would lose genuine stationary
points and is not justified.

6. Complete Hessian
--------------------
In the B-eigenbasis put EK=[K,Db]. For invertible X, the exponential-chart
Hessian is

    Hess(K,L)=Re eta * {
      tr(adj(X)([K,[L,Db]]+[L,[K,Db]])/2)
      +det(X)[tr(X^-1 EK)tr(X^-1 EL)
               -tr(X^-1 EK X^-1 EL)] }.

At a stationary point this is the intrinsic second variation; every
mixed root direction matters. The direct inverse formula is used only
as an independent verification example.

For a polynomial formula valid also at singular X, use (1). Let
Lell,Nell be its three source pairs, C_ell=R^T Lell R, and J,K be real
skew6 generators lifted from the su4 flag. Then

    Hess(J,K)=Re eta/2 * sum_ell tr C_ell *
       ([J,[K,Nell]]+[K,[J,Nell]]).                    (5)

The API qap_flag_derivatives uses (5), has no inverse, and covers zero
and repeated spectra and singular Da+UDbU*. It includes all 12 tangent
directions and all 78 independent Hessian entries.

7. The missing maximum principle
----------------------------------
The global proof target is not the overly strong assertion that every
noncommuting stationary point has a positive Hessian direction. A local
maximum below the permutation support is harmless. What is required is
an actual-source identity or inequality showing that (4), together with

    Re eta*det(Da+UDbU*) > max_pi Re eta*product_i(ai+b_pi(i)),

forces a positive direction in (5). A positive weighted sum of these
Hessian directions, using the coupled coefficients (3) and the strict
24 support gaps, would provide such a mechanism. No such inequality is
claimed by the present calculation. Root and other agents are examining
the equivalent global block and three-mode Bogoliubov formulations.
