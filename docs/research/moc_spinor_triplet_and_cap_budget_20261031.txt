Homogeneous spinor triplet frames and the full canonical cap block
2026-10-31. Exact identities; no unrestricted MOC proof is asserted.

The three-critical-budget criterion has now been refuted by direct finite
Gaussian-integer spectra. The identities below remain valid. They retain
all eight projective labels and expose where the other ten cap budgets enter.

1. A homogeneous spin-one frame

For four spinors u_i=(u_i0,u_i1), set d_ij=[u_i,u_j] and

  s_ij=(2u_i0u_j0, u_i0u_j1+u_i1u_j0, 2u_i1u_j1).

Let C(u) have rows

  d01 s23 - d23 s01,
  d01 s23 + d23 s01,
  d02 s13 + d13 s02.

Define x=(d01d23,-d02d13,d03d12), so x1+x2+x3=0, and

  Q = [[0,0,1],[0,-2,0],[1,0,0]],
  D_x=diag(x1x2,x1x3,x2x3).

The polynomial identities are

  C(u) Q C(u)^T = 8 D_x,
  det C(u) = -16 x1x2x3.

Thus no affine chart or noncollision assumption is needed for the identities.
The determinant formula and every homogeneous polynomial coefficient were
checked with exact symbolic arithmetic. The Gram identity, Pluecker relation,
and the matching formula below are also checked by Lean.

2. Matching Fourier moment

The frame carries a real signed-permutation form rho31 of the standard
three-dimensional representation of S4. All 24 matrices are stored in the
JSON artifact. For two four-spinor lists u,v, let

  z_pi = product_i [u_i,v_pi(i)].

Then

  2 sum_pi z_pi rho31(pi) = C(u) Q C(v)^T = M.

With the conventional Fourier coefficient

  F31(z)=(3/24) sum_pi z_pi rho31(pi),

we have F31(z)=M/16. The affine MOC products are obtained by
u_i=(a_i,1), v_j=(-b_j,1). The homogeneous identity also covers infinity.

When all x_i,y_i are nonzero, choose diagonal square roots. From the two
frame Gram identities,

  M D_y^(-1) M^T = 64 D_x,
  R=(1/8)D_x^(-1/2) M D_y^(-1/2) satisfies R R^T=I.

This supplies an actual O3(C) source parameter rather than merely a nonic
equation. Complex conjugation remains essential when imposing actuality.

For R=X+iY, X,Y real,

  XX^T-YY^T=I,  XY^T+YX^T=0.

In particular X is invertible and its least singular value is at least one.
Writing S=X^(-1)Y gives S+S^T=0 and X(I-SS^T)X^T=I. In dimension three,
S=r[n]_cross with |r|<1, giving the explicit rotation/boost form

  R=O(P_parallel+cosh(lambda) P_perp+i sinh(lambda)[n]_cross).

This is a useful six-real-parameter description of the O3(C) factor. It is
not by itself a proof that actual supported costs belong to the full SOS cone.

3. All thirteen cap budgets in the standard block

Use zero-based lexicographic matching indices, star tau=(0,3,2,1), and
replace columns by their tau relabeling. The canonical cap is

  g=t f34+sum_(j in Z) v_j r_j,  t>0, v_j>=0,
  Z=(0,7,10,13,17,18,20,22,23,27,28,30,31).

Let h0=(1,1,1), h7=(1,1,-1), h10=(1,-1,1), h13=(-1,1,1).
The complete exact Fourier block is

  F31(g)= t/2 I + 1/2 diag(v28,v23,v18)
          +1/4 sum_(j=0,7,10,13) v_j h_j h_j^T
          +1/2(v30-v31) E13
          +1/2(v22-v27) E21
          +1/2(v17-v20) E32.

Here Eij has one entry 1, with one-based matrix indices. For actual
g=h-Re(eta z), the same block is -Re(eta M)/16. This formula shows both
the positive tetrahedral contribution and three directed off-diagonal
differences. Ignoring the latter six ordinary features loses essential
certificate directions, as the exact obstruction in Section 5 demonstrates.

4. A small [22] identity

Set b'=(b0,b3,b2,b1). For affine row spectra define

  x1=(a0-a1)(a2-a3),
  x2=-(a0-a2)(a1-a3),
  x3=(a0-a3)(a1-a2),

and define y using b'. Then x1+x2+x3=y1+y2+y3=0 and

  -z0+z1+z6+z23-z18-z9
     = -(x1y1+x2y2+x1y2).

Consequently, for actual support gaps and cap coordinates,

  t+v18+v23+v28 = Re(eta[-x1y1-x2y2-x1y2]).

These identities are in MocTrianglePlucker.lean. They do not imply the
refuted three-critical-budget bound.

5. Exact obstruction to an eight-feature repair

Consider SOS on only

  q=(f34,r0,r7,r10,r13,r18,r23,r28),

plus arbitrary nonnegative multiples of the other six ordinary squares
r17,r20,r22,r27,r30,r31. Define the integer functional y on the 24 matching
values by

  y=(-1,1,1,-1,-1,1,1,0,-1,2,1,-1,
      0,0,2,0,-1,1,2,-1,-1,1,3,-1).

For EVERY real or complex coefficient vector x,

  sum_pi y_pi |q(pi).x|^2
       = |x5+x6+x7|^2 + |x0-x4|^2 >=0.

The associated 8-by-8 dual matrix has rank two. The functional annihilates
all six remaining ordinary indicator squares. On cap coordinates,

  y.g = -t+v13+v18+v23+v28.

Thus this restricted certificate cone requires

  t <= v13+v18+v23+v28.

The direct integer actual counterexample recorded by the positive-
representation agent gives

  y.g = -4594283034535663630512.

It therefore lies outside this eight-feature repair cone. It still has an
exact positive-definite Gram certificate in the full matching SOS cone;
this is NOT a MOC counterexample and NOT a full-SOS counterexample. Cross
terms involving the remaining six ordinary features are essential here.

One can also derive necessary scalar conditions from any such eight-feature
Gram. Let k=Gram(f,f), S=v18+v23+v28. Evaluation at the four opposite-cycle
points and six transposition points gives

  Gram(f,Ui)=(t-k)/2,
  sum_(critical i<j) Gram(Ci,Cj)=-k.

Therefore S-2k is the squared norm of the critical-sum vector, while
2k+vUi-t is the squared norm of f-vector minus Ui-vector. Their sum recovers
the displayed linear obstruction. In particular k is FREE; setting k=t
would incorrectly conclude that the eight-feature cone equals the old
triangle cone. That erroneous preliminary claim was corrected immediately.
