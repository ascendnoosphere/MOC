Fast exact-form polynomial evaluation

The recovered global septic was compiled into342720 merged degree7
matching monomials.  Vectorized prefix/suffix products evaluate its value
and analytic gradient; no division by individual matching values is used,
so zeros of these features are allowed.  Long-double products and value
accumulation reduce cancellation; the gradient's105 feature sums use
double precision.  Finite differences at five coordinates of each exact
initial root agreed with the analytic gradient, with errors around1e-6
on derivatives as large as130 (relative errors around1e-8).

For g(v), put x=g-F(g)e/Q(g), then

  N9(g)=Q(g)S7(x),
  grad_g N9=(grad Q)S7(x)+Q[grad S7(x)
                  -(e.grad S7(x))grad(F/Q)].

The search uses u_j=(y.r_j/B)v_j, so its objective is sum_j u_j and its
nonnegativity bounds are well scaled.  The equality residual is
N9(g)/(1+sum v_j)^2; the objective is bounded above by its initial value
during the search.  The displayed exact formula and chain rule supply
the constraint gradient.
