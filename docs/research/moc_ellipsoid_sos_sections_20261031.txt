Projector Gram sections, ellipsoid fitting, and an exact obstruction
31 October 2026

Unrestricted MOC remains unresolved. This note records a constructive
certificate family, its tested domain, and an EXACT ACTUAL spectral
counterexample to one member. It does not record a MOC counterexample.

1. Intrinsic certificate problem

Let V be the14-dimensional real matching-function space on S4. The
previous Clifford/sign-gauge proof makes any g in V that is a sum of
squares of elements of V a valid MOC support certificate. The intrinsic
cone identification is in moc_intrinsic_matching_sos_20261031.txt.

For a nonnegative cost g, remove its zero coordinates. Choose a full-rank
evaluation matrix R on the remaining n coordinates for those functions
in V vanishing at every zero of g. A certificate is a matrix C>=0 with
diag(R C R^T)=g. This is an ellipsoid-fitting problem.

The correlation-matrix interpretation and the projector-diagonal inner
approximation are classical; see Saunderson, Chandrasekaran, Parrilo,
Willsky, SIAM J.Matrix Anal.Appl.33(4),1395-1416(2012), Theorem4.2 and
AppendixA.2, DOI10.1137/120872516. Primary full text:
https://willsky.lids.mit.edu/publ_pdfs/219_pub_SIAM.pdf
Their coherence<1/2 sufficient condition does not hold automatically on
our actual costs. The weighted formulas below adapt the construction
to these matching costs; no claim of historical novelty is made.

2. Explicit weighted section

For real p, put S=diag(g^p)R, let Q have orthonormal columns spanning S,
and put P=Q Q^T. Solve

                  (P entrywise_squared) lambda = g^(1+2p).

Then X=P diag(lambda) P has the required transformed diagonal. If
Q^T diag(lambda) Q>=0, the matrix

                W=diag(g^-p) X diag(g^-p)

is PSD, has diagonal g, and range contained in range R. Hence it gives
an intrinsic matching-SOS certificate. Lambda need not be nonnegative.
When the linear system is singular, consistency must be checked; its
kernel contributes zero to X, because P entrywise_squared is a Gram
matrix of the row outer products of Q. A pseudoinverse is legitimate
only with a verified interpolation residual.

For p=-1 the formula is entirely rational for rational g,R. In root
coordinates set

 M=R^T diag(g^-2) R, H=R M^-1 R^T, B=H entrywise_squared,
 mu=B^-1 g,
 C=M^-1 R^T diag(mu) R M^-1.

Then diag(R C R^T)=g exactly. The contact-Gram note proves B>0 on
each generic two-contact face, for every positive remaining22gaps,
by exact independence of22 row outer products. PSD of C is unproved.

4. Exact actual obstruction to p=-1/2

A targeted cap search, followed by rationalization, found beta

 (1/2-33i/25, 1/4+33i/50, 37/100-33i/100, -1).

An exact four-dimensional supporting facet has contacts[8,12,20,23].
Its gap is an integer24-vector, and a Mobius transformation supplies
finite original a,b,eta and a verified finite support height. The
explicit p=-1/2 rational congruent Gram has the integer witness

                   (6,5,3,2,3,-1,-11,-5,-11,7),

with strictly negative exact rational quadratic form (approximately
-101.3238109058). All24 cost equations are exact. Root replay passed.
