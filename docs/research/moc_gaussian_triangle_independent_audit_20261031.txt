INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE ALL-DIMENSIONAL GAUSSIAN MOC THEOREM
31 October 2026

Verdict: valid as stated in Section 1 of moc_extensive_theory.txt.
The result is an asymptotic theorem for independent standard complex Gaussian
spectra and an independent Haar unitary. It is not a worst-case MOC proof.
No correction was required.

I independently checked the following steps.

1. The deterministic three-product certificate is exact. On a four-index
block, the identity, the first transposition, and the two disjoint
transpositions give z0, z0*q1, z0*q1*q2. If both q values differ from
omega by less than 1/10, the three normalized vertices differ from the
equilateral triangle by at most 21/100. Every support function value
therefore remains at least 1/2-21/100=29/100. This implies containment
of the centered disk: a compact convex set is the intersection of its
supporting halfspaces. This inference does not presuppose that zero is
inside the perturbed triangle. Radius 1/4 is consequently valid.

2. The swap target is correct: t=(-2+i sqrt(3))/7 gives
2t/(1+t)=(-1+i sqrt(3))/2 exactly. For disks of radius r=1/200, the
numerator perturbation is at most 5r+4r^2, the denominator perturbation
at most 4r+4r^2, and its original modulus exceeds 2/3. Their ratio error
is bounded by (9r+8r^2)/(2/3-4r-4r^2)=1356/19397<1/10.
The denominator bound is positive, so the local ratios are defined.

3. A Gaussian disk around c has mass at least
r^2 exp(-(|c|+r)^2). The eight variables are independent. Their summed
exponent upper bound is 22751/5000<5, yielding the claimed lower bound
p0=exp(-5)/200^16. Distinct four-index blocks use disjoint a and b
variables, hence the no-good-block probability is at most
(1-p0)^floor(n/4). Selecting a block after seeing the spectra is legitimate.

4. The baseline factors a_i+b_i are independent circular Gaussians of
variance two, so |a_i+b_i|^2/2 are independent mean-one exponential
variables. This proves the Mellin transform and logarithmic mean used
in the note. Chernoff at s=1/10 yields the displayed gamma-function factor.

The log-gamma series is consistent with NIST DLMF 5.7.3, which I checked
directly at https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.7.E3 . Substituting z=-x and expanding
-log(1-x) gives gamma*x+sum_{k>=2}zeta(k)x^k/k for |x|<1.

The bounds log(2)>693/1000, gamma<578/1000 and zeta(2)<1645/1000 have
the stated finite rational certificates. H_1024-log(1024) is a strict
upper bound for Euler's constant; replacing log(2) by its positive-series
lower bound preserves that direction. The integral test gives a strict
upper bound for the zeta tail. Bounding every 1/k by 1/2 for k>=2 gives
the stated geometric-series bound. The resulting log Chernoff factor
is below -393/152000, which is below -1/400. There is no floating-point
gap in this constant calculation.

5. The Haar second-moment identity was independently audited earlier:
E|d|^2=(n+2)(n+3)/6. Markov at exp(n/100)/4 gives precisely
(8/3)(n+2)(n+3)exp(-n/50). No conditioning on a good block is used.

6. If all three good events hold, a selected three-product triangle
contains the disk of radius exp(n/100)/4 and d belongs to that disk.
The final union bound is valid even though the good-block, baseline and
determinant events are correlated. Independence is required only where
the note states it: across disjoint spectral blocks, across baseline
factors, and between U and spectra in the Haar moment calculation.

I reran code/research/verify_moc_gaussian_triangle_constants.py; all exact
rational and algebraic assertions passed. Its 200000-block simulation is
clearly separated from the proof and is not used in the lower bound p0.
No statistical confidence claim is needed for the theorem.

The finite-n bound is valid after truncation at one. Its three terms have
exponential decay up to a polynomial prefactor, which can be absorbed
into constants C exp(-c n). Because p0 is about 1.0281e-39, the displayed
bound is vacuous at campaign dimensions through 1000. The note states
this limitation accurately. The result establishes high-probability MOC
for the specified ensemble, while leaving rare and structured inputs
and the unrestricted deterministic conjecture open.
