1. The full-family context and the exact interior point
------------------------------------------------------
The canonical marked six-contact child13 is

All seven coefficients are independent and nonnegative. Direct cubic
calculation gives

In particular F<0 always, and Q<0 on the entire compensator cube.

2. Why the point is nevertheless not actual
-------------------------------------------
An actual lift requires a real y with Hy=0 and gradF(y)=gradF(x).
Compute an exact two-column rational kernel basis K, write y=K(u,v), and
put U=u²,V=uv,Z=v². The fourteen gradient equations are linear in U,V,Z.
Their augmented RREF has pivots(0,1), rank2, and twelve zero rows. Thus
U=alpha+beta Z, V=gamma+delta Z for exact rational coefficients.
The remaining necessary Veronese identity is UZ−V²=0, with Z>=0.

After multiplying by a negative rational factor, the left side has the
primitive integer polynomial
 2573771220003498823790635840000 Z²
 +297891681814321068231832326417200 Z
 +8593199221018410086710908315163369.
All three coefficients are strictly positive. Hence the actual left side
UZ−V² is strictly negative for every Z>=0. No real y can exist.
The exact checker recomputes all fourteen equations and every coefficient.
It also finds det(E|kerH)<0, so the simpler kernel-Q sign test does not
exclude this point either.
