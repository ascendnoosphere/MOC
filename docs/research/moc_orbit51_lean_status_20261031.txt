Orbit51 r-star scalar formalization, 31 October 2026

Lean 4.19.0 accepted code/lean/MocOrbit51.lean with compiler exit status 0.
The accepted declarations are in code/lean/MocOrbit51.olean. The complete log is
build/moc_moonshots_20261031/ag/orbit51_lean_complete.log; machine-readable scope
and hashes are in the adjacent orbit51_lean_status.json.

All conclusions below derive from p,q>=0, r>1 and r(p+q)<1. The conditional
D/Q results also assume a,b>=0. No desired coefficient sign, denominator sign,
or branch conclusion is supplied as an extra hypothesis.

The module proves A>0, B<0 and C0<0 for the polynomials in the orbit51 proof.
It derives p+q<1 and pq<1/4 before using these bounds. The new complete branch
statements are:

  r_star_D_zero_implies_Q_positive: D=0 implies Q>0;
  r_star_Q_zero_implies_F_negative: Q=0 implies F<0;
  r_star_D_zero_derivative_positive: D=0 implies a*A+B>0;
  r_star_actual_Q_ne_zero: F-hQ=0 implies Q!=0 for every real h.

The D=0 proof avoids square roots and division. For arbitrary alpha>0,
gamma<0, a,b>=0 and ab*alpha+(a+b)*beta+gamma=0, it proves

  (a+b)(alpha(a+b)+4beta)=alpha(a-b)^2-4gamma>0,
  hence alpha(a+b)+4beta>0.

For the specific star, the separately checked polynomial estimate
-4B>A0*(pq+r(p+q)+1), with A=(r-1)A0 and A0>0, then gives Q>0.
The Q=0 proof uses 4ab<=(a+b)^2 and pq+r(p+q)+1<4 to force F<0.
The derivative conclusion imports neither rank nor inertia information; it is
a consequence of the scalar hyperbola equation and the proved signs.

The source retains the AG lane's four cube/star identities and general scalar
lemmas. Its initially incomplete secant_positive_branch proof was repaired,
and all end-to-end r-star conclusions now compile. The old draft remains in
build/moc_moonshots_20261031/ag/MocOrbit51AGDraft.lean.txt.

The audited declarations depend only on propext, Classical.choice and
Quot.sound. There is no sorry or custom axiom. Three compiler warnings concern
unused hypotheses in general inherited lemmas; they do not affect acceptance.

This is a complete formal proof of the requested scalar algebra, not a Lean
proof of the entire orbit51 closure. The matching-variety/HMSV bridge, exact
Hessian determinant identity, connectedness and constant-inertia argument,
triangle-capacity partition, and final universal weighted-minor implication
remain outside this module. Their independent mathematical audit is recorded
separately in the AG lane.
