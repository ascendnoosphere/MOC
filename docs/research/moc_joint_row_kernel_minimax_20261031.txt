Full row-kernel positivity at a compact minimax point: a bound and a relaxation obstruction
31 October 2026

The full fixed-b Clifford kernel does give a stronger quantitative inequality
than separate first-derivative planes. This note derives that inequality and
shows exactly why full kernel PSD, row KKT, and spinor balance alone do not
exclude a negative minimum. The counterexample below is a signed-functional
relaxation with rank12, not an actual orthogonal amplitude functional (whose
kernel has rank at most8). It does not refute MOC or a proof using the full
unitary/mixed-Hessian conditions.

1. The all-row-jet mean lies in a unit ball

Fix four unit column spinors v_j and a real orthogonal O. Let c_pi be its
signed determinant amplitudes; sum c_pi=1. For each unit row spinor u_i,
choose an orthogonal unit companion u_i^perp. Define the sixteen values

 p_I(pi)=product_i [u_i^(I),v_pi(i)],

where u_i^(I)=u_i^perp if i belongs to I and u_i otherwise. These include
the original permutation product, all first row derivatives, and every
higher row derivative in the chosen tangent frame.

For each pi, the vector (p_I(pi)) has squared norm1: the two bracket
amplitudes at each row form an orthonormal expansion of a unit spinor, so
the product of their squared-norm sums equals1.

The fixed-b theorem implies the full moment matrix

 R_IJ=sum_pi c_pi conjugate(p_I(pi)) p_J(pi)

is PSD. To see this for arbitrary column spinors, choose a projective chart
with no column at infinity. Each p_I is a common nonzero scalar times a
squarefree polynomial in the assigned column coordinates. The full sixteen
fixed-b features therefore contain these sixteen functions.

The constant function1 is also in their span: choose a common row covector
whose bracket with every v_j is nonzero; its four-row product is a nonzero
constant over the permutations. Consequently the constant-feature Schur
complement gives

 R-conjugate(d)d^T>=0,       d_I=sum_pi c_pi p_I(pi).

Taking traces, using tr R=sum c_pi=1, proves the joint mean-jet bound

                   sum_(I subset[4]) |d_I|^2 <=1.             (1)

No first-derivative plane has been substituted for the full row kernel.
All16 row derivatives enter one inequality.

2. What the compact row KKT equations add

At a hypothetical negative compact global minimum, let lambda be any full
KKT active-contact law and put mu_I=sum lambda_pi p_I(pi). The row sphere
and phase equations give

 mu_empty-d_empty=m real,       mu_{i}-d_{i}=0  for i=1,...,4.

Here p_{i} is the complex-orthogonal row tangent feature. Substituting these
first moments into (1) and discarding the other nonnegative terms yields

             |mu_empty-m|^2+sum_i |mu_{i}|^2 <=1.             (2)

This is a concrete necessary inequality using the full kernel and row KKT.
The remaining jet means also obey (1), but first-order KKT does not identify
them with the contact law. Neither (1) nor (2) gives an order relation between
the two moment matrices R_c and R_lambda. Both are PSD separately; their
difference need not be PSD. A negative m is algebraically compatible with
(2) unless additional actual-unitary or contact information is supplied.

3. An exact full-PSD signed functional with a negative actual support gap

The old exact fixed-b SOS obstruction can be completed to make this limitation
literal, rather than inferred from a failed SDP. Use the actual a,b,direction
and unique contact pi0=1 in moc_multiaffine_sos_obstruction_20261031.txt.

That certificate supplies a real rational vector y, and a24-by11 rational
complex evaluation matrix E spanning all fixed-b functions vanishing at pi0,
such that

 E(pi0)=0,       K=E^*diag(y)E<0,       y dot g>0,

where g_pi=hmax-Re[(-25+i)z_pi]>=0 is the actual support gap.

Set A=-K. Let delta>0 be the smallest certified strict diagonal-dominance
margin of A (using |Re offdiag|+|Im offdiag|); then A>=delta I. Define

 u=-E^*y,       S=1+||u||^2/delta,

 c=(-y+(sum y+S)delta_pi0)/S.                                (3)

These are exact rational numbers. Sum c_pi=1. In the twelve-function basis
[1,E], the c-moment is exactly

                 (1/S) [ S  u^* ]
                       [ u   A  ].                         (4)

Its Schur complement is at least1/S, because
u^*A^{-1}u<=||u||^2/delta. Thus (4) is positive definite. The function space
has dimension12, so this proves PSD of the ENTIRE16-feature fixed-b moment
matrix, with rank12. Nevertheless

                   c dot g=-(y dot g)/S<0.                  (5)

The exact verifier computes the unnormalized gap as approximately
-3828838.602395423; the sign and all PSD certificates use rational arithmetic.
Normalizing all row and column spinors and applying the unit phase of
(-25+i) multiplies (5) by a common positive factor, so a negative compact
support gap still exists for this signed functional.

4. Row criticality and balance do not repair this relaxation

For the fixed normalized column spinors, minimize the signed-functional gap
over the compact product of the four row-spinor spheres. Its minimum is
negative by (5). At a minimizer all row KKT equations hold, with a positive
active-contact law. The full fixed-b moment remains PSD because it depends
only on the column spinors, not the row choice. Thus full row KKT is compatible
with this exact positive-kernel relaxation counterexample.

One can also impose the balanced-spinor condition without changing this
conclusion. Allow the column spinors to range over the closure of their common
SL2(C) orbit after individual normalization, and allow all row unit spinors.
This domain is compact. Full moment PSD persists along this column orbit:
the product feature vector transforms by an invertible tensor-power map and
a common scalar, so its signed moment changes by congruence. PSD also persists
at orbit limits by continuity.

The domain is invariant under a simultaneous normalized SL2(C) action on all
eight spinors. At a negative global minimum on this enlarged domain, applying
exp(tH), H traceless Hermitian, multiplies all permutation products and their
signed average by the same positive normalization factor. Its derivative must
vanish, forcing

                    sum_(k=1)^8 w_k w_k^*=4I.

Hence full row-kernel PSD, negative row criticality, and exact balance can all
hold simultaneously in this relaxation. The column variations here span only
the common Mobius orbit; independent column cross-ratio variations have not
been imposed. Nor is there an actual O realizing (3), a unitary Hessian, or
the mixed Schur conditions from moc_compact_mixed_hessian_20261031.txt.

The rank distinction is decisive and explicit: (4) has rank12, whereas every
actual O4 fixed-b kernel has rank at most8. This construction therefore does
not rule out a closure argument using the rank-eight Clifford factor, full
column KKT, or the actual dependence of c on O. It rules out a contradiction
deduced merely from full PSD plus the row KKT and balance equations.

6. Strengthening: both actual-spectral kernels and all ordinary rays

The same contact-mass completion applied to the two-sided exact separator
gives a stronger explicit signed functional. There are now two matrices
A_s>0, vectors u_s, and bounds q_s=||u_s||^2/delta_s, one for each fixed
spectral feature space. Choose

 S=1+q_row+q_column
       +sum_(ordinary rays r with r(pi0)=1) max(0,y dot r-sum y).

Formula (3) then makes BOTH complete16-feature moments PSD, each with
rank12. Every ordinary singleton/two-subset ray has nonnegative c score:
for contact-vanishing rays this follows from y dot r<=0, and for rays with
contact value1 it follows from the displayed choice of S. Nevertheless the
same actual support has a strictly negative c gap, approximately
-3497970.7278277795 before compact spinor normalization.

Using this stronger functional in Section4 also rules out a degenerate column
orbit limit as a location of the negative row/global-SL2 minimum. A divergent
Mobius sequence acting on four distinct column points has a projective limit
with all columns coincident, or three coincident and one exceptional. In the
first case every permutation product is identical. In the second, it depends
only on the row assigned the exceptional label; its c average is a convex
combination with the four nonnegative singleton-ray scores. Thus the gap is
nonnegative on that boundary. The negative minimizer stays in the genuine
Mobius orbit, where the row moment rank remains12 by invertible congruence.

This gives a balanced negative row-critical relaxation with full row PSD,
all ordinary-ray inequalities, and rank12. The column-side PSD verified at
the original seed is not asserted after independent row minimization: full
row variations alter that kernel's fixed row spectrum. Keeping both kernels
throughout would restrict both spectral configurations to their Mobius
orbits and would lose their independent cross-ratio KKT equations. These
distinctions identify the exact missing interfaces rather than claiming a
counterexample to the stronger actual-unitary minimax system.
