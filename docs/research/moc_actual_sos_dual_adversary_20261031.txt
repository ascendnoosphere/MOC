2. Four-dimensional reduction removes x and the support direction

Common Mobius normalization fixes the first three projective a-points to
0,1,infinity. With the fourth point x, the products have form

 z_pi(x)=u_pi x+v_pi.

For a fixed y and four beta-points, all x and all eta are handled by one
convex-hull membership problem in C^2:

 (sum y u_pi,sum y v_pi) in conv_R{(u_pi,v_pi)}.

Indeed the support directions (eta*x,eta) cover the dense set with second
component nonzero in C^2; the remaining directions follow by continuity.
Thus this is a full-scope reduction at n4, not a restriction to a special
spectral family. The parent project has a separate proof/Lean audit.
