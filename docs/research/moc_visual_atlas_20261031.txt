What the visual pass added

1. Failed methods occupy different parts of the sample.

The combined figure corpus/07_cross_method_failure_sets.png uses the same
3,178 guarded n4 records for all comparisons. The explicit covariance formula
fails on nine, whitened heat flow initially expands on 21, and the winding
test misses on 78. The three sets are pairwise disjoint. Consequently the
covariance formula or winding test succeeds on every record in this shared
subset. These are numerical sufficient tests with their own tolerances.
They are not a proof that a hybrid argument covers arbitrary inputs.

This is useful for the proof search: an obstruction to one construction need
not be an obstruction to the desired hull containment. The exact Walsh
counterexample to the covariance formula already has a positive alternative
law; the exact rational heat example has a positive replacement law after
the proved smoothing threshold. The task is to change the construction at
its genuine obstruction, not insist that every desirable marginal survive.

2. The positive-amplitude support candidate makes a substantial restriction.

The candidate keeps matching products with
  Re[sgn(pi) product_i U_i,pi(i)/det(U)] > 0.
In 3,497/4,095 eligible records this removes at least one vertex of the full
matching hull. The smallest retained/full hull-area ratio is 0.284848. Every
one of the 4,095 tested determinants still lies in the retained hull.
The remaining 598 hulls are unchanged. This confirms that the numerical test
usually checks a smaller set, rather than merely rediscovering ordinary hull
membership with redundant interior points removed.

The explicit covariance-corrected formula is stronger and fails in 14 cases
at its stated threshold, including five larger failures. Conditioning does
not explain them all: one larger failure has covariance condition number
about 2.29, and failures occur in all four condition-number quartiles.
The exact minimum -2529/589540 on a separate Walsh example proves the global
formula false. Positive-amplitude support selection itself remains unproved.

3. Outward heat motion has an exact, narrow cancellation mechanism.

For H(t)=exp(-4t)X+exp(-6t)Y, initial radial expansion is equivalent to
  4|X|^2+6|Y|^2+10 Re(conj(X)Y)<0.
It requires 1<|X|/|Y|<3/2 and angle(X,Y)>168.463 degrees. The phase diagram
shows all 64 eligible noncontrol raw expansion cases inside this exact region.
The modes almost cancel, and their different decay rates release the
cancellation. Whitening removes much of the apparent tail but leaves
24 initial expansion cases among 3,442 retained observations.

The canonical heat law is also negative in every one of the 5,006 stored
mixers. Near identity its negative mass is approximately
  5/6-(7/6)(nonmonomial score/4).
The measured slope 1.166675 in 662 small-mixing records matches the derived
7/6 tangent. Identity itself has an obvious positive point mass law, so the
large canonical negative mass cannot be interpreted as MOC difficulty.

4. The apparently uninformative plots prevent false proof directions.

The AG mixing score versus moment-kernel determinant has rank correlation
0.00372 on its retained population; every within-family absolute correlation
is at most 0.081. This is a measured absence of a monotone signal, not an
independence theorem. The AG local-equality Hessian is exactly the zero 8 by 8
matrix. It is shown as zero rather than omitted. It cannot supply a strict
quadratic-curvature proof.

For circular Gaussian n4 Haar inputs, absolute determinant phase versus
normalized clearance has correlation -0.043; an exploratory shuffle gives
an ordinary-sized tail fraction around 0.30. Rotation symmetry supplies an
exact explanation for the null relation under that ensemble, as recorded
in the corpus report. Absolute phase is not a useful search coordinate there.

The variational figure includes an identically zero two-dimensional slice
of the cubic x1*x2*x3. A coupled three-coordinate direction still escapes.
The direct floating determinant difference loses the cubic signal below
roughly 3.7e-6 in the displayed grid. Two floating differences are exactly
zero and explicitly marked. This illustrates how a flat or noisy slice can
conceal a genuine higher-order mechanism without proving global flatness.

The Möbius-transformed hulls overlay the originals exactly after dividing
out one complex multiplier. Their visual variety does not add independent
planar geometry. Residual spikes at permutation labels are roundoff, and
no line is drawn suggesting continuity in that arbitrary label order.

5. Large-dimensional bands can come from experimental design.

All 813 reflector target-underflow flags belong to the paired-cancellation
family. The pooled one-sixth plateau is largely the proportion of that
family. The conspicuous cancellation bands correspond to reflector ranks
2, 4, and 8. At n>=600 their median log10(det/modulus-of-local-disk-radius)
values are about 4.33, 8.94, and 19.23.

The local sparse rule changes only four identity pairs. Reflector cancellation
aligns cancellation with the identity pairing; dense cancellation shuffles
that pairing and uses a different spectral generator. Thus the dense-versus-
reflector contrast does not isolate the mixer. A tiny absolute determinant
can still be too large for a triangle built from exceptionally tiny identity-
based products. Wider sampled triangles can succeed on the same input.

All 1,298 dense first-128-permutation disk replays succeed numerically, with
the weakest radius about 5.156 times the determinant modulus. The rigorous
Gaussian/Haar failure-probability bound remains vacuous through n=1000.
Near-real linear disk growth has R-squared only 0.109, and its fixed-rank
reflector dimension correlation is about 0.028. These weak plots are retained.
Neither generic interior concentration nor ineffective current constants
settles the worst-case unrestricted question.

6. Several visual patterns expose omissions in the original search.

The old 88 targeted mixed examples all had a definite moment kernel. The
exact Gaussian-integer example occupies the omitted indefinite chamber.
It satisfies a weighted support certificate, so it refutes that proposed
geometric restriction without violating MOC. The picture contains a
counterexample to a method's premise, not to the conjecture.

The SOS laws produce 1,056 relabeled exact inequalities beyond the earlier
weighted-law library, yet no additional actual spectral cost in the replay
needed them. The corpus's 32,950 guarded SOS support tests are ordinary.
This is a coverage gap. It persists after substantial computation and cannot
be repaired by declaring all successful rows evidence about the missing
nonordinary faces. Exact targeted inputs produced the strongest advances.

7. Topological drawings require the abstract graph, not arbitrary crossings.

The winding figures show actual attained disks. Another exact example gives
two Givens edges with the same complex determinant at rational parameters
but distinct normal-matrix lifts, separated by Frobenius distance about 4.174.
A planar crossing therefore does not itself authorize splicing matrix paths.
The proof uses closed walks in the abstract transposition graph. It supplies
inner attained regions; it does not assert an outer bound on the determinant
range. All 163 eligible silent winding cases remain visible in the raw plots.

Proof status retained alongside the images

The audited five-parameter orbit-51 closure, full cross-spectral family for
every U(4), three universal SOS laws, positive fourteen-weight mixer family,
explicit open nonordinary neighborhood, and all-dimensional local covariance
theorem are substantive positive results. The Lean modules check the stated
scalar components; the full unrestricted n4 theorem is not Lean-verified.
See docs/research/moc_seven_moonshots_20261031.txt for exact theorem scopes,
independent audits, and the remaining geometric/formalization gaps.

The visual pass also followed up one family pattern exactly: the cross-family
hull's full height/width ratio has maximum sqrt(3)/5 at r=2+sqrt(3), the same
parameter at which the right support changes and product labels collide.
That geometric identity is a fact about an already proved restricted family.
It is not promoted to a claim about general n4 spectra.

Reproduction and inspection

Each focused lane has a moc_visual_<lane>.py script, corresponding report
under docs/research/, and machine-readable metrics beside its PNGs. The
appendix inventories all scalar fields and exact aliases and retains null,
constant, error, and sampling-control fields. Source-separated trends prevent
the pooled source order from being interpreted as a mathematical variable.

The root inspected all seven corpus figures and selected mathematical figures;
each lane researcher opened every final figure in its own lane. The appendix
researcher inspected every appendix page. Inspection led to actual revisions:
overlapping labels, inadequate inset scales, misleading connected label
indices, insufficient zero annotations, and ambiguous scope text were fixed.
The inspection manifests preserve final hashes. The assembler builds the
offline gallery and PDFs from these final PNGs without changing their data.

The exact certificates and empirical records have not been merged into one
claim of proof. The unrestricted MOC4 and all-dimensional statements remain
the unsolved targets of this investigation.
