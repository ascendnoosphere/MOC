01_attainable_regions.png

Four panels show actual images of closed walks in the ABSTRACT permutation
Cayley graph, their exact certified disks, the matching-product convex hull
as a reference, and a magnified Walsh neighborhood. The three exact squared
disk radii are 3721/585, 5, and 961/4225. The Walsh radius is sqrt(5), centered
at -43-24i. Arrowheads show the orientation of the walks; their winding is -1.
Backtracking and collapsed edges are retained, rather than cosmetically
replacing the actual walk with a simple hull boundary.

The large gray hull is not claimed to be an outer bound on all determinant
values. The proved statement is that every point in each blue OPEN disk is
attained by some unitary. Boundary circles are dashed. The small Walsh disk
looks almost invisible on the full-range panel; the magnified panel shows
its tangency to the nearest walk segment and makes its actual scale visible.
The first and third examples have unequal real/imaginary plotting extents;
equal aspect ratio preserves geometric distances.

Visual inspection: labels and arrows are readable; disks are round; the
legend distinguishes hull, walk, and attained region. The full Walsh cycle
includes a real-axis backtrack, which is mathematically part of the certificate.

02_crossing_lifts.png

This is a newly constructed rational example of why a planarized drawing is
not automatically a graph of matrix paths. The spectra are

  a=(1+2i, 2+i, -1-i, 1/3),
  b=(-2-i, 1+i, -1-2i, 1/5+2i/7).

The Givens segments 1234--4231 and 2134--2431 intersect at

  q=-485604002/15450645 + (3467568/1030043)i.

Their exact interpolation parameters are 149203/294298 and 72241/147149,
both strictly between zero and one. Thus both actual matrix paths have this
determinant value. Their corresponding normal matrices have unequal diagonal
entries; the exact rational differences are saved in metrics.json. The plotted
Frobenius distance is approximately 4.173995478.

The middle panel retains two separate points in the abstract graph. The right
panel displays the absolute entries of the difference of their normal-matrix
lifts. This demonstrates a missing gluing step, not disconnectedness of the
determinant fiber: a path between the two preimages inside that fiber might
exist, but the mere crossing does not establish one. The figure explicitly
says so. Intersection and diagonal inequality are exact; matrix heatmap
entries are floating-point visualizations of the exact Givens expressions.

Visual inspection: the two colors consistently identify the two edges and
their preimages; the matrix difference is visibly nonzero. Endpoint labels
do not overlap. The diagram makes the abstract-versus-planar distinction
concrete without claiming a nonexistent fiber obstruction.

03_positive_weight_family.png

The first panel plots the four classes of individual permutation weights in
the exact fourteen-permutation law for V(t)=(tI+K)/sqrt(t^2+3). Multiplicities
are displayed: one identity, three double transpositions, four oriented
three-cycles, and six transpositions. Curves within a class coincide by an
algebraic identity; this is not an empirical pattern or an additional fit.

The identity weight becomes nonnegative at

  tau=0.6925048425718423...,  tau^4+4tau-3=0.

The shaded region shows where THIS law has a negative coefficient. It is not
marked as an MOC violation or as impossibility of every other positive law.
The middle panel shows group masses on t>=tau. They sum to one exactly by
the polynomial mass identity; the maximum floating-point plotting residual
is 4.44e-16. The final panels display squared entries of the dense mixers at
t=1,2,4. The light off-diagonal entries remain positive in all three.

The law reproduces all 70 squared minors exactly and therefore works for
every complex spectrum on the stated family. This is a restriction on mixers,
not an unrestricted U(4) proof. The relation to the gauged Walsh escape arc
is proved in the mathematical report.

Visual inspection: the sign-changing weight, positive domain, and unit mass
are clear. The stack plot does not conceal the multiplicities. No stochastic
noise should be inferred from these deterministic curves.

04_raw_winding_diagnostics.png

Every one of the 8,324 computed small-matrix records is represented in the
accounting panel and in the distance scatter. This is the n=3,4,5,6 portion
selected from the immutable 20,127-record corpus. The exclusions are visible:

  1,406 controls;
  1,714 noncontrols excluded by shape or graph-distance guards;
  5,041 eligible noncontrols with a nonzero-winding cycle;
    163 eligible noncontrols where this inner criterion is silent.

Eligibility requires minor RMS axis >1e-7, minor/major axis >1e-4, and distance
from every graph segment >1e-9 in the producer's common scaling. The vertical
change near log10(graph distance)=-9 is partly imposed by this guard. It is not
a newly discovered phase transition. The 319 graph distances and one vertex
distance at or below 1e-18 are clipped for display and counted in metrics.json.

The family panel gives both numerator and denominator. All n4,n5,n6 eligible
silent cases are clustered spectra:

  n4: 78/518 clustered cases are silent, 15.06%;
  n5: 46/170, 27.06%;
  n6: 37/106, 34.91%.

The corresponding other-family silence counts are all zero in these finite
guarded samples. At n3, the only two silent cases are Gaussian; 0/97 eligible
clustered cases are silent. The heatmap's scale extends to 0.40, so the larger
clustered fractions are not clipped or shown as identical by saturation.

The distance scatter deliberately retains overlap and apparent clutter. Its
triangular envelope is largely the elementary inequality

  distance to the graph <= distance to its vertex set,

not evidence of a new MOC mechanism. A graph distance by itself does carry a
retrospective association with silence: its pooled eligible AUC is 0.78090,
and vertex distance gives 0.75022. These are in-sample, family-confounded
descriptions, not prospectively validated predictors. Neither distance has a
threshold separating every eligible outcome: their distributions overlap.
Median graph distances are 0.00071535 for inner-certificate cases and
0.0125742 for silent cases. The image therefore avoids claiming either a
perfect separator or complete absence of a relationship.

The final panel is the weak-extrapolation view. Guarded success fractions
are 526/528, 3100/3178, 919/965, and 496/533 in dimensions 3 through 6. They
decrease here. Thus this criterion does not become uniformly more successful
merely as n grows; the family panel shows why a pooled dimension story is
incomplete. Family composition, sampling laws, and eligibility also differ
between n. These fractions are not probabilities that MOC fails.

Most importantly, a silent record is already an attained determinant value
by its construction. It shows a limitation of this sufficient inner-region
criterion, not a violation of the original convex-hull conjecture.

Visual inspection: all four panels are legible; counts match the stored rows;
the final heatmap uses the full observed fraction range; the plotted controls
and rejected observations remain visible instead of disappearing behind the
eligible summary. No unrestricted theorem is inferred from the scatter.
