Independent audit of the reduced PPT logdet engine
31 October 2026

The discovery engine passes the mathematical and saved-output checks
below. The candidate certificates are numerical; this audit is not an
exact proof of their PSD inequalities or an unrestricted MOC4 proof.

Analytic objective and derivatives

The engine uses real coordinates in an orthonormal basis of complex
Hermitian matrices. Each contact-face matrix F, its exceptional
four-coordinate partial-transpose corner C, and each ordinary weight w
are linear functions of these real coordinates. It minimizes the convex
Phase-I barrier

 t - mu sum_blocks log det(M+tI) - mu sum_weights log(w+t).

For a matrix block S=M+tI and its coordinate direction D_i, the correct
derivatives are

 gradient_i = -mu tr(S^(-1) D_i),
 Hessian_ij = mu tr(S^(-1) D_i S^(-1) D_j).

The phase coordinate adds I as its direction in every matrix block and
1 in every ordinary-weight block. The linear objective additionally
adds1 to its gradient. The engine includes all these cross terms.

The complex trace formula needs no conjugation between its two factors:
it is the trace of their matrix product. To check the implementation
independently, the audit diagonalizes S and forms the Hermitian whitened
directions W_i=S^(-1/2) D_i S^(-1/2). The Hessian is then the real
Frobenius Gram matrix mu Re tr(W_i^* W_j), manifestly PSD. This is
algebraically equal to the trace expression used by the engine.

On the one-sided197-variable and two-sided366-variable initial barrier
points, the independent full gradient agrees to at most1.78e-15 and the
full Hessian agrees to at most2.14e-14. Objective values agree to4.45e-16.
The independently reconstructed exceptional-corner direction matrices
also agree. The Hermitian-coordinate basis is orthonormal to1e-12.

Face, affine, and termination bookkeeping

With physical jet columns j_pi, the contact basis N is the nullspace of
the conjugated contact rows, so N^* j_contact=0. The physical coefficient
matrix is T14=N F N^*. The reduced cost uses the columns N^* j_pi, and
the corner is the parameter partial transpose of T14[0:4,0:4]. The
engine's conjugation and transpose conventions implement these formulas.

Dropping linearly dependent affine rows with pivoted QR does not weaken
the final claim: acceptance checks the reconstructed physical cost on
all24 permutations. The affine cache is keyed by contact set and number
of orientations; those data determine its structure because the
auxiliary jets and ordinary rays are independent of the actual spectra.

During this review, the owner added explicit acceptance checks on the
full14-dimensional minimum eigenvalue and contact-annihilation norm.
The empty ordinary-weight condition is now vacuous. The latter is
exercised by the negative control below, which has no ordinary rays.
An identically zero supplied support cost is explicitly recognized as
having a trivial zero certificate rather than divided by zero.

A negative Phase-I shift is sufficient, but is not required by the
implemented stopping condition: that condition checks the unshifted
matrices and weights directly. Therefore the phase-shift value cannot
by itself create a false feasible classification. Barrier iteration or
line-search failure remains unresolved, not an infeasibility proof.

Independent audit of every saved actual cost

All254 saved outputs were reloaded, including the249 fresh hull-edge
costs from32 spectral pairs and the five earlier difficult examples.
No discovery cost matrix, jet generator, ordinary-ray constructor, or
partial-transpose helper was reused for these checks.

The audit constructs all32 jet entries directly from products in the
auxiliary spectrum (0,1,i,w), constructs ordinary minor-indicator rays
from their set-image definition, and performs the exceptional partial
transpose by its four-index definition. It checks the saved reduction
is an isometry, reconstructs the physical jet from that reduction, and
matches every saved ordinary-ray column to a valid ray.

Actual product clouds and support gaps are recomputed using exact
Fraction arithmetic in real and imaginary parts. For the fresh campaign,
the exact integer eta strings are retained; the resulting exact gaps
are checked against every saved integer gap and exact contact set.
Thus cancellation in floating-point hull calculations or clamping of
near-zero costs is not used to validate the actual input gaps.

The five primary checks are original support-cost agreement, full14 PSD,
exceptional4-corner PT PSD, nonnegative ordinary weights, and contact
annihilation. All249 fresh costs satisfy the1e-9 numerical criterion.
The largest violation is1.4432899320127035e-15. All248 claimed strictly
feasible outputs independently satisfy the stricter positive-margin
conditions. There are no false strict-feasibility claims.

The remaining output, fresh32_pair_21_edge_07, is correctly reported
unresolved at the barrier limit. Its contact-face minimum eigenvalue
is2.941734239592681e-11, below the engine's strict1e-10 threshold, though
its full numerical feasibility checks pass. It is not a numerical
counterexample or an inferred cone obstruction.

Exact falsifiable formal negative control

Use the formal nonnegative permutation cost

 g(pi)=1[pi(2)=2]+1[pi(1)=3]
       +1[pi({0,1})={0,2}]-1[pi({0,2})={1,2}].

Its24 values are nonnegative and its maximum is2. This is the f34 control
from moc_cayley_imaginary_quartic_20261031.txt. The independent audit
reconstructs the rational orthogonal witness

 U=[[-1/2, 1/2, 1/2, 1/2],
    [ 1/2,11/14,-5/14,1/14],
    [ 1/2,-5/14,1/14,11/14],
    [ 1/2,1/14,11/14,-5/14]],

checks U^T U=I exactly, and computes the signed Leibniz expectation of
g exactly as-3/98, or-3/196 after normalization. Consequently no genuine
certificate in the proved universally positive cone can represent this
formal cost. This exclusion follows from the exact witness, not from
numerical nonconvergence. No actual spectral lift is asserted, so it is
not a counterexample to MOC.
