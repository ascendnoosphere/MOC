Exact counterexample to Kovacec's 2026 stronger polygon conjecture

Status: a locally checked exact counterexample to Conjecture 7 of
Alexander Kovacec, "A conjecture more precise and stronger than the
one by Marcus and de Oliveira", CMUC preprint 26-11 (7 April 2026),
https://www.mat.uc.pt/preprints/ps/p2611.pdf. Independent audit passed;
see kovacec_polygon_independent_review.txt. Root also inspected the
source statement and reran the exact verifier after adding connectivity,
Euler-characteristic, nonzero-denominator, and exact-area checks.
The example is strictly inside the original Marcus-de Oliveira
convex hull; it does not disprove or settle the original conjecture.

The preprint forms a straight-line graph with one vertex
z_pi=prod_i(alpha_i+beta_pi(i)) per permutation and an edge between
vertices whose permutations differ by one transposition. Its definition
of exterior is ray visibility: a point is exterior if it can be joined
to arbitrarily distant points by segments avoiding every graph edge.
Conjecture 7 asserts that, when this exterior has a Jordan boundary J,
every det(diag(alpha)+U diag(beta) U*) for unitary U belongs to the
closure of the bounded component of the complement of J. For n=4 the
graph has 24 vertices and 72 edges.

Here, writing i=sqrt(-1), take

 alpha=(638-1075i, -1495-569i, 555-1223i, -861-515i)/1000,
 beta=(-1429-1493i, 2125-2624i, -1080+511i, 2082+866i)/1000.

Let K=(R+i I)/1000, where

 R = [[0,-200,-395,281], [200,0,1024,25],
      [395,-1024,0,1098], [-281,-25,-1098,0]],
 I = [[564,-1518,-176,-187], [-1518,-796,-1242,-251],
      [-176,-1242,-222,-216], [-187,-251,-216,-278]].

R is antisymmetric and I symmetric, so K*=-K. The rational Cayley
transform U=(Id+K)(Id-K)^(-1) is exactly unitary. For these spectra,

 det(diag(alpha)+U diag(beta) U*)
 = [1152757738553382860717275886823045310903
    -243199353456090443350853544871623343201 i]
   / [30455402278742255382898961 * 10^12]
 = approximately 37.85068172808221 - 7.98542574582384 i.

The exact verifier code/research/verify_kovacec_polygon_counterexample.py
reads data/certificates/research/kovacec_conjecture7_counterexample.json
and checks the following with rational/integer arithmetic:

  1. The 24 matching points are distinct. All 72 graph segments are
     planarized by exact intersections; no two original segments are
     collinear. The resulting embedding has 317 vertices and 343 face
     cycles. Its unique outer-face cycle has 14 distinct vertices and
     is a simple polygon; every other face cycle has positive area.

  2. In counterclockwise order every edge of this polygon has strictly
     positive cross product with k=(65,3) minus its initial vertex.
     Hence k belongs to the strict kernel of the simple polygon, and
     the polygon is star-shaped with respect to k.

  3. The ray from the stated determinant in direction (-19,6) misses
     every one of the 72 closed graph segments strictly. For each
     segment the ray-intersection parameters show it is behind the
     ray start (35 segments), or outside one of the two segment ends
     (14 and 23 segments). No ray is parallel to a segment. Thus the
     determinant is in the open unbounded face of the graph, not on
     its boundary.

  4. The determinant lies strictly inside the triangle of matching
     points with zero-based lexicographic permutation indices 0,1,5.
     This is an exact orientation test and places it in the interior
     of the original MOC convex hull.

The Jordan and visibility qualifications are essential. The planar
half-edge traversal proves the outer boundary is a simple Jordan polygon,
and all graph segments lie in its closure. For an interior point every
ray to infinity meets this polygon's graph boundary. For an exterior
point p, the ray p+t(p-k), t>=0, misses the polygon: otherwise a later
point q in it would make p a point of [k,q] inside it, by star-shapedness.
Therefore the preprint's ray-visibility exterior is exactly the exterior
of this polygon, and its J is the certified Jordan polygon. The stated
unitary determinant lies in its open exterior, contrary to Conjecture 7.
The graph-boundary calculation is not replaced by the original convex
hull test.
