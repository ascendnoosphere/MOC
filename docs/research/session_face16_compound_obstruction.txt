Exact compound-matrix obstruction on a face-16 determinant branch

On the rays (3,16,21,30,53), take weights (1,b,2,d,1), and put

  R=3d^2-4d-8,  b=4(d^2+d+2)/R,  d>(2+2sqrt(7))/3.

All weights are nonnegative and R>0. The recovered height is
h=4d/(5d+4)>0. The exact Hessian determinant at x=g-h e is zero,
but its principal 12-minor omitting original selected coordinates
1 and 2 is

  -28 d^10 (5d+48)^2 / [(5d+4)^4 R^2] < 0.

The global neutral-inertia theorem says every principal 12-minor is
nonnegative at an actual midpoint, including higher-corank points;
therefore no point of this entire branch is actual. The symbolic
identity is checked by code/research/verify_neutral_hessian_face16_branch.py.
At d=3, b=8, the point is independently shown to possess a complex
critical-pair lift by code/research/verify_neutral_hessian_face16.py.
The determinant identity alone is not a proof of complex lift at
other points on the branch, and no such claim is made here.

As a separate broader slice, with weights (1,b,2,3,1), the trace of
the 12th Hessian compound is negative for all b>=7. One fixed
principal 12-minor is negative for b>=8. This slice includes only
b=8 on the zero-determinant locus for b>=7, so the determinant
obstruction already excludes its other points. The independent
polynomial identities and the sign certificates are checked in
code/research/verify_neutral_hessian_face16_family.py.

These statements prove exclusions, not the global nonnegative-support
inequality.
