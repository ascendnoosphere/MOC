Generic statements, with all finite cardinalities universally quantified:

1. Nonnegative weights with sum one, whose weighted x- and y-gradients
   vanish, give a lower bound on every epigraph height of a finite family
   of real-affine planes. The bound is the weighted constant coefficient.
2. The same statement is proved for Re(alpha_i*z)+beta_i in complex
   coordinates, with the sign in Re(alpha*z) explicitly handled.
3. If a strictly positive normalized weighted average attains a common
   upper bound, then every selected plane attains that bound. This is the
   tight-certificate contact principle used to prove uniqueness.

Scope:
This is a reusable dual-certificate checker and a fully checked scalar
