Concrete TL4 conditional expectation and an actual-support obstruction
31 October 2026

Result and scope

The canonical separated induction does not prove the global n4 claim,
even when it uses every positive TL3 function, every one of the four
wire-deletion conditional expectations, all ordinary matching squares,
and an arbitrary separate positive spin-zero correction. An actual
four-by-four spectral support gap lies outside that entire cone.

The same actual gap has a verified positive Gram on the full thirteen
contact features. Thus the obstruction isolates indispensable cross
moments; it is not a MOC or full matching-SOS counterexample. General
Jones constructions retaining those cross moments are not ruled out.

Concrete fourteen-diagram algebra

On H4=(C^2)^tensor4 define U_pi by

 U_pi |x0,x1,x2,x3> = |x_pi(0),x_pi(1),x_pi(2),x_pi(3)>.

Let e_i=I-U_(i,i+1), i=0,1,2. All matrices are16 by16 integers.
Direct multiplication gives e_i^2=2e_i, e_i e_(i+1)e_i=e_i, and
e_i e_j=e_j e_i for |i-j|>1. A fourteen-element diagram basis is given
by the following words, with the empty word equal to I:

 (),0,1,2,01,10,02,12,21,012,210,021,102,1021.

Their Markov Gram is Tr(A^* B)/16 and is nonsingular. Every one of the
twenty-four U_pi is expanded in this basis and checked exactly.

Use the five-word TL3 basis (),0,1,01,10 on H3. The trace-preserving
conditional expectation E4:TL4->TL3 is the normalized partial trace of
the last qubit, E4(X)=Tr_last(X)/2. Its images on the fourteen words,
in the order above, are

 I, e0, e1, I/2, e0e1, e1e0, e0/2, e1/2, e1/2,
 e0e1/2, e1e0/2, e0e1/2, e1e0/2, e1/2.

For the Jones projection p=e2/2, the exact identities are

 E4(p)=I/4,
 p(A tensor I2)p=(E3(A) tensor I4)p,  A in TL3,

where E3 is the analogous normalized last-qubit trace on TL3.
The25 products A p B, A,B in the five-word TL3 basis, have rank13.
They span the full nontrivial ideal Mat3 directsum Mat2 in TL4. The
functions Tr(J^*(U_pi-I)) for thirteen independent such products span
all thirteen matching coefficient functions vanishing at the identity.
Thus this construction exposes the entire contact space, without
discarding the six ordinary-feature cross directions.

What Markov lifting does to a positive TL3 law

Let d_k(pi) delete the label k from its permutation cycle. Partial trace
of a permutation operator has the elementary exact formula

 E_k(U_pi)=s_k(pi) U_(d_k(pi)),
 s_k(pi)=1 if pi(k)=k, and 1/2 otherwise.                   (1)

For a TL3 coefficient function h(sigma)=Tr(A U_sigma), its canonical
lift is L_k h(pi)=Tr(A E_k(U_pi))=s_k(pi)h(d_k(pi)). Therefore EVERY
positive Gram representation of the same TL3 cost g3 gives the SAME
lifted cost:

 sum_a |L_k h_a(pi)|^2=s_k(pi)^2 g3(d_k(pi)).              (2)

There is no hidden freedom in choosing a different n3 Gram here.

The TL3 coefficient space is the five-dimensional subspace of functions
on S3 with zero alternating sum. Every nonnegative such function is a
nonnegative combination of the nine Boolean functions

                 r_ij(sigma)=1_(sigma(i)=j).

One elementary proof is to match its three even values to its three odd
values by a nonnegative3 by3 transport table; their totals agree. Each
even/odd pair is exactly the support of one r_ij. Hence all positive n3
Markov lifts, on all four wire choices, form the cone generated by the
following36 vectors:

 m_(k;i,j)(pi)=s_k(pi)^2 1_(d_k(pi)(i)=j), i,j!=k.         (3)

Their entries are0,1/4,1. In particular this includes every possible
actual n3 source restriction and is more permissive than using only
known n3 formulas.

Leakage and its exact cancellation space

An individual lifted square generally is NOT in the fourteen-dimensional
matching space V14. Put a=1_(pi(i)=j), b=a 1_(pi(k)=k), and
q=1_(pi({i,k})={j,k}). Directly from (1),

                         4m_(k;i,j)=a+q+2b.              (4)

The ordered two-assignment term b must not be confused with the
unordered subset-matching term q. The former can carry the missing
[211] component. Dropping it gives an incorrect ordinary-cone argument.

Exact integer linear algebra gives

 dim span{m}=14,
 dim(span{m}+V14)=23,
 dim(span{m} intersection V14)=5.

A correct basis for the intersection consists of the four functions
1+3*1_(pi(i)=i), together with the function omega described next.
It does not contain a freely adjustable constant as a fifth diagonal
coordinate.

Let S4 act on the three pair partitions

 (01|23), (02|13), (03|12),

giving a quotient q:S4->S3. Then

 omega(pi)=1_(q(pi)=(1,2,0))-1_(q(pi)=(2,0,1)).

The rank statements and this exact intersection basis are verified by
nonzero rational minors, not inferred from floating singular values.

Every vector (3) has zero S4 alternating sum. Indeed for each sigma in
S3 there is one fixed-k extension of sign sgn(sigma), and three cycle
insertions of k with sign -sgn(sigma). The weighted alternating sum is
therefore1/4 times the alternating sum of the TL3 input, which is zero.

Why a separate positive spin-zero repair cannot fix the obstruction

The spin-zero central projector has the concrete formula

 S=sum_(i<j) U_(ij),  P0=(S-2I)(S-6I)/12.

It is an exact rank-two orthogonal projector; P0 TL4 P0 has dimension4.
Every Klein-four permutation acts as identity on its range. Thus every
spin-zero coefficient function, any squares thereof, and any separate
positive correction supported solely in that block and the trivial
block factor through q:S4->S3.

Allow an arbitrary NONNEGATIVE function h(q(pi)) as this correction,
which is even more permissive than allowing only spin-zero SOS. Suppose

 g=ordinary + sum_k MarkovLift_k(g3_k) + h(q(pi))           (5)

belongs to V14, with every g3_k nonnegative in TL3 and every ordinary
coefficient nonnegative. V14, the ordinary functions, and all Markov
lifts have zero S4 alternating sum, so h has zero alternating sum on S3.
It is therefore a nonnegative S3 V5 function and decomposes into the nine
Boolean functions 1_(q(pi)(i)=j).

Each of those nine quotient indicators is an ordinary matching sum.
Choose the representative pair I of input partition i and the two
complementary pairs J,J^c of output partition j. Then

 1_(q(pi)(i)=j)=1_(pi(I)=J)+1_(pi(I)=J^c).                (6)

Both terms are ordinary two-subset matching rays. Thus any separate
positive spin-zero correction compatible with (5) can be absorbed into
the ordinary part. It cannot cancel the missing [211] cross moments.

Exact actual-spectrum separator

List permutations lexicographically and let the integer functional be

 Y=(-5,11,1,-5,-5,3,11,11,11,11,-7,-5,
      11,-7,3,11,3,11,11,-5,11,1,11,11).

Direct exact calculations give Y.r_j>=0 for all34 ordinary rays, and
Y.m_(k;i,j)>=0 for ALL36 Markov square lifts. By (6), Y is also
nonnegative on every allowable separate spin-zero correction in (5).

Use the finite Gaussian-integer spectra

 a=(100000+100000i, 0, -300000-100000i, 7256-11910i),
 b=(-340+953i, -1240+2373i, 5750-9071i, -1484+2182i),
 eta=-802442+596731i.

Set z_pi=product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)), h=max_pi Re(eta z_pi),
g_pi=h-Re(eta z_pi). Direct integer products give unique contact index9
and

                    Y.g=-225046691487234511347712<0.     (7)

Consequently this ACTUAL supported cost admits no decomposition (5),
even with all four deletions, arbitrary positive TL3 data, all ordinary
matching squares, and an arbitrary separate positive spin-zero term.

This functional is not positive on the full matching square space. In
the complete thirteen-dimensional ordinary contact basis with ray
indices

 (0,2,3,4,5,7,8,9,10,16,17,22,23),

the coefficient vector

 (5,-5,-2,0,1,14,2,2,2,-1,0,11,-2)

defines a contact-vanishing function f with Y.f^2=-6456. This explicitly
locates a square direction missing from the separated construction.

The very same actual gap HAS a positive Gram on the full thirteen
contact features. The verifier rechecks all24 values and all13 positive
LDL pivots from build/moc_future_hit_exact_gram/exact_positive_gram.json,
including the exact10^30 rescaling from that file's finite rational
spectra to the integer spectra above. Thus (7) refutes only the stated
separated inductive mechanism.

What remains for a Jones proof

The25 products A p B already span all13 nontrivial operator directions,
so no algebraic basis direction is missing. What is missing is positivity
for their JOINT Choi second moments. Restricting to positive TL3 laws
passed through E and adding a separate positive spin-zero block cannot
supply it. A successful construction must retain cross terms between
these module pieces, or use a nonlinear actual-source constraint that
controls those terms. The actual eight-spinor source and its O3(C)
frame supply such constraints but no global positive completion is
established here.
