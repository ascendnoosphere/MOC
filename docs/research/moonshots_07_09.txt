Moonshots M07--M09: exact tests of cap inequalities and real-form obstructions
2026-10-30

M07: an actual spectral counterexample to the proposed linear strengthening.
The standalone code/research/verify_f34_weighted_asymmetry.py constructs finite
spectra and a nonzero complex support multiplier, computes all 24 products,
and verifies that their exact gap is
  f34 + (1/2) r18 + 2 r23,  with h=1/2.
Thus the canonical triangle coordinates are lambda=1, p=1/2, q=2, r=0.
The stronger proposed inequality lambda<=p+r fails strictly. The weighted
triangle bound still holds, at equality, since sqrt(pq)=1.

The 24 products in this explicit realization are all nonzero but include
coincidences. This is not a restriction for the counterexample: with eta
fixed, all gap coordinates are continuous in the spectra, including at ties
because h is a finite maximum. Both lambda>0 and lambda>p+r are strict, so
they persist in a sufficiently small neighborhood. Generic spectra there have
all 24 products distinct and nonzero; their gaps stay in the canonical cap
because the other cap coordinates are nonnegative gaps. No claim is made that
the perturbed spectra remain on this particular two-ray boundary.

M08: a canonical-cap compound-PSD exclusion, with its limitation explicit.
On g=f34+(1/2)r18+2r23+T r0 for T>=0, write F=F(g), Q=D_eF(g),
W=QH_F(g)-F H_F(e). Exact arithmetic in
 code/research/verify_f34_compound_slice.py
gives F=-(T+1), Q=-2 and, deleting rows/columns 1 and 2,
 det W[complement of {1,2}] = -576 T(T+1)^4(27T+44).
The global actual-neutrality theorem makes the 12th compound PSD, so it
excludes all T>0. However, on this slice det W=-331776 T^2(T+1)^4, so the
older Hessian-determinant obstruction already excludes T>0. This is an exact
executed family calculation, not a claimed strengthening of that obstruction
or a proof of the global weighted-triangle budget.

M09: neutral inertia does not imply a real lift, even on a smooth secant.
Let w1 be the selected matching-product vector of real spectra
 a=(-3,2,3,5), b=(0,-2,-5,-1),
and w2 that of
 a=(-4,-2,2,3), b=(-3,-7,7,5).
Their midpoint x is a real point of the complex critical secant. The exact
checker code/research/verify_neutral_not_sufficient.py verifies both endpoint
gradients vanish, rank H_x=12, and inertia (6 positive,6 negative,2 zero).
For a rational kernel basis K, the Groebner basis of
 grad F(K(u,v))=grad F(x) includes the positive even quartic
  v^4+(3660332374203350/20409599059)v^2
      +52209881076663671875/20409599059.
It has no real root, hence no real lift. The checker also finds rank 2 for
the kernel-restricted gradient coefficient matrix B_x; the previously
audited contraction theorem then gives smoothness of the secant at x. Thus
neutral inertia and smoothness together are strictly weaker than real-lift
existence. This is a pointwise real-form obstruction, not a counterexample
to MOC or a classification of all neutral secants.
