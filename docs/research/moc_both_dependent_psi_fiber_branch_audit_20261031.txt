Complete psi fiber and branchwise exclusion for the both-dependent normal
31 October 2026

Result
------
For the exact normal n in
  build/moc_both_dependent_double_contact_escape/exact.json,
the projective quadratic map psi has exactly TWO complex projective
preimages. Both preimages are rational real rank-two matrices. They have
psi(H)=alpha*n with alpha<0, so there is NO real affine solution of
psi(H)=n and no real lift onto the positive oriented ray of n. There are
four normalized complex affine lifts, two purely imaginary signs for each
projective lift.

For EACH lift, the legitimate differential condition

                     M(g)*vec(H)=0

admits no nonzero matching cost with g>=0 and g_0=g_7=0. This is proved
by exact positive Stiemke certificates, not a numerical LP status and not
the derivative of a possibly ramified source-label quotient.

The fiber audit also excludes exceptional lifts from the base locus of
psi. The two computed lifts therefore exhaust the closed graph fiber.
The two affine differential images have rank nine separately and rank ten
together. This directly proves singularity of the nine-dimensional image
cone at n; the earlier 32-label derivative observation was insufficient
for that conclusion by itself.

The replay uses rational polynomial arithmetic and exact linear algebra.
It needs no LP solver. Runtime is about two seconds.

1. Reduction of every possible lift to four coordinates
-----------------------------------------------------
Let L be the standard tetrahedral 4-by-3 orthonormal matrix and let P be
the singleton matrix of n. Singleton recovery for the universal pencil
gives

                       C=4*L^T*P*L=cof(H)

when psi(H)=n. Projectively the equality has an arbitrary nonzero scalar.
For this normal C=u*v^T, where

  u=(-4,-51/625,-2673/1250)^T,
  v=(1,3/10000,7701/10000)^T.

Any projective lift has rank two: a rank-three H has full-rank cofactor,
and a rank-at-most-one H has zero cofactor. Its two nullspace identities
are H*v=0 and u^T*H=0. Consequently EVERY lift is

  H=A*X*B^T,

       [-51/2500  -2673/5000]       [-3/10000  -7701/10000]
  A=   [ 1          0      ], B=   [ 1          0       ],
       [ 0          1      ]       [ 0          1       ]

with X a 2-by-2 matrix and det X nonzero. Exact expansion gives

                   cof(H)=-(det X)/4*C.

Substituting H into all fourteen quadratic coordinates of psi produces
four independent homogeneous quadratic equations expressing

                   psi(H)=-(det X)/4*n.

2. Complete projective fiber
---------------------------
Write X=[[x0,x1],[x2,x3]]. In the chart x3=1, the exact lexicographic
Groebner basis is equivalent to

  19508123584005328883271*x0
    -1082366318403221576535*x2-46737484031732046027554=0,

  6502707861335109627757*x1
    +7490889472339524487632*x2-386983280916665072355=0,

  (115293213417*x2+93639480343)
    *(196279158657*x2-178993785823)=0.

The det-X-saturated x3=0 chart has Groebner basis [1], so there are no
missing projective solutions at infinity. Both displayed roots are simple
and give nonzero determinant. Explicitly the two matrices X are

  X1=[[271023631703/115293213417, 38243531741/38431071139],
      [-93639480343/115293213417, 1]],

  X2=[[480175904573/196279158657, -64837907531/65426386219],
      [178993785823/196279158657, 1]].

For Hi=A*Xi*B^T, the scales alpha_i=-(det Xi)/4 are approximately

                     -0.7897389423, -0.8375312646.

Their strict negativity is checked rationally. Thus the real projective
fiber has two points, the complex projective fiber has the same two points,
and the normalized affine solutions are exactly

                    +/- i*Hi/sqrt(-alpha_i), i=1,2.

In particular, the negative affine orientation is essential. Generic real
source labels do not imply that the positively oriented normal has a real
affine psi lift.

3. No hidden lifts from the base locus
------------------------------------
The exact base locus can be determined without a numerical search.
If psi(H)=0, singleton recovery forces cof(H)=0, hence rank H<=1.
Projectively write H=a*b^T. Each two-set coordinate factors into a
row factor and a column factor:

  +/-[(w_I*a)(w_Ic*a)]*[(w_J*b)(w_Jc*b)].

For I=01,02,03 the three row quadratics span precisely

                         a0^2-a1^2, a0^2-a2^2.

Their common projective zeros are the four rows of L. The same holds for
b. Therefore the full base locus is exactly the eight projective planes

           a proportional to a row of L, or b proportional to a row of L.

Let Gamma be the closed graph of the projective map psi. The adjugate
identities imply throughout Gamma that

                   H*C(n)^T and C(n)^T*H

are scalar multiples of the identity. This is a closed bilinear incidence
condition. If H=a*b^T is a rank-one base point, those scalar multiples
must be zero. For C(n)=u*v^T this requires

                         u^T*a=0, b^T*v=0.

But all four entries of L*u and all four entries of L*v are nonzero in
the present example. Equivalently P has no zero row or column. Thus no
point of any base plane can lie in the graph fiber over n.

The graph is proper and generically birational over its image: on the
invertible chart the cofactor map is projectively birational. Its fiber
over n consists only of the two nonbase points found above. Near these
points it is the graph of a map from smooth projective eight-space, and
the projective differential has rank eight. Locally these give the two
smooth normalization branches; no additional branch can appear from an
exceptional graph point.

4. Exact branchwise Stiemke certificates
--------------------------------------
Let F be the twelve contact-null ordinary features with IDs

                   2,3,6,7,8,9,12,17,18,22,24,25.

They span the full contact space W={g in V14:g_0=g_7=0}, because they are
independent and the two contact evaluations are independent on V14.

For each Hi form the 9-by-12 exact matrix

                  Ti=[M(F_j)*vec(Hi)]_j.

Its rank is nine on BOTH branches. Consequently the costs satisfying the
legitimate branch condition form a three-dimensional subspace

                         V_i=F*ker Ti.

For each V_i the verifier gives a rational vector y_i with

                  V_i^T*y_i=0,
                  (y_i)_0=(y_i)_7=0,
                  (y_i)_pi>=1 for every other permutation.

All but three noncontact entries are exactly one. The exceptional entries
are approximately

  branch 1: pi15=1.63016623, pi16=4.53741390, pi23=17.10483401;
  branch 2: pi16=4.46282261, pi20=1.69641333, pi23=17.95563088.

Every exact fraction is in the JSON. These approximate values are only
for readability; the proof checks all three rational signs directly.

If a nonzero g in V_i were pointwise nonnegative, its contact values would
be zero and y_i^T*g would be strictly positive, contradicting V_i^T*y_i=0.
Hence the nonnegative cone in each legitimate branch is just {0}.

The replay additionally constructs the requested derivative-plus-gauge
form. It solves exactly for delta Hi and the complete ten-dimensional
matching-annihilator gauge Z*b_i, adjusting only the two irrelevant contact
entries, to obtain a functional v_i satisfying

  v_i = Dpsi_Hi(delta Hi)+Z*b_i,
  (v_i)_pi>=1 outside the contacts.

Here Dpsi_H(delta H)(g)=2*vec(delta H)^T*M(g)*vec(H). The recorded
identity is checked on the full fourteen-dimensional matching basis.
Thus these are genuine branchwise Gauss/Stiemke exclusions, not the
stronger condition of annihilating all 32 original source-label derivatives.

The completeness statement needed for LIMITING conormals also has a direct
compactness proof. Take generic pairs (g_k,psi(H_k)) with
M(g_k)*vec(H_k)=0 tending projectively to (g,n). After a subsequence the
projective matrices H_k converge. Their limit lies in the closed graph
fiber over n, which has just the two nonbase points Hi. Choosing compatible
homogeneous representatives and taking limits gives M(g)*vec(Hi)=0.
Thus every limiting conormal pair over this normal satisfies one of the
two separately excluded equations. No assertion about the tangent of a
ramified label quotient is needed for this argument.

5. Direct singularity check and scope
------------------------------------
The two affine psi differential images have dimension nine separately.
Their combined span has dimension ten, checked exactly. The affine image
cone has dimension nine, since psi is generically finite and has rank
nine at each lift. Every differential image lies in the Zariski tangent
space at the common projective image. Thus that tangent has dimension at
least ten and the image cone is singular there.

This validates singularity using the actual quadratic map. It also shows
why annihilating the sum of both tangent images would have been an
unjustified strengthening of the branch condition: a conormal may belong
to either branch. The two separate exact certificates avoid that issue.

The original example remains a valid counterexample to the normal-only
positivity shortcut. It is NOT a Gauss-compatible cost or a MOC
counterexample. The new audit establishes that neither local psi branch
can pair this particular normal with a nonzero nonnegative cost having
the two specified contacts. It does not assert a uniform result for all
both-dependent normals or solve unrestricted MOC.
