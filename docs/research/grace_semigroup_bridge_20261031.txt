Grace-like stability: finite composition identity and an exact boundary
31 October 2026

The unrestricted Marcus--de Oliveira conjecture is not resolved here.
This calculation rules out a tempting stronger stability argument and records
the exact operation represented by the Grace-like semigroup. The product
input restriction in MOC must be retained by any subsequent proof.

Primary sources actually read

1. David Ruelle, Grace-like polynomials, arXiv:math-ph/0009030v1.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0009030v1
   Proposition8 states that det(diag(z)-U diag(w) U*) is a reduced
   Grace-like polynomial for every complex unitary U. Proposition5 gives
   a representation as a linear combination of matching products.
   The coefficients of this representation are not asserted positive.

2. Petter Branden and David G. Wagner, A converse to the
   Grace--Walsh--Szego theorem, arXiv:0809.3225v1.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.3225v1
   Propositions3--4 equate the stability of the plus-symbol polynomial
   with stability preservation by the corresponding permutation operator.
   The paragraph following Proposition4 gives semigroup closure.
   Neither cited result supplies pointwise convex hull contraction.

Exact operator conventions

Let M_U,k[I,J]=|det U[I,J]|^2 for k-subsets I,J of {0,...,n-1}.
For unitary U, complementary minors identify the action on degree-k
monomials with

  T_U(x_J)=sum_I M_U,k[I,J] x_I.

Equivalently T_U is a real linear combination of variable permutations.
The real Leibniz coefficients

  c_pi=Re(sgn(pi) product_i U_i,pi(i)/det U)

reproduce every M_U,k and sum to one. In a real orthogonal example no
real-part operation is needed. Consequently T_U commutes with simultaneous
translation of every variable by the same complex scalar. Its plus symbol
on the matching product is the normal determinant, up to the consistent
inverse convention on permutations; hence it preserves stability by the
above primary theorem.

All-dimensional finite phase identity

For ANY two complex n-by-n matrices U,V, simultaneously for every k,

  M_U,k M_V,k
    = 2^(1-n) sum_(d in {+1,-1}^n, d_0=1) M_(U diag(d) V),k.       (1)

No integration, genericity, or unitary hypothesis is needed for (1).
Cauchy--Binet expands a minor of U diag(d) V as

  sum_L det U[I,L] det V[L,J] product_(l in L) d_l.

Its squared modulus has diagonal terms giving the left side of (1).
For distinct equal-cardinality sets L,L', an off-diagonal term contains
the Boolean character product_(l in L symmetric_difference L') d_l.
The symmetric difference cannot consist solely of {0}, because its
cardinality is even. Thus a free independent sign occurs, and its average
is zero. Every diagonal character is one. This proves (1), including
k=0,n and all zero or singular cases.

Thus composition of two determinantal Grace operators is the average of
2^(n-1) actual unitary profiles when U,V are unitary. It is NOT the profile
of their coherent matrix product. For H2=2^(-1/2)[[1,1],[1,-1]],

  M_H2,1^2 = [[1/2,1/2],[1/2,1/2]],
  M_(H2 H2),1 = I.

The exact verifier checks three instances at n=2,3,4. The entrywise L1
differences between composed profiles and the coherent product profiles
by order k are respectively

  n2: (0,2,0), n3: (0,1,1,0), n4: (0,6,9,6,0).

The n3 pair uses explicit rational complex Hermitian Cayley matrices.
The n4 pair is the rational real Walsh matrix multiplied by itself.
These are concrete checks of the algebra, not proofs inferred from sampling.

Exact counterexample to contraction on ALL stable inputs

The following proposed shortcut is false:

  If T_U preserves stability and f is stable and multiaffine, then
  (T_U f)(a) belongs to conv{f(a_pi): pi in S_n} for every complex a.   (C)

Let

 Q=(1/59)[[-25,46,16,22],[-46,-31,20,-2],
           [-16,20,-11,-52],[22,2,52,-17]],
 a=(-1+2i,3+3i,-2-2i,-3+2i).

Exact multiplication gives QQ^T=I. In pair order 01,02,03,12,13,23 set

 c=(498-1250i,875-850i,905-820i,
    1250+1250i,530+530i,107+1004i),
 f(x)=14195+sum_(i<j)c_ij x_i x_j.

In lexicographic permutation order the 24 real values Re f(a_pi) are

 (17250,0,22540,19090,3450,17250,
  0,0,0,0,3450,3450,
  5290,19090,0,0,13800,0,
  0,13800,0,17250,10350,13800).

Their minimum is zero. Direct exact squared-minor multiplication gives

  (T_Q f)(a)=(-49910+8065732i)/3481.                         (2)

This has negative real part and is outside the permutation hull.
To obtain a STABLE input, put

 p(x)=x_0 x_1 x_2 x_3+f(x),
 q(x)=p(x_0+200i,...,x_3+200i),
 a'=a-200i(1,1,1,1).

For Im x_j>0 write z_j=x_j+200i, so |z_j|>200. The sum of the bounds
|Re c_ij|+|Im c_ij| is exactly9869. Therefore

 |p(z)/product_j z_j -1|
   <=9869/200^2+14195/200^4
   =78954839/320000000 <1.

Hence p(z) is nonzero and q is stable. Both q and T_Qq are stable,
using Branden--Wagner for the latter. Since the full monomial is fixed
by T_Q, and T_Q commutes with uniform translations,

  min_pi Re q(a'_pi)=Re product_j a_j,
  Re (T_Q q)(a')=Re product_j a_j -49910/3481.

This disproves (C) with a strict rational separating margin. There is
no inference from numerical infeasibility: every displayed score, minor,
and separation is checked by exact SymPy rational arithmetic. The
stability bound is a direct triangle inequality valid on the entire
open upper-half-plane product.

Why this is not a counterexample to MOC

The polynomial q is not a product of four affine factors in the individual
variables. Before translation, p has leading coefficient1, no cubic
terms, and nonzero quadratic terms. A product product_j(x_j+b_j) with
leading coefficient1 and all cubic coefficients zero would have every
b_j=0 and therefore all quadratic coefficients zero. Uniform translation
preserves being such a product. Thus q is outside the product-input class
used by MOC.

In particular, a proof must retain the multiplicative relations among
product-input coefficients. Replacing them merely by stability, or by
semigroup closure of stability preservers, creates a false statement.
An eventual positive state on the permutation-value algebra must use
the rank-one product tensor or a comparably strong property. No such
state is constructed here.
