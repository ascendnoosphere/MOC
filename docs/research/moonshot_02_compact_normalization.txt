M02: exact compact normalization and fractional-linear covariance

STATUS: certified normalization, not a proof of the residual inequality.
The intended gain is a compact, bounded spectral search domain and exact
transport of counterexamples. No numerical evidence is promoted to proof.

Use the difference convention: the eight points are a_i and c_j=-b_j.
Choose a farthest pair x,y among them. Unless all eight points coincide,
x!=y. Set z'=(z-x)/(y-x). Every normalized point lies in the compact lens
|z'|<=1, |z'-1|<=1. Indeed |z-x| and |z-y| are both at most |x-y|.
The chosen two points are 0,1. Every determinant and every matching
product is multiplied by the same nonzero factor (y-x)^(-4).
This follows directly from A'-C'=(A-C)/(y-x). Convex-hull inclusion and
strict failure are therefore preserved exactly. There are only finitely
many choices of labeled farthest pair (28 unordered pairs, with a choice
of orientation). Permutations inside the two spectra and exchanging the
two matrices reduce their category to AA, AC, or CC. The all-coincident
case has A=C=xI and determinant zero, and is closed directly.

For the full fractional-linear map f(z)=(az+b)/(cz+d), ad-bc!=0,
provided both denominators are invertible, the ordered matrix identity is

 (cA+dI)[f(A)-f(C)](cC+dI)=(ad-bc)(A-C).

This is valid without commutation between A and C: expand the left side
as (aA+bI)(cC+dI)-(cA+dI)(aC+bI). The AC terms cancel in their original
order. Normality is preserved by rational functional calculus. Taking
determinants shows that all matrix determinant values and all matching
products scale by the identical nonzero complex factor

 (ad-bc)^4 / [det(cA+dI)det(cC+dI)].

Thus Möbius transformations transport both MOC proofs and counterexamples,
with no phase restriction. Pole cases require a limit argument and cannot
be evaluated by dividing by zero; the two-circle note handles those
limits for its particular application. Generic spectra can normalize
three labeled points by Möbius transformation, but this is not combined
with the compact lens normalization: their parameter budgets differ.

Executed checks: code/research/verify_compact_mobius_normalization.py
checks an exact noncommuting 4x4 instance, all24 permutation products,
and a farthest-pair lens chart in Gaussian rational arithmetic. The proof
above, rather than the sample, establishes the universal transformation.

Limitation: compactness supplies no finite grid proof without a certified
uniform error budget near equality. This step is useful for bounded
adversarial searches; it does not resolve the positivity obstruction.
