An exact obstruction to the natural four-state feedback ARE embedding
31 October 2026

Use the full35-parameter star block-Gram equations from
moc_star_block_gram_affine_20261031.txt.  With the primary block D and
the cost fixed, the auxiliary lift has the affine form

                      C=C0+L(B),  ell(B)=beta,

where B is6-by4, C is symmetric4-by4, and

                   ell(B)=1_6^T B (1,-1,0,1)^T.

The linear operator L is independent of D and the source cost.  Its
extension away from the ell-hyperplane depends on the choice of ten
interpolation rows, but its restriction to ker ell is intrinsic.

The proposed feedback reduction asks for U in Mat_(6,4) and A in
Mat_(4,4) satisfying, for EVERY symmetric4-by4 X,

                L(U X)=A^T X+X A,  ell(U X)=0.             (1)

If nonzero full-rank U existed, B=B0+UX would make the Gram Schur
residual a standard algebraic Riccati expression with quadratic term
-X U^T D^-1 U X.  This specific construction is impossible:

        THEOREM.  The only solution of(1) is U=0,A=0.

Proof by exact coefficient certificate.  The symmetric X has ten
independent entries.  For each entry, equate the ten symmetric entries
of the first equation and the one scalar second equation.  This gives
a homogeneous110-by40 rational linear system in the24 entries of U
and16 entries of A.  The exact system has an INTEGER40-by40 minor with
determinant64.  Hence its rank is40 and its kernel is zero.  The full
coefficient matrix, the40 selected row indices, their equation labels,
and the determinant are exported by the checker below.

The checker rebuilds L directly from the eleven star interpolation
equations and the invertible ten-row C coefficient block.  It does
not rely on a floating-point fit or on a saved optimized Gram.

Two structural necessary conditions are useful for interpreting the
test.  First, ell(UX)=0 for every symmetric X is equivalent to
1_6^T U=0, since X(1,-1,0,1)^T ranges over R4.  Second, L(UX) is
PT-invariant on this kernel.  If J_Q is the symmetric Hessian of
UT-RS, then the first equation requires

                         A J_Q+J_Q A^T=0.

Thus A would belong to so(2,2).  The rank40 certificate proves that
even after allowing all such possibilities and all24 entries of U,
no nonzero embedding satisfies the complete interpolation operator.

Scope.  This result is independent of the chosen primary Gram D,
including a Cauchy primary with arbitrary pair phases.  It also permits
U and A to be selected separately for each source cost: the same
zero-kernel linear test applies at each selection.  It does not rule
out B depending on X by a more general linear operator, a change of
state/auxiliary identification, higher-dimensional feedback, a
nonlinear source-dependent construction, or a Bezoutian identity.
It is not an obstruction to a positive full matching Gram or to MOC4.
