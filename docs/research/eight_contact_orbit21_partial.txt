For g=f34+a r0+b r7+c r22+d r23+e r28, set A=a+1, B=b+1. Then
 F=-(AB+c), Q=-(c+de+1)<0. The complete five-parameter projected nonic is
 N=(de-AB)(AB+c)M, with
 M=AB(d+e+1)+c(A+B-AB+d+e+de)+(A+B-1)de.
The verified necessary spectral determinant identity is
 Q^3 det(H_g-(F/Q)H_1)=-4Q N^2. Thus actual gaps satisfy N=0. The
first branch de=AB is weighted-minor safe because AB>=1. The remaining
branch M=0 needs further work; it can enter the unsafe region de<1, and
its existence cannot be dismissed by positivity of the determinant alone.

For example, A=B=4, c=391/119, d=e=1/4 gives M=0 and de=1/16;
h=(AB+c)/(c+de+1)=2160/487. For that exact point H_x, x=g-h1, has a
2-dimensional kernel. In the verifier's rational nullspace basis, writing
y=u k1+v k2, component 10 of grad F(y)-grad F(x)=0 requires
  237169 v^2+4665600=0.
This is impossible for real v, and excludes this exact point by the full
real lift. A floating-point exploration found no positive quadratic-pencil
coefficient among the 35 currently verified metrics at this point; that
numerical observation is not used in the proof or a global claim.

On the whole M=0 branch with c>=0 and de<1, its denominator
 S=AB-A-B-d-e-de is strictly positive and
 c=[AB(d+e+1)+(A+B-1)de]/S.
This provides a promising rational parametrization. No sign or real-lift
obstruction on all four remaining parameters has yet been certified.
