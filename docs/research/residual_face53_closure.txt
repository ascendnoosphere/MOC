A REAL-QUADRIC OBSTRUCTION ON A RESIDUAL MOC4 FACE
2026-10-30

Scope: the original target is unrestricted complex MOC4. This note treats
one whole coefficient face, not the unrestricted target. The algebra has a
standalone exact verifier, code/research/verify_residual_face_53.py; its complete exact run passed (184.45 seconds). No priority claim.

For rho(I,J)(U)=|det U[I,J]|^2, use one-based indices and set

 r3=rho(1,4), r21=rho(12,34), r27=rho(13,34), r30=rho(14,14),
 f53=rho(2,2)+rho(3,3)+rho(12,34)-rho(13,13).

These are the repository's canonical ray labels. Let g_pi=h-Re(eta z_pi),
where z_pi=product_i(alpha_i+beta_pi(i)), eta!=0 and h=max_pi Re(eta z_pi).
Suppose the 24 gap values agree with the permutation values of

                 g=a r3+b r21+c r27+d r30+f53,      a,b,c,d>=0.       (1)

The theorem proved below is: if h>0, then cd>=1. Consequently the support
inequality holds for all complex unitary mixers for any actual gap of this
form. A positive coefficient of f53 can be normalized to one by scaling
eta and h. A zero mixed coefficient is an ordinary nonnegative minor sum.

Imported exact background
-------------------------
The model closing_moc4_model_verify.py constructs the real homogeneous cubic
F in 14 selected matching coordinates, checks all its coefficients, and checks
that grad F(z)=0 for all actual spectral matching products. Write e=(1,...,1),
H_v=H_F(v), Q(v)=D_e F(v)=sum_i partial_i F(v). The verifier checks H_e e=0.
For x=g-he and real imaginary-part lift y, actual spectra therefore satisfy

              H_x y=0,       grad F(y)=grad F(x),       F(x)=0.       (2)

The last equation follows directly: x.grad F(y)=y^T H_x y/2=0 and the
second equation plus Euler's identity gives 3F(x)=0. Since H_e e=0,
F(x)=F(g)-hQ(g), and Q(x)=Q(g). These are necessary conditions only;
no converse spectral-realization assertion is needed.

The proved weighted-minor theorem relabels exactly to

                f53+t r27+t^(-1) r30>=0     (t>0, U in U(4)).       (3)

1. Solve the spectral branch, preserving its full parameter domain
-----------------------------------------------------------------
In the fixed 14-coordinate order, (1) is

         g=(d+1,1,d,0,0,1,0,0,0,0,0,c,b,a).

Exact cubic evaluation gives

        F(g)=-(a+b+1),             Q(g)=b(d-1)-cd-1.                 (4)

Thus h>0 implies Q<0 and h=-(a+b+1)/Q. In particular Q!=0.
Put A=a+1 and

 D=d A^2-A[c(d^2+1)+(d+1)^2]+c^2 d,
 P=bD-A(c+1)(d+1)(A-cd).

The verified polynomial identity is

       det(QH_g-F(g)H_e)=-4 Q^12 (a+b+1)^2 P^2.                   (5)

The first equation of (2) forces det H_x=0 unless y=0. If y=0,
grad F(x)=0 also, which likewise forces H_x singular since H_x x=2grad F(x)
and x!=0 (F(g)!=0 prevents x=0). Hence (5) gives P=0.

Assume for contradiction cd<1. Then A>=1 and cd<1 give A-cd>0. The equation P=0 has strictly positive right side, so b>0,D>0 and

                  b=A(c+1)(d+1)(A-cd)/D.                         (6)

At d=0 one has D=-A(c+1)<0; thus d>0. This is a rational parametrization
of the entire dangerous branch, with no missing D=0 component.

2. A constant two-dimensional kernel, with a nondegenerate Q restriction
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let p(t)=det(H_g-t H_e). The exact verifier establishes:

 (i) p is cubic, p(0)=0, p(h)=p'(h)=0 on (6), h=F/Q;
 (ii) rank H_x<=12 identically on that rational branch;
 (iii) p'(0)=4d(c+1)(a+b+1)L R, where

   L=b(Ad-c)-cA(d+1),
   R=ab+acd+ac+ad+a+bc+b+cd+c+d+1.

For (ii), exact elimination of ten variables leaves a 4x4 system. Its
upper-right 2x2 block is [[d,-A],[-1,c]], determinant cd-A!=0. Its remaining
2x2 Schur complement vanishes identically after (6) and h=F/Q. The initial
ten pivots are nonzero at an exact branch point, so this proves rank<=12
on a nonempty rational open chart. Every 13x13 minor then vanishes
identically as a rational function of (a,c,d), and hence everywhere on
D>0,Q<0 by continuity. This does not assume each elimination pivot stays
nonzero throughout the domain.

All factors of p'(0) are strictly positive. Indeed R>0, and (6) gives

          L=A(d+1)[D+(c+1)A(d+1)^2]/D>0.                         (7)

A cubic with roots 0 and the double root h!=0 has the form
p(t)=k t(t-h)^2. Therefore

           [z^2] det(H_x+zH_e)=p'(0)/h>0.                       (8)

A matrix of corank at least three would make the determinant vanish to
order at least three in z. Thus (ii),(8) give rank H_x=12 everywhere.
In an orthonormal kernel/complement decomposition, the coefficient in
(8) is the determinant of the invertible complementary Hessian block
times det(H_e restricted to ker H_x). Consequently the latter determinant
is nonzero everywhere. In particular Q(y)=y^T H_e y/2 is a nonsingular
binary quadratic on this continuously varying kernel. Nonzero coefficient
(8) alone would NOT prove corank two; step (ii) is essential.

3. Connectedness fixes the sign, without a parameter grid
---------------------------------------------------------
The dangerous domain in (A,c,d) is

       A>=1, c>=0, d>0, cd<1, D>0, Q<0,

where b is the rational function (6). Its c>0 interior is path connected.
Here are the details, also isolated in session_face_connectivity.txt.

For fixed c,d with cd<1, D(1)<0. Since the leading coefficient is d>0,
D>0 with A>=1 is precisely A>A_+(c,d), the larger root of D. This root is
continuous and greater than one. Direct differentiation gives

 db/dA=-(c+1)(d+1)[c(A-cd)^2+A^2(d+1)^2]/D^2<0,
 lim_(A->infinity) b=(c+1)(d+1)/d.

For d<=1, Q<0 throughout A>A_+. For d>1, Q decreases from +infinity to
(d^2-d-c-1)/d, so the allowed fiber is nonempty exactly when
c>d^2-d-1, and then is a ray above its unique Q=0 threshold. This threshold
joins A_+ continuously as d decreases to one. The base with c>0 is

 0<d<delta,     max(0,d^2-d-1)<c<1/d,

where delta is the unique positive root of d^3-d^2-d-1. Its continuous
lower and upper boundaries give a path-connected strip. Raising A above
the threshold along a compact base path proves connectedness of the full
domain. Every admissible c=0 point is a limit of c>0 points satisfying the
same strict inequalities.

At the rational interior point

       (a,b,c,d)=(20,3486/1109,1/4,1),

the verifier computes rank H_x=12 and proves Q|ker H_x positive definite
by exact positive leading principal entry and determinant. The nonsingular
binary form in section 2 therefore stays positive definite on the connected
interior, and by nondegeneracy and continuity also at admissible c=0 points.
This is a global sign argument, not inference from finitely many samples.

4. Contradiction and recovery of the support inequality
------------------------------------------------------
For the actual y in (2), positivity gives Q(y)>=0. But summing the second
identity in (2) gives

                         Q(y)=Q(x)=Q(g)<0,

a contradiction. Thus cd>=1 for every actual positive-height gap (1).
In particular c>0, and (3), with t=c, yields

 g(U) = [f53(U)+c r27(U)+c^(-1)r30(U)]
          +a r3(U)+b r21(U)+(d-c^(-1))r30(U) >=0.

This reconstructs exactly the original support inequality for this face,
for every U in U(4), with no simple-spectrum or distinct-product assumption.
The h=0 case cannot occur because F(g)=hQ(g) but F(g)<0. For h<0, the
established n=4 external-half-plane theorem supplies the support inequality;
no claim that (6)'s positive-height sign proof covers that other component.
