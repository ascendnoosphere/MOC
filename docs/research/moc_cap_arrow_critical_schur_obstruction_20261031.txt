1. The proposed common completion

Use the canonical features F=(f34,r_j:j in Z), with
  Z=(0,7,10,13,17,18,20,22,23,27,28,30,31),
and cost g=lambda*f34+sum beta_j*r_j. Let p=beta18,q=beta23,r=beta28
and S=p+q+r. The ordinary Gram diagonal is necessarily beta_j: at each
of the13 zeros of f34, exactly one of these13 ordinary features is1.

Allow arbitrary f34-to-ordinary couplings and an arbitrary critical3x3
block on(r18,r23,r28), while setting all other ordinary cross terms to
zero. This includes couplings from f34 to all six directed features,
not merely the eight-feature subsystem considered previously.

The resulting17 unknown Gram entries obey11 independent interpolation
constraints. ALL solutions have the following six-parameter form:
  parameters a,x,z,s,c12,c13;
  G_ff=k=lambda-2(a+x+z+s);
  G_f,j=a,                         j=0,7,10,13;
  G_f,17=G_f,20=x;
  G_f,22=G_f,27=z;
  G_f,30=G_f,31=s;
  G_f,18=-a+x+s;
  G_f,23=-a+x+z;
  G_f,28=-a+z+s.
The critical block C has diagonal(p,q,r) and
  C12=c12, C13=c13, C23=2a-lambda-c12-c13.
All other off-diagonal entries vanish. Exact coefficient comparison
checks diag(F G F^T)=g at all24 permutations. The rank11 constraint

2. Explicit Schur reduction

Let O be the ten noncritical ordinary coordinates and u their displayed
couplings to f34. For positive beta_j on O, eliminate their diagonal
block. The entire Gram is PSD precisely when the4x4 matrix

  [ kappa                  (-a+x+s,-a+x+z,-a+z+s) ]
  [ (-a+x+s,-a+x+z,-a+z+s)^T          C          ]

is PSD, where
  kappa=lambda-2(a+x+z+s)-sum_(j in O)u_j^2/beta_j.
The verifier checks the exact triangular congruence, not just equality
of determinants. At a zero ordinary budget, PSD requires its coupling
to vanish, after which that coordinate can be deleted.

3. A fixed rank-two obstruction to the WHOLE pencil

If the Gram were PSD, write Fvec for its mixed-feature vector and
P,Q,R for the three critical feature vectors. The interpolation
parameterization gives the exact identity

  4||P+Q+R||^2 + ||2Fvec+P+Q+R||^2 = 5S-6lambda.    (*)

Indeed ||P+Q+R||^2=S+4a-2lambda and the second squared norm is
S+2lambda-16a. Therefore PSD requires S>=6lambda/5, independent of
all ten other budgets and of how the six free parameters depend on them.
This obstruction also applies on zero-budget boundary faces.

5. An exact diagnostic for missing Gram couplings

The integer cost functional
  y=(-4,5,2,-4,-4,5,5,0,0,8,2,-4,
     0,2,8,0,-4,5,8,-4,0,2,5,0)
satisfies y.g=5S-6lambda. In canonical feature coordinates let
  Y=4ww^T+vv^T,
where w is1 on the three critical coordinates and0 elsewhere, and
v=w+2e_f. Then
  F^T diag(y) F = Y+E.
Here E vanishes on every entry permitted in the six-parameter pattern.
Its48 nonzero off-diagonal ordinary edges are listed exactly in the
certificate. For ANY full Gram giving g,
  5S-6lambda=tr(YG)+tr(EG).
The first term is nonnegative for PSD G. Consequently low-S actual
costs must acquire a negative contribution from the excluded ordinary
cross terms. This is a concrete budget-transfer requirement for the
full completion problem; it is not a PSD construction or a dual
separator for the full intrinsic matching-SOS cone.
