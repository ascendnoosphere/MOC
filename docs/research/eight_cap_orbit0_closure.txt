Full five-coefficient eight-contact cap [0,7,10,17,28,34]
======================================================

Claim and scope
---------------
Let g=a r0+b r7+c r10+d r17+e r28+f f34, with all coefficients
nonnegative. The desired support inequality holds for every actual gap
in this cone, including unequal ordinary coefficients. This is orbit0
of the 58 marked canonical eight-contact cap types in
verify_eight_uniform_profiles.py; its stabilizer orbit has 12 patterns.
It is not the earlier nine-contact face12, and does not close all58
unequal-coefficient types.

For f=0 the function is ordinary and nonnegative. Normalize f=1 otherwise.
Use the checked cubic F and e0=ones(14), and write Q=D_e0 F. The ray data
and model are reconstructed independently in
code/research/verify_eight_cap_orbit0.py. For clarity below e is the
coefficient of r28, not the vector e0.

Model and exact pencil
----------------------
Put B=b+1,C=c+1,A=a+d+1 and
 Q=de-d-1,
 U=BC+e(b+c+1-d),
 V=A U+e(d+1)^2,
 P=A U+de(d+e+1).
Then F(g)=-BC A<0. Thus F=0 and F=Q=0 are impossible in this normalized
cone. A positive spectral height h=F/Q requires Q<0. Negative height is
covered by the established negative-height argument.
The exact pencil identity is
 det[H_F(g)-t H_F(e0)]
 =4t(a+1)^2 BC [BCde(d+e+1)-t Q U][eBC A-t V].
At t=h=F/Q the first bracket is BC P and the second is BC A P/Q.
Consequently
 det H_F(g-he0)=-4 (a+1)^2 B^4 C^4 A^2 P^2/Q^2.
Equivalently the projected nonic is
 N=(a+1)B^2 C^2 A P.
An actual real-gradient lift makes H_F(x) singular. Indeed, if y!=0,
H_F(x)y=0 supplies a kernel; if y=0, gradF(x)=0 gives H_F(x)x=0, with
x!=0 since F(g)<0 excludes g=h e0. Therefore P=0 is necessary.

The branch and its connectedness
--------------------------------
Expanding P as a polynomial in a gives
 P=a U+(d+1)[BC+e(b+c+1)]+de^2.
Its constant coefficient is strictly positive, so a>=0 and P=0 imply
U<0 and hence d,e>0. The unique a is
 a=[(d+1){BC+e(b+c+1)}+de^2]/[de-BC-e(b+c+1)]>0.
For fixed b,c>=0 put D0=b+c+1+BC/e. The branch is exactly the graph of
this a over
 b,c>=0, e>0, d>D0, Q<0.
For e<=1 there is no upper d bound; for e>1 the last condition is
 d<1/(e-1).
For each b,c the feasible e range is (0,E(b,c)), where E>1 is the unique
root of (e-1)D0(e)=1. Its derivative for e>1 is
 b+c+1+BC/e^2>0, so the root exists uniquely and varies continuously.
The (b,c,e) base is path connected: lower e to1/2, vary b,c, then raise
e to the target value. These paths are feasible since E>1 everywhere.
The d fibers have continuous bounds and the continuous section
 m=max(e-1,0), d*=D0+(1-mD0)/(1+m).
It lies strictly inside every fiber. Connect each point to this section,
travel along the section, and return within the destination fiber.
Thus the full branch, including b=0 and c=0, is path connected. An
independent construction is in eight12_branch_connectedness.txt.

Everywhere-valid Schur rank certificate
---------------------------------------
Let L(z) be the four coordinates B00,B01,B10,B11 used by the exact model
for Q(z)=B00 B11-B01 B10. The verifier gives the explicit integral change
of basis T obtained by adjoining L to ten complementary standard rows;
det(T)^2=1. In this basis H(e0) is zero in its first ten rows and columns.
Let J be the leading9 block of T^T[H(g)-tH(e0)]T. Direct exact calculation
gives
 det J=2(a+1)^2 BC U V.
On P=0 we have V=P-eQ=-eQ>0, while U<0; hence J is invertible at EVERY
branch point. The 5-dimensional Schur complement S has upper block
 A2=[[0,-e],[-e,0]],
and off-diagonal block
 B2=[[-(d+1),e-1,1],[0,0,-h]].
The verifier checks all15 bordered10-minor identities determining S.
Its further Schur complement is exactly
 R3=BC/(2e Q U) w w^T,
 w=((d+1)(d+e+1), -(de-d-e-1), de-d-e-1).
Since e>0, Q<0, U<0 and the first entry of w is positive, R3 has rank1
everywhere. A2 has rank2, so the Hessian rank is9+2+1=12 everywhere.
There are no omitted charts or exceptional rank-drop loci.

For independent reproduction of the full pencil identity, the verifier
uses the same bordered10 minors and clears their common denominator2UV.
After eliminating A2, its3-by-3 determinant is computed by six products
in the exact multivariate polynomial ring. This avoids a slow generic
14-by-14 expansion and verifies the displayed pencil coefficientwise.
No cached matrix or floating-point computation enters this verifier.

Short exclusion using the reusable actual-pencil theorem
---------------------------------------------------------
The independently audited actual-pencil theorem in actual_pencil_sign.txt
states that [z^2]det(H_F(x)+zH_F(e0))<=0 at every actual spectral x.
On the P=0 branch, the pencil is proportional to t(t-h)^2, and the
coefficient at x=g-he0 is p'(0)/h>0, where
 p'(0)=4de^2(a+1)^2 B^3 C^3 A(d+e+1).
This contradiction already excludes the full branch, without using any
rank or connectedness calculation. The following independent argument
uses only the exact cubic identities and real-gradient lift equations;
it does not require the reusable theorem.

Kernel Q positivity and independent exclusion
---------------------------------------------
At a branch point the two bracketed pencil factors vanish at t=h>0.
The derivative of the whole pencil at t=0 is
 p'(0)=4de^2(a+1)^2 B^3 C^3 A(d+e+1)>0.
Since p(t) is proportional to t(t-h)^2, the coefficient of z^2 in
 det[H_F(x)+z H_F(e0)] is p'(0)/h>0. With rank H_F(x)=12 already proved,
this means H_F(e0) restricted to its2-dimensional kernel is nonsingular.
The kernel bundle and restricted quadratic vary continuously on the
connected branch, so their inertia is constant.
At the exact interior reference
 (a,b,c,d,e)=(71,1,1,8,1), F=-320,Q=-1,h=320,
the verifier computes the complete Hessian kernel and obtains
 Q|ker H_F(x)=[[9/2164820,-31/86592800],
               [-31/86592800,60561/13854848000]].
Its first entry is positive and its determinant is1/55419392000>0.
Thus Q is positive definite on the kernel throughout the branch.
But any actual real-gradient lift would satisfy
 H_F(x)y=0, gradF(y)=gradF(x),
and hence Q(y)=Q(x)=Q(g)<0. This contradicts kernel positivity and excludes
all potentially violating positive-height lifts in this full cone.
