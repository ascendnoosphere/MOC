At a generic smooth x in S, H_x has rank12. This is independently checked

    K^T H_v K=0 for every v in T_x S.

every k in K. Also H_k x=H_x k=0 and x!=0; hence det H_k=0 and k lies
in S. Thus the full projective kernel line P(K) lies in S.

Conversely q(x) is itself normal to S, because S lies in {F=0}; its

    S^* = N8'.

3. Complete SL4 chart audit for a single contact

The SL4 invariant singleton matrix for two four-column groups R,C is

    P=(adj(R)C) Hadamard (adj(C)R)^T.

Each row/column sum is det(R)det(C). If R is invertible, T=R^-1 C
identifies all common-minor coordinates with signed Leibniz amplitudes
c_pi(T)=sign(pi) product_i T_i,pi(i), up one overall scalar. The height
condition n.e=0 says det T=0.

Strict positivity of every off-diagonal singleton forces rank T=3 and
full support of its left/right nullvectors. If n=lambda c(T) and
adj T=u v^T, set S=lambda diag(v) T diag(u). Then S=P is real, balanced,
and has strictly positive off-diagonal entries. Its diagonal is minus
the row outdegree. Adjugate covariance gives c(S)=kappa n, with
kappa<0 by the strictly positive Markov cofactor sign. Thus

    n=-positive_scalar c(S).

If C is invertible use the transposed chart. If both groups have rank3,
their adjugates factor and P=a b^T has rank<=1 and zero row/column sums.
Such a real matrix cannot have all off-diagonal entries positive in
dimension4: P00 P21=P01 P20 forces P00>0, contradicting its zero row
sum. If either group has rank<=2, P=0. Thus no chart exception remains.
