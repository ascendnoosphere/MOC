What Lean proves

The scalar ring R is any commutative ring. Two arbitrary ten-entry arrays
b,c define symmetric four-by-four matrices B,C. Eight explicit amplitude
vectors, with coordinates given by actual Fin2/Fin3 matrix determinants,
implement the relevant even-Clifford coordinates. Their grade-four
coordinates use c in place of b, representing the top-grade conjugation.
Formal conjugation of a vector interchanges b and c.

For all nine base subset-pair types in the general kernel proof, Lean
checks the difference between the actual selected-row matrix determinant
and the eight-coordinate Gram entry. Six differences vanish freely.
The other three equal exactly the stated multiples of entries of BC-CB.
The four-by-four determinant expansion used for the two full-row types
is itself proved in this module from Matrix.det_succ_row_zero.
The polynomial identities are certified by ring after finite-sum and
small-determinant expansions; no Python algebra result is trusted.

The theorem nine_base_cases_of_commute combines all nine identities into
their simultaneous determinant/Gram equality under the single hypothesis
B*C=C*B. In particular no invertibility, nonzero spectral differences,
or numerical tolerance occurs in these finite identities.

For arbitrary finite complex amplitude arrays W, gram_quadratic expands
the associated quadratic form as a finite sum of squared amplitudes.
gram_quadratic_nonnegative proves its real part nonnegative.
positive_of_eight_amplitudes supplies the corresponding client for an
eight-column Gram factorization of any finite kernel.

Scope not formalized here

Lean does not yet identify these eight vectors with a general finite-set
Clifford construction, prove the simultaneous-permutation orbit reduction,
prove Jacobi's complementary-minor step, pass to singular spectra by
continuity, or derive the selected-row determinant from the original
orthogonal signed permutation weights. Those parts are supplied by the
separate mathematical proof in
docs/research/moc_fixed_b_clifford_kernel_20261031.txt and its exact audit.
They are not assumed in this Lean module as axioms.

The generic Gram client takes the factorization as an explicit hypothesis.
It does not assert that the original MOC support gap admits a positive
representation, and this module does not prove unrestricted MOC.
