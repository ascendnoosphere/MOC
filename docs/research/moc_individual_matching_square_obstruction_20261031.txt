Status: exact global obstruction to a restricted REAL-square construction.
This is not an obstruction to full matching-SOS, which succeeds on the
example. Complex modulus squares can hide cancellation between their real
and imaginary parts and are not excluded by this statement.

Let V be the full real14-dimensional matching function space. Consider
the smaller cone generated by real h^2 such that BOTH h and h^2 lie in V.
This forbids nonmatching components from canceling between different real
squares. The cone is strictly too small for actual spectral support costs.

Use the exact finite distinct-spectrum example

Its24 support gaps are exactly
 g=f34+r23+r28=f34^2+(r23-r28)^2.
The eleven zeros are C={1,6,7,8,12,15,17,18,20,22,23}.

Every real square in ANY representation of g must vanish at C. The matching
subspace vanishing at C has dimension3 and basis(f34,r23,r28). Put
f=f34,r=r23,s=r28 and write an arbitrary possible factor as af+br+cs.
With O the orthogonal projector onto V-perp, exact rational linear algebra
gives independence of the three columns
 O(rs),O(fr),O(fs),
and identities O(f^2)=2O(rs), O(r^2)=O(s^2)=0. Hence
 O[(af+br+cs)^2]
   =2[(a^2+bc)O(rs)+ab O(fr)+ac O(fs)].
Thus this individual real square lies in V exactly when
 ab=ac=0, a^2+bc=0.
These equations force a=0 and bc=0. The only nonzero possibilities are
multiples of r23 or r28. Positive sums of their squares have zero mixed
cap coefficient and cannot equal g, whose coefficient on f34 is1.

The full two-square certificate works precisely because

 O[(r23-r28)^2]=-2O(delta5).
Their forbidden components cancel. Equivalently one can write
