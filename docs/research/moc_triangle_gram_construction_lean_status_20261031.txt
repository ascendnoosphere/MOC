EXACT RESULT
For arbitrary real a,b,c >= 0 and t > 0, define

  D(a,b,c) = a+b+c
    - max(0, 2 max(sqrt(a),sqrt(b),sqrt(c))
                 -sqrt(a)-sqrt(b)-sqrt(c))^2.

If 2t <= D(a,b,c), diagonal_budget_factor_exists constructs a factor

  W : (Fin 2 + Fin 3) -> Fin 3 -> R

whose Gram B has diagonal (a,b,c) and B01+B02+B12=-t. The factor has five
rows. This is a sufficient criterion proved constructively from explicit
formulas; the module does not currently prove the converse criterion.
The normalized form t=1 is exactly D(beta0,beta1,beta2) >= 2.

CONSTRUCTION CHECKED
1. diagonalFactor uses one sqrt(v_i) per diagonal row.
2. mixedFactor concatenates sqrt(theta) W with sqrt(1-theta) diagonalFactor.
   Its Gram is theta Gram(W) + (1-theta) diag(v).
3. Signed length case: p=(x,y,-z), rank-one Gram p p^T; its upper off-diagonal
   sum is -(2z(x+y)-2xy)/2. Mix with theta=2t/(2z(x+y)-2xy).
4. Triangle case: for a=x^2, b,c and nonzero x, use the two rows

   [x, (c-x^2-b)/(2x), (b-x^2-c)/(2x)]
   [0, sqrt(H)/(2x), -sqrt(H)/(2x)]

   where H=2x^2 b+2x^2 c+2bc-x^4-b^2-c^2.
   The diagonal is (x^2,b,c), off-diagonal sum=-(x^2+b+c)/2.
   Mix with theta=2t/(x^2+b+c).
5. For nonnegative lengths x,y,z with z largest and z<=x+y, H factors as
   (x+y+z)(x+y-z)(x-y+z)(-x+y+z), so is nonnegative. The x=0 case is handled
   separately and reduces to y=z. The z>x+y case uses the signed rank-one
   factor. All three choices of the largest coordinate are covered exactly.
