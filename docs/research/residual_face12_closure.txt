EXACT CLOSURE OF THE MOC4 FACE (3,12,21,30,53)
2026-10-30

Scope: the whole displayed coefficient face and its coefficient boundaries,
not unrestricted complex MOC4. The standalone verifier is
code/research/verify_residual_face_12.py. It recomputes the model, rays,
determinants and bordered minors from repository sources and reads no
generated build files. The sign and connectedness arguments below are
analytic arguments, supported by exact symbolic identities.

Use one-based indices and rho(I,J)(U)=|det U[I,J]|^2. The canonical labels are

 r3=rho(1,4), r12=rho(4,1), r21=rho(12,34), r30=rho(14,14),
 f53=rho(2,2)+rho(3,3)+rho(12,34)-rho(13,13).

Suppose the 24 values of an actual spectral support gap
 g_pi=h-Re(eta product_i(alpha_i+beta_pi(i)))
have the nonnegative coefficient representation

            g=a r3+b r12+c r21+d r30+f53,   a,b,c,d>=0.

A positive mixed coefficient is normalized to one by positive scaling of
g,eta,h. We prove that no such actual gap has h>0, that h=0 is impossible,
and that the support inequality for h<0 follows from the imported external
half-plane theorem. A zero mixed coefficient is already a nonnegative sum
of ordinary squared minors, including the zero-vector boundary.

Imported spectral background
-----------------------------
Let F be the cubic in code/geometry/closing_moc4_model_verify.py, e=ones(14),
Q=D_e F and H=H_F. The exact model checks grad F(z)=0 on all actual complex
spectral matching vectors and H(e)e=0. For x=g-he and real y the imaginary
part of eta z, expansion gives

                H(x)y=0,       grad F(y)=grad F(x).

Euler's identity yields 3F(x)=x.grad F(y)=y^T H(x)y/2=0. Therefore
F(g)=hQ(g) and Q(x)=Q(g). Moreover H(x) is singular: this is immediate if
y!=0, while y=0 implies H(x)x=2grad F(x)=0. In the latter case x!=0,
since x=0 would force F(g)=h^3F(e)=0, contrary to the strict formula below.
These are necessary conditions only; no converse realization is asserted.

The main real-variable statement is this: for a,b,c,d>=0, h>0,
h=F(g)/Q(g), and det H(x)=0, the quadratic Q restricted to ker H(x) is
positive semidefinite. Its proof covers every exceptional rank chart.
Since Q(g)<0, the necessary real lift is then impossible.

1. Determinant reduction

Put A=a+1, B=b+1, p=AB, s=A+B. Exact calculation gives
F(g)=-p-c<0, Q(g)=cd-c-1. Thus h>0 implies q=-Q=1+c-cd>0.
Put
W=ab c-ab-a-b-c-1,
U=-ab c+ab-a cd+ad+a-b cd+bd+b+2cd+c+2d+1,
T=3cd+c+d,
L(t)=dW+tU, S(t)=L(t)-tT+cd,
R(t)=(tQ-F)S(t)-AB L(t).
The pencil identity is
 det[H(g)-tH(e)]=4AB L(t)R(t).
L is linear and R is quadratic. Define P=Q L(F/Q). Then
 R(F/Q)=-AB P/Q,
 det H(x)=-4 A^2 B^2 P^2/Q^2.
Hence P=0 is necessary. Its compact form is
P=(c-1)p^2+[c(d-1)-d]ps
  +[c^2(d^2-d+1)-c(d^2+4d+1)+d]p
  -c^2(d-1)^2 s-4c^2d.
P is irreducible over the rationals.

For 0<=c<=1, its three quadratic Bernstein coefficients in c are all
negative. The first is -p[p+d(s-1)], the middle is
-p[p+s+d(s+2)+d^2+1]/2, and P(1) is strictly negative by its expansion
in a,b,d (all coefficients negative). Therefore c>1 on the dangerous
branch. The condition q>0 says 0<=d<1+1/c.

2. The real branch and connectedness

Let v=(c-1)s-4c, k=c-d(c-1), K=hk-cd. The conditions above imply k>0.
Also h=(p+c)/q>d, since
p+c-dq>=1+c(d^2-d+1)-d>0.
The equation L(h)=0 gives
(h-d)[(c-1)p-cs]=-hd v.
It follows that
(A-B)^2=v*[v+4c+4hd(c-1)/(h-d)]/(c-1)^2.
The bracket is positive, as v+4c=(c-1)s>0. Thus v>=0.
If K<=0, then p=hq-c<=cdq/k-c=-c^2(d-1)^2/k<=0,
which is impossible. Hence K>0. Define
h0=c(c+1)^2/[(c-1)^2 q].
Solving for p,s gives
p=hq-c,
v=(h-d)(c-1)^2 q(h-h0)/K,
s=(v+4c)/(c-1).
Thus h>=h0. Equality holds exactly at
 a=b=(c+1)/(c-1).
For h>h0, the two choices of sign A-B specify the two branches.
The condition a,b>=0 is exactly
ab=h{q[1-d(c-1)]h+cd(c+d+1)}/K>=0.
Consequently each c>1 and 0<=d<1+1/c admits the interval
h0<=h<infinity if d(c-1)<=1, and
h0<=h<=hmax=cd(c+d+1)/[q(d(c-1)-1)] otherwise.
At h0 the product ab=(c+1)^2/(c-1)^2>0, so hmax>h0.
The upper endpoint is continuous and tends to infinity at the transition.
Conversely, these intervals introduce no extra real points: K is positive
from h0 onward, p>0, and s>=4c/(c-1)>2. The displayed discriminant identity
then gives two positive real roots A,B; ab>=0 and s>2 force both A,B>=1.
The diagonal a=b=(c+1)/(c-1) is an actual coefficient point of P=0 for every
base pair (c,d), so the earlier h>d bound also applies at h0. Thus this
parameter description omits no admissibility condition or component.
With d>0 and h>h0, the open interiors of the two sign branches are each
path connected: the base (c,d) is path connected, and on any compact base
path one can choose a continuous h strictly between its lower and upper
bounds. Boundary points a=0 or b=0 are approached from the same interior.

3. Nonzero pencil coefficient off the exceptional sets

On P=0,
U=d^2 v/(h-d)=d^2(c-1)^2 q(h-h0)/K.
Thus U>0 when d>0 and h>h0. Differentiating R at t=h gives
R'(h)=q(hT-cd)-pU.
This is strictly positive everywhere on the branch. Here is a direct
proof. N=K R'(h) is quadratic in h. At h0, N>0, because
 K(h0)=c^2[cd-c-d-1]^2/[(c-1)^2 q]>0,
 R'(h0)=c^2 J/(c-1)^2>0,
 J=(c-1)^2 d^2+2(c+1)(c+3)d+(c+1)^2.
Moreover
 N'(h0)=2c^2 T[cd-c-d-1]^2/(c-1)^2>0.
Its quadratic coefficient is q C2, with
 C2=kT-d^2(c-1)^2 q.
If C2>=0, N is increasing and positive for h>=h0. If C2<0, put
r=d(c-1). Necessarily r>1, since for 0<=r<=1,
 C2=c/(c-1)*[c(c-1)+cr(2-r)+r(r-1)(r-2)]>0.
Thus h has the finite upper endpoint hmax. At that endpoint ab=0, the
constant coefficient R(0)=ab c^2d vanishes, and the positive root h obeys
 R'(h)=-R_1=c(a+b+1)d(c+d+1)>0.
Concavity of N and positivity at both endpoints prove the assertion.

The coefficient of z^2 in det[H(x)+zH(e)] is therefore
 4AB U R'(h)>0
when d>0 and h>h0.

4. Exact Hessian ranks

The verifier computes three bordered 13-by-13 minors, with common
principal block I={0,...,10,12} and border indices 11,13. After t=F/Q,
all three numerators are divisible by P. At (a,b,c,d)=(0,5,6,1), h=12,
the determinant of block I is 36578304. Irreducibility of P now lets us
work in the fraction field of Q[a,b,c,d]/(P). This block is invertible,
and the three bordered identities say its symmetric Schur complement
is zero. Thus rank is at most12 over that field, so every 13-minor
vanishes modulo P. This gives rank<=12 at every real point P=0.
The nonzero z^2 coefficient from section3 gives rank>=12 off d=0 and
the diagonal. Hence the rank is exactly12 there.

On the diagonal, delete rows/columns7,13. The exact principal12 minor is
 4 c^5(c+1)^4 D1 D2^2 /[(c-1)^12 q^4],
where
 D1=c(c-1)^2 d^2+2(c^2-1)d+c(c+1)^2>0,
 D2=c(c-1)^2 d^2-(c-1)^2(c+1)d+(c+1)^2(2c+1)>0.
For D2, the bound d<1+1/c gives
D2>(c+1)^2[c+3-1/c]>0. Thus rank12 holds on the diagonal.

When d=0, P=0 gives c=AB/(ab-1), with ab>1. Deleting rows/columns11,13
gives the principal12 minor
 a^4 b^2(a+1)^4(a-b)^2(b+1)^4(3ab^2+3ab+a+b^2)^2
 /[(ab-1)^2(2ab+a+b)^4].
It is nonzero off a=b. The remaining diagonal is already covered.
Thus rank H(x)=12 throughout the entire dangerous branch.

5. Inertia and exclusion

For a real symmetric pencil with rank H(x)=12, the nonzero z^2
coefficient is the product of an invertible12-block determinant and
the determinant of H(e) restricted to the two-dimensional kernel.
Hence that restricted quadratic form is nonsingular on each connected
open branch in section2. Its inertia is constant there.

At (a,b,c,d)=(0,5,6,1), h=12, the exact Gram matrix of Q on the kernel
in the basis returned by SymPy is
 [[1/144,-1/126],[-1/126,1/63]], determinant1/21168>0.
At the reflected point (5,0,6,1), it is
 [[1/144,-2/267],[-2/267,112/7921]], determinant1/23763>0.
Both are positive definite. Rank12 and the nonzero pencil coefficient
hold at these boundary references, so positivity transfers to nearby
interior points in the respective sign branches. Connectedness gives
positive definiteness on both open branches.

Every remaining point (d=0, the diagonal, a=0 or b=0) is a limit of these
open branches. The Hessian rank stays12 everywhere by section4, so its
kernel varies continuously. Positive semidefiniteness of Q on the kernel
therefore extends to every such point. Since Q(x)=Q(g)=-q<0, the equation
Q(y)=Q(x) is impossible for y in ker H(x), as claimed.

6. Zero and negative heights; completion of the face

The formula F(g)=-AB-c<0 excludes h=0 by F(g)=hQ(g). For h<0, the closed
half-plane {w:Re(eta w)<=h} excludes zero and contains every permutation
vertex. Drury's external-half-plane result places every unitary-orbit
determinant in the same half-plane, giving the support inequality.

The imported source is S. W. Drury, The external vertices conjecture in
case n=4, Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra 11 (2004), 180--191,
Theorem 1.1 and the half-plane assertion in the proof of Corollary 1.2,
page 181:
 https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/263/263/263

The paper uses a subtraction convention, absorbed by negating the second
spectrum. This external theorem is distinct from the positive-height
kernel argument. No claim about the negative-height component is inferred
from the connectedness argument in section 2.

For a zero f53 coefficient, the support gap is an ordinary nonnegative
minor sum. For a positive coefficient, positive normalization preserves
the sign of h. Thus the argument proves the support inequality on the
entire coefficient face and its boundaries, including row/column/inversion
images. The other residual face types and unrestricted complex MOC4 remain
separate targets. No simple-spectrum or distinct-product assumption is used.

Independent audit

The promotion independently checked the Bernstein coefficients, the bounds
h>d and k>0, the endpoint value of R'(h), the finite-endpoint signs, and the
lower bound for D2. The generic-rank argument uses irreducibility and a
nonzero exact block; the exceptional minors separately supply rank12 on the
diagonal and d=0. Consequently the limiting-kernel argument does not assume
rank constancy where it has not been proved. The two exact positive reference
forms propagate on the two connected open sign branches, and only positive
semidefiniteness is asserted on their exceptional boundaries.
