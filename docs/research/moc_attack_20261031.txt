MOC RESEARCH RESULT, 31 OCTOBER 2026

Unrestricted complex MOC4 remains unproved. This pass produced a complete
proof for a previously open five-parameter family, checked Lean theorems,
a reproducible dataset covering every dimension 1 through 1000, and an
exact obstruction to a promising variational strategy. The proof,
formalization, and experiments have different scopes, specified below.

The target throughout is

 det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*) in conv_R{product_i(a_i+b_(pi i)): pi in S_n}

for arbitrary a,b in C^n and unitary U. Finite data cannot cover all such
inputs. No numerical search here is a proof of this target.

One full four-dimensional family is now closed

The canonical family is

 g=f34+a r0+b r7+c r10+d r18+e r22,  a,b,c,d,e>=0.

Here g is a spectral support deficit in the repository's exact 14-coordinate
model; r_j are the canonical ordinary-minor rays and f34 its mixed ray.
The conclusion concerns actual spectral deficits, not arbitrary coefficient
vectors with the same form. Equal a=b and the boundary d=0 were previously
proved. The new argument closes the entire unequal d>0 branch.

Its decisive identity replaces a large polynomial P by a shifted quadratic:

 P=e^2 A(w),  w=c+p(c+1)/e,  p=(a+1)(b+1),
 A(w)=q w^2-p T w-p S,
 q=p+d(a+b+1),
 T=ab+d(a+b)-d^2-2d-2,
 S=(ab-1)(d^2+1)-(a-1)(b-1)d.

The identities A(ab-1)=-ab d(a-b)^2 and
S=(ab-1)(d-1)^2+d(p-4) force a unique simple positive root. It supplies
paths to c=0,d=1 and then to one of two rational anchors. A nonvanishing
Hessian-pencil coefficient is positive at those anchors and hence along
every path, contradicting its necessary nonpositive sign for actual spectra.
This is a global sign argument on the whole family, not repeated subdivision.

The full determinant factorization passed all 38,760 exact nodes of a
degree-14 unisolvent grid. The grid proves a polynomial identity; it is
not sampling of the positivity assertion. Both old boundary verifiers
were replayed. An independent agent checked the analytic proof, including
zero-coordinate boundaries, and independently replayed the exact grid.

Lean: precise target and checked progress

code/lean/MocUnrestricted.lean defines the exact unrestricted target for
every finite dimension, with no reality, distinctness, nonzero, genericity,
or circle assumptions. It proves dimensions 0 and 1, and permutation,
monomial, diagonal and commuting-mixer cases in every dimension.

code/lean/MocTwo.lean proves MOC.conjecture_two : MOC.Conjecture 2.
For arbitrary complex spectra and arbitrary unitary U, it establishes

 det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*) = t z_identity+(1-t) z_swap,
 t=|U00|^2,  0<=t<=1,

and then proves real convex-hull membership. This is a full unrestricted
dimension-two theorem, used as a verified foundation rather than claimed
as a new mathematical result.

code/lean/Moc0710Core.lean contains 37 compiler-checked theorems for the
new family. These include the shifted quadratic, positive-root existence,
uniqueness and continuity, complete c-path preservation, d-path inequalities,
affine endpoint safety and intermediate-value sign transport. Lean errors
were repaired against the explicit formulas rather than by weakening the
statements. No sorry/admit or custom axiom remains in these files. Axiom
reports show only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

The scalar d-path assembly and continuous concatenation are not complete
in Lean. The spectral cubic, Hessian factorization, actual-spectrum sign
condition, nondegeneracy certificates and boundary theorems also need
formal bridges. Thus neither the complete family theorem nor unrestricted
MOC4 is currently a Lean theorem. The mathematical unrestricted gap is
larger than these formalization obligations.

What the data actually taught us

1. Thin hulls create misleadingly small raw margins. Among n=4 Haar draws,
the median Gaussian-to-anisotropic margin ratio is about 23,000 before
affine whitening and about 1.15 afterward. Hull membership is invariant
under invertible affine changes of coordinates. The contrast survives
restrictive condition-number filtering, so the original raw-margin signal
largely measures flattening. Clustered spectra remain closer to the hull
boundary after conditioning; they are a more useful stress family here.

2. Large generic dimensions become easy for a precise reason. For independent
centered variance-one spectral entries and independent Haar U, we derived
and independently checked the exact identities

 E|det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*)|^2=(n+2)(n+3)/6,
 E|z_pi|^2=2^n.

The proof first establishes, for fixed U,
 E|det|^2=sum_(k,I,J) |det U[I,J]|^4,
and then computes Haar minor fourth moments. At n=1000 the ratio of root
mean squares is about 1.25e-148. This is an ensemble ratio, not an expected
pointwise ratio, a typical-case theorem or a convex-hull theorem.

For a single uniformly oriented complex Householder reflection the exact
second moment instead equals

 2^n (28n-4)/[(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)].

Its suppression is polynomial. The difference quantifies how strongly the
mixer distribution can bias a broad numerical campaign. A separate audited
argument proves determinant/natural-product ->0 for a fixed number of
random reflections and iid Gaussian or disk spectra. None of these facts
settles angular hull containment or worst-case MOC.

3. Extreme products can destroy floating-point hull geometry. A single
global scale initially made 5,260 structured hulls appear degenerate.
Bounded log-radius subsets recovered 5,216 numerical inclusion witnesses.
The final records retain both classifications and their different scales.

4. Pair rotations are useful optimizers but do not supply a proof. The exact
SU(2) pair formula is c+A cos(2t)+(Bx cos(phi)+By sin(phi))sin(2t), so four
determinants determine the exact pair maximum. It repaired all 45 saved
nonmonomial stationarity candidates in this campaign. However, exact
suboptimal permutation local maxima already prevent global convergence.
A new dense nonmonomial example is stronger: every pair fiber is maximized,
the full Hessian is negative semidefinite, and a coupled direction still
increases the objective at cubic order. For
a=(1,-1,-3i,3i), b=(-3,3,-i,i), support multiplier i and a dense Walsh U,
the support starts at 24. An explicit skew matrix K with K^2=-3I gives

 F((cI+sK)U)=24+384 c s^3,  c^2+3s^2=1.

The tangent Hessian has eigenvalues -96 (six times) and zero (six times),
but along exp(tK) the third derivative is 2304>0. At c=s=1/2 the path
reaches a signed permutation with support 48. The original determinant
has an exact strictly positive triangle representation inside the MOC
hull. This is an obstruction to a proof strategy, not to MOC itself.
The construction extends to every r>1 by replacing 3 with r in the spectra.
Writing d=r(r^2-1), every pair gap is d, the Hessian is -2d times a sum
of six squares, and the coupled support is d(1+16cs^3). Exact checks at
five r-values prove these identities because their r-degree is at most four.
An independent audit confirmed that polynomial degree argument and the
full sixteen-coordinate Hessian, including its diagonal gauge directions.
