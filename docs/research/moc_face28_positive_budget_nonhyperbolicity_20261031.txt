The full orbit28 nonic has nonreal roots on a strictly positive budget ray
31 October 2026

This exactly rules out a definite symmetric/Hermitian Lax pencil for the
nonic whose positive cone contains the ordinary positive-budget cone.
It does not rule out an indefinite determinantal pencil, a rational Schur
representation, or a full matching-SOS Gram construction.

Take the full5778-term degree-nine polynomial in
build/moc_face_03717_full_nonic/full_face_nonic.json. Its mixed f34
coefficient is fixed at1 and its ordinary budget order is

 (r7,r13,r17,r18,r20,r22,r27,r28,r31).

Substitute the SAME variable s into all nine budgets. Exact summation
of every term gives

 P(s)=240s^9+1232s^8+2748s^7+3608s^6+3052s^5+1700s^4
      +617s^3+140s^2+18s+1

     =(2s+1)^2(3s+1)(2s^2+4s+1)
                 *(10s^4+18s^3+17s^2+7s+1).

The quartic is strictly positive for every real s, by the exact identity

 10s^4+18s^3+17s^2+7s+1
   =10(s^2+9s/10+1/5)^2 +(49/10)(s+17/49)^2 +1/98.

Thus the quartic contributes four nonreal roots. The remaining factors
contribute five real roots with multiplicity: -1/2 twice, -1/3 once,
and the two distinct roots of2s^2+4s+1. These do not coincide.
No floating root approximation is needed for this conclusion.

Consequences for a Lax/PSD representation

Let N(t,v) denote the homogeneous degree-nine polynomial with mixed
coefficient t. The ordinary direction

                         e=(t=0,v=(1,...,1))

has N(e)=240!=0. Since P(s)=N(f+s e) has nonreal roots, N is not
hyperbolic in this strictly positive ordinary-budget direction.

Suppose, contrary to this obstruction, that on the full face

 N(t,v)=kappa det(t A_f+sum_j v_j A_j), kappa!=0,

with Hermitian9-by9 matrices, and that the ordinary direction has a
positive semidefinite pencil matrix B=sum_j A_j. Its determinant is
nonzero because N(e)!=0, so B is positive definite. Every root of
det(A_f+s B) must then be real: if (A_f+zB)u=0 for nonzero complex u,
then u* A_f u+z u* B u=0, with the first term real and the second
coefficient strictly positive real. Hence Im(z)=0. This contradicts
the exact quartic factor above.

In particular no such representation can assign a positive semidefinite
matrix to every ordinary budget generator. The necessary-condition test
has failed, so searching for that definite Lax cone should be abandoned.
An indefinite symmetric pencil can still exist and is being constructed
independently through the inverse-K quadratic form.
