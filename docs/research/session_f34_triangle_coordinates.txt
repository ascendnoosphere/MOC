Exact gap coordinates for the f34 cap and triangle capacity

Use lexicographic permutation indices and let g_i be the support gap
at index i. The 14 vectors f34 and the ordinary rays listed below
form a basis for the 14-dimensional permutation-product space. The
ordinary coefficient in this cap is the indicated individual gap:

  ray    0  7 10 13 17 18 20 22 23 27 28 30 31
  gap    1 17  6 22 12 18 20  8 14 15  9 23  7

Indeed these are exactly the 13 zeros of f34, and the corresponding
ordinary-ray evaluation matrix is the identity. The mixed coefficient
is lambda=g0-g1-g6-g14-g23. Thus for any nonnegative gap, all ordinary
coefficients are automatically nonnegative, and the vector is in this
cap if and only if lambda>=0. The three weighted-pair capacities have
coefficients p=g18, q=g14, r=g9. Their permutations have relative
differences equal to the three double transpositions; together with
permutation 5 they form a Klein-four coset.

All 24 reconstruction identities and full rank are checked exactly by
code/research/verify_f34_triangle_coordinates.py. For the known actual
gap outside the ordinary cone (actual_gap_outside_ordinary_cone.txt),
lambda=42246975256, p=65769370688, q=216282432, r=58010468072.
Here p>q+r, and

  p(q+r)-lambda^2=2044729819574190561216 > 0.

Consequently that example satisfies the proposed triangle capacity
bound. This is a single test, not a proof that the bound holds for
every actual gap, and it is not a proof of MOC.
