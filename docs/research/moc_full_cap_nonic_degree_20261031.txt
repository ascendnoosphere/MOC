1. General critical-direction Hessian degree lemma

Let F be any homogeneous cubic on a finite-dimensional vector space over
characteristic zero. If z is nonzero, gradF(z)=0, and rankH_z=r, then

                degree_t det(H_b+tH_z)<=r-1.              (2)

Proof. Put H_z into a constant congruence block diag(A,0), with A
nonsingular of size r. The coefficient of t^r in the determinant equals
det(A) times det(H_b restricted to kerH_z). Euler gives H_z z=0, so
z belongs to that kernel. Cubic polarization gives H_b z=H_z b. Hence
for every k in kerH_z,

  k^T H_b z=k^T H_z b=0.

Thus the restricted form has nonzero radical vector z, its determinant
vanishes, and the degree drops from at most r to at most r-1. This proves
(2). The lemma requires no nonsingular H_b, spectral realization,
positivity, or genericity.

2. Uniform multidegrees of the full cap nonic

Every ordinary cap ray r_j is critical and has rankH_rj=4. The source
model also gives gradF(e)=0, rankH_e=4, H_e e=0.

For fixed g0, let g(t)=g0+t r_j. Criticality of r_j implies that F(g(t))
and Q(g(t)) are affine in t. There are two cases.

For j in {17,18,20,22,23,27,28,30,31}, H_e r_j is nonzero. On the dense
set of g0 where the slope of Q(g(t)) is nonzero, F/Q stays bounded as
t goes to infinity. Applying (2) with r=4 and with
b=g0-(F/Q)e shows

  det H_(g(t)-(F/Q)e)=O(t^3).

Equation(1) now gives N9(g(t))^2=O(t^5). Since the left side is the
square of a polynomial, its degree is even, hence at most4. Therefore

             degree_(v_j) N9<=2                         (3)

for all nine of these coordinates. Vanishing of the higher coefficient
polynomials on a dense set extends(3) through zero-slope and Q=0
specializations; no division at Q=0 is used to infer realization.

For j in {0,7,10,13}, H_e r_j=0 identically. Now Q(g(t)) is constant.
Where that constant is nonzero, F/Q=alpha+beta t. Thus

  g(t)-(F/Q)e=b+t z,  z=r_j-beta e.

Because both e and r_j are critical and H_e r_j=0, z is critical for
every beta. Direct exact symbolic rank over Q(beta), or the dimension
bound for the critical M8 cone, gives rankH_z<=8 everywhere. Applying
(2) gives determinant degree at most7. Equation(1), with constant Q,
then gives

             degree_(v_j) N9<=3.                        (4)

Again polynomial continuation handles Q=0. The complete multidegree
bound, in the ordered13cap coordinates, is therefore

          (3,3,3,3,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2).

Together with total degree9, this leaves at most242900 possible
monomials, rather than497420 for a general degree9 polynomial in13
variables. More concretely: fixing any12other coordinates makes the
secant equation at most quadratic in any of the nine nonsingleton
ordinary coordinates. This uniform fact does not require choosing a
five- or seven-parameter face.
