01_formula_and_exact_failure.png

The left panel includes all 4095 eligible n4 observations, including all 14
formula failures at the recorded weight threshold -1e-9. All 4095 targets
still pass the hull test using only permutations with positive real signed
amplitudes. The 4081 formula passes are numerical diagnoses, not exact
certificates for every floating-point input.

The five larger failures, below -1e-6, lie 0.16099 to 0.49522 inside that
restricted hull in whitened coordinates. Formula failure therefore does not
mean that the support-selection candidate failed or that the target was
close to a hull boundary. The exact Walsh example on the right makes the
distinction without floating-point uncertainty: the affine formula has
minimum -2529/589540, while a known eight-permutation law has positive
weights 1/8 and reproduces the same determinant.

The support restriction makes a real change in this dataset. It removes
full-hull vertices in 3497/4095 cases (85.4%), leaving the hull unchanged in
598. The minimum ratio of restricted to full hull area is 0.2848477527.
The counts of removed full-hull vertices are
  0:598, 1:1312, 2:1251, 3:642, 4:237, 5:45, 6:8, 7:2.
No universal theorem follows from these successes. The positive-amplitude
support hypothesis remains unproved.

02_covariance_conditioning.png

Both panels retain all 4095 observations. The original common-scaled product
coordinates determine the weighted covariance eigenvalues, evaluated by SVD.
Whitening is used to calculate the affine correction. The 72 previously
excluded records are all from the anisotropic family and remain separately
listed rather than silently counted as successes.

The condition number does not give a clean pass/fail split. Its four empirical
quartiles contain respectively 2,4,3,5 failures out of approximately 1024
records each. Median condition numbers are 14.85 for passes and 43.25 for
failures. The five larger failures span condition numbers 2.2929 to 1749639.75,
so at least one failure is well conditioned. Successful numerical records
extend to condition number 4.99e16; those extreme values are diagnostics and
should not be read as reliable exact positivity certificates.

The failures comprise five Haar cases and nine near-permutation cases.
By family they are Gaussian2, anisotropic2, near-pair-cancellation5,
clustered4 and disk1. The nine small negative cases remain numerical
uncertainties for purposes of a mathematical obstruction. The exact Walsh
failure supplies the rigorous global limitation independently.

03_local_theorem_scaling.png

This figure uses the actual nonordinary spectral tuple, divided by 400, and
  K=(1/3)[ 0  1  2 -1
          -1  0  1  2
          -2 -1  0  1
           1 -2 -1  0 ].
It plots U(t)=exp(tK) on both sides of zero, for 160 logarithmically spaced
absolute values from 1e-4 to 10^(-0.2). This is a genuinely nondegenerate path:
the transposition covariance coefficient C has eigenvalues approximately
0.01622149387 and 0.03548339615. Its positive leading principal minor and
positive determinant were checked exactly with rational arithmetic. The
matrix and exact determinant are stored in metrics.json.

Fitted slopes on |t|<=0.003 are
                       t>0          t<0
  negative mass       2.999703     2.999703
  covariance minimum  2.000393     2.000401
  maximum correction  0.999379     0.999374.
These agree with the independently audited O(|t|^3), O(t^2), and O(|t|)
arguments. The smallest plotted correction factors remain approximately
0.74270 and 0.74739 for the two signs. The displayed interval is a numerical
illustration; the analytic theorem guarantees a sufficiently small interval
depending on the spectra and K. This plot does not establish uniformity over
directions with singular C or positivity away from this path.

04_exact_obstruction_geometry.png

The same rational Q and denominator20 spectra from the exact Schur-block
obstruction appear in both panels. The large shaded region is the full MOC
product hull. For this particular example its seven vertices coincide with
the positive-amplitude hull; no further support-selection conclusion is
drawn from this coincidence.

The left constrained region consists of determinant means from permutation
laws preserving the two-row DPP distribution for rows1 and4. It is the
Minkowski sum of six weighted four-product hulls and has 22 rendered vertices.
The right region preserves the S4-to-S3 perfect-matching quotient, hence the
small Schur block. The six quotient-fiber masses form a line segment; taking
the convex hull of the two endpoint Minkowski sums gives its full feasible
region, with 21 rendered vertices. These polygons were computed from the
exact marginal constraints, then rendered numerically.

The insets show the determinant below each constrained region and its exact
separating line. The support excesses are
  fixed rows1,4: 78528447/278480000,
  fixed small Schur block: 107258179/556960000.
The red star nevertheless has the already-verified positive three-product
MOC certificate. Thus the picture displays failures of additional marginal
requirements, not failures of MOC. The complete exact slacks and weights are
in build/moc_moonshots_20261031/fermionic/quotient_exact.json.

Interpretation

The figures retain an unsuccessful explicit formula, ill-conditioned and
ambiguous numerical cases, a condition-number diagnostic without a separating
threshold, and two false marginal-preservation requirements. They also show
the valid local theorem and a stronger support-selection statement that is
still only a candidate. None of these panels closes unrestricted MOC.


05_positive_support_restriction.png

This additional figure uses the existing frozen 4095 diagnostic rows. The
original four figures and all three per-record JSONL files were preserved.
The maximum-reduction example was reconstructed from the source record,
with its area ratio checked against the frozen diagnostic value.

Restricting to positive real signed amplitudes removes at least one full-hull
vertex in 3497/4095 records (85.4%); 598 hulls are unchanged. The removed-vertex
counts for 0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7 vertices are 598,1312,1251,642,237,45,8,2.
The retained-area minimum, first quartile, median, third quartile and maximum
are approximately 0.284848, 0.854651, 0.957949, 0.999950 and 1. The 14 formula
failures all have changed hulls; their area ratios are marked beneath the
histogram. The histogram retains the unchanged 598 in gray.

The largest area reduction is 71.5152%, at small_n04_04144, a Haar mixer with
near-pair-cancellation spectra. Of 24 products, 19 have positive amplitudes.
Four of seven original hull vertices are removed; the restricted hull has
six vertices because interior products can become exposed. Its determinant
is inside the restricted hull, and the affine formula passes with minimum
weight 0.0002116221. The plotted coordinates whiten the full product cloud;
this invertible affine transformation preserves the ratio of polygon areas.

The scope remains numerical: 4095 eligible n4 records and 72 nearly flat
excluded records. This is evidence that positive-support selection can make
a substantial restriction while retaining the determinant. It does not prove
the universal candidate.
