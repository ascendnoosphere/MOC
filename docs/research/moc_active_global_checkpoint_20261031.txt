Main global route now under investigation
========================================
docs/research/moc_compact_spinor_minimax_20261031.txt
code/research/moc_compact_spinor_exact_audit.py

Encode the 2n spectral labels by unit spinors u_i,v_j in C^2. Put
 p_pi=product_i [u_i,v_pi(i)],
 d=det(U Hadamard ([u_i,v_j]))/detU,
 h=max_pi Re(p_pi-d).
This is an exact compact formulation of the unrestricted problem, including
all support directions. Common SU2 rotation moves every finite collection
away from the affine pole, and the common scalar conversion preserves hull
membership. Thus no unproved spectral condition is silently imposed.

Any failure attains a negative global minimum m. A single active probability
law lambda cancels ALL first-order tangent gradients. Its matching tensor
P=sum lambda p-d satisfies P=m and partial_k P=m conjugate(w_k). SL2
invariance then gives sum_k w_k w_k*=n I2. Multiplicity at least n is
excluded by an exact two-point/block-minor calculation. A contraction
embedding also gives |d|<=1. These are necessary conditions, not exclusion
of a negative minimum.

composition in code/lean/MocBalancedSpinors.lean, with only standard Lean
axioms. The analytic compactness/subgradient extraction is written mathematics,
not an accepted Lean theorem. Scope and compiler commands:
