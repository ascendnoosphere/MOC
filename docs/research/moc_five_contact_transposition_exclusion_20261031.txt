1. Complex kernel inheritance at full actual rank8

LEMMA. If A is a complex matrix with rank_C A<=4 and its entrywise real
part has rank8, then every real h in ker(Re A) belongs to ker A.

Proof. Since rank(A+bar A)=8, the inequalities

 8<=dim_C(im A+im bar A)<=rank A+rank bar A<=8

are all equalities. Thus both ranks are4 and the two images have zero
intersection. For real h with Re(A)h=0, Ah=-bar(A)h lies in that
intersection, so Ah=0. QED.

For an actual support gap write

 z_pi=eta prod_i(a_i+b_pi(i)),  g=h0 e-Re z.

The global source theorem gives rank_C M(z)<=4, while M(e)=0. If
rank_R M(g)=8 and h is a real kernel vector of M(g), the lemma gives

                         M(z)h=0.                         (1)

Thus the ENTIRE complex source profile z belongs to the complexified
kernel of B_H:d->M(d)h. This needs no Terracini, smooth-source, Hessian
rank12, or source-openness assumption. Those may still be needed to
derive the separate Morse necessary condition.

3. Repeated row or column spectra cannot occur at actual rank8

Suppose g is invariant under swapping two row labels. Let P be that
transposition on four coordinates, R=L^T P L the induced orthogonal
reflection on the tetrahedral3-space, and Q its left action on vec(H).
Leibniz parity gives the EXACT covariance

                     Q^T M(g)Q=-M(g).                     (4)

The plus and minus eigenspaces of Q have dimensions6 and3. Equation(4)
forces M(g) to be off-diagonal between them, of block shape

                          [[0,T],[T^T,0]],  T:3-by6.

Consequently rank M(g)<=6. The same argument applies to a column
transposition, using right action on H. Every spectral collision
a_i=a_j makes the full support cost row-swap invariant, and b_i=b_j
makes it column-swap invariant. Thus actual rank8 implies all four a_i
are distinct and all four b_j are distinct.

4. A contact transposition edge forces at least six contacts

Let pi,pi' in C differ only by swapping their images u,v at rows i,j.
Their exact matching-product difference is

 z_pi-z_pi'
   =eta(a_i-a_j)(b_v-b_u) prod_(k notin{i,j})(a_k+b_pi(k)). (5)

By(3) the left side is zero. Rank8 excludes eta=0 and, by Section3,
excludes all row/column spectral collisions. Thus one of the two

Both contacted products are then zero. Equation(3) gives w=0. The
support height h0=Re(w) is zero because these are active contacts.

therefore gap zero. There are exactly3!=6 such permutations.

is assumed.
