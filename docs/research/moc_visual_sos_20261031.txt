01_support_coverage.png
The main null result is displayed alongside the constructive result.
All20127 raw rows were scanned and all6619 dimension-four rows selected.
4114 rows supplied all their hull-edge directions;1596 adversarial rows
supplied saved optimized directions. The909 degenerate or thin hull rows
were explicitly excluded. All32950 resulting support costs passed the
ordinary-minor cone test numerically. In the separate161 targeted actual
spectra,73 costs were ordinary and88 required the old weighted laws.
None required a new SOS law. These are observations, with repeated spectra
and optimizer starts, not independent probability trials.

The three formal controls show why the exact result remains meaningful:
the old continuous library certifies mixed coefficients3/100,3/16,3/32,
whereas the new exact certificates reach1 in each case. No spectral
realization of those formal costs is asserted. The plot keeps that
statement separate from the actual-data coverage counts.

02_exact_sos_certificates.png
The exact separating dual takes values-97/100,-13/16,-29/32 on the three
new laws, while it is nonnegative on the entire old continuous weighted
library. This is an exact mathematical separation, independent of sampling.
The three Gram ranks are exactly19,18,18. Their relabeling counts are
192,288,576. The two rank18 laws have two permutation contacts: one pair
related by a double transposition, the other by a single transposition.
All three laws passed independent exact polynomial and PSD audits.

The eigenvalue plot is deliberately labelled as a numerical display of
exact rational matrices. Its zero eigenvalues are known from exact kernels,
not threshold inference. The first certificate has a much smaller relative
positive eigenvalue than the boundary certificates' positive parts, showing
why naive rounding would be risky even when floating-point spectra look
nonnegative. Positive rational LDL/congruence arithmetic proves positivity.
The exact certificate has838 checked polynomial coefficient positions in
each case; overlapping plotted eigenvalues do not mean omitted coordinates.

03_eight_contact_ledger.png
The current canonical eight-contact ledger records14 full closed orbit
types, covering207 patterns, in its58-type/927-pattern census. Orbit51 is
highlighted separately and contributes3 patterns. Its closure includes
five independent nonnegative coefficients and their boundary values.
The19 scalar Lean statements do not formalize the entire geometric bridge.

The grid gives orbit indices, not percentages. Gray means that the current
ledger does not record a full closure for that orbit. The existing uniform-
coefficient tests on all58 types do not prove their full coefficient families.
The contact matrix is bookkeeping for the chosen canonical representatives;
visual differences between individual contact labels are not claimed to be
invariant geometric obstructions. The finite census measures neither a
fraction of unrestrictedMOC solved nor the relative difficulty of its cases.

04_failures_and_null_results.png
Three successful numerical Gram searches with common off-triangle coefficient
t=1,10,100 gave t*beta approximately.01392,.01065,.01044. The plot retains
this weak trend without promoting three numerical candidates to an
asymptotic theorem. The exact point t=1,beta=1/50 is marked separately.
Two SLSQP runs at t=0,.1 reached1500iterations with negative eigenvalues.
They supply no feasible certificate, infeasibility proof or counterexample.

For the88 targeted nonordinary actual costs, the scatter compares old and
expanded certified capacities, each divided by the mixed coefficient.
Several budgets improve, but every old ratio is already at least1. The
region where a new law would change an actual classification is empty.
Thus enlarging the universal positivity cone and obtaining new actual
spectral coverage remain different achievements. The sample cannot decide
whether every actual spectral gap lies in the old or the enlarged cone.

Visual inspection.
Every initial PNG was opened with view_image. I fixed clipped tick labels
and a legend collision in figure1, moved dual-value labels inside bars
and wrapped a long caption in figure2, and added cell separators to the
ledger contact matrix. All four final PNGs were then opened and inspected
again. Labels, units, legends, exact/empirical distinctions and scope notes
are visible in the final images. No screenshots or generated images were
used as substitutes for the underlying plotting data.

Reproduce from the workspace root:
 python code/research/moc_visual_sos.py
The script writes the four images and numerical/source metrics. Human/model
image inspection is recorded separately after the render; regenerating
correctly resets that status to require a fresh visual inspection.
