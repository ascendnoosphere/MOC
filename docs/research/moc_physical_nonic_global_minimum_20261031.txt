2. Compactification eliminates the unbounded-budget issue

Write u(g)=24^(-1) sum_pi g(pi). The set

 K={g in V: g>=0 pointwise, u(g)=1, N9(g)=0}

is compact: each coordinate lies in[0,24], and all constraints are closed.
SO4 is compact as well. Therefore, if ANY nonnegative N9 cost has a
negative physical expectation, the normalized joint problem attains a
negative global minimum m at some(g,O). This stronger set includes the
actual future costs. Proving its nonnegativity would suffice, without
assuming that the real-lift image itself is closed.

The normalization is an actual positive matching law, rather than an
arbitrary coefficient sum. That fact makes the next condition strict.

3. Exact smooth-face Gauss-normal condition

Let S={pi:g(pi)=0} at a negative global minimum. Put

 W_S={h in V: h(pi)=0 for every pi in S}.

The point g is in the relative interior of its pointwise-nonnegative face.
Let n=D N9(g), regarded as a functional on V. Assume its restriction to
W_S is nonzero. This is a smoothness condition on the CONTACT FACE, not
merely nonvanishing of the ambient N9 gradient.

Homogeneity gives n(g)=9N9(g)=0, while u(g)=1. Hence n|W_S and u|W_S
are independent. Ordinary Lagrange multipliers on that relative face give

 (L_O - m u)|W_S = lambda n|W_S.                       (A)

The coefficient of u is exactly m: pair the multiplier equation with g.
No inequality multiplier regularity is needed, since the calculation is
performed inside W_S. In ambient24 coordinates, (A) means

 c_O-m uniform-lambda n_lift
       belongs to V^perp+span{delta_pi:pi in S}.        (B)

Here n_lift is ANY extension of n to R24. The10-dimensional freedom
V^perp is essential and cannot be discarded when forming moment matrices.

4. Strictly positive conormal moment completion

The alternating function belongs to V^perp. The uniform moment is positive
definite on V: every nonzero matching function has a nonzero value on at
least one permutation.

Let E be any full-column-rank evaluation table for W_S. For every24-vector
y write M_S(y)=E^T diag(y) E. Contact atoms give zero in this compression.
By (B), for SOME eta in V^perp,

 M_S(lambda n_lift+eta)
  =M_S(c_O+delta_O sign-m uniform) >0.                (C)

Strictness follows from m<0. The space W_S is nonzero because it contains
g. Thus lambda cannot vanish. A negative global minimum at a smooth
contact-face point therefore forces an N9 Gauss normal, up to either
overall sign, to admit a STRICTLY POSITIVE matching-moment completion on
the contact-null feature space.

5. A weaker concrete necessary sign test

Every ordinary ray r_j is a0/1 matching function and L_O(r_j)>=0. If a
canonical representation has nu_j>0, then r_j vanishes at every contact of
g: all summands of g are nonnegative. Hence r_j lies in W_S, and (A) gives

 lambda D_(r_j)N9(g)=L_O(r_j)-m u(r_j)>0.             (D)

Consequently all N9 ordinary-budget partial derivatives corresponding to
POSITIVE coefficients must be NONZERO and have the SAME sign at a
negative smooth-face global minimum. A mixed-sign theorem on the
available ordinary rays would contradict(D), before using the whole
moment condition(C).

There is an important automatic identity that invalidates naive sampling:

 e=sum_(j in Z) r_j-3 f34.

N9 is invariant under addition of e and homogeneous of degree9. At t>0,
N9(g)=0 therefore implies

 sum_j (nu_j+t/3) partial_(nu_j)N9=0.                 (E)

Mixed signs among ALL13 partials when all13 budgets are positive are
therefore automatic. Those samples do not test the proposed lemma. At a
zero budget, its derivative must be omitted from(D); then(E) alone does
not force mixed signs on the remaining available rays.

The13 ordinary coefficients are exactly the costs at the13 zero entries
of f34, in the following order:

 j:      0  7 10 13 17 18 20 22 23 27 28 30 31
 pi_j:   1 17  6 22 12 18 20  8 14 15  9 23  7

Thus nu_j=0 creates the actual permutation contact pi_j. These identities
are directly checked in the compiled matching table.

7. Exact Gauss-gradient recovery by a two-by-two Schur complement

The mixed-sign test need not use the342720-term Reynolds polynomial.
At any rational point g with Q!=0 and projected Hessian rank12, set

 X=Q(g) H_g-F(g) H_e.

The verified square identity is N9(g)^2=-det(X)/(4 Q(g)^12). Choose an
invertible principal12-by12 block A=X[I,I], let J be the remaining two
indices, and use the kernel chart

 K[I,:]=-A^(-1) X[I,J],  K[J,:]=I2.

For an ordinary direction r_j define

 D_j=(D_j Q) H_g+Q H_(r_j)-(D_j F) H_e,
 R_j=K^T D_j K.                                      (F)

The leading term of the Schur complement under a perturbation h is
sum_j h_j R_j. All base Schur-complement entries are zero, so the degree2
part of det(X(g+h)) is det(A) det(sum_j h_j R_j). Derivatives of the
nonzero factor Q^(-12) do not enter that degree2 term. Hence, for any
reference direction a with det(R_a)!=0,

 (D_j N9)/(D_a N9)
   =[det(R_a+R_j)-det(R_a)-det(R_j)]/[2 det(R_a)].     (G)

All quantities in(G) are rational at rational g. The unknown overall sign
and square normalization of N9 cancel. This proves an exact algorithm for
recovering the conormal; it is not a universal sign statement.

9. A physical mixed-variation condition along a linear Gauss leaf

Suppose a linear subspace G of cost vectors is contained in the relevant
N9 component and contains g. At a negative joint global minimum let

 v in G, u(v)=0, v(pi)=0 for every active contact pi.

Because every remaining cost coordinate of g is strictly positive,
g+t v is feasible for both signs of sufficiently small t. The objective
L_O(g+t v) is affine in t. Global minimality makes its first derivative
zero, and its second derivative is identically zero.

Now vary the mixer as O(s)=exp(sA)O, A real skew-symmetric. The two-variable
objective L_(O(s))(g+t v) has a PSD Hessian at(s,t)=(0,0). Its t-t entry is
zero, so its mixed s-t entry MUST vanish:

                       D_A L_O(v)=0 for every A.       (I)

This uses the Hessian of the actual smooth objective on a feasible leaf;
it does not assume a fixed nonsmooth contact multiplier is PSD. It also
avoids division by matrix entries, including at mixers with zero entries:

 D_A L_O(v)=sum_pi sign(pi) v(pi)
             sum_a (AO)_(a,pi(a)) product_(i!=a) O_(i,pi(i)).

Thus the physical mixer must be stationary for EVERY contact-preserving
Gauss-leaf direction, as well as for the original cost g. This is stronger
than merely requiring a conditional PSD completion of an arbitrary normal.

If the independently proposed generic Gauss fiber is a projective P4,
then its cone G has dimension5 and contains e. The normalized affine leaf
has dimension4. With k independent contacts, the available directions in
(I) have dimension at least4-k. Moving along such directions preserves
the negative value and the chosen mixer until additional contacts occur.
Consequently a minimum on a generic compact leaf section can be pushed
to a vertex with at least four active contacts. The projective fiber input
and singular limits are separate geometric obligations; this paragraph
does not prove them from dimension counting alone.
