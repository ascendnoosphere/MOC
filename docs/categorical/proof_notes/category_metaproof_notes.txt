1. THE THREE DIFFERENT QUANTIFIER ORDERS

Let H_n=(C^2)^{tensor n}. Let T_pi permute tensor factors, with a fixed consistent action convention. The actual compound kernel R(U) has entries
 R(U)[I,J]=|det U[I,J]|^2 if |I|=|J|,
and zero otherwise. Let u=otimes_j u_j be a product input and w=otimes_i w_i a complex-linear product output covector.

Universal common permutation law:
 For every U there exists a probability mu_U such that
 R(U)=sum_pi mu_U(pi)T_pi.
The same law must work for every input and every output.

Input-dependent product-orbit law:
 For every U and every product u there exists a probability mu_{U,u} such that
 R(U)u=sum_pi mu_{U,u}(pi)T_pi u.
The same law must work for every output covector, including entangled ones.

Scalar product-test law:
 For every U, every product u and every product w, there exists a probability
 mu_{U,u,w} such that
 wR(U)u=sum_pi mu_{U,u,w}(pi)wT_pi u.
This last statement is precisely unrestricted complex MOC after the rank-two matrix factorization C_ij=w_i(u_j), generic row/column scaling to C_ij=a_i+b_j, and continuity.

4. A POSITIVE INTERMEDIATE CATEGORY WITH A PROVED GLUING LAW

Define B_n to be normalized nonnegative GL2-equivariant Boolean Markov kernels. Define H_n subset B_n by the additional requirement:
 for every source inclusion B subset C, |C|=|B|+1, the two output column laws R_k[-,B] and R_{k+1}[-,C] admit a coupling supported on X subset Y.
This is a finite system of weighted Hall inequalities. Equivalently there are stochastic kernels L_k on incidence edges (B,C) such that the two endpoint marginals of L_k are exactly the respective R columns.

THEOREM. H_n is convex and closed under composition. The family(H_n) is closed under tensor products. Every actual compound kernel belongs to H_n. Every R in H_n satisfies the product-input orbit theorem whenever its local input factors lie on at most three projective lines and one of the classes has cardinalityone.

Proof of closures. Convex combinations of couplings give a coupling of the corresponding mixtures. For a composite S R, first sample a nested pair from the coupling for R at the source edge, then conditionally sample a nested pair from the coupling for S at that intermediate edge. The resulting marginals are the columns of SR and nesting is preserved. At the level of incidence kernels this is just L_k(S)L_k(R), so associativity is ordinary Markov-kernel associativity. Identity kernels use deterministic identity edge maps.

For a tensor product, an adjacent source pair changes in exactly one factor. Use an adjacent coupling in that factor and an identical shared sample from the unchanged-factor column. Union of the two sampled subsets preserves inclusion and gives the required tensor marginals. The GL2-equivariance and Boolean normalization conditions are themselves preserved under these operations.

Actual membership follows from the exterior Hall argument for every adjacent pair of column subsets, not merely one fixed pair: x is the wedge of the smaller orthonormal column family, y=x wedge v, and projected-vector Cauchy-Schwarz gives all Hall cuts.

Positive input theorem. A global GL2 change sends two distinct input lines to e0,e1. The exceptional factor is alpha e0+beta e1. Up to a global scalar the input is alpha|B>+beta|C> for an adjacent pair. A common nested coupling yields
 R(alpha|B>+beta|C>)
 =sum_{X subset Y} mu_XY(alpha|X>+beta|Y>),
and each summand is a permutation image of that same input. Undo the global change using equivariance. Coincident lines and zero coefficients follow directly or by continuity. The convex coefficients are independent of alpha,beta and of the numerical representatives of the three lines.

Conversely, requiring the product-orbit conclusion just for every input |B>+|C> forces a nested coupling: every permutation image is|X>+|Y> with X subset Y, and comparison of the two different weight blocks gives its marginals. Thus H_n is exactly the category capturing this three-line singleton input law, not the unrestricted product-input law.

Logical position:
 conv{tensor permutations} subset conv{actual kernels} subset H_n subset B_n.
The first inclusion is strict already in dimension4 by the volume's actual common-law counterexample. The last inclusion is strict in dimension5 by the explicit R above. Equality or strictness of other inclusions is not asserted. This construction does not declare all of H_n to satisfy unrestricted MOC; only the proved three-line singleton stratum follows.

The category with chosen incidence lifts as part of a morphism has genuine functorial composition; selecting arbitrary couplings independently is not claimed to give a canonical functor. The forgetting map to Boolean Markov kernels is a well-defined functor. This is the precise local-to-global compatibility supplied here. It glues adjacent-rank laws, not the unsupported full branching Boolean family needed for multiple exceptional labels.
