Research notes, 17 October 2026. Exact results and explicit boundaries.

1. THE FULL COMMON-LAW OBJECT AND ITS SIGNED LIFT.

For U unitary, R(U)[I,J]=|det U[I,J]|^2 for equal-cardinality subsets.
A common permutation law is R(U)=sum_pi lambda_pi R(P_pi), with nonnegative
lambda summing to one. It implies MOC for every pair of complex spectra.
It is strictly stronger than the scalar MOC assertion.

For a real orthogonal U there is a distinguished signed law

 omega_pi = sgn(pi) product_i U[i,pi(i)] / det(U).

Its sum is one. Summing over permutations carrying I onto J and using
Laplace expansion gives

 sum_{pi:pi(I)=J} omega_pi
 = (-1)^(sum I+sum J) det U[I,J] det U[I^c,J^c] / det U
 = det U[I,J]^2,

the last step being the complementary-minor identity for orthogonal U.
Thus nonnegativity of every determinant-expansion term is a sufficient
condition for a full common law. This proof is elementary and retains all
Boolean weights simultaneously. It does not apply to an arbitrary
invertible matrix without the orthogonality/complementary-minor step.

Kenyon, Multideterminantal measures, arXiv:2501.18349v2 (2025), Section 5,
Theorem 6 relates positive determinant permutation measures to Pfaffian
bipartite graphs. Sections 6 and 6.1 describe pure colored measures and
the sign restrictions on their products of Pluecker coordinates. These
results provide a useful geometric interpretation of the signed lift.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.18349

2. AN EXPLICIT POSITIVE FOUR-SITE SEED; ALREADY PRESENT IN THE VOLUME.

For real a,b,c, let s=a^2+b^2+c^2>0 and

 C = [[ 0, a, b, c],
      [-a, 0, c,-b],
      [-b,-c, 0, a],
      [-c, b,-a, 0]],       U=C/sqrt(s).

Direct multiplication gives CC^T=sI, and det C=s^2. Every nonzero signed
determinant term is positive. The three permutations consisting of two
disjoint transpositions have weights a^4/s^2, b^4/s^2, c^4/s^2. The six
four-cycles occur in inverse pairs with respective weights
a^2 b^2/s^2, a^2 c^2/s^2, b^2 c^2/s^2. Their sum is one. Section 1 proves
that these nine weights give the entire squared-minor kernel, and hence
MOC for all complex spectra.

This seed is monomial-equivalent to the reflection family and nine-point
law already established in the supplied volume, D189/D191. It therefore
must not be advertised as a new positive family. The Pfaffian viewpoint
rediscovers it and explains its particularly simple determinant law.
It is eligible as a four-site seed in the newly proved hyperforest
composition theorem in category_variational_notes.txt.

If abc is nonzero, every row and column has exactly three nonzero entries.
This rules out a decomposition on four sites into a site--gate incidence
forest using only nontrivial two- and three-site gates: a connected such
forest has sum_g(|support(g)|-1)=3. The possibilities are three two-site
gates, or one three-site plus one two-site gate. The latter always has a
row or column supported on at most two coordinates. For the former,
consider the earliest gate touching a leaf of the three-edge site tree:
more directly, remove the first or last leaf-edge in chronological order;
the standard tree-circuit structural induction gives a row or column
with at most two nonzeros. An alternative short proof uses any leaf edge:
the leaf is affected once; if the neighbor has no earlier incident gates
the leaf output row has support at most two, and if it has no later gates
the leaf input column has support at most two. In a three-edge tree, some
leaf edge necessarily has one of these properties (check the path and
star shapes). Thus the four-site seed can enlarge the permitted seed set.


5. INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE INCIDENCE-FOREST COMPOSITION THEOREM.

I checked Sections 1--6 of category_variational_notes.txt independently.
The theorem is sound: R(UV)=R(U)R(V) uniformly for all U,V respecting
specified partitions if and only if their bipartite incidence multigraph
is a forest, with two parallel edges counted as a cycle.

Sufficiency: admissible Cauchy--Binet intermediate subsets have prescribed
degrees at every graph vertex. A forest has at most one such 0/1 edge
subset, by leaf elimination. Thus no cross term is possible.

Necessity: on any 2r-edge simple cycle, including r=1, choose real nonzero
two-site rotations at its alternating vertices and identity elsewhere.
Use the alternating coordinate subset for both exterior input and output.
Exactly two intermediate terms survive, both nonzero and real. Their
cross term is 2ab, which is nonzero. Extra coordinates in the same blocks
cause no further terms because they are fixed and excluded.

The extension to a hyperforest of arbitrary common-law seeds follows by
induction on supports meeting each previous connected component in at
most one coordinate. Pairwise support intersections alone are insufficient.
Arbitrary three-site seeds are legal because Birkhoff supplies weight one
and Jacobi identifies weight two with the same permutation law.

The theorem is exact, all-dimensional and spectrum-independent. Cycles
invalidate uniform exact composition, not necessarily a common law.

6. INDEPENDENT DERIVATION OF THE SINGLE-CYCLE PARITY CORRECTION IDENTITY.

Let P and Q be perfect matchings on 2r sites whose union is one cycle.
Let S_e denote the Boolean permutation action of swapping the endpoints
of e. For a matching P define A_P=product_{e in P}(I-S_e).

On a Boolean subset containing zero or two sites of a pair, I-S_e is zero.
Thus A_P is supported only at weight r and on P-transversals (subsets
choosing one endpoint from each pair). On that space A_P=f_P f_P^T, where
f_P(I)=+/-1 is the product of the local endpoint signs. Similarly
A_Q=f_Q f_Q^T. The only subsets transversal to both matchings are the two
alternating subsets K0,K1. Complementing all r local choices multiplies
each f by (-1)^r, so

 f_P^T f_Q=2 sigma,  sigma=f_P(K0)f_Q(K0) in {+1,-1}.

Expanding the product of the two matching operators therefore yields

 sum_{all 2r gate bits b} (-1)^(sum b) R(P_left(b)P_right(b))
                    = 2 sigma f_P f_Q^T.

This proves the factor two and the support/rank-one structure of the
signed outcome correction without numerical computation. It will be used
to audit the variational agent's phase coefficient and positivity range.

7. COMPLETED AUDIT OF THE POSITIVE SINGLE-CYCLE THEOREM.

Sections 9--12 of category_variational_notes.txt have now been checked.
They are sound. For arbitrary complex two-site layers U,V on P,Q, and a
diagonal intermediate phase D, define kappa by the actual reference
exterior cross term, c=Re(exp(i theta) kappa). Local column orthogonality
in U and row orthogonality in V show that all other cross terms differ
only by f_P(I)f_Q(J). Thus

 R(UDV)=R(U)R(V)+2c f_P f_Q^T,
 |kappa|=product_g sqrt(p_g(1-p_g)).

Together with Section 6 this yields the corrected outcome weights

 lambda_b=product_g p_g^b_g(1-p_g)^(1-b_g)+sigma c(-1)^(sum b_g).

They sum to one. They are all nonnegative whenever
|c|<=product_g min(p_g,1-p_g). In particular every balanced matching-cycle
circuit, in every even dimension and at every phase, has a full common
law. Arbitrary biased gates have an open allowable phase interval around
quadrature. The result handles genuine nonzero coherent interference.

The determinant-specific defect also checks exactly:

 2c product_{(p0,p1) in P}(a_p1-a_p0)
    product_{(q0,q1) in Q}(b_q0-b_q1).

If any displayed paired difference vanishes, the classical product law
represents the actual determinant at every phase, regardless of biases.
For r=1 the bit outcomes repeat permutations, so nonnegativity of every
unaggregated corrected weight is sufficient but not necessary; this does
not affect any theorem stated in the variational notes.

8. NEW DERIVATION: EVERY U(2) TENSOR U(2) HAS A FULL COMMON LAW.

THEOREM. Let A,B be arbitrary complex 2x2 unitary matrices. Then the
4x4 unitary A tensor B has a common permutation law, independent of all
spectral parameters. Consequently it satisfies MOC for every pair of
complex four-dimensional spectra. The theorem also supplies new legal
four-site seeds for incidence-hyperforest composition in every dimension.
Historical novelty is not claimed; this family was not identified in the
supplied volume during the present search.

PROOF OF THE REDUCTION. Every 2x2 unitary is equivalent under left and
right diagonal unitary factors to

 R(p)=[[sqrt(1-p),sqrt(p)],[-sqrt(p),sqrt(1-p)]], 0<=p<=1.

Tensoring the diagonal factors only introduces diagonal 4x4 factors,
which leave R(U) unchanged. Swapping an input or output coordinate in
either 2x2 factor changes p into 1-p, up to diagonal factors. Such
monomial transformations simply relabel a common permutation law. The
perfect-shuffle permutation interchanges the two tensor factors. We may
therefore reduce to U=R(p) tensor R(q), with 0<=p<=q<=1/2.

Set x=1-2q, y=2(q-p), z=2p. These are nonnegative and x+y+z=1.
Here is an explicit law, with permutations in one-based row-image form:

 permutation(s)            weight for EACH listed permutation
 1234                      x^2+xy+y^2/4
 1243, 2134                xy/2+xz/2+y^2/4+yz/2+z^2/8
 1432, 3214                xz/2+yz/4+z^2/8
 2143                      y^2/4
 2341, 4123                yz/4+z^2/8
 3421, 4312                z^2/8

All other permutations have weight zero. Every displayed coefficient is
nonnegative. Their sum is (x+y+z)^2=1.

FULL-KERNEL IDENTITY. Write c=sqrt(1-p), s=sqrt(p), d=sqrt(1-q), e=sqrt(q).
The matrix is

 [[ cd, ce, sd, se],
  [-ce, cd,-se, sd],
  [-sd,-se, cd, ce],
  [ se,-sd,-ce, cd]].

Its squared entries are products from {p,1-p} and {q,1-q}. For weight
two, order both row and column subsets as 12,13,14,23,24,34. The complete
squared-minor matrix is

 [ P, 0, u, u, 0, p^2 ]
 [ 0, Q, v, v, q^2, 0 ]
 [ u, v, X, Y, v, u   ]
 [ u, v, Y, X, v, u   ]
 [ 0,q^2,v, v, Q, 0   ]
 [ p^2,0,u, u, 0, P   ],

where P=(1-p)^2, Q=(1-q)^2, u=p(1-p), v=q(1-q),
X=(1-p-q)^2, Y=(p-q)^2. This follows by 2x2 determinant expansion.
All these entries, as well as weight-one entries, are quadratic in p,q.

Substitute the displayed ten nonnegative weights and sum those for which
pi(I)=J. They reproduce the weight-one matrix and every entry of this
weight-two table. Weight zero and four follow from normalization; weight
three follows from the complementary-minor identity and complementation
of the corresponding permutation event. This proves the full common law.

The companion standard-library script category_tensor_verify.py verifies
all 70 identities symbolically over Q[p,q], not at sample parameters. It
expands each minor in c,s,d,e, squares, substitutes their squared-coordinate
relations, and compares the resulting exact rational polynomial with the
permutation sum. Every odd exponent is checked to cancel, and degree at
most two is explicitly checked. It reports PASS for all 70 entries.
