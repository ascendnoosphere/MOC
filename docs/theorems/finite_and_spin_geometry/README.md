## Continuous geometry and support duals

[Full rank-two geometry](continuous_full_rank_two_geometry.md), [rank-two torus](continuous_rank_two_torus.md), and [pair fibers](continuous_pair_fibers.md) describe the motion under study. The [Householder argument](continuous_householder_meta_proof.md), [finite witness](continuous_finite_witness.md), and [holomorphic face reduction](continuous_holomorphic_face_reduction.md) give complementary reductions. [Plateau transport](continuous_plateau_transport.md) and the [dimension boundary](continuous_reversible_dimension_boundary.md) specify continuation constraints.

For support estimates, see [local-to-open transport](dual_local_to_open_theorem.md), the [common-facet sharp bound](dual_common_facet_sharp_bound.md), [isoclinic support](dual_isoclinic_support_geometry.md), and the [quadratic CP cone](dual_quadratic_cp_cone.md). The remaining `continuous_*` and `dual_*` notes retain their individual constructions. [Zero-gap reflection rigidity](ZERO_GAP_REFLECTION_RIGIDITY.md) isolates the contact statement.
