Auxiliary PPT-jet numerical campaign audit
31 October 2026

Scope: numerical feasibility only. These floating-point matrices are not
exact proof certificates and do not establish unrestricted MOC4.

Selection and checks

The campaign tested all nine hull-edge support directions of the integer
nonordinary seed a=(0,100,-214+146i,269-294i),
b=(-207-148i,-28-84i,-59+82i,83+104i). It also tested four actual
nonordinary perturbations from targeted_mixed_enrichment.jsonl, selecting
the most negative recorded ordinary-capacity dual at each perturbation
scale 0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, and two nearly extremal triangle-boundary
costs, trials18 and103 of targeted_triangle_search.jsonl. The latter
selection used distinct recorded contact multiplicities. Every actual
support cost was recomputed as max_pi Re(eta z_pi)-Re(eta z_pi), then
normalized by its maximum.

Every final NPZ was independently reloaded. The audit checked cost
agreement against both saved and unclamped actual support gaps, PSD of
each coefficient matrix, PSD of its required partial transpose,
nonnegative ordinary weights, and annihilation of all selected contact
vectors. Every one of the five violations is below1e-9 in all15 cases.
The largest final violation is 9.816019524988562e-10.
The largest clamping error in any actual support gap is 1.705397997959424e-14.

Cone comparison and convergence

Full32-dimensional PPT with two orientations reached tolerance on10/15
costs by20000 Douglas--Rachford iterations. The reduced14-dimensional PSD
cone with PPT required only on its leading parameter2-feature2 corner
reached13/15 by20000. This latter cone is justified by the companion QFT
proof; its prototype checks the exact structural dimensions and numerical
orthogonality/reconstruction of the reduction before every run.

The two apparent residual plateaus did not prove a cone obstruction.
For targeted_mixed_0037, affine correction of the strict one-sided seed
certificate gave an exact-cost start with modest negative eigenvalues
and weights;200 DR steps then reached tolerance. For targeted_mixed_0066,
continuing saved DR states after20000 cold steps reached tolerance after
another8400 steps. Row-transporting the seed contact from1 to7 was a
worse initialization for0066 because its cost changed by0.387; that
separate attempt eventually passed after45100 steps. All attempts remain
saved. The campaign supplies no numerical reason to enlarge the cone.

this classification is numerical, and no LP miss is asserted as an
exact separation proof.

Saved data

build/moc_auxiliary_ppt_jet/ contains the full-PPT attempts.
build/moc_auxiliary_corner_ppt_jet/ contains the reduced attempts, each
with JSON histories and NPZ actual inputs, jet tables, ordinary rays and
weights, coefficient matrices, reduction bases, and saved algorithm
states. The two successful follow-up labels are
mixed0037_seed_warmstart_80000 and mixed0066_continue_80000.

The compiled final report is campaign_all_final_audit.json.
campaign_all_raw_history.csv contains2362 snapshots from both primary
campaigns and the two successful follow-ups. The alternative transported
0066 attempt has its own complete JSON history. The scientific plot
campaign_feasibility_trajectories.png displays the maximum of all five
feasibility violations and marks the independently audited final points.

Reproduction

python code/research/moc_auxiliary_ppt_jet_campaign.py --cone full --workers 4 --iterations 20000
python code/research/moc_auxiliary_ppt_jet_campaign.py --cone corner --workers 4 --iterations 20000

Warm starts and continuation are exposed as initial_t, initial_w, initial_dr_states
arguments in moc_auxiliary_corner_ppt_probe.py. The transport helper is
moc_auxiliary_corner_warmstart.py. Final report, CSV, and plot generation:

python code/research/moc_auxiliary_ppt_campaign_analysis.py
