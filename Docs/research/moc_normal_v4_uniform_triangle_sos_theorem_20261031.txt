UNIFORM NORMAL-KLEIN-FOUR MATCHING SOS THEOREM
31 October 2026

Theorem. Let g be a real matching function in V14 on S4, nonnegative
at every permutation, with g=0 on the normal Klein four subgroup

                   C={id,(01)(23),(02)(13),(03)(12)}.

Let M(g) be the universal real symmetric nine-by-nine inverse quadratic
pencil, normalized as in moc_universal_inverse_quadratic_pencil.py.
If the negative inertia index of M(g) is at most four, then

               g=sum_l f_l^2,   f_l in V14.            (T)

In particular (T) holds for EVERY ACTUAL normal-V4 support cost

        g_pi=h-Re(eta prod_i(a_i+b_pi(i))),
        h=max_pi Re(eta prod_i(a_i+b_pi(i))).

This includes colliding spectra, zero budgets, equality cases, and
singular source charts. It proves this one finite contact-face case;
it does not assert the other remaining contact-face cases or full MOC.

The actual implication uses the independently proved global source
bound n_-(M(g))<=4 from
moc_actual_inverse_pencil_rank_four_20261031.txt. The argument below
proves the stronger matching-function statement (T), so it needs no
source reconstruction, N9 equation, projective genericity, or density
passage once that inertia bound is known.

1. Complete V4 coordinates and ordinary-square criterion

The standard S4 matching space is V14=[4]+[31]+[22]. Its subspace
vanishing on C has dimension ten. Here is a complete evaluation chart,
which also fixes all signs in the argument. Use ten feature functions

                    d, f0,f1,f2, x0,x1,x2, y0,y1,y2.

For completeness, V4 acts in the standard representation by the four
diagonal product-one sign matrices, and acts trivially in [22]. Four
contact zeros therefore set the three standard diagonal coefficients
to zero and impose one scalar/[22]-trace relation. The remaining six
standard off-diagonal coefficients give x,y. On S4/V4=S3 the residual
quotient function has zero identity value and no sign representation;
the latter is exactly c++c-=sum beta. This gives the remaining four
coordinates, establishing completeness of the chart.

They vanish on C. The other twenty permutations are grouped as follows:

 * For each i, four transposition-quotient-coset points: d=0,
   f_i=1, x_i=epsilon, y_i=delta, with epsilon,delta independent +/-1;
   every other f,x,y coordinate is zero.
 * Four plus-cycle points: d=1, f_i=1, x_i=s_i, y_i=0,
   where product_i s_i=1.
 * Four minus-cycle points: d=-1, f_i=0, x_i=0, y_i=s_i,
   where product_i s_i=1.

These features belong to V14; their exact twenty-four-value table and
membership in the ordinary singleton/pair feature span are verified
by moc_normal_v4_prism_duality.py and the theorem replay named below.

Every g in this ten-dimensional face space has a unique expression

            g=sum beta_i f_i - c- d + p.x + q.y,
            c++c-=sum beta.                           (1)

Its twenty values are beta_i+epsilon p_i+delta q_i on the three
transposition cosets; c++sum s_i p_i on plus; c-+sum s_i q_i on minus.
Thus nonnegativity gives

 beta_i>=|p_i|+|q_i|,
 c++sum s_i p_i>=0, c-+sum s_i q_i>=0 (product s_i=1). (2)

The twelve binary ordinary matching features vanishing on C are

             A_i^+/-=(f_i +/- x_i)/2,
             B_i^+/-=(f_i-d +/- y_i)/2.               (3)

Each is zero or one, so it equals its own square. In the repository's
ray numbering, the six with the signs used later are

 A_i^+ : [17,28,24],
 B_i^+ : [22,18,29], B_i^- : [27,19,32].

If c+>=sum|p_i| and c->=sum|q_i|, g is a nonnegative combination of
the twelve features (3). To prove this directly, choose numbers a_i
in [|p_i|,beta_i-|q_i|] with sum a_i=c+; the sum interval contains c+
because of the two assumed l1 inequalities. Put b_i=beta_i-a_i. Then

 g=sum_i [(a_i+p_i) A_i^+ +(a_i-p_i) A_i^-
          +(b_i+q_i) B_i^+ +(b_i-q_i) B_i^-],          (4)

with all coefficients nonnegative. Hence (T) is proved in this case.

If any beta_i=0, (2) forces p_i=q_i=0. Each tetrahedral inequality in
(2) then attains the unrestricted negative l1 sum, by selecting the
otherwise irrelevant sign in the zero coordinate. Thus both l1 bounds
hold and (4) proves (T) on that boundary.

2. Exact normalization to one simplicial nonordinary cap

The first inequality in (2) implies

                 sum beta>=sum|p|+sum|q|.             (5)

Together with c++c-=sum beta, it shows that the two l1 bounds cannot
both fail. Inverting permutations exchanges (p,c+) with (q,c-). Thus
if (4) does not already apply we may take c+<sum|p|.

In this case every p_i is nonzero and product_i sign(p_i)=1.
Otherwise the sign pattern attaining -sum|p| is allowed in (2).
Left translation by an element of V4 changes the three p signs by
an arbitrary product-one sign pattern. We may therefore take p_i>0.
The allowed pattern with p_i positive and the other two signs negative
in (2) now gives

                 t=sum p-c+>0,   t<=2p_i.            (6)

These transformations preserve nonnegativity, V14, C, and C0. They
also preserve the inertia of M(g): V4 translation gives conjugation
of row-major H by a real diagonal sign matrix, and inversion gives
H->H^T. This covariance is an exact polynomial identity of the
universal pencil, also checked in the replay. Only even V4 translations
and inversion are used in this normalization.

Define the nine nonnegative budgets

 u_i=2p_i-t,
 v_i^+=beta_i-p_i+q_i, v_i^-=beta_i-p_i-q_i.             (7)

Put A_i=A_i^+, and retain B_i^+/- from (3). Define the binary-feature
linear combination

                         Z=-d+sum A_i.                (8)

The pointwise products A_i A_j, for all three pairs i!=j, are the
SAME function Jplus: the indicator of the single all-plus cycle point.
The mixed extremal ray r58 satisfies the exact identity

                         r58=Z^2-2Jplus.              (9)

Its values are zero at the three other plus-cycle points, two at the
all-plus cycle point, zero at each epsilon=-1 transposition-coset point,
one at each epsilon=1 point, and one at all minus-cycle points.
In this normalization Z itself equals r58 pointwise; its square differs
at the single all-plus point, exactly as recorded in (9).

The exact cap decomposition is

              g=t r58+sum u_i A_i+sum v_i^+ B_i^+
                                          +sum v_i^- B_i^-.       (10)

All ten generators in (10) are independent. Thus the normalization
has covered every nonordinary nonnegative cost in this face, with
all signs/orientations and closed boundaries retained.

3. An explicit two-dimensional negative-plane lemma

Put U=sum u_i. For u_i>=0 relabel its largest square root as a=sqrt(u0), and put
b=sqrt(u1), c=sqrt(u2). Thus a>=b,c>=0. Define

 T0=a(b+c)-bc,
 K(t)=diag(a^2-t,b^2-t,c^2-t)+(t/2)11^T.              (11)

LEMMA. If t>0 and t>T0, K(t) has at least two strictly negative
eigenvalues. If b=c=0, it has two negative eigenvalues for every t>0.

Proof. First suppose b+c>0, so a>0 and T0>0. Use the explicit rank-two
matrix B and its two-by-two compressions

 B=[[-b,-c],[a,0],[0,a]],
 N=-2 B^T K(T0) B,   P=B^T(2I-11^T)B.

Their exact entries satisfy

 N00=(a-b)[ab(a-b)+a^2c+2abc+b^2c],
 N11=(a-c)[ac(a-c)+a^2b+2abc+bc^2],
 N01=-(a-b)(a-c)(ab+ac+bc),
 det N=4a^2bc(a-b)(a-c)(ab+ac+2bc)>=0.                (12)

Both diagonal entries are nonnegative, so N is positive semidefinite.
Meanwhile

 P00=(a+b)^2, P11=(a+c)^2,
 P01=-a^2+ab+ac+bc,
 det P=4a^2(ab+ac+bc)>0.                             (13)

Hence P is positive definite. The exact identity

             -2 B^T K(t)B=N+(t-T0)P                 (14)

is strictly positive definite for t>T0. The range of B is therefore
a two-dimensional strictly negative subspace for K(t).

If b=c=0, use the invertible matrix with columns
(0,1,-1), (1,0,0), (0,1,1). Its congruence of K(t) is

       diagblock(-2t, [[a^2-t/2,t],[t,0]]).

The two-by-two block has determinant -t^2<0, so it has one negative
eigenvalue. Together with -2t this proves the lemma, including a=0. QED

This polynomial compression proof was found independently by the QFT
branch after the original capacity/secular proof. It gives a stronger
threshold and a single Gram formula, with no triangle-case distinction.

4. The inverse-pencil obstruction forces the stronger Gram budget

In row-major H, reorder its nine entries as

 w=(H22,H11,H00), r=(H01,H20,H12), s=(H10,H02,H21).

The exact universal pencil in chart (1) is

       8M(g)=[ -diag beta   -diag q       -diag p
               -diag q        C+0            0
               -diag p         0             C-0 ],                (16)

 C+0=diag(c+-beta)+Off(p), C-0=diag(c--beta)+Off(q),
 where Off(p)_ij=p_k for {i,j,k}={0,1,2}, with zero diagonal.

We have already covered beta_i=0. For beta_i>0 the first block is
negative definite. Eliminating it by real congruence gives a Schur
complement S6 whose first three-by-three principal block is

                 Cplus=diag(c+-beta_i+q_i^2/beta_i)+Off(p).          (17)

Write beta_i=p_i+s_i. By (2), s_i>=|q_i| and p_i>0. Hence

       -s_i+q_i^2/(p_i+s_i)<=0,
       Cplus<=C0:=diag(c+-p_i)+Off(p).               (18)

Using p_i=(t+u_i)/2 and c+=(t+U)/2,

 C0_ii=(U-u_i)/2,   C0_ij=(t+u_k)/2,
 2C0=E K E,   E=11^T-I,   K as in (11).              (19)

The real matrix E is invertible. If t>T0, the two-plane lemma gives
two negative eigenvalues of C0. Inequality (18) therefore gives two
negative eigenvalues of Cplus, hence of S6. The negative definite
leading block in (16) contributes three further negative eigenvalues.
Thus

              t>T0  implies  n_-(M(g))>=5.            (20)

The hypothesis n_-(M(g))<=4 now forces the NONSTRICT condition

                           0<t<=T0.                  (21)

The b=c=0 branch is excluded for all t>0 by the same negative-index
argument. Thus T0>0 throughout the nonordinary branch.

This step uses no determinant-zero or corank assumption. In particular
all degeneracies in (16)--(21) have been handled without a limit.

5. Explicit full C0 construction, including equality

Condition (21) gives an explicit positive-semidefinite three-by-three
matrix G with diagonal u_i and off-diagonal sum -t. Put

 v=(a,-b,-c), lambda=t/T0 in (0,1],
              G=(1-lambda)diag(u)+lambda vv^T.         (22)

Both summands are PSD, diag(vv^T)=u, and

                  sum_{i<j}v_i v_j=-ab-ac+bc=-T0.

Thus G has precisely the required diagonal and off-diagonal sum.
The same formula holds if one budget vanishes or t=T0; there is no
strict positivity or density assumption.

Now put the ten matching features in the order

             F=(Z,A0,A1,A2,B0+,B1+,B2+,B0-,B1-,B2-)

and choose the block-diagonal Gram

              T=diagblock(t,G,diag(v0+,v1+,v2+,v0-,v1-,v2-)).       (23)

It is PSD. Because A_i^2=A_i and A_i A_j=Jplus, (9) and (22) give

 F T F^T
   =t Z^2+sum u_i A_i+2(sum_{i<j}G_ij)Jplus+sum v_i^+/- B_i^+/-
   =t r58+sum u_i A_i+sum v_i^+/- B_i^+/-
   =g.                                                            (24)

Factoring T gives (T), with every square in V14. This completes the
uniform normal-V4 theorem.                                           QED

Optional capacity consequence and rational implementation. The original
three-budget capacity is

 kappa=U-max(0,a-b-c)^2.

It satisfies

        kappa/2-T0=[(a-b-c)^2-max(0,a-b-c)^2]/2>=0.

Thus actual costs also satisfy kappa>=2t, as originally discovered.
For a rational certificate when a,b,c form a triangle, one can avoid

and its principal two-by-two minors are one quarter of the Heron
polynomial 2sum_{i<j}u_i u_j-sum_i u_i^2>=0. Then

              G=(1-2t/U)diag(u)+(2t/U)H

is an alternative PSD Gram whenever U>=2t. The exact rational control
in the replay uses this formula; the uniform theorem uses (22).

6. Why actual costs satisfy the hypothesis

The source theorem proves rank_C M(z)<=4 for z_pi=prod(a_i+b_pi(i)).
It comes from the polynomial identity

       Q_z(H)=-u^T adj(H^T A0+B0 H^T)v,

which becomes a two-by-two determinant of linear forms in H after
constant row/column changes, so its quadratic form has complex rank
at most four. At zero u or v it is identically zero; the formula and
bound hold for all finite spectra. Constants are killed by M, so

                         M(g)=-Re(eta M(z)).

A complex symmetric rank-at-most-four matrix factors CC^T with at
most four columns. Writing C=X+iY gives Re(CC^T)=XX^T-YY^T. Hence its
real positive and negative inertia indices are each at most four.
Multiplying eta and the sign can be absorbed in the complex factor.
Thus actual costs satisfy n_-(M(g))<=4, exactly as required.

No simplicity of spectra, smoothness, exact four-contact count,
strict positivity away C, or generic source-rank hypothesis occurs
in this proof. Additional contacts and all equality strata are
included by the nonstrict PSD construction (22)--(23).
