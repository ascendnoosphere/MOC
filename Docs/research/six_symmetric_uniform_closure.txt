A UNIFORM-ORDINARY SUBFAMILY OF THE SYMMETRIC SIX-CONTACT CHILD
2026-10-30

Scope: the marked six-contact child has ordinary rays
(0,7,10,13,18,23,28) and mixed ray 34. This note closes the four-parameter
subfamily in which the first four ordinary coefficients are equal. It does
not close the child's full seven-parameter coefficient cone and does not
prove unrestricted complex MOC4.

Write A=r18, B=r23, C=r28, f=f34, and O=r0+r7+r10+r13. Every ordinary
ray here is a canonical squared-minor function. Consider an actual spectral
support gap whose permutation profile is

               g=t O+p A+q B+r C+f,    t,p,q,r>=0.                 (1)

Then g(U)>=0 for every complex unitary U. A positive coefficient of f can
be normalized to one by positive scaling; this preserves equality of the
four ordinary coefficients. A zero mixed coefficient is an ordinary
nonnegative minor sum. The eight displayed rays have exactly six common
permutation contacts, as checked from the reconstructed canonical rays.

The portable exact verifier is
 code/research/verify_six_symmetric_uniform.py.
It reads no generated build files. It checks the spectral model, ray labels,
six contacts, pencil degree, four exact pencil evaluations, determinant
identity, Gram algebra, and the three continuous weighted-compensator
relabelings. The latter inequality is imported from the already proved
weighted-minor theorem in docs/geometry/global_weighted_minor_notes.txt,
including its three-compensator appendix; it is not a new analytic input.

1. The determinant forces a cubic Gram boundary
------------------------------------------------
Let F be the repository's verified homogeneous spectral cubic in its fixed
14 matching coordinates. Put e=ones(14), H_v=H_F(v), and Q=D_e F.
Actual spectra have a real lift x=g-he,y satisfying

 H_x y=0,       grad F(y)=grad F(x),       F(x)=0,
 F(g)=hQ(g),    Q(x)=Q(g).

These follow from grad F(-x+iy)=0, Euler's identity, and H_e e=0. The
Hessian H_x is singular even when y=0: then H_x x=2grad F(x)=0, while
x=0 would force F(g)=0, contrary to the strict formula below.

Set

 u=2t+1>=1,    v=(u^2+1)/2,
 D=1+pq+pr+qr,
 C_u=u^3-u(pq+pr+qr)+2pqr,
 L_p=up+v,    L_q=uq+v,    L_r=ur+v.

Direct cubic evaluation gives

 F(g)=-(pqr+uv)<0,    Q(g)=-D<0,
 h=(pqr+uv)/D=u/2+C_u/(2D).                                    (2)

Thus every actual gap (1) has positive height; zero and negative heights
cannot occur in this normalized subfamily.

The exact Hessian identity is

        det H_(g-F/Q e)=-4 L_p^2 L_q^2 L_r^2 C_u^2/D^2.           (3)

All L factors are strictly positive, and D>0. Singularity therefore forces

                              C_u=0,    h=u/2.                   (4)

No converse spectral-realization assertion is needed.

For reproducibility, the verifier establishes that det(H_g-hH_e) has degree
at most three in h. Rank H_e=4 gives the upper bound four, while the nonzero
vector e is a radical vector of H_g restricted to ker H_e: H_g e=H_e g.
This makes the degree-four coefficient vanish. Four exact evaluations of
det(H_g-(u/2+z)H_e), at z=0,1,-1,2, determine every coefficient and prove
(3) after the height substitution in (2). In particular,

 det(H_g-uH_e/2)=-L_p L_q L_r C_u^2/2.

The zero-ordinary slice t=0 is included: then u=v=1,
C_1=1-pq-pr-qr+2pqr, and the necessary support height is h=1/2.

2. The cubic boundary forces an established weighted certificate
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Each of the three pairs (A,B), (A,C), (B,C) is an exact row/column/inversion
relabeling of the proved weighted-minor inequality. Hence, for every z>0,

 f+z A+z^(-1)B>=0,   f+z A+z^(-1)C>=0,
 f+z B+z^(-1)C>=0.                                             (5)

If any of pq,pr,qr is at least one, one of (5), plus the unused ordinary
terms, already proves (1) nonnegative. This includes equality boundaries.
Assume therefore that all three products are strictly less than one.

Normalize P=p/u, Q=q/u, R=r/u. The equation C_u=0 becomes

                      1-PQ-PR-QR+2PQR=0.                       (6)

This is the determinant of

       G=diag(1-P,1-Q,1-R)+w w^T,   w=(sqrt(P),sqrt(Q),sqrt(R)).

All normalized pair products are less than one because u>=1. They allow
at most one of P,Q,R to exceed one. They also prevent two from equaling
one. If all three were less than one, G would be positive definite. If
exactly one equaled one and the others were smaller, its determinant would
be (1-Q)(1-R)>0 after relabeling. Both contradict (6). Thus exactly one
normalized variable exceeds one; relabel so that P>1.

Rearranging C_u=0 now gives

              p(q+r)=u^2+qr(2p/u-1)>=u^2>=1.                    (7)

Also q+r>0, since otherwise C_u=u^3>0. Put

                    z=1/(q+r),       lambda=q/(q+r).

The convex combination with weights lambda and 1-lambda of the first two
inequalities in (5) is

                       f+z A+q B+r C>=0.                       (8)

Equation (7) says p>=z. Adding (p-z)A and tO to (8) proves g(U)>=0
for every U in U(4). The other possibilities for the variable exceeding
one use the other two pair combinations. This proves the claimed support
inequality on the whole subfamily (1).

3. What transfers, and what remains open
-----------------------------------------
The theorem includes t=0 and every t>=0, arbitrary nonnegative p,q,r,
coefficient boundaries, repeated spectra and repeated matching products.
It is a concrete certificate for a family of actual six-contact gaps.
It does not assume that the necessary cubic boundary is sufficient for
spectral realization.

The equalities of the four coefficients on r0,r7,r10,r13 are essential to
the determinant calculation used here. The argument has not been extended
to independent a,b,c,d in a r0+b r7+c r10+d r13+p A+q B+r C+f.
Positivity for its uniform slice does not imply positivity on that larger
coefficient cone. A general rational Hessian reduction is preserved in
build/six_symmetric/general_reduced6.pkl for further exploration; it is
not read by the portable verifier and is not a proof of the full child.
