1. Exact determinant computation

For each listed remaining budget set J, set
  g=f34+sum_(j in J) nu_j r_j,   nu_j>=0,
and use the universal symmetric9x9 pencil M(g). The exact sparse integer
determinant algorithm computes det(32M(g)); every coefficient is divisible
by8192 and the quotient is N_J(nu)=2^32det M(g), with N_J(0)=1.

The cap coefficient t is fixed1 only for computation. For every t>0,
  det M(t f34+sum nu_j r_j)=t^9 det M(f34+sum(nu_j/t)r_j).
Thus every normalized result is uniform for arbitrary positive t. When
t=0 the cost is an ordinary nonnegative combination and already SOS.

The determinant expansion uses row/subset dynamic programming in exact
signed128-bit integers. The bound
  9! [max_(matrix entry) sum(abs(linear coefficients))]^9 <2^127
is checked separately for every input, so intermediate and final
coefficients cannot overflow. Four-bit monomial digits cannot carry
because total degree is at most9. Every factor list is multiplied out
and compared coefficientwise with the computed integer polynomial.

Actual support costs have rank M<=8 by the independently proved complex
rank-four source factorization. Consequently det M=0 is a necessary
condition. Nothing about irreducibility or global projective duality is
needed for the no-root conclusions below.

3. Every residual family really does have interior formal nonic roots

For each of the other49 families, the certificate gives a strictly
positive rational budget vector v and one exact factor R such that
  R(0)>0,  R(v)<0.
The polynomial R(sv) therefore has a real root0<s<1 by the intermediate
value theorem. At that root every remaining budget is strictly positive.
Since the canonical ordinary features are delta functions on the cap's
13 zero rows, the zero set of this cost is EXACTLY its listed contact
set. The full nonic vanishes there because R is an exact factor.

Thus uniform no-N9 exclusion is false for all49 residual families. This
does not establish actuality, neutral inertia, or failure of SOS. It
only rules out that particular proof route and directs the remaining
work toward Gram, inertia, or actual-source constraints.

4. Three independently verified uniform SOS cores

For nonnegative a,b,c use these cap-plus-ordinary costs and conditions:

  D1=a*b*c-a-c,       core f34+a r13+b r18+c r27;
  D2=a*b*c-a*b-a-b-c-1, core f34+a r7+b r10+c r20;
  D3=b*c-1,           core f34+b r18+c r23.

The D1 core is SOS when abc>0 and D1>=0. D2>=0 forces a,b>0 and ab>1,
and its core is SOS. D3>=0 forces b,c>0, and its core is SOS.

The verifier constructs four matching features for each core, checks
all24 cost identities with a rational4x4 Gram, and checks these rational
square completions exactly. Write the four feature values as x0..x3.

D1 core:
  q=(x3-x0+x1+x2)^2
    +(a+c)(x0-c*x2/(a+c))^2
    +b(x1-x2/b)^2
    +D1*x2^2/[b(a+c)].

D2 core, with d=b-1/a:
  q=(x3-x2)^2
    +a(x0-x1/a+x2/a)^2
    +d(x1+(1+1/a)*x2/d)^2
    +D2*x2^2/(ab-1).

D3 core:
  q=(x2-x0+x1+x3)^2
    +b(x1+x3/b)^2
    +D3*x3^2/b.

Every denominator is positive under the stated assumptions. The D1
condition abc>0 must NOT be dropped: a=c=0 gives D1=0 without a PSD
core. The root-domination argument below proves abc>0 automatically.

5. Exact whole-family factor domination

All24 cap-stabilizer actions permute the canonical13 ordinary features
EXACTLY and preserve the entire14-dimensional matching function space.
Both statements are checked on their complete evaluation bases. Thus
each transformed core has the same SOS theorem. There are21 distinct
D1/D2/D3 core conditions under these actions.

For a mixed-sign exact factor R, separate its positive and negative
parts. A certificate chooses one core D=P-Q, where P is the core's
positive monomial, and gives a polynomial M with nonnegative coefficients
such that the negative part is P*M. It then verifies exactly
  R+M*D=H,
where H also has nonnegative coefficients. Since R has positive constant,
its positive part is strictly positive throughout the budget orthant.
At any factor root R=0,
  P*M=R_positive>=R(0)>0.
Therefore P>0 and M>0. Also M*D=H>=0, so D>=0. The transformed small
core is SOS, and adding all other nonnegative ordinary budgets preserves
SOS. Zero budgets and the D1 denominator qualification are handled by
the automatic deduction P>0 at the factor root.

All coefficient arrays M,H and their exact polynomial identities are
stored for each certificate. A family is marked CLOSED only when EVERY
mixed-sign factor has such a certificate; all other factors have positive
constant and nonnegative coefficients and hence cannot vanish.

This proves the entire nonic zero family is SOS for22 more budget sets.
For [7,13,18,28,31], one factor uses D3 on[18,28], and the other uses a
D2 image on[13,7,31]; both are needed. Proving only the first factor
would not close that family.
