An exact 1000 by 1000 MOC certificate from the sampled cancellation family
2026-10-31

This is one proved complex, noncommuting instance in dimension 1000. It uses
two noncommuting Householder reflections, and both spectra contain four points
that do not lie on any generalized circle. The proof never evaluates the
target determinant. It bounds that determinant inside a small disk and proves
that three actual permutation products enclose a much larger disk.

The example is a new exact rationalization derived from the original spectral
seed of large_1000_pair_cancellation. It is not an extra record in the frozen
20,127-record campaign and is not asserted to be identical to its floating-
point rank-eight source. The first two original reflector directions are used
and rounded before exact normalization.

Explicit data and verifier

All 2000 complex eigenvalues, both 1000-component reflector vectors and the
three permutations are specified explicitly in

  build/moc_extensive_20261031/exact_n1000_certificate.json.

Its SHA-256 is
4cc69757772595bbafe6468b94f52c002b316a85bc858f4df8ec269ed11175a8.

Spectra are Gaussian-integer numerators divided by q=1000. Reflector vectors
v,w are Gaussian integers; their common scaling is irrelevant. Define

  A=diag(a), B=diag(b),
  H_v=I-2vv*/(v*v), H_w=I-2ww*/(w*w), U=H_v H_w.

Every matrix entry is therefore an exact rational complex number. The
standard-library-only verifier is

  python build/moc_extensive_20261031/verify_exact_n1000_certificate.py.

It uses Python integers for every decisive check. It does not use NumPy,
floating-point determinants, interval heuristics, or a numerical hull routine.
Auxiliary construction angles and logs in the data file are ignored by the
verifier. They helped choose the three permutations; they play no role in the
proof. The construction script is make_exact_n1000_certificate.py in the same
directory, and the accepted output is exact_n1000_verification.json.

First bound: the target determinant is smaller than 2^-1000

For any nonzero v, H_v is exactly Hermitian and unitary:

  H_v^2=I-4vv*/(v*v)+4v(v*v)v*/(v*v)^2=I.

Thus U is unitary and rank(U-I)<=2. Put D=A+B and Delta=UBU*-B. Then

  Delta=(U-I)BU*+B(U*-I),       rank Delta<=4.

Writing M=max_i|b_i|, unitary invariance gives ||Delta||op<=2M. Hence

  ||Delta||F^2<=4(2M)^2=16M^2,
  ||A+UBU*||F^2<=2||D||F^2+2||Delta||F^2
                    <=2 sum_i |a_i+b_i|^2+32M^2.

The exact numerator comparison in the data is

  [2 sum_i |a_i+b_i|^2+32M^2]/1000
       =226788766/1000000000 < 1/4.

Hadamard's row-norm inequality and arithmetic-geometric mean imply

  |det(A+UBU*)|
    <= (||A+UBU*||F^2/1000)^500
    < (1/4)^500 = 2^-1000.                                  (1)

This deliberately coarse bound is sufficient. It avoids all determinant
computation and uses no near-singular inverse or large-matrix factorization.

Second bound: three permutation products contain a disk of radius 2^86

The three permutations are indices 9,124,54 of the reconstructed original
128 random permutations, listed in circular order after selection. Their
products are computed as exact Gaussian-integer numerators X_1,X_2,X_3 divided
by the common positive denominator Q=q^1000. Numerators have approximately
10055--10067 bits; this is small enough for direct integer multiplication.

For each cyclic edge from X_j to X_(j+1), define

  C_j = Re(X_j) Im(X_(j+1)) - Im(X_j) Re(X_(j+1)),
  E_j = |X_(j+1)-X_j|^2.

The verifier checks the exact integer inequalities

  C_j>0,
  C_j^2 > 2^172 Q^2 E_j,                   j=1,2,3.            (2)

The first set places the origin strictly inside the counterclockwise
triangle. The distance from the origin to each supporting edge line is
C_j/(Q sqrt(E_j)); the second set makes each distance greater than 2^86.
Therefore the triangle contains the closed disk of radius 2^86 centered at
zero. Each triangle vertex is an actual permutation product of the same exact
spectra.

Combining (1) and (2) proves

  det(A+UBU*) in conv{prod_i(a_i+b_(pi_1(i))),
                     prod_i(a_i+b_(pi_2(i))),
                     prod_i(a_i+b_(pi_3(i)))}.

This triangle is a subset of the full permutation hull, proving MOC for this
specified instance. Its slack exceeds 1086 binary orders of magnitude between
the determinant upper radius and the certified triangle inradius.

Checks excluding simple commuting controls

The verifier computes v*w, v*v and w*w as Gaussian integers and checks

  0<|v*w|^2<(v*v)(w*w).

The rank-one projectors onto v and w are therefore neither orthogonal nor
identical; they do not commute. Consequently H_v H_w is not Hermitian and
cannot be a single Hermitian Householder reflection.

The verifier also computes one off-diagonal entry of UBU* exactly using only
two rows of U. That entry is nonzero and a_1 differs from a_2, so
[A,UBU*] is nonzero. Finally, it checks that the cross ratio of the first four
entries of each spectrum has nonzero imaginary part. Each spectrum therefore
fails to lie on a generalized circle, in particular on a straight line.

These checks establish that this is a complex noncommuting example, not a
hidden diagonal or single-reflection control. It remains one explicitly
specified instance. The dimension-independent disk and norm argument supplies
a sufficient test, not a proof of unrestricted MOC for all inputs.
