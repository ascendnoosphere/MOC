4-by4 matrix S write

 c_S(pi)=sign(pi) product_i S_i,pi(i),   n_S=-c_S.

The proposed generic N8' normal chart uses[I4|T]. If detT=0 and rankT=3,
write adjT=r l^T. Its singleton matching marginals are l_i T_ij r_j.
They form a matrix with zero row and column sums. If the real normal has
strictly positive singleton marginals away from the identity permutation,
this matrix is a real balanced Metzler generator: positive off-diagonal
entries, zero row and column sums, negative diagonal entries.

                 n(g)=0,   g>=0,   g(id)=0.             (1)

If that normal lies in the generic real rank3 N8' chart described above,

non-chart conormals and singular restrictions of N9 to the contact face
also require separate treatment.
