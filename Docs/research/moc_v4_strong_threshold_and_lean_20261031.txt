1. An explicit two-dimensional negative compression

Write the three critical budgets as u=(a^2,b^2,c^2), with a>=b,c>=0, and

 K_t=diag(u-t)+(t/2)J,
 T0=a(b+c)-bc,
 B=[[-b,-c],[a,0],[0,a]].

The plane is a*x0+b*x1+c*x2=0. Set N0=-2 B^T K_T0 B. Its entries are

 N00=(a-b)[ab(a-b)+a^2 c+2abc+b^2 c],
 N01=-(a-b)(a-c)(ab+ac+bc),
 N11=(a-c)[ac(a-c)+a^2 b+2abc+bc^2].

Both diagonal entries are nonnegative and

 detN0=4a^2 bc(a-b)(a-c)(ab+ac+2bc)>=0.

Hence N0>=0. This requires a>=b,c, NOT a>=b+c.

The increment P=B^T(2I-J)B has

 P00=(a+b)^2, P11=(a+c)^2,
 P01=-a^2+a(b+c)+bc,
 detP=4a^2(ab+ac+bc).

If a>0 and b+c>0, then P>0, and the exact identity

 -2 B^T K_t B=N0+(t-T0)P

makes K_t strictly negative on this two-plane whenever t>T0. No eigenvalue
root or sign-continuation argument is needed.

The zero-budget exception b=c=0 has an equally explicit plane:

 v(x,y)=(y,x-(a^2+t)y/(2t),-x-(a^2+t)y/(2t)),
 v^T K_t v=-2t*x^2-(3t/2)*y^2<0 for t>0,(x,y)!=0.

This also covers a=b=c=0. Therefore a two-dimensional strict negative
compression exists above T0 for ALL nonnegative budget degeneracies.

The older capacity threshold satisfies

 kappa/2-T0=[(a-b-c)^2-max(0,a-b-c)^2]/2>=0.

Thus the new source obstruction t<=T0 is stronger than t<=kappa/2 even
in the balanced triangle case. For u=(1,1,1), it gives t<=1 instead of the
SOS-only capacity t<=3/2.

2. The stronger bound directly constructs a Gram

Let v=(a,-b,-c). It has diagonal vv^T=u and off-diagonal sum -T0.
If 0<t<=T0, put theta=t/T0. Then

 G=(1-theta)diag(u)+theta vv^T >=0,
 diagG=u, sum_(i<j)Gij=-t.

Together with the exact V4 identities

 Z^2-2A_i A_j=r58,  A0A1=A0A2=A1A2,

this gives the full Gram diagblock(t,G,diag(v6)) in features(Z,A3,B6).
No optimization or scalar root is involved.

3. The Schur argument as an explicit five-dimensional lift

The V4 prism has first block -diag(beta), cross blocks -diag(q),-diag(p),
and upper3-block Cplus0. Nonnegative ordinary budgets give

 p_i=(t+u_i)/2>0,
 s_i=(vplus_i+vminus_i)/2 >= |q_i|,
 q_i=(vplus_i-vminus_i)/2,
 beta_i=p_i+s_i,
 d_i=s_i-q_i^2/beta_i>=0.

Let E=J-I, so detE=2 and 2C0=E K_t E. For z in R3 and y in R2, use

 r=E^(-1)B y,
 w=z-diag(q/beta)r,
 third block=0.

Completing the square gives EXACTLY

 q_prism(w,r,0)
   =-sum beta_i z_i^2+(1/2)y^T B^T K_t B y-sum d_i r_i^2.

If t>T0, this is strictly negative on every nonzero(z,y) in R5. The map
from(z,y) to(w,r,0) is linear. An actual complex determinant2 quadratic
cannot have a five-dimensional negative compression, so t<=T0 follows.

This is the full source-index bridge without invoking Sylvester inertia
as an unformalized black box.
