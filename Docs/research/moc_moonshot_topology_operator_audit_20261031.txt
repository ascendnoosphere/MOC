Independent audit of the topology and operator moonshot claims
31 October 2026

Verdict: PASS, with the scopes stated by the producers. The winding theorem
certifies attained inner regions. The fourteen-weight law proves unrestricted
complex-spectra MOC on the stated one-parameter mixer family. The operator
certificate proves full planar containment on its explicitly bounded spectral
and unitary neighborhood. None proves unrestricted MOC for all 4 by 4 mixers.

1. Closed graph walks, orbit topology, and attained disks: PASS

An abstract edge starts from a diagonal normal matrix with entries b_pi(i).
Conjugating its i,j block by a real Givens rotation produces the diagonal
matrix with those entries exchanged at the other endpoint. The endpoint is
an actual matrix in the normal orbit, independent of choices of a unitary
representative. Hence concatenating an abstract closed graph walk genuinely
produces a closed loop in the orbit. Repeated eigenvalues merely collapse some
edges or identify vertices; they do not break the concatenation.

For multiplicities m_1,...,m_r, the stabilizer is the connected group
U(m_1) x ... x U(m_r). Its inclusion into U(n) maps fundamental groups by
(k_1,...,k_r) -> sum_j k_j: one can see the generator explicitly by rotating
one coordinate in any block through exp(2 pi i t). This map is surjective.
The homotopy exact sequence of the quotient bundle therefore gives a trivial
fundamental group for the normal orbit. This covers all multiplicities,
including the scalar orbit consisting of a single point.

The determinant along a Givens edge is the claimed convex segment. The audit
rechecked the arbitrary-symbol 2 by 2 determinant identity modulo c^2+s^2=1,
so no real-spectrum hypothesis is present. The rest of the matrix contributes
the unchanged product of its diagonal pair sums.

A null-homotopy of the orbit loop supplies a disk whose determinant image
has the polygonal walk as boundary. If the image avoided q, this would be a
disk in C minus q; its boundary winding would have to vanish. Nonzero winding
therefore implies attainment. If rho is the distance to the compact boundary
walk, B(q,rho) avoids the walk and is connected, so its winding is constant.
Every point of that open disk is attained. No assumption that a continuous
image of a simply connected space is itself simply connected is used.

The abstract-graph qualification is necessary and correctly retained. A
planar crossing does not authorize jumping between unrelated lifted paths.
The proof does not fill arbitrary faces of a planarized segment drawing.

The exact n=3 and two n=4 example windings and minimum squared distances
were recomputed from the spectra. The independent crossing calculation used
the ray in direction 1+2i instead of the producer's horizontal ray; all
integer windings agree. Segment distances were independently computed by
rational orthogonal projection. Every audited walk closes at its original
permutation and every edge swaps exactly two positions.

2. The fourteen-weight dense mixer family: PASS

For V(t)=(tI+K)/sqrt(t^2+3), skew symmetry and K^2=-3I give orthogonality.
The audit independently checked each of the 70 equal-cardinality squared-
minor identities as a polynomial identity in Q[t], after clearing the
positive denominator. It also checked normalization. Thus the result is valid
for every real parameter in the stated positivity domain, not only the three
displayed parameter examples.

The displayed numerator weights are nonnegative for t>=0 and
t^4+4t-3>=0. For t>=1, putting x=t-1 gives nonnegative coefficients in every
weight numerator; their five total masses are exactly 16,16,12,4,1.

The bridge from these identities to arbitrary complex spectra is valid:
principal-minor expansion and Cauchy--Binet express the determinant as
sum_{I,J} |det V[I,J]|^2 a_{I^c} b_J. Expanding each matching product gives
the same monomial with coefficient 1_{pi(I)=J}. The 70 identities therefore
equate the entire polynomials in eight independent complex variables. No
positivity or reality assumption is imposed on those variables.

Gauge invariance also checks. Multiplication on the left or right by
diagonal unitary matrices preserves squared minor moduli. Row and column
permutations relabel the spectra and matching labels. Transposition changes
the common law by inverting its permutation labels, because the minor
indices are exchanged. Hence all stated gauges retain a positive law.

The Walsh path is not merely numerically similar to the certified family.
The audit verifies exactly that P=((I+K)/2)W is a signed permutation and

  (cI+sK)W = (xI-yK)P,
  x=(c+3s)/2, y=(c-s)/2,
  x^2+3y^2=c^2+3s^2.

On c>=s>=0 and c^2+3s^2=1, x>=y>=0. If y>0, the middle matrix is precisely
V(x/y)^T with x/y>=1; if y=0 it is the identity. This proves the entire
specified ascent arc lies in the certified arbitrary-spectra class.

3. Positive Gram bound and determinant-free center: PASS

The one-vector bound follows directly from Hadamard's determinant inequality.
Complete v/||v|| to an orthonormal basis, apply Hadamard to the corresponding
columns of C, then apply AM--GM to the other n-1 column norm squares, whose
sum is T-t. This yields

  |det C|^2 <= t ((T-t)/(n-1))^(n-1).

It remains valid at t=0, and does not assume C normal. The k-dimensional
compression version follows from the positive block determinant inequality
and AM--GM on the complementary block. Singular compressed blocks follow
by continuity, or because a zero quadratic form of a positive semidefinite
matrix is in its kernel. There is no unmentioned invertibility hypothesis.

For the supplied rational U0, the audit independently checked U0*U0=I,
reconstructed C0, and recomputed its Frobenius trace and the fixed-vector
Rayleigh quotient. They agree with the producer's exact rational numbers:

  T=10462524828662/34787905 <310000,
  t=210085915021693/34805090225070 <7.

No determinant of C0 was evaluated in the independent audit. All three
matching products were recomputed from their permutations. Their oriented
edge crosses are positive, and every exact squared-distance comparison
cross^2>10^18 edge is strict. For a triangle, positive crosses place the
origin on the inner side of each oriented edge; the minimum perpendicular
edge distance is therefore its centered inradius. The disk conclusion is
correct, rather than an angular sampling heuristic.

4. Spectral and unitary neighborhood: PASS

For entrywise spectral radius epsilon=1/100 and unitary operator-norm
radius delta=1/1000, expand the conjugation difference using the original
D_b and the perturbed D_b'-D_b separately. Since ||D_b||<255 and U' remains
unitary,

  ||C'-C0|| <= 2epsilon + 2*255*delta = 53/100.

The original fixed vector consequently gives ||C'v||/||v||<3+0.53<4,
and ||C'||_F<557+2*0.53<559. Substitution in the same Gram inequality
proves the exact conservative bound |det C'|<2*10^8. The audit checks the
integer comparison 16*559^6<27*(2*10^8)^2.

Each pair sum changes by at most 1/50, while every original pair sum has
modulus less than 700. Expansion of the product over changed factors gives
the valid uniform perturbation bound

  Delta=(700+1/50)^4-700^4
       =171507350140001/6250000 <30*10^6.

Moving each of three triangle vertices by at most Delta changes every unit
support function by at most Delta. Support-function order characterizes
containment of compact convex sets, so the perturbed triangle still contains
the centered disk of radius greater than 970*10^6. This comfortably exceeds
the determinant bound; no assumption that the orientation stays numerically
unchanged is needed.

For the claimed nonordinary spectral neighborhood, the audit recomputed
the exact separator value -42246975256. It independently checks that the
separator is nonnegative on every singleton and every two-subset permutation
indicator (52 tests, including complementary duplicates), hence on the
entire ordinary-minor cone. Its perturbation is bounded by

  (|sum w|+sum |w|)*26*Delta
     =15607168862740091/1562500 <42246975256.

The absolute value on sum w is necessary and is present. This handles the
change in the maximizing support height as well as every product. Thus the
fixed direction remains strictly outside the ordinary cone throughout the
stated spectral neighborhood, while full MOC holds for all mixers in the
stated neighborhood of U0.

5. Scope and verification limits

The exact tests and the written mathematical bridges pass. They are not
Lean proofs of the homogeneous-space topology, winding argument, or Gram
inequality. The audit does not promote the producer's floating raw-data pass
counts to exact or probabilistic statements, and does not rerun those scans.

The positive topology result supplies inner attainable regions, which cannot
exclude an exterior determinant value. The positive mixer law covers the
specified one-parameter family and its gauges, not all of U(4). The operator
family bounds both the spectra and mixer; it does not prove MOC for every
mixer at those spectra. These limitations are stated accurately by both
producers and leave the unrestricted conjecture unresolved.
