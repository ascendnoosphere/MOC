For every real orthogonal O4 and every complex b, let
 c_pi=sgn(pi) product_i O_i,pi(i)/det O,
 f_I(pi)=product_{i in I} b_pi(i),
 G_IK=sum_pi c_pi conjugate(f_I(pi)) f_K(pi).
Then G is PSD and rank at most8. For B=O diag(b) O^T, its Gram vectors
are the even Clifford vectors

with ONLY their grade-four coefficient conjugated. The twist matters.

and either sign of det O. Nine base cases reduce to ordinary identities
in two independent symmetric matrices B,C. Six residuals vanish; three
are explicit multiples of BC-CB. Jacobi complementation and a 70-sign
identity give all256 entries. Continuity handles singular B.
