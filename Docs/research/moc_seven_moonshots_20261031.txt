Seven parallel attacks on unrestricted Marcus-de Oliveira
31 October 2026

This campaign produced complete family certificates, a full canonical-face
closure, universal minor inequalities, and an all-dimensional topological
attainability theorem. These are scoped results; unrestricted MOC remains
open. Exact certificates, numerical evidence, and Lean statements are
identified separately below. See README.md and next_unrestricted_research.md
for the latest synthesis and reading order.

The target is unchanged:
  det(D_a+U D_b U*) in conv_R{product_i(a_i+b_pi(i))},
for arbitrary complex spectra and every unitary U.

The seven lanes cover random matrix theory, algebraic geometry,
topology/cohomology, fermionic representation theory, sum-of-squares positivity,
operator theory, and higher variations. Independent audits checked the
strongest results through exact calculations, inequalities, and certificates.

1. Algebraic geometry closes a full five-parameter family

Canonical eight-contact orbit 51 is now closed for every nonnegative choice
of its five coefficients:
  g=f34+a r17+b r20+p r18+q r23+r r28.
Every actual spectral support gap of this form satisfies the established
weighted-triangle capacity inequality, hence is nonnegative on all U(4).

The proof uses geometry with a direct payoff. A full exact Hessian determinant
factors into a square. In the only remaining capacity-failure region its
zero set is a connected positive hyperbola family. The HMSV secant theorem
and an order-two determinant argument force Hessian rank 12 throughout that
family. An exact anchor has inertia(5,7,2), so the whole connected family does.
Actual rank 12 real spectral vectors have inertia(6,6,2), excluding the family.
The proof includes zero coefficients, Q=0 and both signs of support height.

An independent computation used a different elimination endpoint and exact
characteristic-polynomial root counts. The algebraic and analytic audit passed.
Lean also accepted 19 scalar theorems for the difficult branch, including
D=0 implies Q>0 and Q=0 implies F<0 under the original domain hypotheses.
The secant identification and connected-inertia step remain outside Lean.
The ledger now records 14 full types and 207 patterns in the specified 58-type,
927-pattern eight-contact census. This is not a percentage of unrestricted MOC.

2. Higher variations lead to a complete spectral-family theorem

For every r>=1 and every U in U(4), MOC holds for
  a=(1,-1,-ir,ir), b=(-r,r,-i,i).
It also holds on the entire pole-free complex Möbius orbit
  a'_i=f(a_i), b'_j=-f(-b_j).

For r>1 the permutation hull has vertices
  -(r+1)^4, max(4r^2,2r(r-1)^2), +/-2ir(r^2-1).
Two of its four supporting edges lie outside the ordinary-minor cone.
Their exact support formulas are positive multiples of the existing mixed
inequality f34+r23+r28>=0 plus nonnegative ordinary-minor terms. The other
two edges have explicit nonnegative ordinary-minor certificates.

The root independently replayed all polynomial identities and checked that
the stored relabelings are genuine row/column permutation actions. The full
family proof is written mathematics plus exact certificates; Lean checks its
coefficient-positivity step. At the Walsh stationary point the effective
Hessian-kernel cubic is exactly 96 r(r^2-1)x1 x2 x3. This explains the coupled
escape that pairwise and second-order tests miss, while the global support
proof bounds every mixer without a stationarity assumption.

3. Real algebraic positivity produces a larger universal cone

Three rational Gram certificates prove new universal mixed-minor inequalities
on every complex U(4). The first uses a positive rank 19 Gram matrix. Two
boundary variants have rank 18 and two permutation contacts, respectively
related by a transposition and by a double transposition. Their positivity
comes from exact LDL/congruence arithmetic and full polynomial identities.
Unitary phase balancing and a dense Hermitian Cayley chart transfer the
certificate to every unitary, including exceptional matrices by continuity.
All three laws passed independent exact audits. Their row/column relabelings
give 192,288 and 576 inequalities, respectively.

An exact dual separates these inequalities from all 144 templates of the old
continuous weighted-law library. Thus they add positive inequalities beyond
conic propagation of those laws. This is a statement about universal unitary
positivity. It does not yet close an additional actual spectral family:
the replay found no actual cost needing them beyond the old coverage.

4. Random matrix theory gives exact finite-time positive representations

For n=4 the determinant's unitary heat mean has exactly two nonconstant rates:
  mu+exp(-4 t) X+exp(-6 t) Y.
The derivation uses the exclusion generator on squared minors and is valid
for arbitrary spectra and initial unitary. An explicit signed 24-permutation
law reproduces every common-minor coefficient before smoothing.

After a sufficient heat time there is a positive common-permutation law.
The sharper cone argument works for t>=-log(r*)/2, approximately 0.10756045,
where r* is the positive root of 2 r*^3+3 r*^2=3. Exact cone-polynomial checks
prove this threshold. A simpler explicit positive-weight formula is also
available; Lean proves its positivity, normalization and complex hull step.
Neither statement lets one undo smoothing at t=0. An exact example starts
at the permutation centroid and immediately moves away under heat flow,
refuting radial-contraction arguments.

All 5006 small n=4 rows were replayed. The exact formulas, source-independent
coefficient proofs, and numerical diagnostics are separate artifacts.

5. Topology and cohomology certify attained regions, not just hull membership

Map the abstract transposition Cayley graph of S_n to matching products,
with straight Givens segments as edges. If a closed graph walk has nonzero
winding about q, then q is attained by an actual unitary determinant.
The loop lifts to the simply connected normal orbit, and a disk filling
cannot avoid a point with nonzero boundary winding. A spanning-tree cycle
basis makes this a finite algorithm. Planar crossings may not be treated as
extra graph vertices; the proof specifically uses the abstract graph.

Exact examples certify entire attained disks, including radius sqrt(5) about
the Walsh determinant -43-24 i. The raw replay evaluated 8324 n=3..6 records;
5041/5204 guarded noncontrols have a nonzero cycle. All 78 guarded n4 misses
were clustered spectra. A miss is only a failure of this inner certificate.

A separate explicit 14-permutation law proves MOC for arbitrary spectra on
  V(t)=(tI+K)/sqrt(t^2+3),
for the stated skew matrix K, whenever t>=0 and t^4+4 t-3>=0. All 70 minor
identities hold symbolically. After the documented monomial gauge this covers
the whole Walsh-to-permutation escape arc. Lean checks the scalar weights;
the matrix identities and topology are not fully formalized there.

6. Operator positivity proves an explicit open nonordinary family

For any n by n matrix C and nonzero vector v, set T=||C||F^2 and
t=||Cv||^2/||v||^2. Positive Gram-matrix inequalities give
  |det C|^2 <= t[(T-t)/(n-1)]^(n-1).
A small compression can strengthen the same bound. Combining it with an
exact three-product disk gives a rational certificate without evaluating det C.

At the specified nonordinary n=4 spectra and dense rational unitary U0,
the proof covers every spectral perturbation of modulus<=0.01 and every
unitary perturbation ||U-U0||op<=0.03. The determinant modulus stays below
8e8 while the selected triangle contains a disk of radius greater than 9.7e8.
The designated support remains outside the ordinary cone throughout.
This proves full planar MOC on that open set, with the stated mixer restriction.
In 4167 n=4 raw cases the Gram test passed 542, versus 51 for Frobenius alone.

7. Fermionic representations identify where positive weights must change

Simultaneously reproducing every exterior-power marginal is too strong.
Exact witnesses also rule out preserving all singleton marginals, nested
DPP flag marginals, or the perfect-matching quotient block while reproducing
the actual determinant. Each witness still satisfies the original MOC.

For arbitrary complex unitary U, the real signed amplitudes
  c_pi=Re[sgn(pi) product_i U_i,pi(i)/det U]
sum to one and reproduce every common-minor coefficient. The proposed rule
using only permutations with c_pi>0 survived all 4095 nonflat complex-unitary
n=4 data rows and targeted tests. Its universal validity remains unproved.
It is a concrete surviving candidate, not a theorem inferred from its pass rate.
The exact quotient obstruction and the surviving tests are recorded separately.

The same signed amplitudes give a constructive local theorem in every fixed
finite dimension. Along U(t)=exp(tK), let v_ij be the complex displacement
from the identity matching product to the transposition (ij) product. If
  C=sum_(i<j) |K_ij|^2 v_ij v_ij^T
is positive definite as a real 2 by 2 matrix, explicit covariance-corrected
weights are positive for all sufficiently small nonzero |t|. Their signed
negative mass is O(|t|^3), whereas covariance is t^2 C+O(|t|^3), leaving
positive correction factors 1+O(|t|). The independent audit passed for
arbitrary complex phases, zero entries of K, and both signs of t.
This proves local MOC under the stated nondegeneracy condition. It gives
neither a uniform radius near singular C nor global positivity.

On the 4095 eligible n4 records the explicit formula passed numerically in
4081 cases; 14 failed the specified tolerance. An exact Walsh example has
minimum weight -2529/589540, proving that formula is not globally positive.
Positive-amplitude support selection is weaker and remains unproved.
