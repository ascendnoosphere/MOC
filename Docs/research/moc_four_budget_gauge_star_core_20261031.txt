Four-budget adjustable Gram core and exact nonic prerequisite
===========================================================

Scope and status
----------------
Consider the canonical matching cost

    g = f34 + a r7 + b r13 + c r23 + d r31,
    a,b,c,d >= 0.

This note proves an exact three-parameter description of every matching
Gram for this five-dimensional contact face, an adjustable sufficient
SOS condition, and a necessary inequality for every formal nonic root.
It does NOT prove that the sufficient condition holds at every nonic
root. Consequently this is not yet a closure of the entire family and
is not a proof of unrestricted MOC4.

Replay:
    python code/research/moc_four_budget_gauge_star_core.py
Data:
    build/moc_four_budget_gauge_star_core.json
Lean:
    code/lean/MocFourBudgetThreshold.lean

The Python replay verifies the actual 24 evaluation rows, the complete
Gram-fiber dimension, the Schur and square identities, every coefficient
in the nonic Bernstein proof, and an exact algebraic example. The Lean
file proves the actual polynomial Bernstein identity and its whole-real-
parameter implication, with no sorry or custom axiom. Its only reported
axioms are propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound.

1. Complete Gram fiber
----------------------
Use the five matching features (f34,A,B,C,D)=(f34,r7,r13,r23,r31).
Their evaluation matrix has rank 5. Quadratic evaluation has rank 12,
so the symmetric 5x5 Gram kernel has dimension 15-12=3.
Every Gram representing g is therefore precisely

    Q = [ 1   0   0    0    k  ]
        [ 0   a   x    y   -k  ]
        [ 0   x   b    z   -k  ],    x+y+z=-1.
        [ 0   y   z    c   1+k ]
        [ k  -k  -k   1+k   d  ]

The dimension statement and all 24 cost identities are exact. A quick
explanation is that AB=AC=BC are the indicator of rows {5,16}, CD is
the indicator of row 16, and f34^2=f34+2(AB-CD).

2. Two Schur complements
-------------------------
Let e=d-k^2 and v=(-k,-k,1+k). The first Schur complement, at Q00=1,
has final diagonal e and final column v. If Q is PSD then e>0: e=0
would force v=0, impossible. The second Schur complement is a 3x3
matrix whose diagonal, after multiplication by e, is

    A0 = a e-k^2,  B0 = b e-k^2,  C0 = c e-(1+k)^2.

Its three off-diagonal entries have sum -T, where

    T = d-2k^2-2k.

Thus full matching-SOS membership on this face is exactly a scalar-k
search followed by a 3x3 PSD problem with fixed diagonal and fixed sum
of off-diagonal entries. This statement includes all three Gram gauges;
it does not freeze a particular diagonal block or lose a completion.

3. A rational sufficient STAR core
----------------------------------
Suppose e>0, B0,C0>=0, B0+C0>0, and

    A0(B0+C0) >= T^2.                                  (STAR)

Choose the scaled triangle Gram

    [ A0   -T B0/(B0+C0)   -T C0/(B0+C0) ]
    [ ...        B0               0       ].
    [ ...         0               C0      ]

Its quadratic form is

    B0(z2-T z1/(B0+C0))^2
    + C0(z3-T z1/(B0+C0))^2
    + (A0-T^2/(B0+C0)) z1^2.

The complete five-feature square decomposition is

    (z0+k z4)^2
    + e (z4+(-k z1-k z2+(1+k)z3)/e)^2
    + [the preceding three squares]/e.

The replay checks this construction algebraically against the complete
Gram fiber. The STAR hypotheses therefore give a genuine matching-SOS
certificate for every parameter tuple satisfying them.

4. A uniform lower bound at every formal nonic root
----------------------------------------------------
The exact restricted nonic is (a+1)(b+1)R(a,b,c,d). Write
R=alpha d^2+beta d+gamma, with

    alpha=(1-ab)(c^2+1)+c(ab-a-b+1),
    gamma=(a+1)(b+1)(ab+ac+a+bc+b+c+1).

For u=b+c>0, substitute d=t(u+1)/u. The three Bernstein coefficients
of u^2 R on 0<=t<=1 are

    B0=u^2 gamma,
    B1=u^2 gamma+u(u+1)beta/2,
    B2=u^2 gamma+u(u+1)beta+(u+1)^2 alpha.

All their coefficients in a,b,c are nonnegative. B0>0 for u>0, B2
has positive constant 1, and

    2B1=(a+1)(b+1)u *
      (ab^2+2abc+ab+ac^2+ac+b^2c+2b^2+2bc^2+5bc+4b
       +c^3+5c^2+6c+2).

Therefore R>0 throughout 0<=d<=1+1/(b+c). The u=0 boundary has
R=(a+d+1)^2>0. We have proved, without any source or actuality
assumption,

    a,b,c,d>=0 and R=0  ==>  (b+c)d>b+c+1.               (LOWER)

The polynomial is symmetric in a,b, so the same holds with a replacing
b. This whole nonnegative statement is compiled in Lean as
whole_nonnegative_root_budget_lower_bound.

5. A positive chart for the remaining scalar problem
-----------------------------------------------------
By symmetry order a>=b. At a root, write

    a=b+Y,  d=1+(1+X)/(b+c),  Y>=0, X>0,
    k=-b/(b+c).

Let u=b+c and multiply all scaled Schur quantities by u^2:

    E=u(u+1+X)-b^2,
    A0=aE-b^2, B0=bE-b^2, C0=cE-c^2,
    T=u(u+1+X)+2bc.

These polynomials all have nonnegative coefficients. For strictly
positive b,c they are strictly positive, so all STAR side conditions
are automatic. What remains is the scalar implication

    R=0, a>=b  ==>  A0(B0+C0)>=T^2.                    (OPEN)

No proof of (OPEN) is claimed here. The cleared polynomial u^2R is
concave quadratic in a, with leading coefficient -u^2(b+1)X. This
suggests comparing its root to the linear STAR threshold

    aStar=(T^2+b^2(B0+C0))/(E(B0+C0)).

Accepted certificates, if found, must prove this inequality or supply
an alternative Gram; numerical root tests are not a proof.

6. Exact example showing why a four-budget construction is needed
----------------------------------------------------------------
Take a=14, b=1/16, c=1/25, and let d be the unique positive root of

    -57968 d^2+1642455 d+42177000=0.

Exact signs place d in (44,45). The previously proposed merged core
and D2 capacities are, respectively,

    (a+b)cd-a-b-d=-(7d+225)/16 <0,
    abd-ab-a-b-d-1=-(2d+255)/16 <0.

Thus their OR does not cover all formal roots. This does not refute
SOS membership: the new STAR construction at k=-25/41 is PSD for
every d>=44. Its STAR polynomial in d=44+X has all positive
coefficients. The example is a verified new region of the sufficient
four-budget core, not a counterexample to MOC or to matching-SOS.

Homogeneity
-----------
For cap coefficient t>0, apply the statements to budgets divided by t
and multiply the resulting Gram by t. The t=0 costs are ordinary sums
of Boolean squares. The unresolved (OPEN) implication is not bypassed
by this normalization.
