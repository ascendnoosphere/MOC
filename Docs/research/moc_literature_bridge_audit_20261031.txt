Primary-literature bridge audit, 31 October 2026

The unrestricted MOC target remains unchanged. No full proof was found or
derived in this audit. The useful changes are a new exterior-power primary
source, a precise rejection of an invalid compression induction, and a
quantifier correction that preserves the global variational route.

Sources checked

1. Y. N. Shitov, A further sufficient condition for the determinantal
conjecture, Izvestiya: Mathematics 89:4 (2025), 862-869,
DOI 10.4213/im9292e. Primary full text:
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=im&option_lang=rus&paperid=9292&wshow=paper

Theorem 1 assumes Hermitian A and at most three nonreal eigenvalues of B.
Theorem 19 lifts a proved base class A(m,k) to A(n,k), n>=m, where A is
Hermitian and B has at most k nonreal diagonal entries. It sends a real
eigenvalue b1 of B to infinity by inversion; A remains Hermitian. Corollary
18 uses Hermitian compression and real interlacing. Neither hypothesis
survives unchanged for a general complex normal A.

2. Yuki Ueda, Finite free position of maximal abelian *-subalgebras of the
matrix algebra, arXiv:2609.39677v1, 30 September 2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39677v1

Theorem 4.4 splits centered exterior-minor matrices into an inherited block
and an orthogonal residual block, giving monotonicity of their squared
Frobenius discrepancies. Theorem 3.1 excludes exact uniform all-order
minor marginals for n>=4. Example 5.5 gives the exact order-four flat
discrepancy. This concerns uniform Haar averaging, a stronger requirement
than spectral-dependent MOC weights, and supplies no support upper bound.

3. S. M. Malamud, Inverse spectral problem for normal matrices and a
generalization of the Gauss-Lucas theorem, arXiv:math/0304158v3.
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0304158v3

The paper provides complex-compression interlacing through majorization,
including exterior-product statements. This does not restore normality of
the compressed operator. Its positive statements can control spectra of a
compression; they do not establish a determinant-of-sum enclosure when the
compressed summand is nonnormal.

Exact computation 1: the failed compression induction

Let
    N = [[0,1,0],[0,0,1],[1,0,0]],
    C = [[0,1],[0,0]],
    B = [[0,1],[1,0]].
N is unitary, C is its leading codimension-one compression, and B is
Hermitian. Yet C has spectrum (0,0), B has spectrum (1,-1), and
    det(C+B) = -2,
while both eigenvalue matching products equal -1.

Thus even a codimension-one compression of a unitary matrix can fail the
eigenvalue version of MOC against a Hermitian second summand in order two.
Replacing Shitov's Hermitian compression by a general normal compression,
then applying lower-order MOC to its eigenvalues, is invalid. Complex
interlacing alone cannot fix this exact failure. This is not a
counterexample to MOC because C is nonnormal.

Exact computation 2: every versus some support maximizer

For n=2, write p=|U11|^2. Direct expansion yields
    D(U) = p (a0+b0)(a1+b1) + (1-p)(a0+b1)(a1+b0).
For any real support functional, a monomial endpoint therefore maximizes.
If a genuinely interior segment point also maximizes, the support must be
constant on the segment. Nonmonomial maximizers do not invalidate the
existence assertion needed by MOC.

For a=(0,1), b=(0,2), and U=[[3,4],[-4,3]]/5,
    D(U)=32/25, while the two monomial values are 0 and 2.
All maximize imaginary support, whose value is zero throughout. The unitary
is dense and the two spectra are simple. Therefore even such a
nondegenerate-spectrum example refutes only the unnecessary universal
assertion that every maximizing unitary must be monomial.

The useful all-dimensional variational assertion remains:
For each fixed a,b and support eta, at least one global maximizer of
Re(eta D(U)) can be chosen monomial. Compactness and planar separation
show that this is exactly equivalent to MOC. This audit supplies no proof
of that assertion in arbitrary dimension.

Exact computation 3: order-four flat residuals

Let |z|=1 and
  U(z)=1/2 [[1,1,1,1],[1,z,-1,-z],
            [1,-1,1,-1],[1,-z,-1,z]].
For the 6 by 6 matrix P2(z) of squared order-two minors, direct Laurent
arithmetic, with c=(z+z^-1)/2, gives
    P2(z) = (1+c)/2 P2(1) + (1-c)/2 P2(-1).
Also
    sum_(I,J)(P2(z)[I,J]-1/6)^2
       = (z^4+6z^2+1)/(16z^2) = (1+c^2)/4.
The 4 by 6 inclusion matrix D satisfies
    D P2 = (J4/4)D,  P2 D^T = D^T(J4/4).
The centered matrix P2-J6/6 is entirely supported on ker D, dimension two.
These identities were independently derived from all 36 minors. The
interpolation can transfer an endpoint positive matching law throughout
this flat family; the existing repository already has Walsh and Fourier
laws, so no new full-family theorem is claimed.

What changes for the next full-proof attempt

The global variational route should target existence of a monomial
maximizer, allowing nonmonomial maximizers on flat support faces. A route
through Shitov or Malamud must retain the full rectangular compression
operator, including its nonnormal coupling; merely controlling its
eigenvalues is insufficient. Ueda's residual blocks give exact coordinates
for that coupling but their discrepancy norms alone have no directional
sign. The missing step is a directional support bound tied to actual
spectral tensors or an independent global optimizer argument.
