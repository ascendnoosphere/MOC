Verdict: PASS for the stated fixed-spectra, fixed-direction local theorem

Assertion 1. The real signed coefficient identity: PASS.

For any complex unitary U, set
 gamma_pi=sgn(pi) product_i U_i,pi(i)/det(U), c_pi=Re(gamma_pi).
The denominator never vanishes. For any equally sized index sets I,J,
expansion by the two row blocks gives

 sum_{pi(I)=J} gamma_pi
 =(-1)^(sum I+sum J) det U[I,J] det U[Ic,Jc]/det(U).

Jacobi's complementary-minor formula and U^(-1)=U* give

 det U[Ic,Jc]
 =(-1)^(sum I+sum J) det(U) conjugate(det U[I,J]).

Thus the sum is exactly |det U[I,J]|^2, already real. Empty and full
subsets are included. Taking real parts gives the same identity for c.
In particular sum c_pi=1. Expanding each matching product in its selected
b-factors gives

 sum_pi c_pi product_i(a_i+b_pi(i))
 =sum_{I,J:|I|=|J|} product_{i notin I}a_i product_{j in J}b_j
                         |det U[I,J]|^2
 =det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*).

The last equality is determinant expansion followed by Cauchy-Binet on
each principal minor. This proves the identity for arbitrary complex
spectra, including repeated entries, zero pair sums, and singular matrix
sums. There is no replacement of a complex determinant by its real part.

Assertion 2. Order-two expansion for arbitrary skew-Hermitian K: PASS.

Let U(t)=exp(tK), with t real. Its inverse determinant factor is
exp(-t trace K). For the identity permutation,

 product_i U_ii(t)/det U(t)
 =1+(t^2/2)sum_i[(K^2)_ii-K_ii^2]+O(|t|^3)
 =1-t^2 sum_{i<j}|K_ij|^2+O(|t|^3).

For a transposition, its negative sign and
K_ij K_ji=-|K_ij|^2 give
c_(ij)(t)=t^2|K_ij|^2+O(|t|^3). Every other nonidentity permutation moves
at least three indices, so its product contains at least three O(t)
off-diagonal factors and has amplitude O(|t|^3).

The derivation permits arbitrary imaginary diagonal entries of K. Their
contribution cancels through order two, as the displayed formula shows.
Separately, all gamma_pi are exactly invariant under left and right
diagonal unitary multiplication of U. Neither observation licenses
deleting the diagonal part of K inside exp(tK) at all orders.

If K_ij=0, the corresponding transposition has no quadratic coefficient.
It may become negative at a later order; the O(|t|^3) bound still holds.
Identity amplitude is positive near zero. Every transposition with a
strictly positive quadratic coefficient is positive for sufficiently
small nonzero t on both sides of zero. Since the permutation set is
finite, its total negative mass satisfies N(t)=O(|t|^3).

The independent symbolic check used a general complex skew-Hermitian
4x4 matrix with sixteen independent real parameters. It checked all
twenty-four amplitudes through degree two, including every diagonal term.
This check supplements the dimension-independent derivation above.

Assertion 3. Positive-part mean and covariance expansion: PASS.

Fix the product points x_pi=(Re z_pi,Im z_pi). Set
q_pi=max(c_pi,0)/(1+N), mu=sum q_pi x_pi, and
Sigma=sum q_pi(x_pi-mu)(x_pi-mu)^T.
The denominator equals 1+N because sum c=1, so it is always positive.

The positive-part map preserves the stated remainder estimates. Therefore
q_id=1-t^2 sum|K_ij|^2+O(|t|^3),
q_(ij)=t^2|K_ij|^2+O(|t|^3), and all other q_pi=O(|t|^3).
Consequently mu=x_id+O(t^2). Writing v_ij=x_(ij)-x_id gives

 Sigma=t^2 C+O(|t|^3),
 C=sum_{i<j}|K_ij|^2 v_ij v_ij^T.

The centering subtraction contributes O(t^4) and hence does not alter C.
Repeated product points are harmless. The hypothesis C positive definite
means that the transposition displacements with K_ij!=0 span the plane.

When N>0, let mu_minus be the normalized mean of negative amplitudes.
The signed identity gives the exact formula
delta=(Re d,Im d)-mu=N(mu-mu_minus).
All these means lie in the convex hull of the fixed finite product set,
so delta=O(|t|^3). If N=0, delta=0 directly; no mu_minus is needed.

Assertion 4. Invertibility, positivity, and exact corrected moments: PASS.

Let lambda_min(C)>0. The covariance expansion gives
lambda_min(Sigma)>=(lambda_min(C)/2)t^2 for sufficiently small nonzero t,
and hence ||Sigma^(-1)||=O(t^(-2)). Since every x_pi-mu remains bounded,

 (x_pi-mu)^T Sigma^(-1)delta=O(|t|)

uniformly over the finite set of permutations. Shrink the interval until
every absolute correction is less than 1/2. Then

 w_pi=q_pi[1+(x_pi-mu)^T Sigma^(-1)delta]>=0.

The correction factors are strictly positive; zero q_pi still give zero
weights. Their sum is one because sum q_pi(x_pi-mu)=0. Their corrected
mean is mu+Sigma Sigma^(-1)delta=(Re d,Im d). Thus these are actual real
convex weights for the determinant, for both positive and negative t.
At t=0 the formula's covariance is singular, and the separate identity
permutation weight gives the target exactly, as the theorem states.

This proof gives existence of epsilon and an explicit finite formula for
the weights. It does not give a numerical epsilon in closed form. The
permutation enumeration still has factorial size; a two-by-two inverse
does not by itself make the construction efficient for large n.

Assertion 5. Exact examples and the global limitation: PASS.

The independent verifier checked all seventy subset-minor identities for
the rational Q from the producer, complex Fourier4, and a complex diagonal
phase gauge of Q. It checked exact signed determinant reproduction and
the affine correction at the stated nonordinary spectra. Q and its phase
gauge each have four negative amplitudes but twenty positive corrected
weights. Fourier4 has eight genuinely nonreal complex amplitudes, while
its sixteen positive real amplitudes already form the positive law.

A further exact direction has nonzero imaginary diagonal entries and only
two nonzero unordered off-diagonal pairs. Its C has determinant
220996413593857681493055473894400000>0, so the local hypothesis is nonvacuous
even with many zero entries of K. The direction and full C are in the JSON.

Finally, the producer's global failure is independently reproduced at

minimum weight -2529/589540. Its normalization and determinant reproduction
