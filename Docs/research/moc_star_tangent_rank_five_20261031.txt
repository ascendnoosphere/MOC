Exact five-dimensional matching SOS from Cauchy-normal tangent planes
31 October 2026

RESULT AND SCOPE

The exact actual nonordinary STAR control has a matching-SOS Gram of rank5
whose six primary vectors occupy only THREE real dimensions. Each primary
orthogonal pair lies in the tangent plane of its intrinsic Cauchy normal.
The remaining auxiliary Schur residual has rank2. This is structurally
different from the previously successful rank6 Cauchy tensor O(2) block:
cross blocks here are anisotropic, with normalized singular values1,Kij.

The certificate uses the EXACT source Cauchy Gram, the rational common
vector e=(1,2,0), and no fitted tangent phases. Eight auxiliary cross
coordinates are explicit rationals; three are specified by a unique
algebraic root in a rational box, proved to exist using exact rational
interval arithmetic. All24 matching identities are proved symbolically.

This is an instance theorem. It does not prove that every actual STAR
source admits these tangent frames or a rank5 completion. The common-vector
rule is a TWO-parameter subset of the full three tangent-phase family.

1. THE ACTUAL CONTROL AND ITS NORMALS

The actual source and all24 exact gaps are the existing control
 build/moc_star_actual_nonordinary_counterexample/exact.json.

The exact normalized STAR cost w=(a,b,c,d,e,f,u,r,s,t) is read from
 build/moc_star_directed_descriptor/exact.json
and divided by the maximum of its24 exact rational gap values. Scaling
back afterwards multiplies the Gram by a positive rational and preserves
its rank.

Let R be the positive-definite reciprocal Fricke matrix of this source,
and put Kij=Rij/sqrt(Rii Rjj). Thus K is an exact positive-definite3-by3
unit-diagonal matrix. No rounding of K is used in the theorem. Numerically

  K01=.9852958004195014,
  K02=.9688301986175681,
  K12=.9967734914591582.

Use the upper Cholesky normals

  n0=(1,0,0),
  n1=(k,y,0),        k=K01, y=sqrt(1-k^2),
  n2=(l,z,t0),       l=K02, z=(K12-k*l)/y,
                         t0=sqrt(1-l^2-z^2).

Then ni.nj=Kij exactly. Positivity of R and its principal minors ensures
all these square roots are positive and the normals are independent.

2. THREE PRIMARY TANGENT PLANES

Project the COMMON vector e=(1,2,0) into each plane ni-perp, normalize to
pi, and put qi=ni cross pi. For the first two normals this simplifies to

  p0=(0,1,0), q0=(0,0,1),
  p1=(-y,k,0), q1=(0,0,1).

For the third normal, with d=l+2z and L=sqrt(5-d^2),

  p2=((1-d*l),(2-d*z),-d*t0)/L,
  q2=(-2t0,t0,2l-z)/L.

The six primary vectors, in coordinate order(A,B,C,D,E,F), are

  sqrt(a)p0, sqrt(b)p1, sqrt(c)p2,
  sqrt(d)q1, sqrt(e)q0, -sqrt(f)q2.                (1)

Write V for this3-by6 matrix. It has rank3, and D6=V^T V has the required
diagonal(a,b,c,d,e,f) and A perpendicular E, B perpendicular D, C
perpendicular F. This is the exact primary block used by the certificate.

For any two tangent frames, the normalized two-by-two cross block has
singular values1 and |ni.nj|=Kij. By contrast, a Cauchy tensor O(2) block
has two equal singular values Kij. Therefore(1) is outside the class of
all the previously tested conformal primary kernels.

3. ENFORCING THE SINGULAR PRIMARY RANGE CONDITION

The full ten-dimensional matching basis has four auxiliary coordinates
(U,R,S,T). A positive completion of D6 MUST have cross block

                         B=V^T X,

where X is3-by4. We build this in from the beginning; no inverse of the
singular D6 is taken. The seven-dimensional reduced Gram is

                      [ I3    X ]
                H =   [ X^T   C ].                       (2)

The original ten-dimensional Gram is obtained by congruence with the
injective embedding diag(V^T,I4), so it has the same rank as H.

For arbitrary D6 and B, all24 interpolation equations reduce to ten
linear formulas determining the symmetric C and ONE additional equation

  sum_i(B_iU-B_iR+B_iT)
    +D_AB+D_AC+D_BC-D_DE-D_DF-D_EF+r-t-u=0.          (3)

Let c=V*(1,1,1,1,1,1)^T. Its first component is nonzero on this source.
We eliminate X00 using(3). The remaining eleven entries of X are free.
The checker proves symbolically that every24 interpolation residual is
an exact scalar multiple of(3), before any source values are substituted.
Thus satisfying(3) proves all24 equalities; interval residuals containing
zero are NOT used as a substitute for an exact identity.

4. A RANK-TWO AUXILIARY RESIDUAL

Put S=C-X^T X. To make H positive of rank5, it suffices to make S positive
of rank2. Use the principal pivot P=[1,2] of S and remaining indices
J=[0,3]. Define the symmetric two-by-two matrix

  F(X)=S[J,J]-S[J,P]*S[P,P]^{-1}*S[P,J].           (4)

If S[P,P] is positive definite and the three entries of F vanish, then
S is PSD of rank2 by its Schur complement. Consequently H is PSD of
rank3+2=5.

Index the eleven free cross entries row-major, omitting X00. Fix indices
[0,1,2,3,4,7,9,10] to the following rationals, in that order:

  (-11884406,1269159,-10550731,-1595531,
                  40848738,1833430,-44140871,1029319)/10^8.

The remaining indices[5,6,8] lie in a box of radius10^-8 about

  (-4724674125346062,-407666377721730,-4418747623282930)/10^16.

These are the ONLY three unknown coordinates. Their defining equations
are the three entries F00,F01,F11 in(4). All other quantities in these
equations are explicitly specified real algebraic numbers or rationals.

5. EXACT ROOT EXISTENCE AND POSITIVITY

The checker uses70-decimal absolute interval rounding. Every operation
is implemented using Python integer/Fraction arithmetic and outward
rounding. Square-root endpoints use integer square roots. This is not
a floating-point tolerance check.

Let f be the three-vector from(4), x0 the rational center, and R0 the
rational inverse-Jacobian proposal supplied in the checker. On the ENTIRE
box B, the exact interval calculation proves

  ||I-R0 Df(B)||_infinity <= .005807140,
  ||R0 f(x0)||_infinity <= 6.545*10^-16.

In particular, with radius r0=10^-8,

  ||R0 f(x0)|| + ||I-R0 Df(B)|| r0 < r0.           (5)

The map x -> x-R0 f(x) is a contraction from the closed box into itself.
It therefore has a unique fixed point. The same bound implies R0 Df is
invertible; a fixed point is exactly a root of f. This proves existence
of an exact isolated algebraic solution, not merely a numerical proposal.

Throughout the box, S[P,P] has positive first diagonal and determinant
at least2.8748880319*10^-6. Hence the Schur pivot is PD at the root.
Together with f=0 this proves the rank-two residual and rank-five Gram.

6. THE FIVE MATCHING FUNCTIONS

The certificate can be read directly as five real matching functions.
Let L2 be the positive Cholesky factor with L2^T L2=S[P,P]. In column
order(P,J), set

  Y=L2*[I2, S[P,P]^{-1}S[P,J]],

then restore the original four auxiliary column indices. Thus Y^T Y=S.
For each permutation, split its exact ten-feature row into v_pi inR6
and q_pi inR4. The five-vector

                  ( V v_pi+X q_pi, Y q_pi )              (6)

has squared norm exactly g_pi. Formula(6) is an exact matching-SOS
representation on the actual benchmark. It uses three primary and two
auxiliary dimensions.

WHAT REMAINS

The new tangent-plane matrix-capacity route survives the exact control
where the canonical descriptor/Pi kernels failed. The uniform problem is
now concrete: choose tangent frames and eleven cross entries from an
arbitrary actual nonordinary source so that the rank-two Schur equations
have a positive root. The eight rational cross coordinates above were
discovered on this instance; they are not a uniform source formula.
No unrestricted MOC theorem follows from this instance certificate.

The strict contraction and pivot bounds also give a qualitative local
statement. The Cauchy normal construction, all radicals, and the matching
completion are analytic near this source. The dependent3-by3 Jacobian is
invertible. Hence the same common vector and eight fixed cross entries
extend to a rank-five completion on an open neighborhood of this STAR
cost, with the three dependent entries chosen analytically. This is a
local persistence statement, not a global source-selection theorem.

Reusable numerical API for additional source-dependent work:

  model=TangentPrimary(cost_coordinates=w10)
  V=model.common_primary([1,2,0],signs=(1,1,-1))
  result=model.fit(V)

The API derives the Fricke/Cauchy matrix directly from the supplied ten
cost coordinates, retains the complete reduced cross/auxiliary space,
and reports numerical feasibility only. The three-phase method is also
available independently of the common-vector subset.
