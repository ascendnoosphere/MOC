A FULL-UNITARY SOS INEQUALITY BEYOND THE WEIGHTED-MINOR LIBRARY
2026-10-31

Exact result. In the canonical 82-ray conventions, every U in U(4) obeys

 f34(U) + r7+r10+r13+r17+r20+r22+r27+r30+r31
        + (r18+r23+r28)/50 >= 0.                              (1)

Here each ordinary r_j is its squared-minor function, and
 f34=r7+r10+r17-r25.
The inequality is universal in U, with no spectral assumptions. It adds a
positivity direction outside the conic hull of all existing continuous
weighted-minor laws and all ordinary minor inequalities. It does not yet
prove that every actual spectral support gap lies in the enlarged cone.

Full-unitary transfer, with its hypotheses.
For every complex unitary U, Idel and Wolf's Theorem2 gives diagonal
unitaries L,R such that W=LUR has all row sums and column sums equal to1.
Every absolute minor is invariant under left and right diagonal phases.
Thus it suffices to prove(1) for balanced W. There is no positivity,
nonzero-entry or genericity condition in this balancing theorem.
Primary source: Martin Idel and Michael M. Wolf, Sinkhorn normal form for
unitary matrices, arXiv:1408.5728v3, Theorem2,
 https://arxiv.org/html/1408.5728
The abstract and source were checked; the repository's prior source-level
review is docs/research/global_sos_first_lemmas.md.

Let H be the real Walsh matrix divided by2, with its first column all1/2.
Balanced W has the form H diag(1,V) H^T with V in U(3). On the dense Cayley
chart V=(I+iT)(I-iT)^(-1), T is arbitrary Hermitian3. Write
 q(T)=(coordinates(T),coordinates(adj(T)),det(T)) in R^19,
where coordinates are11,22,33,Re12,Im12,Re13,Im13,Re23,Im23.
The exact verifier proves

 det(I+T^2) times left_side_of(1) = q(T)^T X q(T), X positive definite.

The canonical coordinates in the verifier additionally swap columns2,3
(zero-based) to move the common contact permutation to identity. The
inequality is invariant under this relabeling of the all-unitary domain.
The JSON lists the precise minor labels after this swap.

Since det(I+T^2)>0, positivity follows on this chart. Balanced unitaries
whose V has eigenvalue-1 follow by continuity, approximating V withinU(3)
by matrices avoiding that eigenvalue. Diagonal-phase balancing then proves
(1) for all U(4). This route does not use an SO(4)-to-U(4) convex-profile
bridge or assert that every unitary is equivalent to a real matrix.

How the identity is checked.
The numerator of every compound minor in the balanced Cayley chart is
linear in the twenty entries(1,T,adj(T),det(T)). The verifier obtains a
rational representative from the rank14 permutation-indicator matrix,
then absorbs the constant/cofactor and T/determinant identities and adds
eight cubic trace identities plus six real quartic identities. Its gauge
parameters, in verify_parity_reduced_completion.py's fixed order, are
 (7/3,0,0,-13/2,13/2,7/2,0,0; 1/3,1/3,1/3,2/3,2/3,2/3).

Crucially, the final check does not infer a functional identity merely
from agreement at24 permutation matrices. It independently represents
each r_j by one actual squared minor, uses the explicit mixed identity
above, expands all400 products of the twenty polynomial coordinates,
and checks all838 resulting monomial coefficients exactly. The same
matrix splits by conjugation parity into9x9 and10x10 rational blocks;
every diagonal entry in both exact LDL factorizations is positive.

Strict separation from the old library.
The exact cap dual in code/research/verify_cap_weighted_library_obstruction.py
has f34 value-1, each of r18,r23,r28 value1/2, and every other capordinary
value0. It is nonnegative on all34 ordinary rays, every weighted law
f_j+t*r_a+t^(-1)*r_b for all48 mixed rays and t>0, and all nonnegative
conic combinations of these laws. Its value on(1) is
 -1 + 3/100 = -97/100.
Thus adding or propagating any number of the existing weighted laws
cannot prove(1). This is an exact separation, not numerical infeasibility.

Immediate sufficient support cone.
Let an actual support gap have canonical cap representation
 g=lambda*f34+sum_{j in Z34} c_j*r_j with lambda,c_j>=0.
If c_j>=lambda on the nine indices in(1), and
c18,c23,c28>=lambda/50, then g(U)>=0 for everyU4. The coefficient c0 can
be any nonnegative number. One may also spend any part mu of lambda on
(1), subtract its ordinary costs, and cover the remaining lambda-mu by
the existing weighted-triangle criterion. All row/column relabelings
supply equivalent sufficient cones.

The known actual mixed seed is already weighted-triangle certified; its
small off-triangle coefficients prevent using(1) for the whole mixed
coefficient at once. The completed coverage replay below found no newly
covered actual spectral family or residual contact face.

Two-contact boundary improvements, exact.
Let O={7,10,13,17,20,22,27,30,31} and T={18,23,28}.
The same all-unitary transfer proves

 f34 + sum_{j in O\{10}} r_j + (1/8)sum_{j in T}r_j >=0,      (2)
 f34 + sum_{j in O\{31}} r_j + (1/16)sum_{j in T}r_j >=0.     (3)

Their exact contact sets are respectively{1,6} and{1,7} in lexicographic
permutation order. Those pairs differ by a double transposition and a
single transposition, respectively. Both Gram matrices have rank18.
The same exact verifier checks all838 coefficients, an explicit nonzero
kernel vector, and a positive18x18 principal LDL factorization. Since the
deleted kernel coordinate is nonzero, principal positivity plus the
kernel identity proves positivity of the full19x19 Gram. This is a finite
rational proof, not a numerical eigenvalue check.
