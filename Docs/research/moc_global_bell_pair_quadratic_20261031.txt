Global coupled-rotation quadratic form of the four-dimensional determinant
31 October 2026

Scope. This is a global exact identity with arbitrary complex spectra. It
reduces the remaining inequality to a source-coupled quadratic optimization
on SO(3) x SO(3). The sharp inequality is not proved here.

1. Why the real two-rotation model covers arbitrary U(4)

The whole-profile convexification in
docs/theorems/profile_geometry/algebra_unitary_realification.md, Sections1-5,
expresses every U(4) squared-minor profile as a positive mixture of at most
four real SO(4) profiles. The proof fixes one real row and decomposes the
remaining three real PSD trace-one effects on its real three-dimensional
orthocomplement into orthonormal bases. Cauchy-Binet transfers this identity
to determinants simultaneously for every pair of complex spectra.

Thus a uniform support inequality for real SO(4), with arbitrary complex
spectra, proves the unrestricted U(4) case. This does not replace SO(4)
profiles by permutation profiles.

The companion moc_global_tracezero_spinor_normal_form.py establishes the
global Mobius critical-centroid reduction to trace-zero spectra. Write

 a=(x0+x1+x2,x0-x1-x2,-x0+x1-x2,-x0-x1+x2)/2

and similarly b in terms of y. In the quaternionic correlation model,

 det(Da+O Db O^T)=q(X+R Y S^T),
 q(C)=[2 tr((CC^T)^2)-tr(CC^T)^2]/16,

where X=diag x, Y=diag y, and R,S range independently over SO(3).
All squares, traces and transposes here are complex bilinear; no conjugation
of x or y is implicit. This distinction matters for arbitrary complex data.

2. An entrywise quadratic, not a quartic in the rotations

Set

 u=sum_i xi^2, v=sum_j yj^2,
 xhat_i=product_(k!=i) xk, yhat_j=product_(k!=j) yk,

 alpha_ij=xi^2 yj^2/4,
 beta_ij=[xi yj(xi^2+yj^2-(u+v)/2)-xhat_i yhat_j]/2,
 c=q(X)+q(Y)-uv/8.

Then the full determinant is exactly

 q(X+RYS^T)
   =c+sum_ij [alpha_ij(Rij^2+Sij^2)+beta_ij Rij Sij].       (1)

For any exposed direction eta, simply replace c,alpha,beta by their real
parts after multiplication by eta. Equation (1) becomes a real quadratic
optimization on two compact three-dimensional rotation groups.

Proof. For an arbitrary complex3-by3 matrix D, put v=tr DD^T and
w=tr XD^T. Direct polarization of q gives the polynomial identity

 q(X+D)=q(X)+q(D)-uv/8
  +tr(X^2 DD^T)/4+tr(X^2 D^TD)/4
  +tr(X^3 D^T)/2+tr(X DD^TD)/2
  -(u+v)w/4-<cof X,cof D>/2.                              (2)

The final cofactor term follows from
tr((XD^T)^2)-tr(XD^T)^2=-2<cof X,cof D>.
Substitute D=RYS^T. Orthogonality and determinant+1 give

 DD^T=RY^2R^T, D^TD=SY^2S^T,
 DD^TD=RY^3S^T, cof D=R(cof Y)S^T.

Also q(D)=q(Y). Collecting entries in (2) yields (1).

3. The classical24 vertices are explicit

Take a permutation sigma of three labels and signs eps_i with product+1.
Let R and S have the same permutation support, with Rij Sij=eps_i on
that support. Their individual signs can be chosen to put both in SO(3).
At this point (1) is

 c+sum_i [2 alpha_(i,sigma(i))+eps_i beta_(i,sigma(i))].     (3)

There are6*4=24 possibilities. Their values are exactly the24 original
products product_i(ai+b_pi(i)). Equivalently they are
q(diag(x_i+eps_i y_sigma(i))). This is the Weyl signed-assignment action
of type D3=A3. The checker verifies all24 polynomials, with x and y free.

The global missing inequality is consequently very concrete:

 max_(R,S in SO3) Re eta RHS(1)
   =max_(sigma,prod eps=1) Re eta RHS(3).                  (4)

Equation (4) is asserted only as the target. Allowing arbitrary unrelated
alpha,beta would be false: the known nonnegative-on-permutations formal
mixed cost f34 has a negative real-SO4 evaluation. The coupled formulas
for alpha,beta in terms of the SAME x,y therefore cannot be discarded.

4. Direct Procrustes stationary/Hessian equations

Use real exposed coefficients alpha,beta,c. The Euclidean gradients are

 G_R=2alpha entrywise-times R+beta entrywise-times S,
 G_S=2alpha entrywise-times S+beta entrywise-times R.

Stationarity is exactly symmetry of R^T G_R and S^T G_S. Put these two
symmetric matrices equal to Lambda_R,Lambda_S. For arbitrary real skew
3-by3 K,L, the full six-dimensional tangent Hessian is

 2sum_ij alpha_ij[(RK)ij^2+(SL)ij^2]
 +2sum_ij beta_ij(RK)ij(SL)ij
 -tr(Lambda_R K^T K)-tr(Lambda_S L^T L).                    (5)

This is a small global source-dependent quadratic system, without contact
classification, surrogate nonic components, or replacing physical rotations.
A genuine Procrustes/no-spurious-above-hull theorem for the specific coupled
coefficients in (1) would prove MOC4 via (4). A generic quadratic-rotation
theorem or an arbitrary coefficient-cone argument cannot be substituted.
