1. The13-parameter quadratic form

Every universal pencil has a unique expression

 Q_g(H)=<A,cof H>+sum_(i,j) B_ij H_ij^2,                    (1)

where A is arbitrary real3-by3 and B has all row and column sums zero.
Here <X,Y>=tr(X^T Y), cofactor means the matrix of signed2-by2 minors,
and vec(H) is row-major. Constants in g disappear. Thus A has9 parameters
and B has4, accounting for all13 dimensions of M(V14).

This directly explains the eight common isotropic three-planes. On
H=l_i v^T, cof H=0 and each coordinate of l_i has square1/4, so the
diagonal term vanishes by the column sums of B. On H=u l_j^T the row
sums give the other four planes.

The converse is part of the exact linear-algebra replay: every one of
the14 universal matching basis matrices has form(1), and the map has
the already proved rank13. Formula(1) is a coordinate form of the
whole pencil, rather than a special family or contact restriction.

2. The24 signed-permutation evaluation formula

For each permutation pi of four labels let P_pi have entries
(P_pi)_(i,pi(i))=1 and put O_pi=L^T P_pi L. These are exactly the
24 signed3-by3 permutation matrices whose three nonzero entries have
product+1. Let

 K=[[0,-1,1],[1,0,-1],[-1,1,0]],
 D=(8/9) K^T B K,
 c=mean_pi g_pi.

Then the full matching function is recovered from(1) by

 g_pi=c+4<A,O_pi>+sum_(i,j) D_ij (O_pi)_ij^2.              (2)

The constant is independent because A contributes zero mean and D is
balanced. Equation(2) is verified at all24 permutations for every one
of the14 basis columns, and hence for ALL real or complex g in V14.

For a fixed ordinary coordinate permutation sigma of three labels,
the four permitted signs epsilon_i have product+1. Nonnegativity of g
is exactly the following24 scalar inequalities:

Thus a global Gram reconstruction can be tested against an explicit
three-by-three signed-assignment problem, with no contact census.

3. Universal rational reconstruction from an invertible kernel

Suppose H is invertible and M(g)vec(H)=0. Put

              T=B entrywise-times H,
              s=sum B_ij H_ij^2.

Then ALL nine cofactor coefficients are determined by B and H:

             A=[2H T^T H-sH]/det H.                       (3)

Proof. The gradient of(1) is

 E=2T+det(H)[alpha H^(-T)-H^(-T) A^T H^(-T)],
 alpha=<A,H^(-T)>.

Multiplying E=0 on the left and right by H^T gives

 det(H) A^T=2H^T T H^T+<A,cof H> H^T.

Euler's identity gives <A,cof H>=-s at a zero gradient, proving(3).
Conversely substituting(3) makes E=0 identically. The exact checker
clears det(H) and verifies all nine resulting polynomial identities
with NINE independent H entries and FOUR independent balanced-B
parameters. No numerical normal or special kernel is used.

The formula applies on the invertible-kernel chart. It does not by
itself dispose of singular-kernel or lower-rank real-neutral strata.
The global normal form(1)-(2) remains valid on those strata.
