EXACT FAILURE OF THE p=-1 PROJECTOR SECTION ON AN ACTUAL SPECTRAL COST
2026-10-31

Result and scope

The explicit weighted-projector Gram section with power p=-1 need not be
positive semidefinite, even on an actual nonnegative4x4 spectral supporting
cost with four contacts. The example below nevertheless has a different,
elementary positive semidefinite matching Gram. It is not a counterexample to
the full matching-SOS cone or to MOC. In particular it does not refute the
stronger proposal combining the old triangle certificate with a half-power
projector section.

Exact independent replay:
  python code/research/moc_projector_section_exact_replay.py

This uses only SymPy and the Python standard library, no input files, numerical
optimization, nonic equation, or future-lift criterion. It verifies all24
finite rational spectral gaps, all20 projector-section interpolation
identities, an exact strictly negative quadratic form, and a separate exact
PSD Gram. The replay passed. The discovery/generation implementation is
code/research/moc_projector_section_actual_obstruction.py.

Actual cost, specified by four small rational column parameters

Let the24 permutations be in lexicographic order and take
  beta0 = -81/100 -203i/500,
  beta1 = -11/1000 +1001i/1000,
  beta2 = -41/200 +3i/500,
  beta3 = -31/125 -32i/125.
Set u_pi=beta_pi(0)*(1+beta_pi(1)), w_pi=u_pi*beta_pi(3), and
  cloud_pi=(Re u_pi,-Im u_pi,Re w_pi,-Im w_pi).
The contacts are C={6,15,20,22}. The three cloud-contact differences have
rank3. Take their primitive integer null vector n and the contact height h;
choose the sign for which h-cloud_pi*n is nonnegative for all pi. Divide
these rational gaps by their common factor to obtain primitive integer g.
All gaps outside C are strictly positive.

This is an actual finite spectral cost, not merely a formal cofactor cost.
After applying the same positive primitive normalization factor k, put
  mu=k(n0+i n1), eta=k(n2+i n3), x=mu/eta,
  a=(-1/2,-1,0,1/(x-2)),
  b_j=1/(beta_j+2),
  eta_fin=2*eta*(x-2)*product_j(beta_j+2).
Then, exactly for all24 permutations,
  g_pi=k*h-Re(eta_fin*product_i(a_i+b_pi(i))).
The standalone replay checks these24 identities directly over Q(i).

Exact failure of the section

Let B be the24x14 basis obtained by taking the first independent ordinary
matching-indicator columns, in increasing k=1,2 and lexicographic subset
order. Let N be SymPy's standard rational nullspace basis of B[C,:] and
R=B[C-complement,:]*N, a20x10 matrix. Write gbar=g/max(g), and on the20
positive entries put
  a_i=1/gbar_i,
  D=diag(a_i^2), M=R^T D R, H=R M^(-1) R^T,
  A=D*(H Hadamard H)*D,
  ell=A^(-1)*a,
  K=R^T diag(a_i^2*ell_i) R.
The proposed p=-1 Gram is congruent to K. Its physical Gram is
M^(-1)*K*M^(-1), whose diagonal evaluations equal gbar exactly.

For the integer vector
  v=(-32,-56,-12,-12,-12,0,44,47,12,-33),
the standalone exact calculation proves
  v^T K v < -2800.
Its approximate value is -2822.547541260465. The full exact rational value
is saved in the certificate; its numerator and denominator together have
12656 decimal characters. No floating point sign test is used in the proof.
For orientation, the correlation-normalized Gram has least eigenvalue
approximately -.0118057962 and the20x20 frame condition is approximately
207345; these numerical diagnostics are not needed for the exact proof.

Separate PSD certificate for this same cost

In the14 canonical features (f34,r_j for j in Z), take the diagonal Gram
diag(lambda,beta_j), with the two symmetric off-diagonal entries indexed
by18 and23 set to -lambda. The identities
  f34^2=f34+2*delta_5,
  r18*r23=delta_5,
  r_j^2=r_j
show exact interpolation. Every coefficient is nonnegative and the only
nontrivial block determinant is
  beta18*beta23-lambda^2
  =308191889385927586334393339521266091939046884657004>0.
Thus this alternate Gram is PSD. The replay also checks that all14 of
these features belong to the ordinary matching-indicator span.

Consequently the obstruction is to this particular projector section.
It supplies no separation from the full intrinsic matching-SOS cone.
