Q1 feasibility and positive H gaps are insufficient for matching SOS
31 October 2026

There is an exact rational H-face cost g with all20 noncontact gaps
strictly positive and a positive definite5-by5 Q1 Gram for
(n,a0,a1,b0,b1), but g is outside the full matching-SOS cone. This only
refutes replacing the nonic condition by Q1 feasibility. The cost does
NOT satisfy N9=0 and is not a spectral or MOC counterexample.

The complete standalone certificate is
  python code/research/moc_h_face_q1_not_sos.py
using only SymPy and the Python standard library. It contains all rational
data, without input files or floating-point sign tests, and passed.

The Q1 Gram has unit diagonal and supplies marginals and correlations.
Its positive rational LDL pivots are checked exactly. The hidden fourth
correlator is chosen as
  D=1-max(|C00-C11|,|C10-C01|)-1/1000000,
so all four double gaps are positive. The16 single gaps are positive too.
The four H contacts are exactly[0,1,6,7].

Let B be the14 independent ordinary matching-indicator columns and let
F=B*ker(B_H) be the rational24-by10 contact feature matrix. The supplied
signed weights y have a STRICTLY positive definite contact moment
  F^T diag(y) F,
proved by ten positive rational LDL pivots, but
  y.g < -3/1000
(approximately -.00337663141). This rigorously excludes every matching
SOS representation of g. Indeed any PSD Gram representing a cost zero
on H must annihilate the four contact evaluation vectors, and therefore
factor through this contact feature space.

The script also evaluates the exact restricted nonic factor and checks
that both det(M) and det(M)*K*D+P6 are NONZERO. Thus this is not an
obstruction to the stronger ALL-N9H inclusion sought by the main proof.
That equality must control the cross-context vectors beyond the5-by5
elliptope condition. The3-by3 hidden-D completion lemma retains those
additional constraints; Q1 alone discards them.
