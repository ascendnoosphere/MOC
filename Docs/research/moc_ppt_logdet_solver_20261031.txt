Logdet discovery for the reduced MOC positivity cone
31 October 2026

Scope

This is numerical certificate discovery for the independently established
14-dimensional PSD / 4-dimensional partial-transpose-corner cone. It does
not prove unrestricted MOC, and a failed or limited run is not an
infeasibility certificate. The returned matrices are retained so they can
be audited independently and, where useful, rounded into exact proofs.

Implementation

 code/research/moc_ppt_logdet_solver.py

The reduced physical cost convention is g_pi=j_pi^* T j_pi. At exact zero
contacts, a basis N of the orthogonal complement of the contact vectors
enforces T=N S N^*, with S Hermitian. Usually S is13-by-13 for a vertex
support or12-by-12 for a two-contact edge support. All cross coordinates
remain free. The exceptional corner is C(S)=PT((N S N^*)[0:4,0:4]).

The unknowns are all real Hermitian coordinates of S, the nonnegative
ordinary-ray weights w, and a real Phase-I shift t. Cost equations
 A(S,w)=g
are maintained through every Newton step. QR row selection removes only
dependent cost equations; all24 original costs are checked afterward.
The barrier minimizes

 t - mu [logdet(S+t I)+logdet(C(S)+t I)+sum_k log(w_k+t)].

For two orientations, both S and corner logdet terms are included.
If t<0 and all shifted inequalities hold, then all unshifted constraints
are strictly positive. The implementation also accepts an unshifted
strictly feasible candidate directly. Acceptance independently checks the
full14 matrix eigenvalue, the exceptional4 corner eigenvalue, all weights,
all24 costs, and contact annihilation. No iteration-limit conclusion is
promoted to infeasibility.

Analytic gradient and Hessian use
 d(-logdet F)[D] = -Re tr(F^{-1}D),
 d2(-logdet F)[D,E] = Re tr(F^{-1}D F^{-1}E).
Finite-difference directional checks of both passed on the seed problem.
An equality-constrained Newton KKT system and backtracking line search
keep the shifted matrices positive definite. The ordinary-weight barrier
is handled analytically as well. NumPy and SciPy suffice; no external SDP
solver or network installation is required.

Contact bases, linear operators, cost matrices and their right inverses
are cached by contact set and orientation count. This is useful when
testing many spectrum pairs with the same permutation contact pattern.

Cold benchmarks

One orientation, without warm starts:

 seed eta=-25+i:39 Newton steps,1.62 seconds;
 mixed0037:53 steps,2.49 seconds;
 mixed0066:48 steps,2.27 seconds;
 seed edge[7,20]:47 steps,1.60 seconds.

All produced cost residuals at approximately1e-16 and strictly positive
contact-face/corner/weight margins. The corresponding projection searches
had long numerical plateaus; mixed0066 required28,400 DR iterations. This
is a performance comparison, not evidence of infeasibility of either cone.

A nine-contact near-triangle test approaches a boundary and was reported
unresolved at its requested barrier limit. Such degenerate faces may need
additional reduction of the exceptional corner. Strict Phase-I is not an
infeasibility oracle for boundary-feasible problems.

Random seed710324 creates32 fresh spectrum pairs:
 12 Gaussian-integer pairs with components in[-9,9];
 8 pairs with unequal integer scales1,3,10,100;
 6 pairs with distinct but nearly repeated Gaussian-integer entries;
 6 fresh integer perturbations of the known nonordinary seed.

All complex products, convex hulls, supporting normals, support heights,
gaps and contact indices are computed using Python integer arithmetic.
The integer convex hull uses exact cross products. Every hull edge is
included. Large exact values are stored as decimal strings, preventing
loss when JSON readers use floating-point numbers. Exact contact sets and
normalized exact gaps are passed directly into the numerical optimizer.

There are249 edge costs, each with two exact permutation contacts:

Reproduction

 python code/research/moc_ppt_logdet_solver.py --label seed_one --check-derivatives
 python code/research/moc_ppt_logdet_campaign.py --workers 4 --max-outer 14 --max-inner 35

The campaign supports using only one orientation; that is the setting
used for the249 results. No universal containment theorem is inferred
from these finite cases. An exact positive certificate for a single cost
requires a separate rational or formal verification.
