ACTUAL INPUT

The universal symmetric9x9 inverse-quadratic pencil is M(g). An actual
complex spectral support cost has rank M(g)<=8, by the separate complex
rank-four source factorization. Consequently det M(g)=0. This rank fact
alone is used; no sign/inertia refinement or projective-duality theorem
is needed below.

In every family write
  g=t f34+a r_i+b r_j+c r_k,  t,a,b,c>=0.
If t=0, g is an ordinary nonnegative sum of Boolean features, already an
SOS. Thus assume t>0. The exact formulas below give2^32 det M(g).

FAMILY1

Contacts C1=(1,6,7,8,9,12,14,17,20,23).
Remaining ordinary indices(i,j,k)=(13,18,27).
The determinant is
  t^4(a+t)(a+c+t)R,
where
  R=-abc+abt+at^2+b^2c+bct+bt^2+ct^2+t^3.
Hence det M(g)=0 forces R=0. Since all parameters are nonnegative and
t>0, the equation implies abc>=t^3>0, so a,b,c are all positive.

In the full contact-null matching-function space, which has dimension4,
the UNIQUE interpolating Gram is
  Q1=[[a+c+t,-t,-c-t,-t],
      [-t,b+t,0,t],
      [-c-t,0,c+t,t],
      [-t,t,t,t]].
The verifier constructs a complete contact-feature basis F and checks
g_pi=F_pi Q1 F_pi^T at ALL24 permutations. The quadratic evaluation map
has rank10, so this is the full Gram, not a restricted feature ansatz.

Pivoting the last diagonal t leaves the Schur complement
  [[a+c,0,-c],[0,b,-t],[-c,-t,c]].
Its first two leading principal minors are a+c and b(a+c), both positive.
Its determinant D satisfies, using R=0,
  D=abc-t^2(a+c)
   =abt+b^2c+bct+bt^2+t^3>0.
Therefore Q1 is positive definite. Equivalently its quadratic form has
the explicit rational SOS
  t(x3-x0+x1+x2)^2
  +(a+c)(x0-c*x2/(a+c))^2
  +b(x1-t*x2/b)^2
  +D*x2^2/[b(a+c)].
All denominators are strictly positive as already proved. Thus EVERY
nonnegative formal determinant zero in this family lies in full C0.

FAMILY2

Contacts C2=(1,6,7,8,9,12,14,20,22,23).
Remaining ordinary indices(i,j,k)=(7,18,27).
The determinant is
  t^3(a+t)^2(c+t)P,
where
  P=abc+abt+act+at^2+b^2c+bct+bt^2+ct^2+t^3.
All coefficients are positive and P>=t^3>0. Every prefactor is strictly
positive for t>0. Thus M(g) is nonsingular, impossible for an actual
support cost. The only actual possibility is t=0, the ordinary case.

FAMILY3

Contacts C3=(1,6,7,8,9,12,15,17,20,23).
Remaining ordinary indices(i,j,k)=(13,18,23).
The determinant is
  t^3(a+t)^2(b+t)(c+t)(at-bc+t^2).
Thus det M(g)=0 forces
  bc=t(a+t)>=t^2>0.
In particular b,c>0.

The UNIQUE full four-feature Gram is

      [-t,b+t,t,2t],

  t(x2-x0+x1+x3)^2+a*x0^2

Thus EVERY nonnegative formal determinant zero in this family lies in
full C0, including the boundary a=0, where this Gram has rank2.
