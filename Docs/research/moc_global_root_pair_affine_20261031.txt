Exact root-pair geometry for a global flag-space maximum principle
31 October 2026

Scope. The identities below hold for arbitrary n, arbitrary complex spectra,
and every physical unitary flag. They supply a global flat-orbit propagation
mechanism. They do not exclude an above-hull stationary maximum and do not
prove unrestricted MOC.

1. The determinant is affine on every elementary SU(2) Bloch sphere

Write C=U*DaU+Db and d(U)=det C. Fix i,j and delta=bj-bi. Let Pij swap these
two eigenvalues of Db, keeping the eigenvectors U fixed, and set

  dij=d(U Pij),       t=det C[complement{i,j},complement{i,j}].

The exact polynomial identity is

  dij-d(U)=delta(adj(C)ii-adj(C)jj)-delta^2 t.                 (1)

No inverse or nonsingularity hypothesis is needed. If a two-dimensional
complement is invertible, the Schur determinant formula reduces the claim
to a 2-by-2 determinant. There the variable block is a fixed scalar matrix
plus a fixed multiple of n.sigma, with n on the unit sphere; its determinant
is affine in n because det(n.sigma)=-|n|^2=-1. Polynomial continuation removes
the invertible-complement assumption. The same proof works for every n.

For K=Eij-Eji, writing c=cos theta and s=sin theta gives

  d(U exp(theta K))
   =c^2 d(U)+s^2 dij+cs delta(adj(C)ji+adj(C)ij).              (2)

For K=i(Eij+Eji), the corresponding formula is

  d(U exp(theta K))
   =c^2 d(U)+s^2 dij+i cs delta(adj(C)ji-adj(C)ij).            (3)

Every phase between these two axes is covered by their linear combination.
Thus the entire pair orbit has an affine Bloch-sphere determinant image.
For generic spectra this image is a filled ellipse in the complex plane.

2. An exact global averaging/Laplacian identity

Normalized Haar averaging on the elementary pair SU(2) kills the Bloch
coordinates and therefore gives the positive identity

  integral_SU2 d(U R) dR = (d(U)+dij)/2.                     (4)

The paired infinitesimal generators obey

  (Kreal^2+Kimag^2)d(U)=4(dij-d(U)).                         (5)

For invertible C, X=C^-1, and zeta=eta detC, the support version is

  Hreal+Himag
   =4 Re zeta[delta(Xii-Xjj)-delta^2 det X[{i,j},{i,j}]].     (6)

Jacobi's complementary-minor identity makes (6) identical to (1) and (5).
In particular this is valid at the original physical U. It neither changes
the spectra nor replaces the unitary by a contraction.

Equation (4) is a genuine positive local expectation. Iterating unconditioned
pair averaging only produces the symmetrized determinant, not a pointwise
positive law on permutation products. Conditioning its continuous process
on an arbitrary final unitary is an additional step; it has not been proved
to preserve a positive permutation representation. A spectrum-independent
claim of that form would conflict with the existing common-profile law
obstructions.

3. Stationary supports are exactly sinusoidal on a whole pair orbit

Let f(U)=Re eta d(U). At a stationary flag both transverse coefficients in
(2)-(3) have zero real support. Consequently, for either axis and indeed
every pair SU(2) rotation,

  f(U exp(theta K))=f(U)+(f(U Pij)-f(U))sin(theta)^2.          (7)

Thus a positive pair Hessian gives an actual finite ascending move. A zero
pair Hessian gives a stronger conclusion than second-order flatness:
the entire elementary SU(2) orbit has exactly the same support value.

At a global maximum, every point of such a flat orbit is itself a global
maximum. Hence its gradient vanishes and its full Hessian is negative
semidefinite everywhere on that orbit. This propagates all maximum
constraints, and permits subsequent tests at any point of the flat orbit.
It is a concrete possible ingredient for a source-dependent degenerate
diffusion or a finite sequence of flat moves followed by an ascent.

If the flat root orbits, including the additional flats obtained after
such moves, generate all of SU(n), f would be constant on the flag space.
For nonconstant f a trapped maximum must therefore have a genuinely smaller
flat-orbit saturation. Excluding an above-hull trapped maximum still needs
an actual-source argument. No graph-connectivity conclusion is assumed.

4. What positivity must still prove

At any stationary flag with invertible C and distinct bi,bj, first order
stationarity is equivalently

  zeta delta Xji = -conjugate(zeta delta Xij).               (8)

In particular |Xij|=|Xji| and

  zeta^2 delta^2 Xij Xji=-|zeta delta Xij|^2 <=0.

The normality of U*DaU=C-Db remains essential. In entries it gives

  [C,C*]ij=(conjugate(bj)-conjugate(bi)) Cij
                         -(bj-bi) conjugate(Cji).          (9)

A sufficient global theorem would show that (8)-(9), full maximum
conditions, and

  f(U)>max_pi Re eta product_i(ai+b_pi(i))

force an ascending direction or flat-orbit propagation that reaches one.
Neither (8) nor (9) alone supplies this conclusion. Positive weights on
the fixed paired traces in (6) cannot manufacture positivity when all
their individual swap gaps are nonpositive; mixed Givens directions or
propagated frames may be necessary. Harmless noncommuting stationary
points must not be mistaken for counterexamples to this above-hull target.

For comparison, no smooth nonconstant globally subharmonic function exists
on a compact flag under a nonsingular elliptic operator. Any proposed
absorbing diffusion must use a noncompact cell, a degenerate/singular
operator, or a source-dependent correction whose sign is actually proved.
Equations (4)-(9) do not yet construct it.
