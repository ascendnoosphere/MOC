Theorem. Let an actual n=4 spectral support gap, in the established canonical
24-permutation coordinates and after positive normalization, have the form

  g=f34+a r17+b r20+p r18+q r23+r r28,   a,b,p,q,r>=0.

Then its three compensator budgets satisfy C(p,q,r)>=1. The established
weighted-minor triangle certificate therefore proves g(U)>=0 for every U(4).
All five coefficients are independent. This closes orbit51, including its
coefficient boundaries, conditional on the already audited repository inputs
listed below. No unrestricted-dimension conclusion follows.

Write u=p+q, v=pq, and define

 F=ab(r-1)-a-b-pqr-1,
 Q=(a+b)(r-1)-pq-pr-qr-1,
 A=(1-r)[pq+r(p+q)-2r-1],
 B=(pq-1)(r^2-r+1)+r(p+q-2),
 C0=(r+1)[pq(1-2r)+r(p+q)-1],
 D=ab A+(a+b)B+C0,
 N=-(a+1)(b+1)(p+1)(q+1)D.

The letter C0 here is a polynomial, not the triangle capacity C.
The exact cubic model gives F=F(g), Q=D_1 F(g), and, whenever Q!=0,

 det H_F(g-(F/Q)1) = -4 N^2/Q^2.                         (1)

The portable verifier reconstructs the original cubic, computes the full
six-variable Hessian pencil by exact fraction-field elimination, and checks
Q^3 det(H_g-(F/Q)H_1)=-4 Q N^2 as a polynomial identity. It does not infer
(1) from sampled parameters or assume a reconstructed nonic is correct.

For an actual support gap the real vector x=g-h1 is the negative real part
of a complex critical matching-product vector. Thus F(x)=0, rank H_x<=12,
and, if that rank equals12, its inertia is (6,6,2). Cubic polarization gives
F(x)=F-hQ. These facts hold for either sign of h.

Suppose, towards contradiction, that the triangle capacity is less than1.
Its explicit formula partitions this region into the following cases:
(i) all three budgets are <=1; (ii) p>1 and p(q+r)<1; (iii) q>1 and
q(p+r)<1; (iv) r>1 and r(p+q)<1. Values equal to1 are included in(i).

1. Cube and the p/q stars.

In(i), A<=0 and B<=0. Also

 -C0/(r+1)=(1-r)(1-pq)+r(1-p)(1-q)>=0.

Equality would require at least two budgets to equal1, and hence capacity
at least1. Thus C0<0 in the insufficient-capacity part of the cube and D<0.
Here F<=-1, so Q=0 is incompatible with F-hQ=0. Equation(1) now contradicts
actual Hessian singularity.

In(ii), put delta=1-p(q+r)>0. Then q,r<1. The identities

 A=(1-r)[-delta+r(q-2)],
 B=-delta(r^2-r+1)+r[pr(1-r)+q-2],
 C0=-(r+1)[delta+rq(2p-1)]

show A,B,C0<0: pr<1, q<1 and 0<=r<1 make the bracket in B negative,
while r^2-r+1>0. Consequently D<0. Again r<1 gives F<=-1, excludes Q=0,
and contradicts(1). Case(iii) follows by the explicit p/q symmetry.

2. The remaining r star.

Now r>1, u=p+q<1/r, and 0<=v=pq<=u^2/4<1/4. Set

 A0=1+2r-v-ru>0, so A=(r-1)A0>0.

The displayed formulas show B<0 and C0<0. First dispose of Q=0 without
using (1). If s=a+b and Q=0, then s=(v+ru+1)/(r-1), with 0<v+ru+1<9/4.
Since ab<=s^2/4,

 F <= [(v+ru+1)^2-4(v+ru+1)]/[4(r-1)]-vr-1 <0.

Thus no actual gap in this star has Q=0. Every remaining actual candidate
must have D=0 by(1).

Put t=-B/A>0 and k=t^2-C0/A>t^2. The positive solutions of D=0 are exactly

 a=t+s,  b=t+k/s,   s>0.                                (2)

Indeed b(aA+B)=-aB-C0>0 forces both b>0 and a>t; completing the product
then proves(2). In particular a+b>4t.

Every point of this entire D=0 branch has Q>0, not merely the actual points.
To see this, set Q0=v+ru+1. From v<1/4 and ru<1,

 -4B>3r^2+5r-1,
 A0 Q0<(9/4)(1+2r),
 -4B-A0 Q0>3(r-1)^2+(13/2)(r-1)+1/4>0.

Therefore 4t(r-1)>Q0 and Q=(a+b)(r-1)-Q0>0.

The base domain r>1, p,q>=0, r(p+q)<1 is connected: use coordinates
r>1, P=rp>=0, R=rq>=0, P+R<1. Formula(2), with s>0, parametrizes the
whole positive D=0 branch continuously. It is therefore connected,
including p=0 or q=0. The entire branch remains in the same r-star domain
and in Q>0.
