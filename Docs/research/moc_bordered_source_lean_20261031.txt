Bordered spectral source: formal determinant reduction
2026-10-31

Status

MocBorderedDeterminantFactor.lean, MocBorderedSourceCongruence.lean,
and MocDeterminantPolarization.lean compile with Lean 4.19.0 and only
propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. No extra axioms or sorry occur.
The canonical universal-pencil source checks are a separate, ongoing
formalization in MocUniversalSourcePencil.lean; this note does not claim
those checks have passed yet.

1. All border charts, with the cofactor transpose fixed

For complex u,v in C^3 and any complex 3-by-3 matrix T, let

 B(u,v,T) = [[0,u^T],[v,T]].

Lean proves det B = -u^T adj(T) v. In particular u is the left argument
and v the right argument of the adjugate, even when T is nonsymmetric.

Choose i,j with u_i v_j nonzero. Delete row j and column i, retaining
the natural increasing orders of the two surviving row/column indices.
For those row/column indices r,s put

 Q_rs = u_i v_j T_rs - u_i v_r T_js
        -v_j u_s T_ri + v_r u_s T_ji.

All nine chart identities are proved by finite case analysis and ring:

 det Q = (-1)^(i+j) u_i v_j (u^T adj(T) v).

Scaling the first row of Q by -1/[(-1)^(i+j)u_i v_j] represents det B
as a two-by-two determinant. When the nine entries of T are arbitrary
real-linear complex-valued forms, the resulting four entries are still
real-linear forms. If u or v is zero, the zero four-form representation
works. Thus no singular T or border chart is removed by a genericity
argument.

Theorems include bordered_complex_linear_factor,
bordered_real_linear_factor, no_negative_five_bordered, and its positive
analogue. The five-dimensional obstructions use the independently formal
four-positive/four-negative-square theorem in
MocComplexDeterminantInertia.lean.

2. The inverse-pencil normalization

For K,W,A,B complex 3-by-3 matrices with KW=WK=I, and a one-by-three row
u and three-by-one column v, Lean proves

 [[0,uK],[Kv,AK+KB]]
   =diag(1,K) [[0,u],[v,WA+BW]] diag(1,K),

and consequently

 det [[0,uK],[Kv,AK+KB]]
   =(det K)^2 det [[0,u],[v,WA+BW]].

It also proves that multiplying an arbitrary block spectral matrix on
the left/right by diag(0,K) produces exactly this bordered source. The
scalar top-left blocks and the unused row/column blocks cancel. A
separate finite reindexing proof identifies the sum-type block matrix
with the explicit Fin 4 matrix used by the border-factor theorem.

3. Reconstruction from symmetric coefficients

For q(x)=L0(x)L3(x)-L1(x)L2(x), with four complex-valued real-linear
forms on R^n, define

 P_ij=[q(e_i+e_j)-q(e_i)-q(e_j)]/2.

MocDeterminantPolarization.lean proves

 q(x)=sum_ij x_i x_j P_ij

using finite sums and linearity. The diagonal formula also gives q(e_i).
This reduces the explicit universal M9 source identity to 45 symmetric
coefficient identities rather than an unstructured expanded degree-six
polynomial identity. The latter checks are verified in Lean, not accepted
from a symbolic package.

Scope

These formal algebraic results do not prove unrestricted MOC4. They make
the necessary actual-source inertia bound available after the explicit
universal-pencil identification is proved. The V4 analytic SOS client is
already formal conditional on that source representation; the separate
V4 prism identification is finite algebra still to be joined to it.
