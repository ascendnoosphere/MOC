Reading the figures
Each panel pair shows record-count distribution and source-separated median/10–90% n trend. Continuous values use sign(x)log10(1+|x|/s), where s is the median nonzero absolute value for that field. Min/median/max are also printed in original units.
Nearly constant large seed controls subtract their transformed median to avoid a floating-offset label collision; this has no effect on ranks or histogram counts. Discrete/constant distributions use original labels. Trend y axes use the signed-log transform even for discrete fields. Count axes are logarithmic. Trend x axes are logarithmic in n; large grids use 18 logarithmic n bins.
Missing entries are not imputed. Coverage percentages refer to all 20,127 rows, not to an eligibility-conditioned subset. A large missing fraction often records analysis scope rather than a failed calculation.
These rows are neither independent nor identically distributed. Optimizer restarts share spectra; families, mixers, n and sources are selected by design. Correlations and envelopes are descriptive diagnostics, not mathematical evidence.

Important diagnostic limits
Constant no-violation or all-ordinary flags describe a finite, selected numerical campaign. They do not prove the conjecture or establish coverage of the difficult mixed-cap region.
Normalized floating errors near zero can have both signs. A sign count alone cannot turn roundoff, an optimizer excess, or a reconstruction residual into a mathematical counterexample.
The n coordinate and many permutation/count/seed variables encode the generator. Strong trends in these controls are sampling artifacts, retained here so they remain visible.
The source-separated n correlations still mix families and mixers within each source. Weak association with n is not absence of structure, and no pooled association is treated as a theorem.
Some null-only paths are container sentinels: topological first_bad_edge is otherwise array-valued, and sparse triangle is otherwise an object. Their nulls are recorded explicitly rather than misrepresented as missing numeric measurements.

Findings from viewing every page

The 13 constant panels and three null-only panels are retained, including all-false robust_violation and outside_ordinary_edges flags, constant sample counts 128 and 256, the schema version and fixed campaign seed. Twenty-six canonical fields are more than 90% missing against the full joined source. Dense verification fields have only 72 observations; one exact bound has one. No scalar numeric nonfinite observation was found.

Several near-zero plots are dominated by numerical scale. F015/F080 (pages 3 and 14) have 2,383 negative sampled-hull margins, but their minimum is only -2.66e-15. Their signed-log scale is 2.31e-66, which creates a dramatic transformed range without a large negative error. F040 (page 7) has 142 negative nonmonomial scores, minimum -3.55e-15. These sign counts are not counterexamples. Optimizer support excess reaches only 1.17e-15; 324 of 3,189 optimizer success flags are false.

Large-n normalization loses information: F063 (page 11) records 800 exactly zero scaled determinant moduli, while F064 has 813 underflow flags. The two recorded diagnostics are not interchangeable. The appendix preserves their difference and does not infer failure from either alone.

Flat medians can conceal the intended stress tests. The input global log scale is zero in 8,871 of 9,364 observations despite a range near -250 to 250. Median and 10–90% curves are therefore flat while the histogram retains the rare tails. Boolean median curves are majority summaries, not success percentages. A single n=4 point is not a trend.

Failures of proposed sufficient certificates remain visible: F091 has 3,506 false affine-weight flags among 6,711 eligible rows; F101 has 1,636 purity-contraction failures among 4,520 rows; F135 has 7,305 negative local disk radii among 15,404 finite observations. F243 has only 51 Frobenius-triangle passes among 4,167 rows, and F245 has 542 Gram-triangle passes. These are failures or successes of particular certificates, not of MOC.

The signed-amplitude affine minimum (F239, page 40) is negative in 31 of 4,095 rows at the raw sign level, reaching about -0.0018. This raw sign count intentionally differs from tolerance-filtered failure counts in the focused analysis. Pinching outside the full hull (F248, page 42) occurs in 910 of 4,167 recorded flags; this diagnoses the proposed pinching construction.

Weak within-source n correlations are retained in the CSV. Examples include determinant phase, rho=0.00180 in small/full and 0.00231 in reflectors; the ellipsoid certificate, rho=-0.00283 in small/full; and optimizer elapsed time, rho=0.00462. Phase is circular, and elapsed time and certificate eligibility depend on the sampling design. These small rank correlations neither establish independence nor erase family-specific effects.

Seed and source-line trends reveal generator ordering. Large seeds were centered after the signed-log transform so floating-offset labels remain readable; their original extrema stay in the inventory. Permutation counts encode the complete-enumeration cutoff and sampled regimes. Their correlations with n are controls, not discoveries.
