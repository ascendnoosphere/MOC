4. The all-orders argument

The real analytic inverse-function theorem gives local coordinates
  (v,u)=(four free parameters, nine gaps g_D).
J is real analytic there because B2 B3>0. Section3 proves J(v,0)=0.
Hadamard's lemma, explicitly by the fundamental theorem of calculus,
  J(v,u)=sum_j u_j H_j(v,u),
  H_j(v,u)=integral_0^1 (partial J/partial u_j)(v,t u) dt.
At the base all H_j=1 by Section2. Shrink the coordinate neighborhood to
a product box with segments (v,t u) inside it. Continuity then gives all
H_j>0 throughout that neighborhood. For an actual nonnegative support,
every u_j=g_j>=0. It follows that J>=0, with equality only if u=0.

This proves an actual open-neighborhood statement, rather than just a
finite-order Taylor test. No assertion about distant actual supports is
made. No explicit numerical neighborhood radius has been certified.

5. Why J implies the requested triangle condition locally

Shrink the neighborhood so B1<min(B2,B3), as holds at the base. The
largest of sqrt(B_i) is then sqrt(B2) or sqrt(B3), so
  max(0,sqrt(Bmax)-sum sqrt(Bothers)) <= |sqrt(B2)-sqrt(B3)|.
Consequently the triangle capacity is at least
  B1+B2+B3-(sqrt(B2)-sqrt(B3))^2
   =B1+2 sqrt(B2 B3) >=2tau.
Thus the full sufficient PSD3 Gram criterion holds for every nearby
actual nonnegative canonical-cap cost, including directions leaving the
five-dimensional critical face.

7. Finite spectra and limits of the conclusion

The common Mobius transformation T(z)=1/(z-2) sends the projective data to
finite spectra
  a'=(-1/2,-1,0,1/(x-2)),  b'_j=1/(b_j+2).
All denominators are nonzero. The 24 finite products equal kappa times
the projective products, with
  kappa=1/[2(x-2) product_j(b_j+2)].
Replacing eta by eta/kappa preserves every gap. This transformation is
analytic on a neighborhood. Conversely the three distinct a-points can
be normalized analytically to 0,1,infinity. Hence the theorem is not
restricted to a formal projective degeneration.

The conclusion rules out local triangle failures at this boundary point.
It does not prove the global triangle condition, global matching-SOS
membership, or unrestricted MOC. A global proof still needs a mechanism
away from this neighborhood, or a classification of every possible
boundary minimum.

8. Historical global triangle stationarity target (subsequently refuted)

The local theorem supplies one controlled neighborhood, not a covering of
the actual canonical cap. The following NO-BAD-MINIMUM target was proposed
before the exact actual counterexample was found. It CANNOT hold globally;
no global triangle proof should now be pursued from this formulation.

Normalize tau=1 and minimize the triangle capacity C(B1,B2,B3) over actual
nonnegative canonical-cap gaps. At a smooth putative minimum kappa<2,
let gamma_i=partial C/partial B_i. If the three square-root lengths form
a triangle, gamma=(1,1,1). If sqrt(B1)>sqrt(B2)+sqrt(B3),
  gamma1=(sqrt(B2)+sqrt(B3))/sqrt(B1),
  gamma2=(sqrt(B1)-sqrt(B3))/sqrt(B2),
  gamma3=(sqrt(B1)-sqrt(B2))/sqrt(B3),
and permute these formulas when another length is largest.

Put t=delta3-delta1-delta9-delta12-delta22 and let I be the active zero
canonical coefficients, identified with their permutation slots. KKT gives
nonnegative multipliers lambda_j and the explicit real 24-vector
  q=gamma1 delta18+gamma2 delta14+gamma3 delta9
       -kappa t-sum_{j in I}lambda_j delta_j.
In the projective chart z_pi=x u_pi+v_pi, stationarity in height, eta, x,
and the four complex b variables requires the finite polynomial system
  sum q_pi=0,
  sum q_pi u_pi=0,   sum q_pi v_pi=0,
  sum q_pi partial_{b_k}(x u_pi+v_pi)=0  (k=0,1,2,3).
These equalities are complex except the first. In particular
  sum lambda_j=sum gamma_i+3 kappa.
The local equality point realizes kappa=2 and lambda_j=1 on the nine
critical contacts, with zero multipliers on contacts8 and15.

A sufficient global advance would prove that every feasible solution of
this KKT system with kappa<2 has a descending feasible direction (possibly
of higher order), or belongs to an already controlled collision/contact
stratum. This avoids the false stronger assertion that every nonpermutation
critical point of a fixed physical support objective is a saddle. Cases
with zero B_i, nonregular contact Jacobian, or noncompact normalizations
must be included; they cannot be discarded as generic exceptions.

There is a useful complex-leaf refinement of this target. Fix one contact
j0 and freeze the COMPLEX values eta(z_j-z_j0) for all other active
contacts j. These holomorphic equations preserve their real zero gaps.
Along every smooth complex leaf the gap coordinates are pluriharmonic.
Because C is concave, C-kappa tau is plurisuperharmonic. A local minimum
therefore forces this function to be constant on that leaf. In the strict
unbalanced region its Levi form is strictly negative unless the three
normalized coordinates B_i/tau are constant on the leaf.

The strict-concavity calculation is explicit. Write l_i=sqrt(B_i), assume
l1>l2+l3, and put dB_i=B_i r_i. Then
  -2 d^2 C
   =l1 l2(r1-r2)^2+l1 l3(r1-r3)^2-l2 l3(r2-r3)^2
   =(l1-l2-l3)[l2(r1-r2)^2+l3(r1-r3)^2]
       +[l2(r1-r2)+l3(r1-r3)]^2.
Its kernel consists exactly of common scaling r1=r2=r3. Thus any proposed
strict-unbalanced negative minimum must lie in the explicit algebraic
rank-drop locus where the complex derivatives of the three normalized
triangle coordinates vanish on the contact leaf. In the balanced region
the analogous necessary condition is constancy of the holomorphic linear
functional corresponding to B1+B2+B3-kappa tau.

The exact counterexample shows that a global classification would have to
retain genuine triangle failures. The broader question whether all actual
support gaps lie in the FULL matching-SOS cone remains open.
