Global positive permutation-law investigation
=============================================

This work follows the redirection to unrestricted MOC mechanisms only.
It does not enumerate or close partial spectral/contact families.

Outcome
-------
There is an exact positive first-step conditioning identity for every
unitary matrix and every pair of spectra. Its residual matrices have a
rank-one defect from unitarity. Three natural repairs are false:

  1. Requiring each conditional residual independently to satisfy a
     lower-dimensional permutation-hull law fails even when the original
     spectra are positive, distinct and real. An exact dense 4x4 example
     blocks every initial row and every initial column choice.
  2. Keeping all Born one-point marginals |Uij|^2 in the final positive
     permutation law is false for unrestricted complex spectra.
  3. Even keeping one arbitrarily prescribed row of Born marginals is
     false. Choosing a favorable row adaptively remains a separate open
     candidate; no claim for it is made.

These are obstructions to proposed proof mechanisms, not counterexamples
to MOC. Both marginal-law counterexamples below have explicit positive
three-permutation representations of the actual determinant.

1. Exact global positive first-step identity
--------------------------------------------
For any invertible T define

    F(a,b,T)=det(Da T+T Db)/det T.

If U is unitary this equals det(Da+U Db U*). Fix any row i. Laplace
expansion and the unitary cofactor identity give

    F(a,b,U)=sum_j |Uij|^2 (ai+bj)
      F(a_without_i,b_without_j,U_without_i_without_j).       (1)

The probabilities |Uij|^2 are nonnegative and sum to one. If Uij=0,
the original polynomial Laplace term is zero; no undefined conditional
ratio need be evaluated at that branch.

The replay checks the generic symbolic 4x4 Laplace identity before
unitarity is imposed. The proof by Laplace expansion is identical for
every n. It is therefore an actual global algebraic identity, not an
assumption of convex-hull membership.

Let T be the deleted minor and let r be the deleted row with the selected
column omitted. Unitarity gives

    T* T=I-r* r,   ||r||^2=1-|Uij|^2.

Thus T has singular values 1 (n-2 times) and |Uij|. The residual is a
postselected rank-one filter, not an (n-1)-dimensional unitary orbit.

2. No adaptive row/column choice repairs independent residual laws
------------------------------------------------------------------
Let v=(1,2,3,4) and U=I-vv^T/15. This is a dense rational orthogonal
matrix. Take positive distinct real spectra

    a=(6,1,8,15),    b=(12,1,11,6).

The following positive-probability branches of (1) have residual values
outside the real interval spanned by all six residual permutation
products. Row/column indices are zero-based:

    deletion    residual        residual vertex interval
      (0,1)     16982/5             [3591,5187]
      (1,0)       3957              [2548,3808]
      (1,2)     20216/5             [2646,4032]
      (2,1)       3199              [3213,4641]
      (3,3)    -39244/15             [680,1989]

These branches cover every possible first row and every possible first
column. All entries of U are nonzero and all factors ai+bj are positive.
Consequently choosing the first row or column adaptively cannot turn
every residual into an independently valid lower-dimensional hull law.
The exact weighted first-step identity nevertheless holds for each row.
A successful recursion must couple residual branches or alter the
conditional observable and probabilities.

3. The full Born-marginal law is false
--------------------------------------
Set U=O/sqrt(525), where

    O=[  5  16 -10 -12]
      [ 16 -11 -12   2]
      [-10 -12  -5 -16]
      [-12   2 -16  11].

The replay checks O^T O=525 I and det O=525^2. Use spectra

    a=(0,1,1+i,-1+i),    b=(0,1,3,-3i).

For all 24 permutations, exactly,

    Re product_i(ai+b_pi(i))=6 cap(pi)+sum_i D_i,pi(i),

where cap is nonnegative (its complete 24-entry list is in the data) and

    D=[-6  4   0  12]
      [ 0  6   0   0]
      [ 0  0  -6   0]
      [ 0  0   0   0].

Any probability law retaining all marginals q(pi(i)=j)=|Uij|^2 therefore
has real expected product at least

    sum_ij Dij |Uij|^2=454/75.

The actual determinant is

    z=21064/3675-(5848/18375)i,
    Re z-454/75=-394/1225<0.

This is an exact separating inequality, not a numerical infeasibility
status. Nevertheless z belongs to the ordinary permutation hull: in
lexicographic permutation order, indices (0,6,10) with weights

    (105949/275625, 5848/165375, 479788/826875)

give z exactly and all weights are positive. Hence the rejected extra
requirement is preservation of the full Born marginals.

4. A prescribed single row is also too restrictive
--------------------------------------------------
Keep the same U and prescribe its row 0. Take

    a=(0,1,197/50-193i/50,7/100-19i/100),
    b=(339/100-31i/50,21/100+4i,43/100+17i/100,
       -21/100+7i/50).

Let Ppi be the permutation products and, for j=0,1,2,3, define the
explicit conditional support bound

    hj=max_{pi(0)=j} Re[(6218+7832i)Ppi].

Any law retaining row0 Born marginals must satisfy

    Re[(6218+7832i) E Ppi] <= sum_j |U0j|^2 hj.

Direct exact arithmetic gives actual minus bound

    45051533162935063/13781250000000 > 0.

Thus this prescribed-row law is false. The data include the four exact
maxima, actual determinant, and a separate positive ordinary-hull
three-vertex representation. This result does not say that every row
fails for the same spectra, and does not refute adaptive row selection.

5. Why harmonic/Clark repair of a single filter cannot be automatic
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For a rank-one polar filter

    T(z)=V(I+(z-1)pp*)W,

with V,W unitary, det T(z) is linear in z. The change in Da T+T Db has
rank at most two. Consequently the filtered determinant ratio has the
global Laurent form

    F(T(z))=A z+B+C/z.

For |z|=1 the matrix T(z) is unitary. Poisson averaging its boundary
observable at an interior z gives A z+B+C conjugate(z), leaving the
pole contribution C(1/z-conjugate(z)). It cannot simply be discarded.

For example, in dimension two take V=W=I, p=(1,1)/sqrt(2), and
a=b=(0,1). Then

    T(z)=[z+1 z-1]/2,   F(T(z))=1/2-z/4-1/(4z).
         [z-1 z+1]

The unitary boundary values are (1-cos(theta))/2 in [0,1], whereas
F(T(1/3))=-1/3. Thus no positive boundary probability measure whatsoever
can reproduce this filtered value independently. A spectral-dependent
Clark measure cannot overcome that interval obstruction without adding
other branches or changing the observable.

Global research implication
---------------------------
Formula (1) supplies positive first-step weights but not a positive
recursive law. A successful unrestricted construction must control the
polar pole terms jointly, or replace the Born probabilities by a genuine
spectrum-dependent transform. Neither independent harmonic repair nor
universal preservation of Born marginals is a viable general theorem.
No unrestricted positive permutation-law formula has been proved here.
