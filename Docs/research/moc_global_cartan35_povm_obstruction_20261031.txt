The Gaussian35 source measurement does not give a uniform MOC law
================================================================

Scope
-----
This is an exact obstruction to one proposed WHOLE-source proof mechanism.
It is not a counterexample to MOC, and none of the formal sources below
is asserted to be an actual spectral source. A measurement that retains
the full14 source coordinates in its positive factors is not excluded.

Fix the physical orthogonal mixer Q59 and its canonical eight Clifford
amplitudes a=V^T x, where x denotes the14 link-pattern source coordinates.
They obey the Cartan equation

  a0*a7-a1*a6+a2*a5-a3*a4=0

on M8. Let b(a) be the35 symmetric three-qubit Pauli quadratics, with the
Cartan quadratic omitted. Consider arbitrary constant Hermitian PSD
effects E_pi on C35, with arbitrary nonzero PSD success sum G, and ask for

  sum_pi (p_pi(x)-d(x)) b(a(x))^* E_pi b(a(x))=0

for EVERY complex spectral source x. This would yield quartic positive
source-dependent permutation probabilities whenever success is nonzero.

Exact obstruction
-----------------
1. The complete scalar linear syzygy space for b on M8 has dimension216.
   An exact basis consists of168 pure-Cartan syzygies and48 mixed ones.
   The latter are the six mixed HMSV critical quadrics times eight a_i.
   The complete basis and its source-ideal witnesses are exported by
   moc_global_cartan35_fierz.py in Q59_full216.json. Completeness uses an
   exact rank lower bound470 for the combined cubic coefficient matrix,
   together with216 independent exact syzygies and ideal rank196.

2. The35 b coordinates are independent on M8. Therefore the proposed
   scalar measurement identity implies that each row of the linear pencil

     L(x)=sum_pi (p_pi(x)-d(x)) E_pi

   is one of these216 syzygies. Hermitian effects may be replaced by their
   real parts: conjugate the identity at conjugate sources and average.
   This preserves PSD and nonzero success. Thus L may be assumed real
   symmetric.

3. Split the14 source coordinates into a8 and six complementary v's.
   Pure-Cartan syzygies have no v coefficients. Write the multiplier of
   the48 mixed syzygies as a35-by48 matrix. Symmetry of the six v-coefficient
   matrices of L imposes a3570-by1680 homogeneous linear system. It has
   FULL column rank1680 over GF(101). Consequently EVERY mixed multiplier
   is zero, before any normalization or PSD condition is imposed.

   The exact rational system is generated from the216 syzygy basis and
   the complementary source coordinates. Whole syzygies are scaled to
   primitive integer tensors; no floating point is used in the rank
   certificate. The1680 independent row indices are saved. A numerical
   QR audit independently finds rank1680, with smallest normalized pivot
   about .0088; it is not used for the proof.

4. Thus L(x)b(a(x))=0 now follows from the Cartan equation ALONE. The
   measurement identity extends to all formal14 sources whose a8 image
   is Cartan-null, with the six complementary coordinates arbitrary.

5. This extension is incompatible with positivity. Let e be the constant
   source, p_pi(e)=1, a(e)=(1,0,...,0). Choose a strictly positive matching
   cost g close to f34+e/1000. Its physical mean is negative. At the base:

     d(g)=-51519/3481000,
     a(g)=(-51519/3481000,30/3481,120/3481,-60/3481,
            24/3481,-78/3481,-12/3481,-352/3481).

   Put P=a1*a6-a2*a5+a3*a4, h=a0-P/a7, and x=h*e-g. Then a(x) is
   Cartan-null, but

     d(x)-p_pi(x)=g_pi-d(g)>0  for EVERY pi.

   At the base the minimum strict gap is55/3481. Small rational
   perturbations retain all these strict inequalities. The verifier
   constructs35 such rational formal sources whose b(a) vectors have
   rank35 over GF(1000003). For each source, the measurement identity is
   a sum of strictly negative numbers times nonnegative Born weights.
   Hence every E_pi annihilates all35 b vectors. Their spanning property
   forces every E_pi=0, contradicting nonzero success.

Consequence for the active moonshot
----------------------------------
The next positive construction must put the six missing source directions
inside its square factors, rather than only in scalar syzygy coefficients.
Two live examples are the mixed77 feature space generated by a_i*x_j,
and the full91 degree-two source ring / spin-one Brauer space. Their
positive interpolation remains unproved.
