Global triality construction: explicit data, source inverse, missing positivity
=============================================================================

This construction produces the sixteen maximal isotropic extensions and an
explicit rank2 complex source matrix, with all24 matching values checked.
It does not produce a positive14x14 matching Gram and does not prove MOC.
No historical novelty claim is made.

1. Exact quotient and sixteen extensions
----------------------------------------
Let M=M(g) have rank8 and real inertia(4,4,1), with radical Rh. Choose an
8-coordinate section and quotient projection P with Ph=0. The verified
identity is M=P^T Q P, Q nonsingular. In a Witt basis C,

    C^T Q C=J=[0 I4; I4 0].

The four row and four column common isotropic3-planes are the images of

    U_i={vec(l_i^T v):v in R3},
    V_j={vec(u l_j):u in R3},

where l_i is row i of the fixed tetrahedral L. The implementation requires
these planes to inject into the radical quotient; this holds in the generic
rank8 situation. No assertion about a failed degenerate chart is made.

Use the explicit Clifford module S=Lambda*(R4). Its eight generators are
the four wedge and four contraction matrices, normalized by

    gamma(v)gamma(w)+gamma(w)gamma(v)=J(v,w) Id.

For each3-plane, the common Clifford annihilator in each parity has
dimension1. These two pure-spinor lines yield two maximal null4-planes
by taking their vector annihilators. The program checks all16 maximal
extensions, their isotropy, and containment of the original3-planes.

2. A concrete universal Fierz identity
-------------------------------------
The Chevalley forms Bplus,Bminus are symmetric8x8 matrices. Define
T(r,c) by

    J(T(r,c),v)=Bplus(r,gamma(v)c).

Coefficient expansion in all16 indeterminate spinor coordinates proves

    2J(T(r,c),T(r,c))=Bplus(r,r)Bminus(c,c).

Polarization gives the full crossed identity

    J(T(r,c),T(r2,c2))+J(T(r,c2),T(r2,c))
      =Bplus(r,r2)Bminus(c,c2).

For the explicit generic actual example, all16 products of a row-plus
spinor and a column-minus spinor are nonzero multiples of

    P vec(l_i^T l_j).

Thus the triality data recover the actual intersection lines, rather
than only matching dimensions.

3. Source complex structure and quadratic extension algebra
------------------------------------------------------------
On the original9-space, rank_C Z=4 and rank_R Re Z=8 imply that Z and
bar Z kill the same real radical h: their complex5D kernels intersect
in at least1D, and their intersection is contained in the complex1D
kernel of Re Z. Thus the imaginary source form descends to the quotient.

Suppose the actual complex source pencil is Z=Q+iR on the8D quotient,
rank_C Z=4, and Q is real nonsingular. Then I=Q^{-1}R is Q-selfadjoint
and I^2=-Id. Indeed rank(Id+iI)=rank(Id-iI)=4, their kernels are disjoint,
and together span the complexified8-space. Hence I has eigenvalues+i,-i
and no remaining eigenspaces or Jordan blocks.

Each universal3-plane U is isotropic for both Q and R. Therefore
IU is contained in U^perp, and U+IU is I-stable and has even dimension.
Its dimension is at least3 and at most5, hence is4. It is totally null:
the Q-pairings within U, within IU, and between U and IU all vanish.
It is therefore one of the two computed maximal extensions.

This gives an input-only way to search for the compatible source I.
For one chirality's eight4-planes W_i, solve the36-variable linear system

    W_i^T S W_i=0, S symmetric8x8.

In the generic cases audited, its solution space has dimension2 and
contains J. A traceless generator T=JS-trace(JS)Id/8 satisfies

    T^2=delta Id.

The API checks this identity. If delta<0, I=T/sqrt(-delta) is a source
complex structure. Positive delta gives a split involution instead.
These alternatives are computed, not identified by signature alone.

There is a closed formula for the generator, so the36-variable solve is
not essential. For a half-spinor r let A_r:V8->the opposite half-spin
space be v |-> gamma(v)r, and let A_r^* be its metric adjoint. The
universal triality identity is

    A_r^* A_s+A_s^* A_r=B(r,s) Id.

For the four row spinors r1,...,r4, put

    T=(1/24) sum_sigma sign(sigma)
        A_rsigma1^* A_rsigma2 A_rsigma3^* A_rsigma4.

This is their decomposable Clifford4-volume. It is selfadjoint and
traceless, and

    T^2=det(B(ri,rj))/16 Id.

For a nondegenerate row4-space, this follows by choosing a metric
orthogonal basis and multiplying the four Clifford generators; the
general identity follows polynomially. The volume anticommutes with
Clifford vectors in the row4-space and commutes with those in its
orthogonal complement. It therefore preserves the pure annihilator
4-planes of all four row and all four column spinors. The implementation
checks this closed formula, its square, its adjoint, its trace, and all8
containment identities exactly. In particular, the sign of delta is the
sign of the row half-spin Gram determinant. This requires a nondegenerate
row4-frame; it is not asserted at a singular frame.

There is also a parameter-free global linear-algebra check: symmetric
9-forms vanishing on the eight original3-planes form a13D space
(45 unknowns, constraint rank32), exactly the universal pencil span.
Consequently the lifted R automatically belongs to that pencil span.

In the generic actual Gaussian-rational example, one chirality has
delta<0; the other has delta>0. The selected four row spinors have
Chevalley signature(3,1), and the four column spinors signature(1,3).
Their cross Gram is zero. For the known nonactual neutral H regression,
both chiralities are split(2,2), and both deltas are positive.

The standard explanation for the Lorentz frames is explicit: write
Q=diag(I4,-I4), I(x,y)=(-y,x). An I-stable maximal null graph O satisfies
O^2=-Id. Orthogonal complex structures on R4 form two quaternionic S2
families. In the half-spin model H+H, these lie in the orthogonal
Lorentz spaces Im(H)+R and R+Im(H). This describes a source real form;
it does not prove a positive matching Gram.

4. Explicit rank2 source reconstruction and its polynomial identity
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The input-certifying inverse first constructs the complex rank4 form
Z=M(g)+iR9. Factor its quadratic form as det(F(H)), where F is a
linear map Mat3(C)->Mat2(C). The images of the four row3-planes have
common left factors alpha_i; the column images have common right
factors beta_j. On their intersection lines, compute lambda_ij from

    F(l_i^T l_j)=lambda_ij alpha_i beta_j^T.

A sufficient explicit open-chart hypothesis for these ruling assertions
is that Re Z has rank8 and its radical, reshaped as a3x3 matrix, is
invertible. If the complex F-image of a real row or column3-plane had
dimension at most1, that real3-plane would contain a nonzero real vector
in ker F, hence the radical, contradicting its full matrix rank. Each
intersection image is likewise nonzero. If a row and column image2-plane
coincided, their real5D preimage sum would be Q-isotropic and would have
to contain the radical. But every matrix in that sum has rank at most2.
Thus every row/column image pair is distinct and belongs to opposite
determinant rulings; the claimed common left and right factors follow.

The tetrahedral sum relations give

    A Lambda=0, Lambda B^T=0,

where A,B have columns alpha_i,beta_j. Thus Lambda has rank at most2.
Rank1 would give F(H)=XHY with X of size2x3 and Y of size3x2. Its
determinant would be a cofactor-linear form with a complex rank1
coefficient matrix. Taking real parts gives a real cofactor coefficient
of rank at most2. The corresponding quadratic9-form has rank at most6:
reduce the coefficient to diag(s1,s2,0), where there are only three
nonzero2x2 quadratic blocks. This contradicts rank Re Z=8. Rank0
would give F=0. Hence Lambda has rank exactly2 in this open chart.
Tight-frame completeness
L^T L=Id gives the full linear-map identity

    F(H)=A*(Lambda Hadamard (L H L^T))*B^T.

The connection to the original source is not inferred from dimensions.
For all eight indeterminate complex spectra, put C_ij=a_i+b_j and take
the explicit Gale matrices with rows

    (a_i-a_1,a_0-a_i,...,a_1-a_0 at slot i), i=2,3,

and similarly for b. Coefficient expansion proves the division-free
identity

 det[A*(C Hadamard (L H L^T))*B^T]
    =-16(a_1-a_0)(b_1-b_0) vec(H)^T M(z) vec(H),

where z_pi=product_i C_i,pi(i). All81 quadratic coefficient entries
are checked symbolically in all8 spectra. Row/column gauges reduce a
generic rank2 Lambda to this form, so the identity justifies the inverse.

The explicit inverse checks M(z)=scale*Z and all24 values

    g_pi=constant+Re(z_pi/scale)

without using the original spectra in its reconstruction. This is an
algebraic source inverse; it does not supply positive permutation weights.

5. A failed candidate kernel and the remaining gap
--------------------------------------------------
The tempting rule that the triality intersection scalars kappa_ij, or
their reciprocals, are row/column scalings of |a_i+b_j|^2 is false.
For a=(0,1,i,2+i), b=(2,3+i,-1+2i,4-i), the normalization invariant

    K_11 K_00/(K_10 K_01)

equals1638/925 for the row-plus/column-minus triality scalars, whereas
the squared cross-distance kernel gives34/45. The opposite chirality
also fails. These are exact actual-source data, not an MOC counterexample.

The missing global step remains a construction of a PSD14 matching Gram
whose24 evaluations equal g. No such matrix has been derived here. The
source inverse alone repackages the complex source and is not a proof of
the required positivity. The explicit Fierz tensor and Lorentz source
branch are available for that construction.
