UNRESTRICTED MOC LEAN STATUS
31 October 2026

The exact unrestricted spectral statement is now defined in
code/lean/MocUnrestricted.lean. Its checked theorems establish full
dimensions zero and one, and several classes in every finite dimension.
The unrestricted four-dimensional conjecture remains unproved.

The definitions use completely arbitrary complex spectra a,b, a square
complex matrix U, and the real convex hull in C:

 matchingProduct a b sigma = product_i(a_i+b_sigma(i));
 determinantValue a b U = det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*);
 IsUnitary U = (UU*=I and U*U=I);
 Holds a b U = determinantValue a b U belongs to the real convex hull
               of all matchingProduct a b sigma;
 Conjecture n = for all a,b:Fin n -> C and all unitary U, Holds a b U.

There are no restrictions on distinctness, nonzero entries, multiplicity,
collinearity, or the sign of any spectral quantity. Conjecture is a
definition of a proposition, not a claimed theorem or an axiom.

Compiler-checked theorems in this file:

* MOC.matchingProduct_mem_hull: each matching product belongs to the hull.
* MOC.holds_of_conjugation_diagonal: an exactly permuted second diagonal
  produces its matching vertex.
* MOC.holds_identity: every pair of spectra with identity mixing.
* MOC.diagonal_conjugation and MOC.holds_diagonal: arbitrary unit complex
  diagonal phases, under the exact conditions u_i conjugate(u_i)=1.
* MOC.permutation_conjugation and MOC.holds_permutation: every permutation
  mixer in every finite dimension.
* MOC.holds_monomial: arbitrary products diag(u) P_sigma with unit phases.
* MOC.holds_of_commute: every unitary U commuting with diag(b).
* MOC.conjecture_zero and MOC.conjecture_one: full unrestricted targets.

All named axiom checks printed only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.
These are the usual Lean mathematical foundations. No theorem depends
on sorryAx; no custom axiom, admitted lemma, or numerical oracle is used.
The source compiled to code/lean/MocUnrestricted.olean with exit code 0.
There is one harmless unused-section-variable linter warning on
matchingProduct_mem_hull. No mathematical or elaboration errors remain.

What the full proof still needs

No current Lean theorem proves Conjecture 4 or Conjecture n for all n.
The noncommuting, unrestricted four-dimensional spectral/unitary case
is the mathematical gap, not merely missing Lean syntax. The extensive
polynomial and cap certificates elsewhere in the repository do not
currently form a complete proof of this target. Their matrices,
spectral realizability conditions, case coverage, and final support
inequality would all need explicit checked linkage before they could
close it. This file makes no axiom out of those missing obligations.

The all-n matrix normalization from arbitrary normal matrices to the
displayed spectral formulation is standard but is not formalized here.
The target directly represents the unrestricted spectral formulation
used by this research repository. No support-separation equivalence was
added merely to enlarge the formalization; it is not needed by the
checked cases above.
