N8' LEIBNIZ CHART AND THE MISSING BOTH-DEPENDENT CHART
31 October 2026

Scope. This audits the elementary coordinate/rank part of the one-contact
conormal argument. The input identifying N8' with the SL4 invariant image
of eight projective3-space labels, including quotient surjectivity, is an
algebraic-geometric premise to be cited from the audited HMSV framework.
The document does not infer the multiple-contact case.

Let R,C be the4-by-4 matrices whose columns are the two groups of four
SL4 labels. Define the degree(1,...,1) singleton invariant

 P_ij=det(R with column i replaced by C_j)
      det(C with column j replaced by R_i).

The replacement positions incorporate the determinant signs; no additional
checkerboard sign belongs in this formula. Matrix notation is

 P=(adj(R)C) Hadamard (adj(C)R)^T.

The invariant height is d=det(R)det(C). Every row and column of P sums to d:
for example adj(R)C adj(C)R=det(C)adj(R)R=d I.

INDEPENDENT GROUP CHART

If R is invertible, put T=R^(-1)C. Then d=det(R)^2 det(T), and

 P_ij=det(R)^2 T_ij Cof_ij(T).

More generally the grade-k invariant obtained by replacing columns I of R
by columns J of C, and columns J of C by columns I of R, is

 det(R)^2 (-1)^(sum I+sum J)
             det T[I,J] det T[Ic,Jc].

These are exactly the common-minor profiles of

 c_pi(T)=sign(pi) product_i T_i,pi(i),

multiplied by det(R)^2. Thus the chart identification agrees with the
Leibniz normalization in all grades, and d is its sum coordinate.

If d=0 then T is singular. Rank(T)<=2 makes adj(T)=0 and every singleton
invariant zero. Hence strict positivity of off-diagonal singleton entries
forces rank(T)=3. Write adj(T)=u v^T. Every u_j and v_i is nonzero, since
otherwise a full singleton column or row would vanish.

Absorb the projective scalar into a nonzero lambda so the real conormal
profile is n=lambda c(T). Set

 S=lambda diag(v) T diag(u).

Then S_ij equals the singleton profile n_ij. Also S1=0 and1^T S=0.
If all off-diagonal singleton values are strictly positive, S is a real
balanced Markov generator: its diagonal is minus the positive row sum.

Put kappa=lambda^3 product_i u_i product_i v_i. Direct adjugate covariance
gives adj(S)=kappa11^T and c(S)=kappa n on every matching coordinate.
Because a strictly positive4-vertex Markov generator has negative principal
3-by-3 cofactor, kappa<0. For example -S with one row/column removed is a
strictly diagonally dominant M-matrix; its determinant is positive by a
continuous diagonal-to-full-matrix deformation. Therefore

 n = -positive_scalar c(S),

with the sign required by the cycle-capacity theorem. If C is invertible
instead, exchange the groups; this transposes singleton profiles and sends
permutations to inverses, preserving the identity contact and positivity.

BOTH GROUPS DEPENDENT: NO EXCEPTIONAL ESCAPE

If rank(R)<=2 or rank(C)<=2, its adjugate is zero, so P=0. Otherwise both
groups have rank3. Write

 adj(R)=r ell^T, adj(C)=s t^T.

The exact replacement formula factors as

 P_ij=[r_i(t^T R_i)] [s_j(ell^T C_j)] = a_i b_j.

Thus rank P<=1. Moreover

 sum_i a_i=t^T Rr=0,
 sum_j b_j=ell^T Cs=0.

Even if the factorization is complex, P is real after the conormal scalar
normalization. A real rank-one matrix with zero row and column sums cannot
have every off-diagonal entry strictly positive for n>=3. Indeed, comparing
two rows in a third column forces their nonzero rank-one row factors to
have the same real sign. Then all column factors have that sign as well,
so diagonal entries are positive, contradicting zero row sums. Equivalently,
a zero-row-sum matrix with strictly positive off-diagonal entries has kernel
exactly the constants and hence rank n-1, not rank1.

This exhausts both-dependent charts, including rank-drop cases and common
hyperplanes. The coordinate argument needs no genericity assumption.

CONNECTION TO STRICT MOMENTS

For a single active matching relabeled to identity, strict positivity of
the contact-null moment matrix makes every off-diagonal singleton indicator
strictly positive, and also makes all three disjoint ordinary indicators
strictly positive. The chart audit therefore either gives an immediate
rank contradiction (both groups dependent) or the strictly positive Markov
generator above. The cycle-capacity theorem then makes its conormal strictly
positive on every nonzero nonnegative matching cost vanishing at identity,
contradicting a compatible conormal pairing of zero.

To promote this to a global stationary-minimum exclusion, retain the precise
KKT hypotheses and the N8' Gauss-normal identification. In particular this
argument addresses ONE active contact. A positive combination of several
contact atoms need not leave all off-diagonal singleton conormal values
positive; the Markov gauge and the conclusion then do not follow.
