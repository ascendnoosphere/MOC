Two contacts: the normal-only bridge fails, and exact Gauss constraints rescue it
31 October 2026

Result and scope

The proposed strong statement

 "an N8' normal with a strictly positive conditional matching-moment
 completion is positive on every nonzero nonnegative matching cost with
 the prescribed contacts"

is false for two contacts. There are exact rational counterexamples for
relative three-cycle and four-cycle contacts, even allowing all ten
matching-annihilator gauges in the moment completion.

These are NOT actual Gauss-contact counterexamples. For every example
below an exact rational Farkas certificate proves that NO nonzero
nonnegative matching cost with those contacts can satisfy the full
Gauss derivative equations. Thus full conormal compatibility rescues
these examples. Euler orthogonality n(g)=0 alone does not suffice.

No MOC or all-N9 counterexample, and no universal two-contact theorem,
is asserted.

Notation and complete gauge space

Order S4 lexicographically. Let B be the exact24 by14 integer basis of
the matching coefficient space, constructed from all singleton and
two-subset matching indicators. Let Z be its24 by10 rational nullspace
basis, B^T Z=0. Every extension of a matching functional n is n+Zb;
testing only the raw Leibniz extension can miss a positive completion.

For contacts C={id,sigma}, W_C is the12-dimensional matching space
whose functions vanish at both contacts. In each calculation twelve
independent ordinary matching indicators form an exact basis F_C.
We exhibit rational y=n+Zb and verify

                       F_C^T diag(y) F_C > 0               (1)

by twelve exact positive rational LDL pivots. Hence this is positivity
on the entire conditional feature space, with no omitted gauge.

Normal chart and orientation

Let L have columns e0-e3,e1-e3,e2-e3, let K be a real3 by3 matrix, and
put S=L K L^T. Then S has zero row and column sums. Assume rank S=3,
and write tau=det K, so adj(S)=tau11^T. The raw profile is

        raw_pi=-sign(pi) product_i S_i,pi(i).

The invariant singleton-normalized orientation is c(S)/tau, not always
-c(S). Its singleton marginal is exactly S_ij. For convenient bounded
records we use the positive rescaling

 n=sign(tau)c(S)/max_pi|c(S)_pi|.

For one-contact Metzler charts tau<0, recovering the familiar -c(S).
For signed multi-contact charts tau can be positive. Both orientations
must be considered; changing the base contact by an odd permutation
can exchange them.

Exact normal-level counterexamples

The first example has contacts permutation indices0 and8, where
sigma=(1,2,0,3). Its integer balanced rank-three matrix is

 S=[[-5674,-1667, 2821, 4520],
    [ 5886,-4261,-2391,  766],
    [-4747, 5031,-4999, 4715],
    [ 4535,  897, 4569,-10001]].

Here tau=-230607709358. Its normal functional has a rational ten-gauge
completion satisfying (1), with all twelve LDL pivots strictly positive.
Nevertheless the pointwise nonnegative matching ray r70 vanishes at
both contacts and has

                n(r70)=-8126822491815/1208724791253086<0.   (2)

The minimum LDL pivot is approximately0.00772; its exact rational
value, all other pivots, the complete rational extension, and the basis
indices are retained in the data rather than inferred from that decimal.

Two four-cycle examples have contacts indices0 and9, with
sigma=(1,2,3,0). Their matrices are

 S1=[[-10000,4697,4378,  925],
     [  3438,-9398,-1319,7279],
     [  1586,2214,-5549,1749],
     [  4976,2487,2490,-9953]],

 S2=[[-10000,2373,2243,5384],
     [ 2559,-6640,1856,2225],

     [-1175,3247,5707,-7779]].

They have exact strictly positive conditional completions, but respectively

 n1(r72)=-12240892970219/1297609983515000<0,
 n2(r76)=-377596793777/63313125420000<0.                      (3)

The rays in (2)-(3) are checked exactly to belong to V14, to be nonnegative
at all24 permutations, and to vanish at the specified two contacts.

Euler orthogonality is also insufficient

For each example choose an ordinary contact-null ray r with n(r)>0.
The explicitly recorded matching cost

                 g_E=cap - n(cap)/n(r) * r

is nonzero, pointwise nonnegative, vanishes at both contacts, and obeys
n(g_E)=0. However it fails the full derivative conormal equations below.
Thus adding only Euler/Gauss orthogonality to the normal-only hypothesis
does not repair the false bridge.

Full Gauss-contact compatibility

Write K_ab for the nine balanced-chart coordinates. The exact derivative
matrix D, of size24 by9, is

 D_pi,ab=-sign(pi) sum_i L_i,a L_pi(i),b
                    product_(j!=i) S_j,pi(j).               (4)

It satisfies the exact Euler identity D vec(K)=4 raw, and D^T B has
rank9 in all three examples. A matching cost g genuinely conormal to
this chart point must satisfy ALL nine equations

                             D^T g=0.                     (5)

Equation n(g)=0 is only one consequence of (5).

For all three matrices the system

 g in V14,  g>=0,  g_C=0,  D^T g=0,  sum_pi g_pi=1

is infeasible. This is proved by the following exact Stiemke/Farkas
alternative, not merely reported from a numerical LP status.

An exact certificate of compatibility failure

For each example the data exhibit rational vectors a in Q^9,b in Q^10
such that

                 v=D a+Z b,
                 v_pi>0 for every pi outside C.           (6)

If g is a matching cost satisfying (5), then

 v^T g=a^T D^T g+b^T Z^T g=0.

If also g>=0 and g_C=0, strict positivity in (6) forces every remaining
entry of g to be zero. This proves the claimed infeasibility for the
ENTIRE conormal fiber at each matrix, without normalization or rounding.

For the three-cycle example, the minimum noncontact entry in (6) is

                     67389429475531/67422002902000>0.

For the two four-cycle examples the minima are

                     727945555132117/728438980000000>0,
                     1766216298023/1767408925000>0.

All certificate coefficients use common rational grids before the final
exact checks. The full vectors a,b,v are saved in the data. A tangent-only
attempt v=Da was not successful for these examples; the matching
annihilator component is retained and must not be silently discarded.

Exploration and limitations

The initial signed-flow campaign retained48000 matrices and all24
profiles, conditional eigenvalues and matching-ray scores, with12000
matrices per second-contact cycle type. It found436 strict-positive
RAW-extension conditional moments and no negative ray among them.
That is only sufficient numerical evidence, not the relevant full-gauge
test. Its eight ordinary-positive/mixed-negative three/four-cycle
samples led to the three repaired examples above.

The partial signed-cycle polynomial certificates in the same directory
concern only raw Leibniz extensions; their conditional-square sums need
not lie in V14. They cannot be used as full-gauge bridge proofs. Their
scope has been marked accordingly.

What remains open is the actual global application: prove that a
nonnegative Gauss-compatible cost cannot coexist with a strict
conditional moment completion, for every contact stratum and every
SL4 chart, or find a genuine Gauss-compatible counterexample. The three
examples here refute the stronger normal-only shortcut while providing
exact certificates of why the full Gauss conditions exclude them.
