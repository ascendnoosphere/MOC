SINGLE-MIXED RESIDUAL CONES: EXACT SIX-CONTACT CENSUS

This is a finite reduction and partial coverage report, not a proof of MOC4.
The independently certified mixed cancellation in
code/research/verify_mixed_cancellation_graph.py reduces any nonnegative
combination of the 82 permutation-positive ray functions to an identical
function with at most one mixed ray. No existing active set or gap function
is changed. If there is no mixed ray, the function is an ordinary
nonnegative squared-minor sum.

For one surviving mixed ray, independent row/column permutations put it at
f34. Its zero set consists of 13 of the 24 lexicographically ordered
permutations. A nonnegative combination which vanishes on a contact subset
S can use only ordinary rays also vanishing on S. Let C(S) be the cone
spanned by f34 and all those ordinary rays. For h!=0 and pairwise distinct
complex matching products, the previously proved disjoint-swap rectangle
obstruction makes S rectangle-free. This restriction is not asserted for
degenerate spectra or zero height.

code/research/verify_single_mixed_face_census.py rebuilds all 82 rays,
the 18 rectangles, the 1152 row/column/inversion actions and all relevant
subsets. It checks the detailed maximal representatives in
 data/certificates/geometry/single_mixed_six_census.json.
Fresh execution passed. Exact results:

  rectangle-free subsets of f34 zeros: 1608 of size 6, 1467 of size 7,
   and 927 of size 8;
  distinct cones C(S) for sizes 6 through 8: 4002;
  maximal cones under inclusion of their ordinary generator sets: 1608,
   each with precisely six common zero permutations;
  f34 stabilizer: order 24 including inversion, order 12 without inversion;
  maximal cones modulo this stabilizer: 98 orbits, sizes distributed as
   45 of size 24, 39 of size 12, 5 each of sizes 6 and 4,
   3 of size 3, and 1 of size 1.

Every seven- and eight-contact cone C(S) lies in some six-contact maximal
cone: choose any six elements of S. The same inclusion holds for larger
rectangle-free active sets. A nine-contact cone cannot contain a maximal
six-contact cone, because each maximal cone's complete common zero set
has exactly six elements. In particular existing nine-contact closures do
not, by that containment argument alone, settle the 98 maximal cones.

The 98 marked orbits are not directly comparable to the old 83 orbits of
closed six-zero faces. The latter are unmarked and may contain multiple
mixed rays; this census marks the unique surviving mixed ray and removes
all other mixed generators. One unmarked face may therefore give several
marked orbits.

The previously proved constant-row certificate for old face82
(docs/geometry/global_six_row_notes.txt) covers one marked orbit, comprising
3 of the 1608 cones. After this particular coverage test, 97 marked orbits
and 1605 cones remain; this does not claim that other published or new
certificates fail to cover any of them.

The standard weighted-minor inequality, transported by exact relabeling,
provides all three f34 compensation pairs (18,23), (18,28), (23,28).
Of the 98 orbits, 39 contain no pair, 42 contain one, and 17 contain all
three. For example, coefficients u,v on any such pair with uv>=t^2 absorb
coefficient t>=0 of f34. This is a partial coefficient-region result;
pair availability alone does not close the rest of a cone. The certificate
checks the relabelings and counts, not a new weighted-minor theorem.
