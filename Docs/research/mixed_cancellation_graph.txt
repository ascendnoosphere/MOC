THE MIXED-RAY CANCELLATION GRAPH IS K48
2026-10-30

Let r0,...,r33 be the canonical ordinary squared-minor rays, and let
f34,...,f81 be the 48 canonical mixed rays. Put an edge between distinct
mixed labels i,j precisely when f_i+f_j belongs to cone(r0,...,r33).
Every pair is an edge. The graph has 48 vertices, 1128 edges, degree47,
one connected component, and adjacency spectrum 47 once and -1 with
multiplicity47. Its full graph automorphism group is S48.

The graph itself therefore carries no special 48-point geometry. The natural
S4 x S4 action and the maximal cancellation ratios retain information that
the uncolored graph discards.

Natural S4 x S4 labels
---------------------
For the raw relabeling T_(a,b)f(p)=f(a p b), the stabilizer of f34 is

 H={(a,tau a^(-1) tau^(-1)): a in A4},     tau=(0,3,2,1).

Both stabilizer projections have order12 and are isomorphic to A4. The
natural mixed-ray action is faithful, of order576, and is transitive.
It has the explicit 48-point model

               (sigma,epsilon) in S4 x {+1,-1},
 sigma=b tau a, epsilon=sgn(a) for the label T_(a,b)f34.

A further relabeling T_(c,d) acts by

        (sigma,epsilon) -> (d sigma c, epsilon sgn(c)).

The verifier checks this formula for all576 relabelings and all48 labels.
The involution (sigma,epsilon)->(sigma,-epsilon) gives 24 equivariant pairs;
f34 is paired with f60. The quotient is the usual S4 left/right bitorsor.
In abstract direct-product notation the 48-point set is
(S4 x S4)/diagonal(A4), up to the displayed conjugation and the inverse in
the second raw relabeling parameter.

This is more specific than a count of 48. It differs from the standard
4 x 4 x 3 Cartesian product action: its vertex stabilizer projects onto A4
in both S4 factors, whereas a fixed coordinate in a natural four-point
factor restricts that projection to a subgroup of S3. The complete graph
alone cannot distinguish an F4 or binary-octahedral labeling from an
arbitrary numbering of 48 points.

Equal-pair flows remove all but at most one mixed coefficient
------------------------------------------------------------
Suppose a cost has a nonnegative ray expansion

              g=sum_k b_k r_k+sum_i alpha_i f_i,
                         b_k,alpha_i>=0.

For every edge choose its certified ordinary expansion
f_i+f_j=sum_k C_(ij,k)r_k, with C_(ij,k)>=0. Any nonnegative edge flows w_ij
satisfying sum_(j!=i)w_ij<=alpha_i give the exact operation

 g=sum_k [b_k+sum_(i<j)w_ij C_(ij,k)]r_k
                       +sum_i [alpha_i-sum_(j!=i)w_ij]f_i.       (1)

Thus cancellation preserves the cost as a function on U(4) and adds only
nonnegative ordinary coefficients.

Write L=sum_i alpha_i. Complete cancellation by equal-pair flows is possible
if and only if 2max_i alpha_i<=L. Necessity follows because the mass consumed
at any vertex cannot exceed the total mass consumed at the other vertices.
For sufficiency, put consecutive intervals I_i of lengths alpha_i around a
circle of circumference L. Match each point with the point half a circle
away, and let

        w_ij=length(I_i intersect (I_j-L/2 modulo L)),  i<j.

No interval meets its own half-turn translate when its length is at most
L/2. The half-turn is an involution, so w_ij=w_ji and the incident flows sum
to alpha_i. This gives an exact deterministic formula in canonical label
order. Rational input coefficients produce rational flows. Averaging this
construction over the576 natural relabelings gives a natural-group
equivariant allocation if that property is needed.

If alpha_d>L/2, the unique dominant vertex d can instead pair with each
other vertex j using w_dj=alpha_j. The only residual is

                 [2alpha_d-L] f_d.                            (2)

Any equal-pair allocation leaves at least this much coefficient at d, so
(2) is optimal for these operations. The included pair_allocation function
implements both cases and checks its exact degree equations. It also
reconstructs the resulting ordinary coefficients in example calculations.

Consequently, positivity for actual spectral costs in the82-ray cone
reduces to positivity for actual costs represented by one mixed ray plus
nonnegative ordinary coefficients. By natural symmetry one may use a single
canonical mixed ray.
This reduction does not prove positivity of the remaining costs: the
ordinary coefficients produced by (1) must still satisfy the needed
spectral support conditions. An exact dual certificate in the JSON file
proves that f34 itself lies outside the ordinary cone, so an isolated mixed
ray cannot disappear by a pure ordinary identity.

Maximal ratios expose the limitation of the uncolored graph
----------------------------------------------------------
The dominant-mass condition concerns equal-pair flows. It is not a criterion
for ordinary-cone membership, because unequal pairs can cancel too.
Define

 T(i,j)=max{t>=0: t f_i+f_j belongs to the ordinary cone}.

Exact primal decompositions and dual separating functionals establish
T(i,j)=T(j,i), with values1,2,3. Each vertex has respectively24,20,3 partners
of these three capacities. For a partner orbit of f34, the capacities are

 partner       35 37 38 41 43 58 59 60 63 65 68
 capacity       1  2  2  1  2  1  1  1  1  2  3

Each dual w satisfies w.r_k>=0 for every ordinary ray, w.f34=-1,
and w.f_partner=T. It rules out t>T. The primal certificate attains T;
reversing the pair attains1/T after scaling. Convexity then proves that
x f_i+y f_j is ordinary precisely when 1/T<=x/y<=T for x,y>0.

There is a closed label rule. For vertices (sigma,epsilon) and
(sigma',epsilon'), put delta=sigma^(-1)sigma' and r=epsilon'/epsilon.

 cycle type of delta        r=+1       r=-1
 identity                   same       1
 transposition                1        1
 double transposition         1        3
 three-cycle                  2        1
 four-cycle                   2        2

The verifier proves this rule on all ordered distinct pairs. The capacity3
relation is six disjoint cubes Q3. Their components correspond to the six
cosets of the Klein four subgroup V4 in S4; each has eight labels. Inside
a component, the three generators are the nonidentity elements of V4,
each accompanied by a sign flip. These are independent generators of an
F2^3 cube. The verifier constructs the eight binary coordinates and checks
every cube edge exactly. The spectrum of the capacity3 graph is therefore
3^6,1^18,(-1)^18,(-3)^6.

Exact weighted allocation and the 14-ray cap normal form
------------------------------------------------------
Unequal certified pairs give additional directed operations: a capacity T
pair can consume T units at one endpoint and one at the other. For example,
3f34+f68 is ordinary. These operations also have a closed allocation rule.
If the mixed coefficients are balanced for equal pairs, use the interval
construction above. Otherwise let d be their unique dominant vertex.
Initially pair alpha_j units at d with every other coefficient alpha_j.
Then increase the consumption at d on edge d,j from alpha_j toward
T(d,j)alpha_j, in canonical label order, until d is consumed or every edge
reaches capacity. Every intermediate ratio is ordinary by convexity.

The remaining mixed coefficient is exactly

       Delta=max(0,alpha_d-sum_(j!=d)T(d,j)alpha_j).             (3)

If it is positive, no pair allocation can do better: each unit consumed at
j can consume at most T(d,j) units at d. Thus full cancellation is possible
precisely when

              alpha_i<=sum_(j!=i)T(i,j)alpha_j for every i.     (4)

The weighted_pair_allocation function implements this rule using exact
rational arithmetic. No matching LP is needed.

There is one dual w_i for each mixed ray that attains all its capacities
simultaneously:

 w_i(f_i)=-1,     w_i(f_j)=T(i,j) for j!=i,
 w_i(r_k) in {0,1} for every ordinary ray.

The JSON file stores a stabilizer-invariant rational representative for
w34; symmetry supplies the other47. The verifier checks every value. Hence
(4) is also necessary for ordinary-cone membership of a nonnegative pure
mixed combination, rather than only for the chosen flow operations.

For w34, the ordinary rays on its zero facet are

        Z34={0,7,10,13,17,18,20,22,23,27,28,30,31}.

These13 rays have rank13. The other21 ordinary rays all have w34(r_k)=1,
and each satisfies an exact integer identity

                f34+r_k=sum_(j in Z34) C_(k,j)r_j,
                                  C_(k,j)>=0.                 (5)

All21 certificates are included; symmetry gives the corresponding
absorptions for every mixed label. For example, f34+r16=r0+r10+r31.
Since w34(f34)=-1, the14 rays consisting of f34 and Z34 are independent.

Write C82=cone(r0,...,r33,f34,...,f81) and COrd=cone(r0,...,r33).
The following exact decomposition now holds:

 C82 = COrd union union_(i=34,...,81) cone(f_i,{r_k:w_i(r_k)=0}). (6)

Each of the48 added cones is a symmetry image of one14-ray simplicial cone.
For a cost g in C82, at most one w_i(g) is negative. If all are nonnegative,
g belongs to COrd. If w_d(g)<0, the unique cap coordinates have the form

         g=-w_d(g)f_d+sum_(k:w_d(r_k)=0) beta_k r_k,
                                     beta_k>=0.               (7)

To prove this, first use weighted pair allocation. A positive residual at
d uses every other mixed coefficient at maximal capacity. Its ordinary
remainder lies on w_d=0. Add the original ordinary coefficients, then apply
(5) until d disappears or every ordinary coefficient with w_d=1 is consumed.
The latter case is exactly (7). The former case is ordinary. Conversely,
w_d is nonnegative on COrd, so a negative value rules out ordinary membership.
Finally w_i+w_j is nonnegative on every generator of C82 for i!=j; two
negative dual values are impossible.

The verifier reconstructs these operations on exact examples and checks
all24 coordinates after both weighted allocation and ordinary absorption.
The certificates prove the formulas for arbitrary nonnegative real input;
the examples serve only to replay their implementation.

Thus a counterexample within C82 must lie in a single canonical14-ray cap,
after symmetry. This replaces a search over mixed supports with one explicit
simplicial normal form. It does not prove positivity of actual spectral
costs inside that cap. The residual spectral problem still permits the13
ordinary coefficients in (7), and a support inequality can hold even when
the cost is outside the ordinary cone.

For comparison, the standard normalized 48 F4/binary-octahedral unit vectors
have inner-product class sizes1,6,6,8,8,18 at a vertex: eight coordinate axes,
sixteen half-cube vectors, and twenty-four normalized two-coordinate vectors
give these counts directly. No union of these intrinsic angle classes has
degree3. Thus the capacity3 cube relation cannot be an intrinsic union of
Euclidean angle classes of that configuration. A bare48-element bijection
supplies no such identification.
