EXACT FULL EIGHT-CONTACT ORBIT 22 CLOSURE
2026-10-30

Scope: This closes the whole five-parameter f34 face whose ordinary rays
are (0,7,23,27,28), with no genericity, distinct-product, or strictly
positive coefficient assumption. It is one orbit of the eight-contact
census, not unrestricted MOC4. The portable exact verifier is
code/research/verify_eight_contact_orbit22.py.

Let
 g=f34+a r0+b r7+c r23+d r27+e r28, a,b,c,d,e>=0,
and set A=a+1, B=b+1, D=d+1. A positive coefficient of f34 can be
normalized to one. A zero coefficient leaves an ordinary nonnegative
minor sum. The established weighted-minor theorem in canonical notation is
 f34+t r23+t^(-1) r28>=0 for all t>0 and all U in U(4).

The exact spectral cubic F and Q=D_1 F yield
 F(g)=-ABD, Q(g)=-(D+ce)<0.
For an actual gap F(g)=hQ(g), so h=ABD/(D+ce)>0, and Q never vanishes.
The model gives H_(g-h1)y=0 and grad F(y)=grad F(g-h1). Its Hessian is
therefore singular. This is immediate for y!=0; if y=0, the second identity
gives H_x x=2grad F(x)=0, and x=g-h1 cannot be zero: otherwise g=h1 and Q(g)=0 because
H_1 1=0, contrary to Q(g)<0.

The verifier computes the full cubic pencil in six indeterminates with
exact fraction-field elimination, then checks the factorization

 Q^3 det(H_g-(F/Q)H_1)
   =4 A^2 B^2 D^4 (D+ce)(ce-AB)^2 M^2,
 M=AB(c+e+1)+(A+B-1)ce.

Here A,B,D>=1, c,e>=0, so Q!=0 and M>0. Spectral singularity forces
ce=AB=(a+1)(b+1)>=1. In particular c,e>0. Taking t=c in the weighted
minor theorem and adding the nonnegative remainder gives

 g=[f34+c r23+c^(-1)r28]+a r0+b r7+d r27+(e-c^(-1))r28>=0.

Thus the support inequality holds on the entire face for every complex
unitary, including boundary and coincident-spectrum cases. No extra
rank or real-lift classification is used. The verifier additionally
checks the census ray order and the eight contacts. Fresh run status is
recorded in build/eight17/verify22.log.
