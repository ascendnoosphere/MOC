The rational-family closure for C=(0,1,2,3) used a constant vector u such
that M(g)u=b(g)v has image-rank one on the entire contact subspace.
Here two independent exact certificates show that this mechanism cannot
work for C=(0,1,6,17). These are obstructions to this proof ansatz, not
obstructions to a uniform Gram/SOS proof or to MOC.

1. NO CONSTANT RANK-ONE IMAGE VECTOR EXISTS AT ALL

Let F_0,...,F_9 be the exact ten-dimensional contact-space basis obtained
by multiplying the universal14-cost basis by ker(eval_C). Put
G_j=32 M(F_j). These integer9x9 symmetric matrices are saved in full.
The fixed combination
  M0=sum_(j=0)^9 (j+1)G_j
has determinant -2962417950720, hence is invertible. Set A_j=M0^{-1}G_j.

If u is nonzero and span{G_j u} has dimension at most one, M0u is nonzero
and spans that same line. Therefore u is a simultaneous eigenvector of
every A_j. Every commutator [A_i,A_j] must consequently kill u.

Exact rational arithmetic gives
  dim ker[A_0,A_1]=3,
  dim(ker[A_0,A_1] intersect ker[A_0,A_2])=0.
This excludes such a nonzero u even over the complex numbers. In
particular there is no identity M(g)u=b(g)v with fixed nonzero u,v.
The common-eigenvector/commutator argument was independently proposed by
the variational-closure agent for the other representative C=(0,1,8,11).

2. NO EXTRA FIXED LINEAR COST RELATION EXISTS IN THE RATIONAL FAMILY

The artifact contains ten fixed integer invertible3x3 matrices H. For each
one, the exact10x10 full-conormal contact-vertex system

is invertible. Its normalized solution g_C(H) is computed over Q, and all
nine kernel equations, all four contacts, and the unit-sum condition are
checked exactly. These vertices are not asserted to be nonnegative.

The ten resulting contact-space coordinate vectors have nonzero exact
determinant. They therefore span the entire ten-dimensional contactspace.
Any fixed linear score vanishing on the whole rational family would
vanish on all ten, and so on the entire contactspace. There are no extra
fixed linear identities beyond the defining four contacts and matching
linear relations.

This is an exact falsification of a structural proof route, rather than
a report that a finite search found no relation. The finite witnesses
prove full span and force a universal conclusion by linear algebra.
Further work on this representative needs an inequality, a nonlinear
identity, or a different positive Gram construction.
