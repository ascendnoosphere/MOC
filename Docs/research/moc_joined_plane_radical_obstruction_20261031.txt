THEOREM. Let C=M(f34), R7=M(r7), R10=M(r10). There is no5-dimensional
real subspace W on which ALL THREE forms C,R7,R10 are nonnegative.
Consequently there exists epsilon0>0 such that, for every0<epsilon<epsilon0,
there is no5-plane W satisfying

  C|W>0, (R7+epsilon C)|W>=0, (R10+epsilon C)|W>=0.          (1)

This refutes the proposed universal common-plane certificate even for
normalized full14 matching-SOS positive-definite duals. Such duals with
y.f34=-1 and y.r7=y.r10=epsilon exist for EVERY epsilon>0, by an independent
exact construction. The obstruction concerns only this sufficient proof
ansatz. It does not refute MOC, actual spectral positivity, or containment
in the full matching-SOS cone under an actual/future hypothesis.

1. Exact signatures and radical vectors

For j=7 and10, Rj has inertia(2+,2-,5zero). Its kernel is5-dimensional,
and the restriction of C to that kernel has inertia(2+,2-,1zero). The
radical vectors can be chosen as

  n7 =(-1,-1, 1,1, 1,-1, 1,1,-1)^T/8,
  n10=(-1, 1,-1,1,-1, 1,-1,1,-1)^T/8.

The exact identities are

  Rj nj=0, C(nj,v)=0 for every v in ker Rj,
  C(n7,n7)=C(n10,n10)=0,
  C(n7,n10)=1/128 !=0.                                     (2)

These are not merely numerical null vectors. For rational bases Nj of
ker Rj, the characteristic polynomial of16 Nj^T C Nj is exactly

       lambda(lambda^2+2lambda-20)(lambda^2+2lambda-4).

Each quadratic has one positive and one negative root, proving the claimed
kernel signatures. The characteristic polynomial of16Rj is exactly

              lambda^5(2lambda-3)^2(2lambda+3)^2/16,

proving its ordinary-form signature. The radical is one-dimensional in
each case. The verifier also identifies nj as the range of the rank-one
nilpotent part Tj-Tj^2, Tj=C^(-1)Rj, but this extra interpretation is not
needed for the dimension argument.

2. A5-plane nonnegative for C and Rj must contain nj

Suppose dim W=5 and C|W>=0, Rj|W>=0. Decompose the ambient space into
the positive, negative and zero spectral subspaces of Rj. Its positive
subspace has dimension2. Hence the kernel K of the positive-coordinate
projection restricted to W has dimension>=3.

For any x in K the positive Rj coordinates vanish. Since Rj(x)>=0 and
all remaining nonzero spectral contributions are negative, the negative
Rj coordinates must also vanish. Thus K is contained in ker Rj.

The form C restricted to ker Rj has signature(2+,2-,1zero). Its largest
nonnegative subspaces have dimension3. Therefore dim K=3. Moreover EVERY
3-dimensional nonnegative subspace of this5-space contains its radical:
after quotienting by the one-dimensional radical, the form has signature
(2+,2-), whose nonnegative subspaces have dimension at most2. A3-space
must consequently intersect the radical nontrivially, hence contain it.
It follows that

                            nj in K in W.                  (3)

Applying(3) to BOTH j=7 and10 puts both n7 and n10 in W.

3. The cross pairing contradicts nonnegativity of C

For a positive-semidefinite bilinear form on W, every isotropic vector is
orthogonal to all of W. Indeed for x with C(x,x)=0 and any v in W,

  C(x+t v,x+t v)=2t C(x,v)+t^2 C(v,v)>=0

for both signs of sufficiently small t, which forces C(x,v)=0.
Since n7 is isotropic and n10 belongs to W, this would force
C(n7,n10)=0, contradicting(2). This proves that NO common5-dimensional
nonnegative subspace exists at zero budget. Positive definiteness of C
was not assumed in this stronger zero-budget assertion.

4. Compactness excludes all sufficiently small positive budgets

Suppose no epsilon0 as stated existed. Then one could choose a sequence
epsilon_m>0 tending to0, with5-planes Wm satisfying(1). The real
Grassmannian Gr(5,9) is compact. Choose orthonormal basis matrices Um for
Wm, extract a convergent subsequence in the compact Stiefel manifold,
and let U be its orthonormal limit.

Each Um^T C Um is positive definite, so its limit U^T C U is nonnegative.
Likewise

  Um^T(Rj+epsilon_m C)Um>=0

converges to U^T Rj U>=0, for j=7,10. Thus the limit5-plane W=im U is
nonnegative for all three zero-budget forms, which Sections2--3 exclude.
This contradiction proves existence of epsilon0>0 and establishes(1)
for all sufficiently small positive epsilon. No numerical threshold for
epsilon0 is claimed or needed for this logical refutation of a universal
all-dual assertion.

5. The duals remain full14-PD while the planes fail

The joined-dual classification provides an explicit full14-PD family for

  rows [5,4,0,11,17,6], weights [1,-1,-1,-1,1+epsilon,1+epsilon].

Adding mass126+16/epsilon at each contact row

             [1,7,8,9,12,14,15,18,20,22,23]

gives y(epsilon).f34=-1 and both singleton budgets equal to epsilon.
The moment on (f34,r7,r10) is diag(1,epsilon,epsilon)>0. Its full14

Choose any epsilon from the sufficiently small interval in Section4.
This y(epsilon) meets the full-PD dual hypothesis, but no plane satisfying

The four separate one-small-budget plane theorems remain valid. Their
planes cannot be combined into a universal theorem by simply allowing
several budgets to be small: the nonorthogonal nilpotent radicals create
an exact obstruction.
