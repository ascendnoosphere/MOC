Rank-eight moment tangents at two exact orthogonal mixers
31 October2026

Question tested. Can rank<=8 of the positive multiaffine moment kernel
at two or more auxiliary crossratios locally recover the realSO4
Leibniz-amplitude manifold? The answer at first order is no: there is
one additional observable tangent direction at both exact examples.
The strongest successful result here is that the actual amplitude
vector is already an exposed extreme ray of each one-kernel PSD cone.
Neither result is a global classification.

Mixers and parameters

Q59=(1/59)[[-25,46,16,22],[-46,-31,20,-2],
           [-16,20,-11,-52],[22,2,52,-17]].
R3701=(1/3701)[[999,2010,-2230,1920],[3090,849,660,-1730],
               [1230,-2860,-2001,30],[1280,-870,2070,2649]].
Both are exact real orthogonal matrices of determinant1. Let
 c_pi=sgn(pi) product_i O_i,pi(i).
The four tested auxiliary spectra are beta=(0,1,i,w) for
 w=2+3i, -2+i, 3-2i, -1-3i.

Definitions and exact method

For fixed beta form the24x16 feature matrix F. The actual kernel
 G0=F^*diag(c)F is PSD rank8. Let N span its kernel, and discard the
four structural directions in kerF. Equivalently, select four
independent columns of F N and call the resulting24x4 matrix V.
The first-order rank-stratum tangent condition on a REAL24-vector v is
 A_beta(v)=V^*diag(v)V=0.                                  (1)
Real and imaginary upper-triangular entries give16 real linear rows.

The stronger linear FACE condition, needed for two-sided affine
perturbations in the PSD cone, is
 F^*diag(v)V=0.                                            (2)
It is not the same condition as (1).

The actual scaled orbit tangent has seven independent columns:
c itself, plus dc/dt for O(t)=exp(tK_ab)O over the six skew generators.
They annihilate every A_beta exactly, using rational complex arithmetic.
Only the scale direction must satisfy the stronger face condition.

All upper/lower rank bounds are auditable. Tangent ranks are computed
overQ using DomainMatrix rational RREF after rowwise denominator clearing.
Modular elimination at prime1000000007 gives matching lower bounds.
For full-face rank23, exact annihilation of nonzero c supplies the upper
bound23, while the modular rank supplies the lower bound23.

Results (identical at Q59 and R3701)

Number of auxiliary parameters included:     1   2   3   4
Combined rank of tangent constraints:       13  16  16  16
Dimension of their real kernel:             11   8   8   8
Combined rank of full-face constraints:     23  23  23  23
Actual scaledSO4 tangent dimension:          7   7   7   7

Each individual parameter has tangent rank13 and full-face rank23.
The exact extra tangent is exported as a primitive integer24-vector in
rank8_tangent_Q59.json and rank8_tangent_R3701.json. It is not the
alternating-sign vector.

More strongly, augmenting the tangent constraints by all70 ordinary
common-minor incidence rows1_{pi(I)=J} gives rank24 modulo the stated
prime, and hence rank24 overQ. Therefore the eight-dimensional tangent
kernel injects into the fourteen-dimensional common-profile space.
The additional direction cannot be discarded as an invisible MOC gauge.

Exposed-ray consequence

For the fixed-beta PSD cone K_beta={q real:G_beta(q)>=0}, consider the
nonnegative exposing functional tr(N^*G_beta(q)N). If it vanishes,
positivity implies G_beta(q)N=0. The exact rank23 of (2) leaves only
q proportional to c. The proportionality coefficient must be
nonnegative because G_beta(c) is nonzero PSD. Thus the actual c is an
exposed extreme ray of K_beta. Since c also belongs to the intersection
over all auxiliary beta, it remains an exposed ray of that smaller cone.
This does not classify the other extreme rays of either cone.

Second-order check and its limit

For aC2 curve c(t)=c+t v+t^2 w+o(t^2) whose kernel ranks remain<=8,
the Schur-complement equality requires
 N^*G_beta(w)N=N^*G_beta(v)G0^+G_beta(v)N.                  (3)
For PSD-only curves the equality is replaced by a PSD inequality, so
failure of (3) would not by itself rule out a PSD curve.

moc_qft_rank8_second_order.py evaluates the right side without a large
pseudoinverse. Choose eight feature indices R for an invertible
principal G0_RR. If Y=G_beta(v)N is in rangeG0, its quadratic form is
Y_R^*G0_RR^{-1}Y_R. The tangent condition ensures that range inclusion.
The actual SO4 generator direction is used as a sanity check.

At both exact mixers, the exported transverse direction passes this
second-order compatibility test across all four auxiliary spectra:
augmenting the linear constraints by the right side increases no rank.
Exact rational augmented ranks and matching modular ranks are recorded
in rank8_second_order_Q59.json and rank8_second_order_R3701.json.
This is a finite compatibility result; it proves neither existence of
an actual rank-eight curve nor positivity at untested spectra.
