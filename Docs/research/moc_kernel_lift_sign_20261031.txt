Notation and the exact lift equation.

Let F be the verified real homogeneous HMSV cubic on the selected14 product
coordinates. Write q(v)=grad F(v), H_v=Hess F(v), and e=(1,...,1).
For Q(g)=e.q(g)!=0 set h=F(g)/Q(g) and x=g-he. The checked cubic identities
give F(x)=0. For all real x,y, polarization gives

 q(x+i y)=q(x)-q(y)+i H_x y.                         (1)

Thus a conjugate critical lift is EXACTLY a real y with H_x y=0 and
q(y)=q(x). These equations also imply F(x)=F(y)=0: pair the equality with
x and y and use H_x y=0 and cubic Euler identities. No secant sufficiency
or numerical finding is used in this equivalence.

Suppose rank H_x=12 and K=[k1 k2] is any real basis of its kernel. For
M=[[u,v],[v,w]] define

 L_x(M)=u q(k1)+v H_k1 k2+w q(k2).                  (2)

The coefficient of v in (2) has NO additional factor of two. Then
L_x(ss^T)=q(Ks). Therefore the lift test is precisely

 L_x(M)=q(x), M>=0, rank M<=1.                     (3)

A rank-one real symmetric PSD matrix factors as ss^T. Allowing the zero
matrix includes the harmless y=0 critical case.

This is also spectral sufficiency, rather than only a necessary real-form
test, once the audited critical-cone identification is used. A nonzero
critical vector p is a point of the affine M8 cone. Over C, surjectivity of
the invariant quotient supplies an eight-point representative. Choose one
projective coordinate chart avoiding infinity for all eight labels, then
rescale the individual vectors to (a_i,1) and (-b_j,1). Each perfect-matching
invariant acquires the same total scalar, absorbed in eta. Consequently
p has the required product coordinates. For a supported nonnegative g with
min g=0, use p=-x+i y; then Re p=-x and max Re p=h, so g is exactly its
spectral support gap. Explicit eight-spinor reconstruction would give a
concrete spectra artifact, but is not a further existence hypothesis.

Why rank L_x=2 is the generic secant geometry.

The primary source is Howard--Millson--Snowden--Vakil,
The geometry of eight points in projective space, arXiv:1103.5255v1:

Theorem3.4 gives the dominant polar map from the projective11-dimensional
secant variety S to the9-dimensional N8'. Corollary3.9 supplies the secant
dimension. Corollary3.12 and Proposition3.19 identify the Hessian divisor
on the cubic with twice S. The repository's exact coordinate identification

the real sign conclusions below are separate deductions.

Here is the deduction. At a general smooth x of S with q(x)!=0, the
projective polar differential on S has rank9 and kernel dimension2. Since
H_x x=2q(x), the projective differential kernel in the ambient tangent
space is naturally ker H_x. In particular dim ker H_x>=2 generically on S.
All13-by-13 Hessian minors consequently vanish on irreducible S by density.
The exact actual anchors below have rank H_x=12, so this is its generic
rank; indeed rank H_x<=12 at every point of the affine secant cone.

Now take a smooth point of the affine secant cone of rank H_x=12. This
cone has dimension12. For each tangent vector z, differentiate the already
proved rank<=12 matrix identity along the cone. In a fixed basis making
H_x=diag(A12,0_2) with A12 invertible, the derivatives of its bordered
13-by-13 minors give

 K^T H_z K=0.                                      (4)

The linear map z -> K^T H_z K/2 is the transpose of L_x after identifying
Sym2 with its trace pairing. Its kernel contains the12-dimensional tangent
space. Hence rank L_x<=2. On every rational kernel chart, this inequality
extends from smooth points to all rank12 secant points by vanishing of the
3-by-3 minors. The exact anchors have rank L_x=2, so generic rank is exactly2.
In particular, rank3 is impossible on the rank12 secant locus. The proposed
unique-moment rank3 chart is not a generic actual chart.

Compatibility also holds generically for a concrete reason. On an honest
complex secant, write x=(p+q)/2 with q(p)=q(q)=0, absorbing the two secant
coefficients into the critical cone points. Then y=(p-q)/(2i) satisfies
(1) over C. Its rank-one complex moment solves (2). Thus the linear target
is compatible; for real x a real particular moment follows by taking real
parts. Compatibility extends through rank2 kernel charts by vanishing of
the augmented3-by-3 minors. This argument concerns the audited secant cone.
It does not replace an exceptional Q=0 projection chart with division.

The affine-line criterion needs no imported complex-lift assumption.

When rank L_x=2 and its target is compatible, all real solutions have the
form

 M(r)=A+r W, W!=0.                                 (5)

Then (3) is equivalent simply to existence of any PSD matrix in (5).
Indeed a nonconstant affine line cannot remain inside the PSD cone for all
r: dividing by |r| in both directions would force W and -W PSD, hence W=0.
Starting at a PSD point, its intersection with this closed cone therefore
has a finite boundary point. A positive definite matrix is an interior
point of the cone, so that boundary point has rank<=1. This proves the
equivalence without assuming N9 already provides a complex lift.

Write

 det(A+rW)=a r^2+b r+c,  a=det W,
 Delta=b^2-4ac.

If a<0 and Delta>0, put r0=-b/(2a) and B=A+r0 W. Then

 det B=-Delta/(4a)>0.                              (6)

The two determinant roots bound an interval of definite matrices. Its sign
cannot change within the interval, so both rank-one endpoints have the
same semidefinite sign. They are PSD exactly when tr B>0, and NSD exactly
when tr B<0. Assuming q(x)!=0 excludes a zero endpoint. Thus this chart has
a literal future/past distinction in the Lorentz cone Sym2_+:

 actual conjugate critical lift <=> tr B>0.          (7)

The discriminant condition in (6) must be checked. If a<0 and Delta<0,
there is no real rank-one endpoint and no PSD solution. If Delta=0 the
only possible endpoint is B itself; use B>=0, equivalently tr B>=0 because
det B=0. No discriminant implication from N9 alone is asserted here.

If a>0, W is definite. Large positive and negative r give opposite definite
signs, so one PSD and one NSD boundary endpoint always exist. In this chamber
there is no actual-versus-nonactual sign obstruction. The discriminant is
automatically nonnegative (strictly positive for the nonzero target case).

The remaining elementary cases cover a=0. If b!=0, there is one determinant
root r=-c/b and the lift exists iff M(r)>=0. If b=0,c!=0, there is no rank-one
endpoint. If a=b=c=0, the entire line has rank<=1; since W!=0 it includes
both PSD and NSD matrices. These statements include zero moments.

A rational positive metric detecting the past sheet.

On a<0 define

 C=tr(W^2) I-tr(W) W.                              (8)

Direct identities give tr(CW)=0 and

 C00=W01^2+W11^2-det W>0,
 det C=(-det W)((W00-W11)^2+4W01^2)>0.

Hence C is PD. Because tr(CW)=0, it lies in the trace-dual row space of L_x.
Solve L_x^T d=(C00,2C01,C11). Only two independent output rows are needed.
The exact verifier consistently uses selected output coordinates0 and1.
Then

 K^T H_d K/2=C>0,
 d.q(x)=tr(CM(r))=tr(CB).                          (9)

Under Delta>0 the sign of (9) is exactly the sign in (7). A negative value
directly excludes a real y: q_d(y)>0 for every nonzero y in ker H_x, whereas
q_d(x)<0. This dynamic metric is not restricted to the previous35 critical
directions. It excludes uniform orbit7 even though all35 tests passed.

All coordinate choices respect real kernel changes: M transforms by real
congruence and PSD/NSD are preserved. The numerical value of tr B depends
on this choice, but its sign on the definite midpoint does not.

Lower moment ranks and scope.

For rank L=3 the unique real moment must additionally have determinant0;
PSD alone does not ensure a lift. A separately proved complex rank-one lift
would force determinant0, giving the initially proposed sign criterion.
This rank3 case does not occur on the rank12 HMSV secant locus above.

For rank L=1 first check target compatibility. Choose a nonzero image vector
v and write L(ss^T)=v(s^T R s), q(x)=lambda v. The exact remaining test is
whether the real binary quadratic form R represents lambda. If lambda>0 it
needs a positive direction; if lambda<0 it needs a negative direction;
lambda=0 always has s=0. For rank L=0 a lift exists exactly when q(x)=0.
Hessian ranks below12 have larger kernel spaces and require their own
quadratic feasibility analysis; no two-dimensional claim covers them.
