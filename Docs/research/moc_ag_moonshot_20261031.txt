ALGEBRAIC-GEOMETRY MOONSHOT: TWO EXACT OBSTRUCTIONS
31 October 2026

The first pass produced two precise results. The spectral cubic has no real
hyperbolicity direction. Also, an actual unrestricted complex spectral point
has an indefinite moment-kernel direction and admits no decomposition as a
sum of two real critical points. These results rule out two global geometric
shortcuts; they do not close an unresolved MOC4 contact family.

Existing geometry and the rejected Pfaffian identification

The repository already identifies the fourteen-coordinate cubic F with the
HMSV skew cubic by an explicit invertible rational Fourier map. Its critical
cone is the six-dimensional affine M8 cone; its projective singular locus
has dimension five. The exact bridge, source checks and real-lift machinery
are in HMSV_COMPATIBILITY.md, M8_PRODUCT_LINKAGE.md and
CORANK_TWO_LIFT_EXACTNESS.md. None of this identification is new here.

A linear equivalence to a hyperplane restriction of the six-by-six skew
Pfaffian cubic is impossible. The latter's singular locus contains the
rank-at-most-two skew matrices, whose projectivization is Gr(2,6), of
dimension eight. Intersecting with any projective hyperplane leaves a
seven-dimensional variety of singular points of the restricted cubic.
That cannot equal M8's five-dimensional singular locus under an invertible
fourteen-coordinate linear map. This dimension obstruction applies before
any choice of real form or positivity cone.

1. No real hyperbolicity direction for the actual cubic

Theorem. Let F be the repository's real homogeneous cubic. There is no real
direction e with F(e)!=0 for which every polynomial t->F(v+t e) has only
real roots, for every real v.

This statement is stronger than the archived obstruction to hyperbolicity
of the projected nonic in one particular direction. It concerns the cubic
itself and every possible direction. No literature-priority claim is made.

The exact point p=ones14 satisfies grad F(p)=0. Let

  E=H_F(p), Q(v)=v^T E v/2, Q(v,w)=v^T E w/2.

Exact reconstruction gives rank E=4 and characteristic polynomial

  t^10 (t^4+8t^3-42t^2-40t+201).

The quartic has exactly two positive and two negative roots, certified by
exact real-root counting. Thus Q has inertia (2,2,10). Cubic polarization
at the singular point gives, identically,

  F(z p+x)=F(x)+z Q(x).

Fix any proposed direction e with A=F(e)!=0, and choose a real vector v.
Write F(te+v)=A t^3+B t^2+C t+D. Then the restriction through z p+v is

  A t^3+(B+z u)t^2+(C+2z w)t+D+z v0,
  u=Q(e), w=Q(e,v), v0=Q(v).

Its discriminant is a polynomial in z. Three cases exhaust all e.

If u!=0, its z^4 coefficient is 4u^2(w^2-u v0). Since Q has two positive
and two negative directions, the Q-orthogonal complement of a positive e
retains a positive direction, and the complement of a negative e retains a
negative direction. Choose v in that complement with u v0>0. Then w=0,
so the leading coefficient is negative. Large |z| gives negative discriminant.

If u=0 and e is not in the radical of Q, choose v with w!=0. The z^3
coefficient is -32 A w^3, which is nonzero. One sign of sufficiently large
z therefore gives negative discriminant.

If e is in the radical, choose v with v0!=0. The z^2 coefficient is
-27 A^2 v0^2<0. Again large |z| gives negative discriminant.

In every case a real line parallel to e has a cubic with negative
discriminant and therefore one real root and two nonreal conjugate roots.
This contradicts hyperbolicity and proves the theorem.

The proof uses only cubic polarization, inertia and the cubic discriminant.
It does not invoke a hyperbolicity localization theorem or infer signs from
samples. The exact verifier checks the three symbolic discriminant leading
coefficients and produces one concrete line from each branch:

  -5t^3+(93/4)t^2-(695/16)t+1255/64, discriminant -82240;
  18t^3-6t^2-5t-1,                    discriminant -9432;
  -12t^3-26t^2+29t-21,                discriminant -4871948.

Each has exactly one real root by exact root counting. Their full direction
vectors, offsets and singular-point shifts are recorded in the JSON.

Consequently no invertible real coordinate change of F can supply a
hyperbolicity cone. In particular a definite Hermitian linear determinant
representation of this cubic is impossible. An indefinite determinantal
representation or a restricted semialgebraic positivity argument is not
excluded by this theorem.

2. An actual point in the indefinite moment chamber

Take eta=1 and

  a=(-i,-1+3i,3+i,0), b=(2,1+2i,-1-i,2-2i).

Let w be its fourteen selected matching products, x=Re w and y=Im w.
The exact vectors, in the established selected-permutation basis, are

  x=(60,10,-16,-68,4,8,28,-48,-40,12,60,80,36,10),
  y=(20,-80,88,24,-22,24,-16,32,8,-8,-80,10,2,-80).

The verifier checks grad F(w)=0, H_F(x)y=0, grad F(y)=grad F(x)!=0, and
rank H_F(x)=12. Both spectra are nonconcyclic and noncollinear: their
generalized-circle determinants are -50 and -38.

Choose the exact rational kernel basis K returned by the retained verifier,
and define the moment map by

  grad F(K(u,v))=B (u^2,u v,v^2)^T.

It has rank two. Its one-dimensional symmetric kernel is spanned by

  W=[ -621/50975  -1322/50975 ]
    [ -1322/50975      1      ],
  det W=-33403159/2598450625<0.

The full affine line B(M)=grad F(x) is

  M(t)=[ 167132/2039-621t/50975   12192/2039-1322t/50975 ]
       [ 12192/2039-1322t/50975                 t        ],

  det M(t)=-16129(t-6400)(2071t-900)/2598450625.

Its two rank-one endpoints are both PSD:

  M(900/2071)=(412,30)(412,30)^T/2071,
  M(6400)=(2,-80)(2,-80)^T.

The second is the supplied imaginary product vector's moment; explicitly
y=K(2,-80). Thus the first endpoint supplies a second conjugate-critical
decomposition, with imaginary part K(412,30)/sqrt(2071). There are precisely
four signed real lifts on this rank-two chart.

No point of the moment line is NSD. Such a point would require t<=0,
where both determinant factors are negative and det M(t)<0. Therefore x
cannot be the midpoint of two real critical points. Indeed if x=(z1+z2)/2
with real grad F(z1)=grad F(z2)=0, then v=(z1-z2)/2 satisfies
H_F(x)v=0 and grad F(v)=-grad F(x). The negative rank-one moment of v would
be an NSD solution of the displayed moment equation, a contradiction.

This is a counterexample to universal definiteness of the moment-kernel
direction on actual data, and to a universal replacement of conjugate
critical secants by split real critical secants. The earlier repository
example with definite W has both kinds of decomposition; this actual
indefinite example has only conjugate decompositions. A real invariant-cone
point need not come from real spinors, so the no-real-critical-pair statement
is stronger than merely excluding real spectra in such a replacement.

The example does not show failure of MOC. It isolates a real geometric
distinction that any global argument through the critical/secant variety
must preserve. The PSD feasibility test remains exact; it simply has a
bounded feasible interval here rather than the definite-direction behavior.

What this changes for the unrestricted attack

The 35 critical-metric inequalities and the exact rank-two lift test cannot
be replaced by one hyperbolicity cone for F, nor can all actual points be
moved to the earlier definite/split-real chart. The useful remaining target
is a certificate that respects both real moment chambers and couples PSD
lift feasibility to the support objective. This pass supplies no such
global positivity certificate and no additional six-, seven-, or eight-
contact closure. Its two counterexamples prevent those omitted hypotheses
from entering a purported unrestricted proof.
