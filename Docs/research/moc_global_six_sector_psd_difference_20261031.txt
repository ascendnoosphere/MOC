1. Coordinates, without contact or budget restrictions

Use the normal form in moc_global_cofactor_diagonal_form_20261031.txt:
 Q_g(H)=<A,cof H>+sum B_ij H_ij^2,
 D=(8/9)K^T B K,
 g(O)=c+4<A,O>+sum D_ij O_ij^2.
The 24 tetrahedral signed-permutation matrices O have product of their
three nonzero entries +1. They split into six sectors sigma in S3, each
containing four sign vectors epsilon with epsilon_1 epsilon_2 epsilon_3=1.
Put c_sigma=c+sum_i D_i,sigma(i), a_sigma,i=4A_i,sigma(i).
Then g_sigma(epsilon)=c_sigma+a_sigma^T epsilon.

The six sectors form the vertices of K3,3, according to permutation parity.
Each of the nine coordinate pairs (i,j) is the edge joining its unique even
sector and unique odd sector. There is no choice of a support contact here.

2. Six positive local blocks

Let C_sigma have diagonal c_sigma and offdiagonal entry (i,j) equal to
a_sigma,k for {i,j,k}={1,2,3}. Set
 T_sigma = [[c_sigma,a_sigma^T],[a_sigma,C_sigma]].
If W has rows (1,epsilon^T), then W^T W=4I and
 W T_sigma W^T=4 diag(g_sigma(epsilon)).
Thus g>=0 on all24 permutations is exactly equivalent to all six T_sigma
being positive semidefinite. Their principal blocks C_sigma are PSD too.

Let E_sigma select the three coordinate edges of sigma from vec(H). Then
the exact identity, valid for every g in V14, is
 8M(g)=sum_even E_sigma^T C_sigma E_sigma
       -sum_odd E_sigma^T C_sigma E_sigma.
Each sum is block diagonal after arranging its three disjoint matchings.
The diagonal identity is B_ij=(c_even(i,j)-c_odd(i,j))/8. The cofactor signs
give the offdiagonal identity. The checker verifies every coefficient on
all14 basis functions, and the local Walsh congruence symbolically there.

3. Exact global C0 Gram gluing formulation

A matching function f in V14 can be written, in sector sigma, as
 f_sigma(epsilon)=b_sigma+sum_{e incident sigma} t_e epsilon_e.
There are six scalar sector coefficients b_sigma, one relation
 sum_even b_sigma=sum_odd b_sigma, and nine edge coefficients t_e: 14
independent features in total. For an SOS use vector coefficients b,t.
The required Gram identities are
 c_sigma=||b_sigma||^2+sum_{e incident sigma}||t_e||^2,
 a_e=2<b_sigma,t_e>+2<t_other1,t_other2>
at both endpoints of every coordinate edge, together with the vector
relation sum_even b_sigma=sum_odd b_sigma.
These formulas follow by expanding the squared norm and using
epsilon_i epsilon_j=epsilon_k. They are equivalent to all24 interpolation
conditions, so any proposed global construction can be checked directly.

The six independent local choices T_sigma/4 are PSD but generally assign
different variances c_sigma/4 to the same shared edge at its two endpoints.
They therefore do not already give a global14 Gram. A neutral difference
of the two nine-dimensional PSD sums suggests using a cost-dependent
generalized spectral decomposition or parallel sum; no synchronization
identity has been established. A common five-plane depending only on the
dual functional was refuted earlier, so that ansatz is not assumed here.
