Coordinates
-----------
Permutations have the lexicographic zero-based order used in the exact
terminal-face census. Write f=r46 and ordinary Boolean matching functions
as r_j. The five contacts are [0,1,6,7,17]. The nonlocal PR46 cap has basis

  [f,r3,r6,r8,r13,r18,r19,r28,r33].

Its costs are

  g0=lambda*f+sum_j v_j*r_j,

where lambda>0 and the eight ordinary budgets v_j are nonnegative. All
nine functions vanish at the five contacts. The four Hardy triples are

  (i,j;k)=(3,8;33),(6,13;28),(3,13;18),(6,8;19).

An exact 4 by 4 identity
-----------------------
For EACH of these four triples, on the feature vector [f,r_i,r_j,r_k],

          [ lambda   0       0      -lambda ]
  G_H =   [ 0        x      -lambda  lambda ]
          [ 0       -lambda  y       lambda ]
          [-lambda   lambda  lambda  z      ]

has pointwise quadratic value

  lambda*f+x*r_i+y*r_j+z*r_k

on all 24 permutations. The proof is direct exact evaluation of all
coefficients in lambda,x,y,z. It uses the Boolean identities r_j^2=r_j
and the actual matching function f; no arbitrary stable substitute enters.

For lambda>0 and x,y,z>=0 this matrix is PSD if and only if

  x*y>lambda^2,
  z*(x*y-lambda^2)>=lambda*(x+lambda)*(y+lambda).                 (1)

Indeed, eliminating the first diagonal entry gives

  [ x       -lambda  lambda   ]
  [-lambda   y       lambda   ]
  [ lambda   lambda  z-lambda ].

The leading 2 by 2 block must be positive definite: at equality x*y=
lambda^2 its positive null vector is not orthogonal to (lambda,lambda).
Its final Schur complement is exactly (1). Thus the cubic

  H_e=z*(x*y-lambda^2)-lambda*(x+lambda)*(y+lambda)

being nonnegative already gives an explicit full matching-SOS certificate
for g0, by using this one block and the remaining ordinary squares.

The small exact boundary regression is especially transparent:

  f+2*r3+2*r8+3*r33
    =(f-r33)^2+(r3-r8)^2+(r3+r33)^2+(r8+r33)^2.

The four squared functions are linearly dependent, so the Gram rank is 3.

A three-parameter common pencil
-------------------------------
Choose four real numbers ell_e whose sum is lambda. Start with the
9 by 9 diagonal matrix diag(lambda,v3,v6,v8,v13,v18,v19,v28,v33).
For each triple (i,j;k), add the off-diagonal entries

  G[f,r_k]=-ell_e, G[r_i,r_j]=-ell_e,
  G[r_i,r_k]=G[r_j,r_k]=ell_e,

with symmetric transposes. The resulting matrix interpolates g0 EXACTLY
for every split. This is a three-free-parameter affine Gram family.
It includes all four one-block certificates, but its universal sufficiency
on the sextic branch has not been established.

For nonnegative ell_e, flipping the signs of f and all four corner
features r18,r19,r28,r33 makes every off-diagonal entry nonpositive.
Consequently PSD completion in this family is an M-matrix problem.
If a positive vector w satisfies G*w>=0 after this sign change, the exact
decomposition is

  G=sum_(i<j) (-G_ij)*w_i*w_j
       *(e_i/w_i-e_j/w_j)*(e_i/w_i-e_j/w_j)^T
       +diag((G*w)_i/w_i).

Every coefficient is nonnegative. This turns such a completion into
explicit squares of linear combinations of at most two matching features.

Relation to the restricted nonic
--------------------------------
Use S60=F*kappa*D0/4-P6/1024, with the precise F,kappa,P6 definitions in
the full factorization note. Adding t*r21 changes D by -4t and gives
S6(t)=S60-F*kappa*t. On the PR46 cap F<0,kappa<0. Thus a nonnegative
off-F nonic root has t>=0 precisely when S60>=0.

On EACH single Hardy subcone, with all five unused ordinary budgets zero,
the exact restriction is

  S60=lambda*(lambda+x)*(lambda+y)*H_e.                        (2)

Therefore the entire single-Hardy off-F branch has the explicit SOS above.
Equation (2) is checked symbolically from the ten-coordinate nonic formula,
independently of the pointwise Gram calculation.

A possible short uniform extension would be

  S60>=0 ==> max(H_33,H_28,H_18,H_19)>=0.                       (3)

This implication is OPEN here. The fixed previously generated parent
corpus (seed 79631, 1000 log-uniform base budgets) has 57 retained
nonnegative-height sextic roots. All 57 satisfy at least one individual
Hardy cubic and therefore numerically fit a one-block certificate. The
corpus and the exact interpolation family are saved separately; finite
numerical coverage does not prove (3).

One bounded coefficient-cone attempt at (3) was also run:

  -(v18+v19+v28+v33)*S60 = R+sum_e A_e*(-H_e),

where R and A_e were required to have nonnegative monomial coefficients,
deg A_e=4 and deg R=7. The numerical LP solver reported infeasibility.
This is only a rejected certificate shape, not an exact refutation of (3).
The script/report are moc_h_five_hardy_union_certificate.py and its
build/moc_h_five_hardy_union_certificate/probe.json.

Complete kernel on this face
---------------------------
The nine-feature quadratic evaluation map has rank 19, so its Gram kernel
has dimension 45-19=26. All of it is generated by the following 28 exact
disjoint ordinary products, interpreted as symmetric Gram directions:

  (3,17),(3,19),(3,22),(3,28),
  (6,18),(6,20),(6,27),(6,33),
  (8,17),(8,18),(8,27),(8,28),
  (13,19),(13,20),(13,22),(13,33),
  (17,18),(17,19),(17,20),(18,19),(18,20),(19,20),
  (22,27),(22,28),(22,33),(27,28),(27,33),(28,33).

The verifier proves that all 28 products vanish pointwise, that their
Gram-coordinate span has rank 26, and that every resulting direction is
annihilated by the full quadratic evaluation matrix. Thus failure of the
three-parameter subfamily would leave 23 further Gram-kernel directions;
it would not by itself be a full matching-SOS obstruction.
