An exact auxiliary-PPT certificate for an actual nonordinary support
31 October 2026

The exact rational certificate below proves one actual spectral support
inequality for every real orthogonal four-by-four mixer. Together with the
established four-dimensional real-profile convexification, it proves that
same supporting inequality for every complex unitary mixer. It proves only
the stated direction, not all directions for these spectra and not MOC4.

The actual input is
 a=(0,100,-214+146i,269-294i),
 b=(-207-148i,-28-84i,-59+82i,83+104i),
 eta=-25+i.
For pi in lexicographic S4, set z_pi=product_i(a_i+b_pi(i)) and
 g_pi=max_sigma Re(eta z_sigma)-Re(eta z_pi).
The unique zero of g has zero-based permutation index 1, and max_pi g_pi
is the integer 209130300207. The certified assertion is

 Re(eta det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*)) <= max_pi Re(eta z_pi)

for every U in U4. The determinant equality relating signed permutation
amplitudes to this actual product cost is essential in the U4 transfer.
No assertion for arbitrary permutation costs is being made.

The certificate is recorded compactly in
 data/certificates/moc_exact_ppt_jet_certificate.json.
It consists of an integer Hermitian 14-by14 proposal and 27 integer ordinary
weights, all with denominator 10^12. The exact verifier applies a specified
rational contact projection and rational affine correction. Thus the file
is a compact recipe for the final rational matrix and weights, rather than
a floating approximation or a claim that the rounded proposal is itself
an exact identity. The final matrix, weights and pivot fractions are
written to
 build/moc_auxiliary_ppt_jet/exact_nonordinary_support.json.

Run the independent-of-optimizer verification with
 python code/research/moc_exact_ppt_jet_certificate.py

The default command requires SymPy only. It does not load numerical search
output, invoke an optimizer, or use floating-point tolerances. The optional
--construct command rationalizes the saved numerical proposal and is not
needed for verification. Both construction and a subsequent default check
have passed exact verification.

The verifier also uses the exact ordinary-ray vectors in
 data/certificates/moc_ppt_ordinary_rankone_witnesses.json.
It rechecks their evaluations and exceptional partial-transpose square
factors, then absorbs all 27 weighted rays into the single matrix
 C_total=C+sum_j weight_j v_j v_j*.
The report records this pure reduced-cone matrix and its own 13+4 positive
pivot certificates. Its 24 costs equal g/max(g) without separate ordinary
terms. The ordinary variables were useful for numerical conditioning;
they do not enlarge the closed auxiliary cone.

The universal positive cone used by the certificate

Let j_pi be the32-vector of coefficients in
 (product_{i in I} beta_pi(i)(w))_I = f_pi,0+w f_pi,1,
where beta(w)=(0,1,i,w), I ranges over the 16 subsets, and j stacks the two
coefficient vectors. This auxiliary beta is independent of the actual b.
Use the physical convention H_O=sum_pi c_pi j_pi j_pi*, where c_pi is
the oriented determinant amplitude for a real orthogonal O. The universal
twisted-Clifford identity and its affine-parameter lift give

 H_O=P_O+Q_O^Gamma, P_O>=0, Q_O>=0.

Here Gamma transposes only the two-dimensional parameter index. The proof
and exact bookkeeping are documented in
 moc_fixed_b_clifford_kernel_20261031.txt and
 moc_qft_auxiliary_ppt_jet_20261031.txt.

Write J for the 24-by32 matrix with rows j_pi^T and W=range(J^T), dim W=14.
The exceptional matrix Q_O is supported on the real four-dimensional
product subspace E=C^2_parameter tensor span(e1,e2). In the 16-feature mask
space its two basis vectors have only pair coordinates:
 e1: 01,23=1; 02,13=-1; 03,12=0;
 e2: 01,23,02,13=1; 03,12=-2.
The real basis matrix E is diag(e,e), ordered parameter first. One has
E subset W, and P_O is also supported on W.

Consequently any Hermitian T supported on W with
 T>=0, (E*TE)^Gamma>=0
gives a universally positive permutation cost j_pi* T j_pi. Indeed
 tr(H_O T)=tr(P_O T)+tr(Q_O^Gamma T).
The first term is nonnegative. Write Q_O=E D E* with D>=0. Because E is
a real product basis, the second term is
 tr(D (E*TE)^Gamma)>=0.
The basis e need not be orthonormal: the displayed compression and trace
identity use its actual matrix entries. Positivity of the entire 32-by32
partial transpose of T is unnecessary.

Ordinary rays r_IK(pi)=1_{pi(I)=K}, |I|=|K|=1 or2, also have positive
expectation: sum_pi c_pi r_IK(pi)=|det O_IK|^2. Of the 34 distinct rays,
27 vanish at the selected contact and can therefore enter the certificate
with strictly positive coefficients.

Exact construction and checks

Choose a full-column-rank Gaussian-integer basis matrix W0 for W from
pivot columns of J^T. Let psi=J conjugate(W0), and let
k=psi_contact^T. The certificate has T=W0 C W0*, and hence its costs are
conjugate(psi_pi) C psi_pi^T. The exact projector
 P=I-k k*/(k* k)
enforces Ck=0. Starting from the stored rational proposal C0 and weights,
set C=P C0 P and solve the remaining 23 rational cost equations by the
Gram right inverse built from the contact-projected feature projectors
and the 27 ordinary rays. The verifier then checks the final identities
directly; correctness is not inferred from a residual bound.

It verifies all of the following with rational complex arithmetic:

 - The 24 costs equal g/max(g) exactly.
 - C is Hermitian and Ck=0 exactly.
 - All 27 final ordinary weights are strictly positive rational numbers.
 - On a rational basis of k-perp, the 13-by13 Hermitian contact compression
   has 13 strictly positive exact Schur/LDL pivots.
 - The 4-by4 exceptional partial-transpose corner has 4 strictly positive
   exact Schur/LDL pivots.

The Schur calculation takes place in the canonical Gaussian-rational
field QQ(i), avoiding expression-based zero or sign judgments. Each pivot
is exactly real and positive. These checks prove C>=0 with its required
contact kernel, and prove the exceptional corner condition. For orientation
only, the smallest 13-face pivot is approximately 1.0652544e-5, the smallest
exceptional pivot 0.089452886, and the smallest ordinary weight 1.5903403e-7.
No approximate number is used in a proof decision.

What this closes and what remains

The same actual support g has an exact separating certificate against the
combined mechanism consisting of fixed-actual-b multiaffine squared
polynomials, fixed-actual-a inverse-permutation squared polynomials, and
all ordinary minor rays. Those obstructions are in
 moc_multiaffine_sos_obstruction_20261031.txt and
 moc_two_sided_sos_obstruction.py.
The closure and inversion theorems in
 moc_qft_auxiliary_cone_closure_20261031.txt
now additionally show that the whole old two-sided cone, with ordinary
rays, is contained in the new reduced auxiliary cone. The exact rank-one
ordinary witnesses show that adding ordinary rays does not enlarge it.
The ingredients are the genuine two-by-two PPT separability theorem,
the exact generic fixed-parameter rank calculation, a frame bound proving
closedness of the cost image, common Mobius normalization of arbitrary
auxiliary spectra, and an exact inversion map whose exceptional sector
is a local invertible map followed by SWAP. Degenerate spectra are
included by the proved closedness. Thus the old exact separation and
the present exact positive certificate establish strict inclusion of
the old cone in the new one. This is a statement about positivity
mechanisms, not universal membership of actual product supports.

This is a rigorous instance of the new universal positivity mechanism,
not a proof of universal actual-cost feasibility. The full remaining
obligation is to show that every actual spectral supporting gap has such
a certificate, or supply a different complete argument. Numerical passes,
strict feasibility at this example, and the exact Clifford kernel alone
do not settle that obligation.
