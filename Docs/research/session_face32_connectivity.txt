Connectedness of the positive-height P=0 branch on face [3,21,27,32,53]

Let a,b,c,d>=0, and set

 F=-(a+bd+b+d+1),  Q=cd-(b+1)(d+1),
 P=N+dT,
 N=(a+1)(c+1)(a+b+1),
 T=-a^2 c+ac^2+a+c^2+c+1
   +b(a^2-ac+a+c^2+c+1).

On P=0, N>0 forces d>0, T<0, and d=-N/T. The coefficient of b
in T is a^2-ac+c^2+a+c+1>0, and T at b=c is
(c+1)(a+c^2+c+1)>0. Hence T<0 implies k=c-b>0. Put c=b+k.
Then

 T=-k a^2+L a+M,
 L=bk+k^2+b+1>0,  M=(b+1)(c^2+c+1)>0.

T has exactly one positive zero, a_T(b,k), so T<0 iff a>a_T.
Furthermore

 d=(c+1)[a^2+(b+2)a+b+1]/[k a^2-L a-M]

decreases strictly in a throughout that ray: its derivative is

 -(c+1)[(L+k(b+2))a^2+2(M+k(b+1))a
          +M(b+2)-L(b+1)]/[k a^2-L a-M]^2.

The corrected constant term satisfies
 M(b+2)-L(b+1)=(b+1)[b^3+2b^2k+3b^2+bk^2+4bk+2b+k^2+2k+1]>0.
It tends to infinity as a descends to a_T, and to (c+1)/k as
a tends to infinity. Since Q=(k-1)d-b-1, Q<0 is automatic when
k<=1. For k>1, it holds on an upper ray in a, which is nonempty
exactly when k^2-k-b-1<0. Thus the projection to (b,k) consists
of b>=0, k>0, b>k^2-k-1. This is a connected epigraph; the
strict positive-b part is path connected. Above every point, the
admissible values of a form an upper ray with continuous threshold.
For k>1 the threshold is the unique zero of Q; for k<=1 it is a_T.
Continuity at k=1 follows by taking any fixed a>a_T and using Q<0
there. Consequently the interior branch is path connected. The b=0
boundary consists of limits of interior points by strictness of T<0
and Q<0. There are no a=0, c=0, or d=0 points on this branch.

For inertia propagation, one must separately prove that H_x has
corank at least two on P=0 and that the z^2 coefficient of
det(H_x+zH_e) is nonzero. The latter alone does not prove the former.
Together they imply corank exactly two and nonsingularity of the
restriction of H_e, hence constant signature on the connected branch.
At (a,b,c,d)=(8,1,2,15), exact computation gives F=-40, Q=-2,
h=20, and the restriction of Q to a nullspace basis has positive
upper-left entry 2672/606841 and positive determinant 135/9709456.
See build/face32/anchor_simple.py.
