1. Necessary spectral cubic and projected determinant
-----------------------------------------------------
Use the exact homogeneous cubic F in 14 selected matching coordinates from
code/geometry/closing_moc4_model_verify.py. Write e=ones(14), H_v=H_F(v),
and Q=D_e F. The verified model gives grad F(z)=0 for actual complex
spectral matching vectors and H_e e=0. For an actual support gap, put
x=g-he and let y be the imaginary part of the scaled spectral vector.
Then

 H_x y=0,      grad F(y)=grad F(x),      F(x)=0,
 F(g)=hQ(g),   Q(x)=Q(g).

The F(x) identity follows from Euler's identity and cubic polarization:
3F(x)=x.grad F(y)=y^T H_x y/2=0. The Hessian is singular also when y=0:
then H_x x=2grad F(x)=0, and x cannot vanish because F(g)<0 below.
No converse spectral-realization assertion is used.

Let e_i denote the elementary symmetric functions of a,b,c,d. Exact
restricted cubic evaluation gives

 F(g)=-(1+e1+e2+e3+pqr)<0,
 Q(g)=-(1+pq+pr+qr)<0.                                         (2)

The verifier checks these identities on a degree3-unisolvent120-node grid.
Thus every actual normalized gap (1) has h=F/Q>0; h=0 and negative height
are impossible in this child. There is no Q=0 component on the nonnegative
coefficient orthant.

Define

              N(g)=Q(g)^2 det H_(g-F(g)e/Q(g)).                 (3)

This rational expression is a polynomial of degree at most18. The model's
rank H_e=4, H_e e=0 and H(g)e=H_e g give the direct block proof in
Section4A of docs/geometry/closing_moc4_notes.txt: the Hessian pencil has
degree at most three, and its cubic coefficient is divisible by Q. After
substituting F/Q, (3) therefore has no denominator and degree18. This degree
bound is a mathematical lemma, not inferred from samples.

The complete exact certificate is

                 N(g)=-4 P9(a,b,c,d,p,q,r)^2.                  (4)

Here P9 is the displayed integer coefficient table, with constant1, total
degree9 and multidegrees (3,3,3,3,2,2,2). The checker verifies equivalently

       det(QH_g-FH_e)=-4 Q^12 P9^2                             (5)

at every lower-simplex node of total degree at most18. By the proved degree
bound and unisolvence, (4) holds identically. Singularity and Q!=0 force
P9=0 for every actual gap (1).

2. Two exact positive generating functions
------------------------------------------
For nonnegative a,b,c,d,x,y,z, define

 Gcube=(1+x)^2(1+y)^2(1+z)^2
        P9(a,b,c,d,x/(1+x),y/(1+y),z/(1+z)).                    (6)

Its exact expansion has 6496 nonzero integer coefficients, all strictly
positive, and constant1. The powers2 suffice because the last three
multidegrees of P9 are two. Consequently

         P9>0 whenever a,b,c,d>=0 and 0<=p,q,r<1.               (7)

For nonnegative a,b,c,d,s,x,y, put L=1+x+y and define

 Gp=(1+s)^4 L^4
      P9(a,b,c,d,1+s,x/((1+s)L),y/((1+s)L)).                    (8)

This expansion has 21195 nonzero integer coefficients, all strictly positive,
and constant2. The two analogous transformations with q and r in the
dominant position independently have the same coefficient counts and signs.
The verifier checks all three, so the sign argument does not assume an
unverified permutation symmetry of the polynomial.

The parametrization in (8) covers exactly the relevant strict star region:
if p>=1, q,r>=0 and p(q+r)<1, take

 s=p-1,  x=pq/[1-p(q+r)],  y=pr/[1-p(q+r)].

All are nonnegative, and L=1/[1-p(q+r)]. Thus the positive constant in (8)
proves

              P9>0 if p>=1 and p(q+r)<1.                      (9)

The same holds for the other two dominant coefficients.

These are explicit finite coefficient certificates, not numerical sign tests.
For reference, if C_(alpha,i,j,k) is the coefficient of a^alpha1 b^alpha2
c^alpha3 d^alpha4 p^i q^j r^k in P9, the coefficient of ordinary monomial
alpha times x^e y^f z^g in (6) is

 sum_(i,j,k) C_(alpha,i,j,k)
    binom(2-i,e-i) binom(2-j,f-j) binom(2-k,g-k).

The coefficient of ordinary monomial alpha times s^l x^m y^n in (8) is

 sum_(i,j,k) C_(alpha,i,j,k) binom(4+i-j-k,l)
     (4-j-k)! /[(m-j)!(n-k)!(4-m-n)!],

where out-of-range factorial/binomial terms are zero. These formulas are
implemented with integer binomial/multinomial arithmetic and produce the
reported positive expansions.

3. Nonic vanishing forces an established weighted certificate
-------------------------------------------------------------
An actual gap has P9=0. Equation (7) therefore implies that at least one
of p,q,r is at least one. Relabel notation so p>=1. Equation (9) then forces

                              p(q+r)>=1.                       (10)

In particular q+r>0. Each edge of (A,B,C) is an exact relabeling of the
proved continuous weighted-minor theorem, so for every tau>0,

 f+tau A+tau^(-1)B>=0,        f+tau A+tau^(-1)C>=0.              (11)

The portable verifier reconstructs all three compensator pairs
(18,23),(18,28),(23,28). The analytic input is the already proved theorem
in docs/geometry/global_weighted_minor_notes.txt.

Choose tau=1/(q+r) and lambda=q/(q+r). The convex combination of the two
inequalities in (11), with weights lambda and 1-lambda, is

                        f+tau A+q B+r C>=0.                    (12)

Equation (10) gives p>=tau. Adding (p-tau)A and the four remaining ordinary
terms in (1) proves g(U)>=0 for every complex unitary U. Other choices of
the dominant coefficient use the corresponding two edges of the triangle.
This handles equality in (10) and all zero coefficient boundaries.

4. Status and limits of the result
-----------------------------------
The complete exact verification closes the full seven-parameter child (1),
with independent a,b,c,d. The earlier
