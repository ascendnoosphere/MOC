Two rational polynomial capacities for the three critical budgets
2026-10-31. All ordinary-ray indices use the canonical cap34 convention.

For arbitrary t,a,b,c>=0, set
  g=t f34+a r18+b r23+c r28,
  S=ab+ac+bc, T=abc,
  D5=tS-2T-t^3, D6=3S-4t^2.
Either D5>=0 or D6>=0 is a sufficient full matching-SOS condition.
In particular EVERY nonnegative determinant zero in this three-budget
family has a full matching-SOS certificate. This is a small-core theorem;
the unrestricted ALL-N9 theorem is false elsewhere and is not inferred.

THE RATIONAL GRAM

If t=0, g is an ordinary sum of Boolean squares. Suppose t>0. Under either
capacity, S>=t^2>0. In the four matching functions(f34,r18,r23,r28), use
the block Gram diag(t,G), where
  G=[[a,-tab/S,-tac/S],[-tab/S,b,-tbc/S],[-tac/S,-tbc/S,c]].
The three off-diagonal entries sum to-t. Direct exact interpolation at
ALL24 permutations proves that its quadratic evaluation is exactly g.

The diagonal principal minors are a,b,c>=0. Its2x2 principal minors are
  ab(S^2-t^2ab)/S^2,
  ac(S^2-t^2ac)/S^2,
  bc(S^2-t^2bc)/S^2.
These are nonnegative because
  S^2-t^2ab=S(S-t^2)+t^2(ac+bc)>=0,
and similarly. Finally
  det G=abc*N/S^3,
  N=S^3-t^2S^2-2t^3T.
Thus S>=t^2 and N>=0 suffice for PSD, by the complete principal-minor
criterion for a real symmetric3x3 matrix. This proof includes zero
budgets; no division by a,b,c occurs.

CAPACITY D5

From D5>=0 and t>0, t(S-t^2)>=2T>=0, hence S>=t^2. The exact identity
  N=(S-t^2)^2(S+t^2)+t^3D5
then proves N>=0.

The universal9x9 determinant on this entire family is
  2^32 det M(g)=-t^3(a+t)(b+t)(c+t)D5.
Consequently a nonnegative positive-cap determinant zero has D5=0 and
the displayed rational Gram is PSD. An actual cost has det M(g)=0 by
the separate source rank-four theorem, but actuality is not needed for
the conclusion once this determinant vanishes.

CAPACITY D6

This sufficient condition covers a different balanced regime; it is not
claimed to be the optimal critical-triangle capacity. D6>=0 and t>0 imply
S>=4t^2/3>t^2. Let A=S^3-t^2S^2+2t^3T>0. The exact identity is
  27 N A = S^3(3S-4t^2)(3S-t^2)^2+4t^6(S^3-27T^2).
Its last defect is explicitly nonnegative. With x=ab,y=ac,z=bc,
  2(S^3-27T^2)
   =S[(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2]
     +6[x(y-z)^2+y(z-x)^2+z(x-y)^2]>=0.
Therefore N A>=0, and A>0 gives N>=0, as required.

WHY BOTH CAPACITIES HELP

They have different algebraic shapes at t=1:
  D5=ab+ac+bc-2abc-1,
  D6=3ab+3ac+3bc-4.
The first includes the entire restricted nonic; the second also covers,
for example, a=b=c=.7, where D5<0. Both yield exact sparse polynomial
core conditions for larger-budget proofs, without square roots or a
numerical Gram search. No historical novelty claim is made.
