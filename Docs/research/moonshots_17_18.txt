17. Rational virtual arcs and sharp edge tangency

For lexicographic matching products P_i, define

  L=P1+P6+P14+P23-P0,  H=max_i Re(eta P_i),
  lambda=Re(eta L)-3H,
  p=H-Re(eta P18), q=H-Re(eta P14), r=H-Re(eta P9).

The full cap has lambda>=0 and the triangle-budget test asks
lambda<=C(p,q,r). If lambda<0 this inequality holds automatically,
since all gaps and C are nonnegative. For each i in {18,14,9}, with
j,k the other two, define

  W_i(t)=(4t L+4t^2 P_i+P_j+P_k)/(12t+4t^2+2),  0<=t<=1/2.

For fixed alpha,beta, the triangle inequality for all complex eta is
equivalent to containment of every W_i(t) in the convex hull of the
24 P_i. The dual edge-budget variables are the symmetric point
(1/2,1/2,1/2) and permutations of (t,1/(4t),1/(4t)) with
0<t<=1/2; the support-function test gives the displayed points.
This is an exact equivalent geometric problem, not a proof of it.

Each W_i is a positive rational quadratic Bezier arc. Its endpoints
are the pair midpoint (P_j+P_k)/2 and the common point

  W_*=(2L+P18+P14+P9)/9,

and its control point is V_i=(L+P_j+P_k)/5. A sufficient strengthening
would put W_* and all three V_i in the hull, but these stronger
statements are not claimed. In gap coordinates they are the four
inequalities 2lambda<=p+q+r and lambda<=p+q,p+r,q+r.

At the independently found real-lift candidate
(lambda,p,q,r)=(1,1/2,2,0), the q-centered dual slack is

  2t+(1/2)/(4t)-1 = (4t-1)^2/(8t).

Thus its supporting line is tangent to the q-centered arc at t=1/4,
while the q-centered control inequality lambda<=p+r fails. Whether
this candidate has a finite spectral reconstruction is a separate
question; a real critical-point lift alone does not establish that.

18. Finite edge normals and two-contact sufficiency

For fixed alpha,beta with a two-dimensional product hull, it suffices
to check the triangle inequality at the outward normals of that hull's
edges. In the normal cone of any hull vertex, every eta is a
nonnegative combination of the two incident edge normals. The support
height, all gaps and lambda are linear on this cone. The function C
is concave and positively homogeneous: combine feasible pair-budget
allocations and use concavity of the geometric mean. Consequently
C-lambda at an interior direction is at least the corresponding
nonnegative linear combination of its values at the edge normals.

Each edge normal gives at least two permutation contacts. Therefore,
to establish the candidate for all actual gaps it is enough to prove
it on actual gaps with at least two contacts. Product hulls of dimension
zero or one are limits of two-dimensional product hulls; perturb the
spectral parameters and use continuity of all products, the maximum,
lambda, and C to pass to the limit. This is a reduction to two-contact
gaps, not a resolution of those gaps or of the candidate.

The Gaussian-integer alpha,beta from
actual_gap_outside_ordinary_cone.txt have a two-dimensional product
hull. Portable code/research/verify_f34_triangle_hull_edges.py checks
the candidate exactly at all nine outward hull-edge normals. The
normal-cone argument therefore proves the candidate for that fixed
alpha,beta and every complex eta, extending the earlier single-eta
check. It does not establish the candidate for arbitrary spectra.
