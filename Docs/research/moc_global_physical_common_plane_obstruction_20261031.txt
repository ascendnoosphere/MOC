1. The common-plane claim being refuted

Let y_O be the actual signed-Leibniz determinant functional of a real
SO4 mixer. Normalize by y_O(f34)<0, and put

A common positive-five-plane comparison working for every nonnegative
matching cost with this physical mixer would in particular require
 C|W>0, (R7+beta7 C)|W>=0, (R10+beta10 C)|W>=0.             (1)
The plane may depend arbitrarily on O. It cannot depend on the actual
spectral source if it is to give a single mixer-only comparison.

Even a more general pointwise positive-map identity has these necessary
conditions. If its positive metric is C0, evaluation at f34 gives
 C|W>=-y_O(f34) C0>0; replacing C0 by C|W/(-y_O(f34)) only enlarges the
ordinary corrections because their y_O scores are nonnegative. Thus it
also implies (1). The sign-reversed inverse-pencil version is excluded
below as well.

functionals, not only for artificial SOS-dual moments. An analytic formula
