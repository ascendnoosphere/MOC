Eight-contact orbit 4: full five-parameter closure

Let

 g=f34+a r0+b r7+c r17+d r18+e r23,    a,b,c,d,e>=0.

For the verified spectral cubic F and Q=D_1 F,

 F(g)=-(b+1)(a+c+1),  Q(g)=-(c+de+1).

Consequently Q is never zero and the actual height is recovered as
h=F/Q>0. An actual gap must have singular H_x, where x=g-h 1.
The exact five-variable determinant identity is

 det H_x = -4(b+1)^4(e+1)^2(a+c+1)^4 P^2/(c+de+1)^2,

 P=(d+1)(1-de)+b(d+1)+a(b+d+1).

Every prefactor in the determinant is nonzero. If de<1, each summand
in the displayed expression for P is nonnegative and its first term
is strictly positive. Thus P cannot vanish, contradicting the actual
spectral Hessian singularity. It follows that de>=1 for every actual
gap in this family. The established weighted-minor inequality for the
pair (r18,r23) then gives f34+d r18+e r23>=0 on U(4), and all other
ordinary terms are nonnegative. The zero mixed-coefficient case is
already an ordinary-minor sum; positive mixed coefficients are reduced
to one by scaling.

code/research/verify_eight_contact_orbit4.py reconstructs the cubic and
the full cubic Hessian pencil from the certified ray data and checks
the cleared polynomial identity coefficientwise. A fresh run passed in
96.98 seconds. The determinant condition is used only as a necessary
condition; no converse spectral-realization claim is made. This closes
this single family, not unrestricted MOC4.
