The sign and factor in the proposed identity are correct. The result
holds for every actual complex spectral source, not just at stationary
points or on a particular contact face. It is a symmetry identity; it
does not establish a positive weighted-Hessian contradiction under a
strict permutation support gap.

Let
  f(U)=Re[eta det(D_a+U D_b U*)],
with arbitrary complex spectra and eta. Use the right exponential chart
  X -> f(U exp X)
on skew-Hermitian matrices. Let g be its differential and H its symmetric
second derivative at0. The12 flag generators are the off-diagonal real
and imaginary generators Eij-Eji and i(Eij+Eji), i<j. Their norm is1 for
the invariant inner product <X,Y>=-tr(XY)/2.

For a diagonal traceless skew-Hermitian D, put
  v=U*DU=v_off+v_diag.
Left multiplication by exp(tD) preserves f, since it commutes with D_a.
Right multiplication by a diagonal unitary preserves f, since it
commutes with D_b. Thus, for every off-diagonal K, BCH gives
  0=d_t d_s f(U exp(tv)exp(sK))
    =H(v,K)+g([v,K])/2,
  0=d_s d_t f(U exp(sK)exp(td))
    =H(K,d)+g([K,d])/2
for diagonal d. Subtracting the second identity with d=v_diag yields
  H(K,v_off)=g([K,v_off]/2+[K,v_diag]).                       (1)

Project the commutators onto the off-diagonal flag, because g vanishes
on diagonal directions. Let B_D have row K equal to the coordinates of
  [K,v_off]/2+[K,v_diag].
Then the complete matrix identity is
  H v_off=B_D g.                                            (2)
Invariance of the inner product makes each projected commutator map
skew, so B_D^T=-B_D. Consequently g^T H v_off=0 even away from
stationarity. In general H v_off is NOT zero away from stationarity;
dropping the connection-gradient term would be incorrect.

At a stationary U, g=0, all left-phase orbit vectors v_off lie in ker H.
Their rank can be determined exactly from U's nonzero-entry graph.
Construct the bipartite graph with row vertices i, column vertices j,
and edge ij when Uij!=0. If it has c connected components, then
  rank[D -> off(U*DU)]=n-c.                                 (3)
Indeed its kernel consists of diagonal D for which U*DU=D' is diagonal.
The equation D U=U D' says d_i=d'_j on every nonzero edge. Both phase
labels must be constant on each graph component. Unitarity makes every
component square, and the one trace-zero constraint leaves dimension
c-1 in the(n-1)-dimensional traceless diagonal algebra. This proves(3).

Therefore, in any dimension, the complete right-flag Hessian obeys
  rank H<=n(n-1)-(n-c)=n(n-2)+c
at a stationary point. For n=4 this is rank H<=8+c. Connected support
gives rank H<=9, even if some entries of U vanish. A monomial matrix has
c=4 and receives no such flag nullity bound. Thus rank<=9 is not an
unconditional assertion about every stationary U.
