Let F be the24-by14 integer straightening matrix for the24 bipartite

The constant matching function is Fp=1 for an explicit real p. If

where F_pi K_j F_pi^T=0 for all24 pi. A successful full-SOS construction
must choose the81 real parameters, or an equivalent nonlocal kernel,

2. Raw local Bergman measures cannot supply the missing Gram

The previously established actual sharp gap

 g=f34+r23+r28=f34^2+(r23-r28)^2

has eleven contacts. The full matching functions vanishing on those
contacts form the3-space span(f34,r23,r28). Write a possible raw critical
source factor as

 v=A f34+B r23+C r28.

Restricting ALL14 defining critical quadrics of M8 to this3-space gives
the exact ideal

 (A^2, B*C).                                                (2)

Thus the only real OR complex critical-source directions in this
contact-null plane are r23 and r28. If g were a positive sum/integral
of |v(source)|^2 with source ranging over ANY part of M8, positivity at
each of the eleven zero entries would force almost every contributing
source into this plane. Equation(2) would then force g into the cone
generated by r23 and r28, which it is not.

This excludes local positive measures of raw critical profiles even if
all source moduli and all entry curves are allowed. It does NOT exclude
nonlocal kernels, normal jets, or contact-conditional projections: their
square factors can leave M8. Those are precisely the mechanisms that
must be retained in a viable positive-kernel construction.

5. Positive holomorphic factors and height dependence

Two elementary global design constraints apply before any theta formula
is tested.

(a) On a complex source patch with a fixed active permutation pi0,
g_pi=Re[eta(z_pi0-z_pi)] is harmonic in the source variables. If a fixed
positive Bergman metric represented it as sum_l |f_l,pi(source)|^2 with
holomorphic factors, applying the Laplacian would force all factors
constant. A nonconstant gap therefore requires an explicit nonholomorphic
positive weight, source-dependent Hermitian metric, or branch selection.
The familiar Herglotz identity Im(phi)=Im(z)*K(z,z) includes exactly such
a nonholomorphic factor and is not contradicted.

(b) The positive-representation agent proved that a symmetric Gram
globally real-analytic in source and affine in height cannot be PSD at
every actual support height across all24 unique-max cells. Contact
null-vector identities analytically continue; symmetry would force24
generic nonzero evaluation vectors to be mutually orthogonal in a
14-dimensional form. A workable global formula may be contact-dependent
or nonlinear in height/phase. A fixed positive metric plus a linear
height term is too rigid.

6. Verified primary theta/modular bridge on the FULL source space

E. Freitag and R. Salvati Manni,
The modular variety of hyperelliptic curves of genus three,
arXiv:0710.5920v1; Trans. AMS363(2011),281-312.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/0710.5920

Section1 uses exactly the14 standard Specht polynomials of eight points
on P1. Theorem2.4 identifies their full invariant ring with the ring of
modular forms on a five-dimensional complex ball quotient. This is an
actual full14 modular setting, not a dimension analogy.

Theorem3.1 and Proposition3.2 give the S8-equivariant Thomae ring map
from the35 nonzero even theta fourth powers to degree12 branch-difference
products. They are CUBIC products of degree4 Specht polynomials. For
example, the partition1234|5678 gives the product of the two four-point
Vandermondes, which is a product of three matching polynomials.

Lemma6.1 writes an explicit cubic expression in the14 source coordinates.
Proposition6.2 proves that the common base locus is56 linear P3 pieces.
Thus theta data do not provide a globally regular linear identification

Theorem5.8 gives another14-dimensional space of weight2 Siegel modular
forms after one linear relation. It is a different graded model: its

For the explicit eight-channel theta/Clifford side, a primary reference is
Q. Ren, S. Sam, G. Schrader, B. Sturmfels,
The Universal Kummer Threefold, arXiv:1208.1229v3.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/1208.1229

7. The real-curve positivity hypothesis cannot be silently imported
