One-eigenvalue convexity: exact dual, and the global obstruction
31 October 2026

Question tested.
Can separate convexity move a hypothetical unrestricted MOC counterexample
to a collision, a zero pairing, or an already proved low-dimensional case?
The answer is not supplied by convexity alone. The exact dual below locates
the missing step and an independently replayable actual example prevents an
unconditional descent-to-degeneracy claim.

1. The affine fiber and its exact optimization dual

Fix n, U, a_2,...,a_n, b_1,...,b_n and a support multiplier eta. Let z=a_1.
For each pi in S_n put

 q_pi=product_(i=2..n)(a_i+b_pi(i)),
 p_pi(z)=q_pi z+b_pi(1) q_pi.

Expand the actual determinant as d(z)=D z+E. The coefficient D is precisely
the determinant of the principal (n-1)-by-(n-1) compression obtained by
deleting row/column1. No normality of that compression is asserted.

Define

 alpha_pi=eta(q_pi-D),
 beta_pi=Re[eta(b_pi(1)q_pi-E)].

Writing z=x+iy gives the real support gap

 h(z)=max_pi (Re(alpha_pi)x-Im(alpha_pi)y+beta_pi).          (1)

This is convex polyhedral in (x,y). Its linear-program dual is

 inf_(z in C) h(z)
   = max {sum_pi lambda_pi beta_pi:
          lambda_pi>=0, sum_pi lambda_pi=1,
          sum_pi lambda_pi alpha_pi=0},                   (2)

provided the dual feasible set is nonempty. If it is empty, strict separation
of0 from the convex hull of the finitely many alpha_pi gives a direction v
with Re(alpha_pi v)<0 for every pi, and h(tv) tends to minus infinity.

Proof of (2).
The primal variables are x,y,t, with inequalities
 t>=Re(alpha_pi)x-Im(alpha_pi)y+beta_pi and objective min t.
The dual displayed above is the ordinary LP dual obtained from nonnegative
multipliers of these inequalities. Primal feasibility is automatic by taking
t sufficiently large at x=y=0. If the dual is feasible it gives a finite lower
bound, so the finite LP attains its optimum and finite-dimensional LP strong
duality applies. Equivalently one can enumerate vertices of its polyhedral
epigraph. If the dual is empty, the strict separating direction just given
proves unboundedness directly. The dual set, when nonempty, is compact.

There is an optimal dual law supported on at most three permutations: there
are at most three independent equality constraints (normalization and two
real slope constraints), and a vertex of this compact feasible polytope has
at most that many positive coordinates. Degeneracies can reduce that number.
This does not say that any selected triple works.

If eta!=0, dual feasibility is exactly

             D in conv_C{q_pi}.                          (3)

Thus the recession obstruction is already the rectangular compression
statement R(n-1,n). The repository proves R(n-1,n) equivalent to MOC(n), in
docs/research/RECTANGULAR_COMPRESSION_REDUCTION.md. Replacing (3) by an
appeal to MOC(n-1) would be invalid: the compressed normal matrix generally
is not normal, and the comparison set consists of injections into n columns.
The case eta=0 is trivial: alpha=beta=0 and h=0.

2. What zero-pair and collision values do and do not prove

In n=4, known zero-pair and repeated-eigenvalue theorems give h>=0 at
 z=-b_j and z=a_i (i>=2), respectively. Those are lower bounds at finitely
many points. Convexity propagates upper bounds from vertices; it does not
propagate these lower bounds to their convex hull. A bounded negative
polyhedral basin could avoid all the known points unless some additional
identity excludes it. Formula (2) states exactly the positive-dual objective
bound that such an identity would need to prove.

3. Exact actual fiber with a unique nondegenerate interior minimum

The verifier code/research/moc_coordinate_fiber_exact_obstruction.py proves
with rational arithmetic that, for the Walsh unitary divided by2 and

 a=(z,-1+2i,2,1+2i),
 b=(-1-i,2i,-2-3i,2+2i), eta=1,

the unique global minimum is z=5i/2 with h=195/4. Three active permutations
give strictly positive dual weights1/80,2/5,47/80. The minimizer has no
collision, no zero pairing and no concyclic a spectrum. Every move from it
to one of those known degeneracies strictly increases the gap.

The detailed rational primal/dual certificate is recorded in
docs/research/moc_coordinate_fiber_exact_obstruction_20261031.txt.
This is a method obstruction, not a MOC counterexample: its minimum is
positive. An additional theorem specific to negative minima remains possible.

4. Primary-paper check

Debus, Riener and Schabert, Slices of Stable Polynomials and Connections to
the Grace-Walsh-Szego Theorem, arXiv:2402.05905v3 (Theorem1.8 and introductory
discussion), https://arxiv.org/html/2402.05905v3, proves few-root statements
for coefficient-linear slices of stable univariate polynomials. Its
coincidence results impose symmetric multiaffine building blocks. With U
fixed, d(a,b) is generally not symmetric in the individual a entries or b
entries, and its support maximum is not coefficient-linear in the Vieta
coordinates. Those hypotheses do not hold automatically for (1). The paper
therefore does not justify a collision reduction of this fiber. This is a
hypothesis check, not a claim that stable-polynomial tools cannot help.

Result of this lane.
No unrestricted proof is established here. The exact counterexample to the
unconditional reduction and the rectangular equivalence prevent recycling
that reduction as a global argument. The surviving concrete obligation is
to prove a nonnegative objective in (2) using structure beyond separate
convexity, uniformly in all remaining spectra and U.
