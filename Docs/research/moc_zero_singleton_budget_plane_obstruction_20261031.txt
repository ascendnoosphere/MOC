Zero singleton budget prevents a fixed common five-plane
31 October 2026

Let C=M(f34) and R=M(r7). There is NO5-dimensional real linear subspace W
on which C is positive definite and R is nonnegative. Nevertheless

                   det(C+tR)/det(C)=(1+t)^3,               (1)

so C+tR keeps inertia(5+,4-) for every finite t>=0. Thus constant inertia
of a positive ray pencil does not establish the existence of a fixed
common positive/nonnegative5-plane.

1. General dimension bound

For any real symmetric C,R, if C|W>0 and R|W>=0, then

  dim W <= n_+(R)+n_+(C restricted to ker R).               (2)

To prove this, decompose the ambient space into the positive, negative
and zero spectral subspaces of R. Restrict the projection to the positive
R-subspace to W. Its kernel has dimension at least dim W-n_+(R).
For a vector in this kernel, R has no positive contribution. Nonnegativity
of R|W therefore forces its negative R-coordinates also to vanish. This
kernel lies in ker R, while C remains positive definite on it. Its
dimension is at most n_+(C|ker R), proving(2).

2. Exact application

R has inertia(2+,2-,5zero); equivalently the characteristic polynomial
of16R is

              lambda^5(2lambda-3)^2(2lambda+3)^2/16.

For the exact rational basis N of ker R recorded in the certificate,

  16N^T C N=[0  0  0 -2 -2;
            0  0  2 -2  0;
            0  2  0  0 -2;
           -2 -2  0  0  0;
           -2  0 -2  0 -4].

Its characteristic polynomial is

       lambda(lambda^2+2lambda-20)(lambda^2+2lambda-4).

Each quadratic has one positive and one negative root. Thus the restricted
cap form has inertia(2+,2-,1zero), and(2) bounds dim W by2+2=4.

The same result applies to singleton rays r0,r10,r13 through the exact
cap-preserving signed permutations recorded in the companion moving-plane
certificate. These obstructions concern the zero-budget limit; they do
not forbid planes depending on a strictly positive budget.

Indeed moc_singleton_budget_tilt_20261031.txt constructs rational moving
5-planes for every epsilon>0 with R+epsilon C positive on the plane, and
handles every other ordinary-ray correction for four explicit full-PD
