The auxiliary PPT cone is intrinsically a real matching-function SOS cone
31 October 2026

This note proves an exact simplification of the established positivity
cone. It does not prove that every actual MOC support cost lies in it.
The global containment obligation remains open.

1. Real auxiliary normalization

Let V be the14-dimensional real space of matching-product functions on
the24 permutations in S4. Equivalently, its irreducible Fourier blocks
are [4], [31], [22]. Use beta(w)=(0,1,-1,w), and write

 f_pi(w)_I = product_{i in I} beta(w)_{pi(i)}
           = f_pi,0,I + w f_pi,1,I,
 j_pi=(f_pi,0,f_pi,1) in R32.

The24-by32 real jet table J has rank14, and its column space is V.
This is checked exactly against the existing matching evaluation table.

Let U have the two unnormalized columns e1,e2 from the reduced-PPT note:
 e1_01=e1_23=1, e1_02=e1_13=-1;
 e2_01=e2_23=e2_02=e2_13=1, e2_03=e2_12=-2.
All other entries vanish. Put E=I2 tensor U and

 K=J2 tensor J2,       J2=[[0,1],[-1,0]].

The exact sign-kernel identity is

 sum_pi sign(pi) j_pi j_pi^T = E K E^T.                 (1)

Both sides have rank4. The verifier checks all1024 entries, with no
orthogonal mixer or spectral inputs specialized.

2. The two-rebit identity eliminates the corner condition

For every real symmetric4-by4 matrix R,

 R-R^PT = (R_03-R_12) K,
 tr(KR) = 2(R_03-R_12).                                (2)

Here PT transposes the two-dimensional parameter index only. Therefore

 tr(KR)=0 implies R^PT=R.                              (3)

Suppose T is any complex Hermitian PSD Gram matrix producing a real
matching cost g_pi=j_pi^T T j_pi. Since J is real, replacing T by ReT
preserves all costs and PSD: for every real vector x,
x^T(ReT)x=x^*Tx>=0. The real symmetric matrix ReT is thus also PSD
as a complex Hermitian matrix.

Every matching cost has zero alternating Fourier coefficient. Apply(1):

 0=sum_pi sign(pi)g_pi=tr(K E^T T E).

Its real4-by4 corner R=E^TTE is PSD. Equations(2)-(3) show R^PT=R>=0.
Thus, for matching costs, a real PSD Gram automatically satisfies the
reduced corner-PPT requirement.

Conversely, every reduced-PPT cost already is a sum of squared moduli
of complex matching functions, hence a sum of squares of real matching
functions by splitting real and imaginary parts. We have proved

 C14+4 intersect V
   = {g in V: g_pi=sum_l f_l(pi)^2, each f_l in V real}. (4)

Every ordinary ray is a0/1-valued matching function, so it is its own
square. Adding ordinary rays does not enlarge the right side of(4).
The space V is preserved by permutation inversion. Consequently adding
the inverse-permutation orientation does not enlarge this cone either:
each of its squared functions remains in V.

3. Agreement with the original complex auxiliary normalization

The Mobius map z -> (1+i)z / ((i-1)z+2) sends (0,1,i) to (0,1,-1).
Set a=1+i,b=0,c=i-1,d=2, and

 L=[[d,b],[c,a]],      A=[[d,c],[b,a]].

If J_old uses (0,1,i,w), the exact32-by32 jet transformation is

 J_real=J_old M^T,    M=(L^{-1} tensor A^{tensor4})/4.  (5)

Indeed the product of the three fixed denominators is4; multiplication
by the remaining denominator clears the fractional parameter change.
On the exceptional feature space A^{tensor4}U=8i U. Consequently

 M E=E[(2i L^{-1}) tensor I2].                         (6)

Thus M preserves the exceptional corner by an invertible local filter
on its parameter factor. Congruence preserves the PSD condition and
corner-PPT condition. Compression back to the14-dimensional jet range
preserves both costs and the corner, since E lies in that range.
Equations(5)-(6) justify using(4) for the original complex-triple cone.
They are verified exactly, not inferred from numerical normalization.

4. A uniform alternating-gauge Clifford Gram identity

For real orthogonal O let c_pi be its signed Leibniz amplitudes. The
independently established Clifford-jet theorem, now with real auxiliary
triple, gives

 H_O=sum_pi c_pi j_pi j_pi^T = P+Q^PT,
 P>=0, Q=r r^T>=0, r=E u with u in R4.                (7)

The holomorphic and exceptional coefficient factors are real in this
normalization. The real rank-one two-rebit identity is

 (u u^T)^PT-u u^T = -det(mat2(u)) K.                  (8)

Write delta_O=det(mat2(u)). Equations(1),(7),(8) yield

 H_O+delta_O H_sign = P+Q>=0.                          (9)

Thus one alternating signed-amplitude gauge makes the complete real
matching jet kernel PSD. This is an explicit universal matrix identity.
For a matching SOS cost the alternating term pairs to zero, so(9)
directly proves its positivity on every real orthogonal mixer. The
established SO4-to-U4 profile convexification gives the corresponding
all-unitary statement in dimension4.

5. What remains

The proposed broad global bridge now has a finite, auxiliary-free form:

 g>=0 pointwise, Q(g)!=0, N9(g)=0  ==>  g is SOS in V. (10)

For actual support costs only, the same implication is sufficient for
MOC4. Neither version of(10) follows from the cone identity(4). The
nonic is not globally hyperbolic in direction f34: the existing
uniform-orbit7 quintic has four nonreal roots. Thus a Garding-cone
argument needs substantially more structure than hyperbolicity of N9.
