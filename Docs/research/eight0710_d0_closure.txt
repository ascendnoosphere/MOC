Eight-contact cap [0,7,10,18,22,34]: closure of the d=0 boundary

Let g=a r0+b r7+c r10+e r22+r34 with a,b,c,e>=0, i.e. the coefficient
of r18 is d=0. Here F=-(c+1)(ab+a+b+e+1)<0 and Q=-e-1<0, so the
height h=F/Q is always positive. The exact full-cap spectral determinant
is -4(c+1)^2 P^2/Q^2, and at d=0 its polynomial factors as
 P=(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)(ab+a+b+e+1) J,
 J=(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)+e(c+1-ab).
A real spectral lift forces P=0. Therefore ab>c+1 and
 e=(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)/(ab-c-1). Put delta=ab-c-1>0,
 Aa=a+c+1, Bb=b+c+1. The following vector k, with omitted entries zero,
is checked to satisfy H_F(g)k=H_F(E)k=0 throughout this branch:
 k0=-(c+1)Bb, k3=(b+1)(c+1), k5=-Aa*Bb, k6=-a*Bb,
 k8=-(a-b)(c+1), k10=-b*Aa, k11=-delta,
 k12=(a+1)(c+1), k13=abc.
Since k11!=0, delete coordinate 11 for a global quotient chart. Put
 V=abc+2ab+ac+a+bc+b, T=ab(a+1)(b+1)(c+1), h0=T/V.
The 13-by-13 principal determinant of H_F(g)-hH_F(E) is
 C13(h)=2h^2(a+1)^2(b+1)^2(c+1)^2(T-hV)/delta.
It vanishes at h0=F/Q, with derivative
 C13'(h0)=-2a^2b^2(a+1)^4(b+1)^4(c+1)^4/(delta V)<0.
The cofactor is obtained as in eight0710_equal_closure.txt: differentiate
the verified full pencil in the e coefficient, and use the generic
rank-13 adjugate rho*k*k^T. The checked identity
 k^T H_F(r22) k=2(c+1)delta^2>0 makes this calculation valid; rational
identities extend to the entire stated domain. Hence the quotient Hessian
has rank 12 at h0, so the full Hessian has a two-dimensional kernel, and
Q restricted to it has rank exactly one with k in its radical.

The branch {a,b>0, c>=0, ab>c+1} is path connected: increase a and b to
make their product larger than all intermediate values of c+1, vary c,
then adjust a and b. At the exact anchor (a,b,c,e)=(1,2,0,6), the Hessian
has rank 12 and Q restricted to its kernel is diag(0,343/144). The nonzero
rank-one eigenvalue is therefore positive everywhere by continuity. But an
actual lift requires Q(y)=Q(x)=Q(g)<0, a contradiction.

The generic full pencil identity is verified by
code/research/verify_eight_contact_cap_0710_pencil.py. The common kernel,
quotient-pencil factors and derivative, and exact PSD anchor are checked by
code/research/verify_eight_contact_d0_0710.py. This closes the entire d=0
boundary of this cap; the interior unequal-weight branch d>0 remains open.
