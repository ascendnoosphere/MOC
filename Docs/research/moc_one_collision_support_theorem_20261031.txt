One shared opposite eigenvalue gives an ordinary support cost
31 October 2026

THEOREM. Let g be any nonnegative matching function in V14 which is
constant on the six-permutation coset E={pi:pi(i)=j}. Then g is ordinary
and matching-SOS. Consequently MOC4 holds for every actual support whose
two spectra have at least one crosscollision a_i+b_j=0, with arbitrary
other eigenvalues and arbitrary supporting phase.

The full constant-coset space has dimension10. Its zero-coset subspace
has dimension9. These must not be confused.

1. Short reduction to the established zero-six cone

Relabel i=j=0, and let h>=0 be the common value of g on E. The ordinary
indicator r00=1_{pi(0)=0} is1 exactly on E and0 elsewhere. Thus

                           u=g-h r00

is a nonnegative matching function vanishing on E. This subtracts h
times an ordinary INDICATOR, not the constant matching function e.
The established zero-six cone has fifteen ordinary rays, so u is
ordinary. Adding h r00 proves the theorem. The constant-coset cone
is the direct sum of that9-dimensional zero cone and the ray r00.

For an actual crosscollision, every product prod_k(a_k+b_pi(k)) is0
on E. Its support cost therefore has the same nonnegative value h
there and falls under the theorem. A shared opposite value can also
be shifted to zero in both spectra without changing any a_k+b_l.

2. Independent complete10-dimensional cone audit

The new verifier does not need the complete82-ray census to establish
this particular cone. It constructs these16 ordinary generators directly:

 - the four indicators pi(0)=j, j=0,1,2,3;
 - the three indicators pi(i)=0, i=1,2,3;
 - the nine indicators pi({0,i})=J, with i=1,2,3 and
   J a two-subset of{1,2,3}.

They have rank10 and span the entire constant-coset subspace. The last
fact follows because the matching space has dimension14 while its
five coset-value differences have rank4. The other fifteen generators
vanish on E and have rank9.

The checker enumerates ALL C(16,9)=11440 possible facet supports.
Every selected9-by10 generator matrix has entries0 or1. All9-by9 minors
are bounded by3^9=19683 by Hadamard's inequality. Arithmetic modulo
1000003 therefore detects its exact rational rank, and balanced modular
cofactor reconstruction uniquely recovers each integer facet normal.
The code also verifies the resulting kernel identity over the integers.

There are exactly19 distinct facets, and they are EXACTLY the19 distinct
pointwise nonnegativity inequalities on the24 permutations (the six
coset inequalities coincide). Thus the cone generated by those16
ordinary indicators equals the full nonnegative constant-coset cone.
No floating facet test or randomized completeness assertion is used.

3. The singular-pencil source mechanism

For the universal complex source matrix M(z), a crosscollision implies

                  M(z) vec(l_i l_j^T)=0,                    (1)

where l_i is the corresponding tetrahedral row vector. The checker
verifies ALL16 identities coefficientwise in the eight arbitrary
complex spectra by substituting b_j=-a_i. The kernel vector is real,
so it is also in ker Re(eta M(z)) for every phase.

Two independent collision kernel vectors therefore force actual pencil
rank<=7; four diagonal collisions b=-a force rank<=5. A SINGLE collision
can instead have pencil rank8. In that case its kernel is a base point
of the quadratic map psi, and the restricted nonic gradient is0. Thus
rank<=7 alone does not exhaust the actual singular-nonic boundary.
The ordinary theorem above closes this entire one-collision family,
including the rank8 singular cases.

The verifier includes an exact rank8 single-collision example and checks
its base-kernel quadratic coordinates. This is a boundary closure, not
a general even-rank assertion.
