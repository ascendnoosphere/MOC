Scope

These are discovery tools for the full canonical13-ordinary-coefficient
cap. The moment-line criterion itself is proved by the separate exact
Hodge verifier. Floating classifications and optimizer outputs are not
proofs of either a counterexample or global exclusion.

Given the14 selected matching costs g, set h=F(g)/Q(g), x=g-h1. On the
generic secant, Hx has a two-dimensional real kernel K. In orthonormal
kernel coordinates, the quadratic-gradient lift equation is

 L(M)=gradF(x),
 L=[gradF(k0), H_F(k0)k1/sqrt2, gradF(k1)],
 M coordinates=(M00,sqrt2 M01,M11).

Its image has rank2, so all solutions are M0+sW. The code uses an SVD
minimum-norm particular, hence M0:W=0, and normalizes ||W||F=1. Its
definiteness test agrees with four independently exact anchors:
the original actual nonordinary seed, another actual mixed-sign seed,
the neutral orbit13 past point, and the uniform orbit7 past point.

When detW>0, some rank-one endpoint is PSD. When detW<0, the determinant
parabola has a finite maximum; real PSD feasibility requires nonnegative
discriminant and positive midpoint trace. Under our orthogonality convention,

 tr(midpoint)=-tr(M0)/(2detW).

Thus the particular trace has the same sign as the midpoint trace in
this chamber. This shortcut is NOT valid for an arbitrary particular.
The canonical positive dual metric is C=I-tr(W)W, with C:W=0 and
C:M0=tr(M0). The code reports both traces and their identity residual.

Q, small Hessian spectral gaps, moment-rank collapse, target residuals,
and near-zero discriminants are guarded. Such cases are marked ambiguous,
not treated as proved nonactual. For a future point the code factors a
PSD rank-one endpoint and reconstructs y in kerH, then checks

 gradF(x+i y)=0.

An EXACT nonzero critical point has an eight-point realization by the
audited HMSV gradient-ideal/GIT identification. Numerical output still
needs exact certification. Direct finite replay can bypass this theorem
in a final concrete artifact.

Direct finite reconstruction

 code/research/moc_critical_spectral_reconstruct.py
 reconstruct(model,critical_selected)

All105 matching products are evaluated from the14 selected coordinates.
Normalize three labeled projective points to infinity,0,1. For each
remaining k, choose a matching R of the other four labels and recover

 z_k=M_[(02)(1k)R]/M_[(0k)(12)R].

The three choices of R are used for conditioning. A common Mobius map
avoids infinity for every point. Its first four points are finite row
spectra a; the negatives of the last four are b. One common complex
scalar eta restores all matching values. The code verifies all105
matching products and all selected spectral products. Two independent
anchors reproduce their gaps with relative errors below4e-14.

One can then round the finite spectra and support direction to Gaussian
rationals and recompute all24 products EXACTLY. A sufficiently strict
inequality persists; the direct replay no longer assumes anything about
the original numerical nonic root or lift classifier.
