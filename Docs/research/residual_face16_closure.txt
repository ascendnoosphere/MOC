Residual MOC4 face [3,16,21,30,53]: exclusion of positive-height lifts

Model and scope. Let e be the all-ones vector in the 14-dimensional model
and Q(z)=D_e F(z). Let

g=a r3+b r16+c r21+d r30+f r53,  a,b,c,d,f >= 0.
The calculations below use the exact model and ray coordinates checked by
code/research/verify_residual_face_16.py. If f=0 then g is a nonnegative
combination of four coordinatewise nonnegative rays, hence is already
coordinatewise nonnegative. Assume f>0 and normalize f=1. At nonzero Q set
h=F(g)/Q(g) and x=g-he. An actual spectral gap gives a real y with
H_F(x)y=0 and grad F(y)=grad F(x). We prove no such y exists if h>0.
The negative-height case follows from the Drury argument. For h=0, F=0
implies b>0,c>0 and cd>a+c+1, so d-1>(a+1)/c>=1/c and Q>0. The linear
pencil factor gives P=Q L(0)=Qd(a+c+1)>0, contradicting Hessian singularity.
This also excludes F=Q=0 in the normalized face.

Put
 A=a+c+1,
 C=acd-ad-a-2cd-c-2d-1,
 N=acd-ad-a-cd^2-cd-c-d-1,
 D=a^2cd-a^2d-a^2-ac^2d^2+ac^2d+acd^2-acd-2ac-ad^2-2ad-2a
   +2c^2d^2-c^2d-c^2+cd^2-2cd-2c-d^2-d-1.
F=bc(d-1)-(a+1)(b+1)-c, Q=(b+c)(d-1)-1. Direct exact determinant
computation shows, with P=-(bD+AN),

 det(H_F(g)-h H_F(e)) at h=F/Q = -4(a+1)^2(b+1)^2 P^2/Q^2.

A lift makes this determinant zero, so bD+AN=0: when y is nonzero it is a
Hessian null vector; if y=0 then grad F(x)=0 and homogeneity gives
H_F(x)x=0. Here x cannot be zero at h>0, since g has a zero contact
coordinate while e is strictly positive; at h=0, x=g is nonzero because
Q(g)>0. Also the pencil linear
factor has coefficient C of h and constant dA. Its vanishing therefore gives
h=-dA/C. When d=0, bD+AN=-(b+1)A^2 is nonzero; hence d>0, and h>0 gives C<0.
D cannot vanish. Indeed D=0 forces N=0, and N=0 gives
 a=(cd^2+cd+c+d+1)/(cd-d-1).
The denominator is positive, since a>=0 and the numerator is positive. After
this substitution D=-c d^2(cd-c-d-1)^2/(cd-d-1). Here c>0 (otherwise the
formula gives a<0), so D=0 implies cd-c-d-1=0. But C=N+d(cd-c-1)=d^2>0,
a contradiction. Consequently b=-AN/D and h=-dA/C throughout the branch.

A single quadratic separator. Define W(z)=partial_0 F(z)+partial_12 F(z),
where coordinates are in the selected-permutation model. The exact verifier
constructs two vectors k,v, with entries regular on the whole branch (their
only denominators are A and D), such that
 H_F(x)k=H_F(x)v=0,  W(k)=0,  B_W(k,v)=0,
 W(x)=-A^2(C-d^2)^2/(C D),
 W(v)/W(x)=C^2 S,
 S=-d^2(ac^2+a-c^2+1)^2+4cA^2[d(ac-a-c-1)-A].
Here B_W is the polar bilinear form. W(x) is nonzero since C<0,D!=0.
We show S<0. Set T=a(c-1)-2(c+1), U=a(c-1)-(c+1),
K=ac^2+a-c^2+1. Thus C=dT-A and S=-d^2 K^2+4c A^2(dU-A).
If c=0 or U<=0, S<0 directly. Otherwise c>1 and
X=a(c-1)/(c+1)>1. The discriminant of S as a quadratic in d is
 16c(a+1)A^3(ac-c-1)[a(c-1)^2-3c^2-2c+1].
The first three factors and ac-c-1 are strictly positive. If the final
bracket is negative, S is strictly negative for every d. If it is nonnegative,
write X=3+r, c=1+2/r+t with r>0,t>=0; then T>0. The verifier proves
2c A U T-K^2>0 by multiplying it by the positive factor
(c-1)^2 r^4/(c+1)^2; the resulting polynomial has 23 strictly positive
coefficients, and its t=0 restriction is 4r(r+1)^2(r+2)^3>0.
Thus the vertex of S(d) lies strictly beyond d=A/T. For the
admissible interval d<A/T, S is strictly increasing and
 S(A/T)=-A^2(ac^2-2ac-a-3c^2-4c-1)^2/T^2 <= 0.
Hence S<0 on the whole positive-height branch, including its boundary c=0.

Rank and exclusion. The two kernel vectors are independent: k_11=1,
k_13=0, W(k)=0, and W(v)=C^2 S W(x) is nonzero. Thus rank H_F(x)<=12.
The verifier calculates the principal 12-minor deleting coordinates 11,13.
It equals rho*(k_11 v_13-k_13 v_11)^2, where
 rho=-d^2(a+1)(b+1)A^2/(C^4 D),
which is nonzero. On the nonempty generic set R=v_13!=0, this minor is
nonzero, so H_F(x) has rank 12 and k,v span the kernel. By the elementary
block-factorization identity for a symmetric corank-two matrix, all principal
12-minors are rho times the corresponding squared Pluecker coordinates of
k wedge v. These are rational identities and extend to the full branch because
A,C,D are nonzero there. At least one Pluecker coordinate is nonzero, so some
principal 12-minor is nonzero and rank H_F(x)=12 everywhere. Consequently
k,v span the real kernel. For y=u k+v0 v in that kernel,
 W(y)=v0^2 W(v), which has sign opposite W(x), unless it is zero. Thus
W(y)=W(x) is impossible, contradicting grad F(y)=grad F(x).

The determinant, kernel, separator, cofactor, denominator and sign identities
are checked independently from exact ray and model data by
code/research/verify_residual_face_16.py. In particular no floating-point
sampling or serialized intermediate matrix is used by that verifier.
