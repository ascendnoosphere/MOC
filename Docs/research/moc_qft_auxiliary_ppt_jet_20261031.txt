A finite PPT cone from the auxiliary-crossratio Clifford kernel
31 October 2026

This is a universal positivity mechanism in dimension four, conditional
only on the independently proved twisted-Clifford identity. It does not
establish that every actual MOC support cost belongs to the cone below.
It strictly avoids assuming a positive cross-parameter kernel. A
single-fixed-b SOS approach has already been refuted by the exact
nonordinary spectral obstruction in moc_multiaffine_sos_obstruction.py.

1. Auxiliary parameter and finite jets

Choose auxiliary numbers beta(w)=(0,1,i,w), independent of the actual a,b.
For every permutation pi define the sixteen-vector
 f_pi(w)_I=product_{i in I} beta(w)_{pi(i)}, I subset{0,1,2,3}.
Since every pi uses the fourth beta value at most once in any subset,
 f_pi(w)=f_pi,0+w f_pi,1.
Let the 32-vector j_pi stack f_pi,0 and f_pi,1. For a real orthogonal O,
let c_pi be its signed Leibniz amplitudes. Define the joint jet matrix
 H_O=sum_pi c_pi conjugate(j_pi) j_pi^T.
This Hermitian matrix need not be PSD. No claim of that form is used.

2. Exact decomposability

Set B(w)=O diag(0,1,i,w) O^T. This is an affine rank-one update of B(0).
Consequently every square minor of B(w) is affine in w, by determinant
multilinearity: selecting the rank-one update in two columns gives zero.

The twisted-Clifford Gram factor has eight columns. Its scalar and six
bivector columns are minors of B(w), hence have the form
 V_h(w)=V_h,0+w V_h,1.
Its pseudoscalar column is the conjugate of a complementary two-minor,
and thus has the form
 V_a(w)=V_a,0+conjugate(w) V_a,1.

Stack the holomorphic coefficient matrices into L=(V_h,0; V_h,1), and
the antiholomorphic coefficient vectors into r=(V_a,0; V_a,1). Put
 P=conjugate(L) L^T >=0, Q=conjugate(r) r^T >=0.
The latter has rank at most one; P has rank at most seven. Comparing
coefficients of 1,w,conjugate(w),|w|^2 in the universal kernel identity
gives the exact finite-dimensional identity
 H_O=P+Q^{T_parameter}.                                      (J)
The partial transpose transposes the two-by-two block parameter index,
leaving each 16-by-16 feature block unchanged. This operation exchanges
the off-diagonal blocks. It is the antiholomorphic pseudoscalar channel,
not a positive joint kernel, that accounts for the second term in (J).

3. A new finite semidefinite sufficient cone

Let T be a 32-by-32 Hermitian matrix satisfying
 T>=0 and T^{T_parameter}>=0.                               (PPT)
Define a real permutation cost
 g_pi=j_pi^T T conjugate(j_pi).
Then for every real O4,
 sum_pi c_pi g_pi=tr(H_O T)
                 =tr(P T)+tr(Q T^{T_parameter})>=0.          (K)
Both traces are nonnegative because their two factors are PSD. This is
an elementary finite-matrix proof; no separability theorem is invoked.
The transpose/conjugate convention can equivalently be changed by
replacing T with conjugate(T), preserving both PSD constraints.

Any actual supported spectral gap g lying in this cone is nonnegative
on every real orthogonal mixer. Its common-minor expansion and the
proved SO4-to-U4 profile convexification then give the full all-unitary
support inequality for those actual spectra.

The cone contains every finite sum of auxiliary-w SOS costs. Indeed
such a term has T=vv^* tensor S for a two-vector v representing w and
a PSD feature matrix S, up to the displayed conjugation convention.
These matrices and their partial transposes are PSD. Their sums remain
PPT. The PPT cone can be larger than the separable cone; whether this
enlargement covers an additional actual spectral family is a separate
question requiring a certificate, not a consequence of the construction.

There are only 24 cost equalities and two finite PSD constraints. At a
permutation contact with g_pi=0, PSD implies T conjugate(j_pi)=0;
this gives an exact linear face reduction before numerical discovery.
Normalization of the auxiliary triple covers the other generic triples
because common Mobius changes preserve the squarefree feature space.
This does not justify throwing away singular feature-rank boundaries in
a proof of universal actual-cost containment.

and arbitrary independent complex beta and conjugate-beta variables.
The universal proof over symmetric commuting B,barB is supplied by the

moc_qft_ppt_jet_exact.py independently forms all1024 entries of H_O and
verifies (J) for Q59 and R3701, using rational complex arithmetic and
explicit seven-column and one-column PSD factorizations. These example
checks audit the jet bookkeeping. Universal validity of (J) follows
from the universal twisted-Clifford identity and the rank-one affine
update argument in Section2, not from the number of checked examples.

The remaining full-closure obligation is unchanged: prove that every
actual spectral support cost in unrestricted MOC4 has a certificate in
this or another established universal positivity cone, or find a different
complete argument. No finite search or tested subset proves that claim.

5. Exact reduction to a 14-dimensional PSD matrix and a 4-dimensional
   partial-transpose corner

There is a substantial simplification of (PPT). Define the real
two-dimensional feature subspace E_22, supported only on size-two subsets,
by the four equations
 sum_{I: |I|=2, i in I} v_I=0, i=0,1,2,3.
The four-by-six incidence matrix has rank four. For example, an orthogonal
basis before normalization is
 e1: v01=v23=1, v02=v13=-1, v03=v12=0;
 e2: v01=v23=v02=v13=1, v03=v12=-2.
Their squared norms are 4 and12. This is the [22] representation of S4.
Let E=C^2_parameter tensor E_22, with dimension four.

For ANY symmetric matrix C, without requiring normality, the pseudoscalar
Clifford column is supported on E_22. Its entries are signed complementary
two-minors. They satisfy v_I=v_complement(I) and
 v01+v02+v03=0.
The last identity follows immediately by expanding the three minors:
 -(C02 C13-C03 C12)+(C01 C23-C03 C12)
 -(C01 C23-C02 C13)=0.
Consequently the antiholomorphic jet vector r in Section2 lies in E.
Partial transpose preserves E, since its feature basis is real and does
not depend on the parameter. Thus both Q and Q^{T_parameter} are supported
on E.

Let W=range(conjugate(J)^T), where J has rows j_pi^T. Exact elimination
gives dimW=14 and E subset W. The joint matrix H_O is supported on W by
its definition. Therefore P=H_O-Q^{T_parameter} is also supported on W.

It follows that a cost has a universal positivity certificate whenever
there is a Hermitian operator T supported on W satisfying
 T>=0, PT_parameter(E^* T E)>=0.                            (R)
Here E can be any real feature basis of the displayed four-dimensional
space, ordered as parameter0:e1,e2; parameter1:e1,e2. The second condition
is just a four-by-four PSD constraint. The proof is exactly the trace
argument (K): the first constraint pairs positively with P, and the
second with Q. No positivity of the remaining partial-transpose blocks
is required. This cone contains the image of the original32PPT cone.

Using a full-column-rank coordinate matrix N for W, write T=N S N^*.
Then (R) is the finite pair of conditions
 S>=0 (14-by-14),
 PT_parameter((E^*N) S (N^*E))>=0 (4-by-4).
This is not a block-diagonal ansatz: all cross terms among the14
coordinates remain free. The second constraint transposes only the
two-dimensional parameter factor of the four-dimensional corner.

The exact verifier moc_qft_ppt_reduced_cone.py checks the grade-four
support identity for all ten independent entries of a symmetric matrix,
and exports N,E and J to ppt_reduced_cone.json. It also supplies conjugate
N for the equivalent physical convention g_pi=j_pi^*Tj_pi,
H=sum c_pi j_pi j_pi^*. Global conjugation preserves PSD, partial
transpose, and the real subspace E, so the conventions are equivalent.

The same decomposition proves two universal spectral bounds on H_O:
 rank(H_O)<=11; number of strictly negative eigenvalues<=1.
Indeed rankP<=7. A rank-one operator on two qubits has partial-transpose
eigenvalues s1^2,s2^2,s1s2,-s1s2 in Schmidt coordinates, so Q^PT has
rank at most4 and at most one negative eigenvalue. Adding PSD P cannot
increase its negative index. This identifies a single two-qubit
entanglement channel as the obstruction to positivity of the full jet.

6. Invisible PPT regularizer and a finite-completion lemma

There is also an exact separable, cost-invisible matrix T0 with
 rankT0=18, rank(T0^PT)=19.
For w=0,1,i choose a basis f of kerF(w), whose dimension is7 at each
of these repeated-value parameters. The product vectors
 p=(1,w) tensor f
belong to kerJ. They span the whole18-dimensional kerJ. Adding the
four vectors (0,1) tensor f for f in kerF1 raises the span of their
partial-conjugated product vectors from18 to19. A generic parameter
w=2+3i changes neither rank. The sum T0=sum p p^* is PSD, PPT, and has
zero cost at every permutation. Its partial transpose is the explicit
sum of the partial-conjugated product-vector projectors.

moc_qft_ppt_invisible_regularizer.py verifies all these statements exactly
and exports factors L,Lpt and a basis K of ker(T0^PT) to
ppt_invisible_regularizer.json. The dimensions are32-by29,32-by29 and
32-by13, respectively. T0=L L^*, T0^PT=Lpt Lpt^*, and J L=0.

For any PSD T with exact desired costs, if D=K^*T^PT K is positive
definite, adding a sufficiently large positive multiple of T0 produces
a finite PPT certificate with exactly the same costs. To obtain an
explicit bound, choose a basis Y of range(T0^PT), set
 C=Y^* T0^PT Y>0,
 M=Y^*T^PT Y-Y^*T^PT K D^{-1} K^*T^PT Y.
Then tau=1+tr(C^{-1}M C^{-1}M) is a nonnegative rational sufficient
choice when all data are rational. This follows from the Schur
complement and ||C^{-1/2}M C^{-1/2}||op<=sqrt(tr(...))<=1+tr(...).

If D is only PSD, finite completion additionally requires
 kerD subset ker(Y^*T^PT K).
Compressed PSD alone describes a closure and does not by itself prove
that a finite tau exists. The reduced14+4 cone in Section5 avoids this
extension issue entirely.
