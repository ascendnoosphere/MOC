EXACT CLOSURE OF THE MOC4 FACE (3,12,21,23,30,80)
2026-10-30

For rho(I,J)(U)=|det U[I,J]|^2, use one-based indices and write

 r3=rho(1,4), r12=rho(4,1), r21=rho(12,34),
 r23=rho(13,13), r30=rho(14,14).

In the repository's canonical ray numbering,

                  f80=r3+r12-r21+r23.                         (1)

The result concerns the whole coefficient face

          g=a r3+b r12+c r21+d r23+e r30+f80,
                         a,b,c,d,e>=0.                       (2)

If its 24 permutation values form an actual spectral support gap, then
c=1. Thus, for every U in U(4),

         g(U)=(a+1)r3(U)+(b+1)r12(U)+(d+1)r23(U)+e r30(U)>=0.  (3)

This conclusion holds at every support height. It does not require distinct
spectra or matching products. A positive mixed coefficient can be normalized
to one by positive scaling; a zero mixed coefficient is already an ordinary
minor sum. In unnormalized coefficients, the r21 coefficient must equal the
mixed coefficient.

The standalone verifier code/research/verify_residual_face_80.py reconstructs
the canonical rays and the spectral cubic from repository certificates,
checks the functional identity (1), and proves the determinant identity below
by exact rational arithmetic. It reads no generated build files.

Functional identity
-------------------
The first canonical row/column relabeling of the standard mixed function that
gives f80 has alpha=(0,1,3,2), beta=(3,2,0,1), in zero-based notation. It gives

 f80=rho(1,4)+rho(3,3)+rho(34,14)-rho(14,24).

The identity equating this expression with (1) holds on all of U(4).
Cauchy--Binet gives the two incidence identities

 sum_(J:|J|=2,1 in J) rho(34,J) = rho(3,1)+rho(4,1),
 sum_(I:|I|=2,3 in I) rho(I,13) = rho(3,1)+rho(3,3).

Subtracting the second from the first and using Jacobi complementation,
rho(34,12)=rho(12,34) and rho(23,13)=rho(14,24), yields

 rho(34,14)+rho(12,34)-rho(13,13)-rho(14,24)
                           +rho(3,3)-rho(4,1)=0.

The verifier checks every label and the exact combination of these incidence
identities. The recovery of (3) therefore uses a functional minor identity,
not just agreement on permutation matrices.

Actual spectra force a singular Hessian
---------------------------------------
Use the exact cubic F and its 14 selected matching coordinates from
code/geometry/closing_moc4_model_verify.py. Let 1 denote the all-ones vector,
H_v=H_F(v), and Q(v)=D_1 F(v). The imported model proves grad F(z)=0 for
all actual complex spectral matching products, and H_1 1=0.

If g_pi=h-Re(eta z_pi), set x=g-h1 and let y be the real imaginary-part
vector of eta z in the selected coordinates. Expanding the quadratic
gradient at -x+iy gives the necessary equations

                H_x y=0,       grad F(y)=grad F(x).             (4)

Euler's identity and (4) imply

 3F(x)=x.grad F(x)=x.grad F(y)=y^T H_x y/2=0.

Since H_1 1=0, we obtain

                    F(g)=hQ(g),       Q(x)=Q(g).              (5)

On (2), direct cubic evaluation gives

              F(g)=-(a+1)(b+1)(d+1)<0,
              Q(g)=(d+1)(c-1)+(c+d)e.                        (6)

Thus Q(g)!=0, h!=0, and h=F(g)/Q(g).

The Hessian H_x must be singular. If y!=0, this follows from (4).
If y=0, then grad F(x)=0, hence H_x x=2grad F(x)=0. Here x!=0:
x=0 would imply g=h1 and F(g)=h^3F(1)=0, contradicting (6).
This covers the zero-imaginary-part case without a genericity assumption.

Exact determinant and its sign
------------------------------
Put

 R=(d+1)[(a+1)(b+1)+(a+b+c+1)e]+(c+d)e^2.

Every coefficient of R is positive and its constant term is one. Therefore
R>0 throughout the full nonnegative coefficient domain. The verifier proves

 det H_(g-F(g)/Q(g) 1)
       =-4(a+1)^4(b+1)^4(c-1)^2(d+1)^2 R^2/Q(g)^2.             (7)

Equivalently, the denominator-free polynomial identity is

 det(Q(g)H_g-F(g)H_1)
       =-4(a+1)^4(b+1)^4(c-1)^2(d+1)^2 R^2 Q(g)^12.           (8)

For reproducibility, use A=a+1, B=b+1, C=1-c, D=d+1. The verifier eliminates
ten matrix entries using exact rational functions in (A,B,C,D,e,h), then
expands the remaining 4x4 determinant. With original row/column labels the
pivots are

 (6,8),(0,13),(2,9),(3,6),(10,1),
 (9,2),(1,4),(4,3),(5,0),(12,11).

The remaining row labels are (7,8,11,13), and columns (5,7,10,12).
The resulting determinant det(H_g-hH_1) has degree three in h. Substituting
h=F/Q and clearing Q^3 proves (8) coefficientwise. The elimination proves
an identity of rational functions, hence of polynomials after denominators
are cleared. It remains valid when an intermediate pivot vanishes,
including a=b or e=0. No parameter region is discarded.

Singularity of H_x and (7) now force c=1. Identity (1) gives (3), completing
the support inequality on this coefficient face. In fact (5)--(6) then give
Q=e(d+1), so actual gaps with positive mixed coefficient have e>0 and h<0.
The proof of (3) itself needs no external-half-plane theorem.

Scope and verification
----------------------
This closes the nine-contact orbit represented by (3,12,21,23,30,80), which
has 288 faces in the existing census, together with its boundary coefficient
faces. The proof uses the real spectral lift to require a singular Hessian
and a positive polynomial R to exclude every c!=1 component. It needs no
connectedness argument and no sampled parameter sign test.
