An explicit rank-eight four-qubit state with positive partial transposes
that is not fully separable
31 October2026

Take the exact Q59 orthogonal matrix from the other research notes,
beta=(0,1,i,2+3i), and determinant amplitudes
c_pi=sgn(pi) product_i Q_i,pi(i). Set
 v_pi=tensor_i (1,beta_pi(i)),
 rho=(1/56)sum_pi c_pi v_pi v_pi^*.
The normalization56 is product_j(1+|beta_j|^2), independent of pi.

The exact audit moc_qft_partial_transpose_rank8.py proves that rho and
all seven inequivalent partial transposes are PSD of rank8. For each
matrix it exports an invertible8x8 principal core and its eight positive
rational leading principal minors. The full matrix has rank8 by exact
Gaussian-rational elimination; therefore the positive core and rank
give positivity of the full matrix by the Schur complement.

The state is not fully separable. Let S denote the fully symmetric
four-qubit projector. Since S v_pi is independent of pi and sum c=1,
S rho S is rank one. In a decomposition into pure product states,
every symmetrized product must lie on this same nonzero line: positive
rank-one sums cannot cancel orthogonal components.

Identify symmetric four-qubit vectors with homogeneous binary quartics.
Symmetrizing a nonzero pure product corresponds to multiplying its four
nonzero linear factors, which cannot vanish identically. Unique
factorization, and the distinctness of the four beta spinors, imply
that every pure product in such a decomposition is proportional to one
of the24 vectors v_pi. The24 projectors v_pi v_pi^* are linearly
independent, as verified by exact rank24 of their256x24 vectorization
matrix. The unique coefficients are thus the displayed c_pi/56.
However c_(0,2,1,3)=-170000/12117361 is negative. This excludes full
separability.

This proves an explicit multipartite PPT-entangled state. It does not
establish genuine four-partite entanglement, and does not assert that
an arbitrary family member is fully PPT without a universal theorem.
The low-rank PPT examples may be useful in studying rigidity of the
actual determinant-amplitude moment variety.

Additional local-rigidity calculation: at Q59 and R3701, one original
rank8 moment plus one single-qubit-partially-transposed rank8 moment
has17 independent real linear tangent constraints. Its7-dimensional
kernel exactly equals the actual scaledSO4 amplitude tangent.
Requiring ordinary moments at four auxiliary crossratios alone leaves
an8-dimensional tangent, so partial transpose supplies a genuinely new
local condition. A global classification and universal fullPPT theorem
remain separate obligations.
