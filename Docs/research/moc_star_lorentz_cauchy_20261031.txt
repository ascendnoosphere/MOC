1. Relation to the actual-source Hessian

In the4-by-4 positive Schur matrix S4, pivot along the constant complement
direction(1,1,1,1). The pivot is2Q^2/F>0. Keep the remaining complement
columns8,9,11, giving S3. The exact polynomial identity is

 2P*F*S3 = -adjM.

The minus sign matters: the actual-source inertia proof gives S4>=0,
hence adjM<=0. At a generic actual star point, M has rank2 and inertia(1,1,0),
not positive semidefinite. The exact actual control has a strictly negative
nonzero eigenvalue. It cannot be treated as a unit-circle/SU2 Gram matrix.

3. Entrywise reciprocal is a positive rank3 kernel

Let R_ij=1/Mij. Since adjM<=0,

 Delta_ij=Mij^2-Mii*Mjj >=0.

These imply all three2-by-2 principal minors of R are nonnegative. The
Fricke equation detM=0 gives the exact factorization

 detR=Delta01*Delta02*Delta12 /
      (M00*M11*M22*M01^2*M02^2*M12^2) >=0.

Along with the positive diagonal, this proves R>=0. Generic distinct
rapidities make all three Delta positive and R strictly positive definite.
The exact control admits a rational positive LDL decomposition, saved in
lorentz_cauchy.json. The nonlinear kernel has rank3; it is not confined to
the failed Schur image/kernel feature planes.

After normalizing the diagonal, Kij=sqrt(Mii*Mjj)/Mij equals

 sech(theta_i-theta_j)=2sqrt(x_i*x_j)/(x_i+x_j), x_i=exp(2theta_i)>0.

This is a Cauchy kernel: an integral Gram is

 Kij=integral_0^infty 2sqrt(x_i*x_j) exp(-(x_i+x_j)v) dv.

The elementary determinant proof above avoids needing an integral or a
physics analogy. It also exposes the exact algebra needed by a Lean client.

4. All-N9 versus actual scope

The exact determinant relation holds for the full face, not just actual
costs. A positive-entry rank2 symmetric M is either PSD itself or indefinite
with nonpositive adjugate; in the latter case its reciprocal is PSD. Thus
there is a two-kernel dichotomy on generic nonordinary N9 points. Only the
indefinite/reciprocal branch is forced by the actual-source Schur proof.
This does not identify either kernel with a full matching-SOS Gram.
