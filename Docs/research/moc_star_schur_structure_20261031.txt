STAR FOUR-CONTACT FACE: EXACT CUBIC AND INTRINSIC PSD SCHUR MATRIX
31 October 2026

Contacts are zero-based lexicographic permutations C=(0,1,2,14): identity,
(23),(12),(02). The central index is2. This note constructs an intrinsic
source-dependent positive matrix. It does NOT yet translate it into the
full matching-SOS Gram required for unrestricted MOC.

1. Full face coordinates

The pointwise nonnegative face has15 ordinary extreme rays

 (1,3,4,7,12,13,18,20,21,25,26,27,28,31,32)

and two mixed rays40,81. Their span has dimension10. The exact identity

 r40+r81=r18+r20+r25+r27+r28+r32

allows eliminating a common positive coefficient on the two mixed rays.
Conjugation by the leaf transposition(01) exchanges r40,r81.

Use the basis (40,1,3,4,7,12,13,18,20,26) with coefficients

 (u,a,d-t,e-u,c-r+u,b,f-u,r-u,t-u,s).

These are algebraic coordinates, not an assertion that all ten listed
coefficients are nonnegative. Retain ALL ordinary rays/pointwise inequalities.
The24 gap values are recorded in structure.json. In particular

 a=g8,b=g15,c=g4,d=g20,e=g12,f=g3 >=0.

In this full ten-coordinate face the verified HMSV cubic and its derivative
along the constant vector are especially simple:

 F=abc+def,  Q=ut-rs.

For Q!=0 put h=F/Q and x=g-h e. Then F(x)=0 exactly.

2. A four-dimensional critical complement

Let B be the24-by-10 cost basis above, ordered by
w=(a,b,c,d,e,f,u,r,s,t). Use the four central-row singleton indicators
N=(r8,r9,r10,r11) as a complement. They partition the constant function.
They span a linear criticalP3 of M8, so the full Hessian restricted to
their span vanishes identically. In coordinates U=[B,N],

 U^T H_x U = [[A,L],[L^T,0]],
 A=Hess_w(abc+def-h(ut-rs)),
 L=Jacobian_w(Q0,Q1,Q2,Q3)^T.

The four quadrics, in complement order8,9,10,11, satisfy sum Qj=ut-rs.
Three sparse formulas are

 Q0=c(f-a-s)-f(d+r+s-t-u),
 Q1=b(d-c+r-t)+d(u-e),
 Q3=a(e+r-b-u)+e(t-f),
 Q2=ut-rs-Q0-Q1-Q3.

All block identities were checked exactly in the full ten-coordinate model.

3. Actual-source inertia supplies positivity

Assume a,b,c,d,e,f>0,h!=0 and a generic actual conjugate critical source.
Then A is invertible and splits as

 H_abc directsum H_def directsum (-h H_(ut-rs)).

The first two3-by-3 blocks each have inertia(1+,2-); the last block has
inertia(2+,2-). Therefore A has inertia(4+,6-).

Here is the intrinsic actual-source argument for the full Hessian inertia.
At a smooth critical M8 cone point p, ker H_p is its six-dimensional complex
tangent space. For v,w in ker H_p,

 w* H_Re(p) v =0,

because H_p v=0 and conjugating H_p w=0 gives w* H_barp=0.
Thus this six-dimensional space is Hermitian-isotropic. For a generic
conjugate pair, Terracini gives ker H_p intersect ker H_barp=0, so its
projection to the nondegenerate quotient of H_Re(p) is still six-dimensional.
At a rank12 secant point this forces inertia(6+,6-,2zero). A sign or complex
scalar in the actual source does not alter this argument.

Sylvester inertia additivity for the block Schur complement now gives

 S4=-L^T A^(-1)L >=0, rank S4=2.

This is a uniform intrinsic NECESSARY positivity statement for generic actual
star costs in this positive-gap chart. It is not a guessed affine section.
Degenerate a..f or source pairs require limits/separate charts.

The inverse of each product block is explicit:

 H_abc^(-1)=[vv^T-2diag(a^2,b^2,c^2)]/(2abc), v=(a,b,c),

and similarly for d,e,f. The determinant2 block inverse is -H_(ut-rs)/h.
Thus S4 is a fully explicit rational4-by-4 matrix.

4. Restricted nonic and exact control

The exact14-by-14 pencil determinant was compiled by nine sparse eliminations
to a5-by-5 residual. It has8068 terms in the u=1 chart and was computed in
about40 seconds. Its actual-height square root is a single irreducible
degree9 polynomial; the complete expression and pivot metadata are saved.
The verified identity is

 Q^3 det(H_g-(F/Q)H_e)=-4 Q N9^2.

Homogenization in u recovers the full face identity; choosing u=1 does not
justify dropping any positivity branch.

At the independently exact actual, nonordinary star counterexample, S4 has
rank2 and all principal minors of orders1,2 are nonnegative exactly. A
positive principal2-by-2 pivot gives a rational factor S4=C M C^T with M>0.
The actual imaginary source coordinates satisfy

 Y_face=-A^(-1)L Y_complement, S4 Y_complement=0.

All these facts are checked by moc_star_schur_exact_control.py. The example
also has an independent full matching-SOS Gram; it refutes ordinary-cone
membership only.

The complementary polynomial

 T4=S4^2-tr(S4)S4+sigma2(S4) I

is PSD rank2 and supported on ker S4. It may be useful when translating
source kernels rather than source-normal images into matching squares.

5. What has NOT worked

The simple positive pullbacks

 (A^(-1)L) S4 (A^(-1)L)^T and L S4 L^T

have substantial nonmatching Fourier components on the actual control
(relative residual about.38). They are NOT matching-SOS certificates.
No universal rank-two SOS claim is made: a separate exact past-N9 example
has a unique rank3 Gram, and its nearby four-contact N9 costs preclude an
all-N9 two-square theorem. Source-dependent ordinary/ordinary cross terms
and the full ten nonmatching cancellations must remain available.
