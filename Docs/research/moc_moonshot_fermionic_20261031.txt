Fermionic permutation weights: exact obstructions and a constructive local theorem
2026-10-31

Unrestricted MOC remains open. This pass gives an explicit spectral certificate,
a local theorem with constructive weights, and an exact obstruction to freezing
the two-dimensional Schur block. The most promising unproved statement left by
the computations is that positive real determinant amplitudes suffice as a
support set. Numerical success does not establish that statement.

Conventions

Let U be unitary, a,b be complex spectra, and
  z_pi = product_i (a_i+b_pi(i)),
  d = det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*).
Permutation indices below are zero-based lexicographic indices in S4. All
convex combinations use real, nonnegative weights.

1. Real signed amplitudes work for every unitary

Define
  gamma_pi = sgn(pi) product_i U_i,pi(i) / det(U),
  c_pi = Re(gamma_pi).
Then, in every dimension,
  sum_pi c_pi = 1,
  sum_{pi:pi(I)=J} c_pi = |det U[I,J]|^2,
  d = sum_pi c_pi z_pi.                                      (1)

Proof. The corresponding sum of the complex gamma coefficients is
  (-1)^(sum I+sum J) det U[I,J] det U[Ic,Jc] / det U.
Jacobi's complementary-minor identity for a unitary makes this exactly the
squared minor. Thus the imaginary amplitudes annihilate every subset-matching
indicator. Expanding the products z_pi in those indicators proves (1).
Equivalently, det(diag(a)U+U diag(b))/det(U) gives the complex expansion, and
its imaginary coefficient vector belongs to the common polynomial kernel.

The amplitudes can be negative. Nevertheless, (1) supplies a canonical real
signed measure whose correction may depend on a,b. This is more precise than
an appeal to fermionic positivity: the initial measure is generally signed.

The exact verifier checks all 70 subset-minor identities for Q below, Walsh4,
and Fourier4, including annihilation by the imaginary amplitudes. Fourier4 has
16 positive real amplitudes equal to 1/16 and eight zero real amplitudes.
Eight complex amplitudes have nonzero imaginary parts.

2. A closed spectral formula and its actual scope

Set c_pi^+=max(c_pi,0), S=sum c_pi^+, and q_pi=c_pi^+/S. Regard each z_pi as
a vector x_pi in R^2. Set
  mu = sum q_pi x_pi,
  Sigma = sum q_pi (x_pi-mu)(x_pi-mu)^T,
  delta = (Re d,Im d)-mu.
When Sigma is invertible, define
  w_pi = q_pi [1+(x_pi-mu)^T Sigma^(-1) delta].                (2)
Direct multiplication gives sum w_pi=1 and sum w_pi z_pi=d. Consequently,
checking the displayed factors are nonnegative is a complete MOC certificate.
This uses a 2 by 2 inverse after evaluating the permutation products; it does
not solve a convex-hull or linear-programming problem.

Let N=sum max(-c_pi,0). If N>0, put
  mu_minus = sum max(-c_pi,0) x_pi / N.
Then S=1+N and delta=N(mu-mu_minus). A sufficient scalar bound is
  N R sqrt((mu-mu_minus)^T Sigma^(-1)(mu-mu_minus)) <= 1,      (3)
where R=max_{q_pi>0} sqrt((x_pi-mu)^T Sigma^(-1)(x_pi-mu)).
This follows from Cauchy-Schwarz in the Sigma metric. N=0 gives the positive
law c itself without this bound. Formula (2) is an affine moment correction;
the generic linear-algebra device also appears in moc_extensive_theory.txt.
The specific choice of q from real determinant amplitudes makes its error
controlled by the negative amplitude mass and yields the following theorem.

Constructive local theorem. Fix arbitrary complex spectra in dimension n and
a skew-Hermitian K. Let U(t)=exp(tK), and put
  v_ij = (Re(z_(ij)-z_id), Im(z_(ij)-z_id)),
  C = sum_{i<j} |K_ij|^2 v_ij v_ij^T.
If C is positive definite, there exists epsilon>0 such that (2) gives
nonnegative weights for 0<|t|<epsilon. At t=0 the identity permutation gives
the determinant. Hence MOC holds on this segment of the specified path.

Proof. Expansion at the identity gives
  c_id(t) = 1-t^2 sum_{i<j}|K_ij|^2 + O(|t|^3),
  c_(ij)(t) = t^2 |K_ij|^2 + O(|t|^3).
Every other permutation moves at least three indices and has amplitude
O(|t|^3). Transpositions with K_ij=0 can be negative at higher order, which
does not affect the estimate N(t)=O(|t|^3). The finite set of product points
is fixed, so delta=O(|t|^3). Taking positive parts in the preceding expansions
gives Sigma=t^2 C+O(|t|^3). Positive definiteness yields
Sigma^(-1)=O(t^(-2)). Every correction factor in (2) is therefore
1+O(|t|), uniformly over the finite permutation set. They are positive for
sufficiently small nonzero t. This proves the theorem.

This is a result along directions satisfying C>0. It does not give a uniform
neighborhood over directions approaching singular C. The theorem is stated
in all n because the same expansion proves it; no unrestricted all-n claim
is inferred. No novelty claim is made for generic local validity near a
commuting pair. The explicit positive-part weights are the useful output.

4. Universal compound coupling and nested flags fail

Write rho(I,J)=|det U[I,J]|^2, now with one-based subset labels. The expression
  f34 = rho(3,3)+rho(2,4)+rho(12,13)-rho(13,23)
is nonnegative at every permutation, while
  f34(Q)=-55/3481.
Thus no permutation law reproduces all compound marginals of this Q.
This is an exact instance of the already-known stronger obstruction, not a
counterexample to MOC.

Nested projection-DPP laws cannot evade the singleton obstruction. If
I_k={i_1,...,i_k} and a permutation law satisfies
  P[pi(I_k)=J]=rho(I_k,J)
for every prefix and J, then
  P[pi(i_k)=j]
    =P[j in pi(I_k)]-P[j in pi(I_(k-1))]
    =sum_{i in I_k}|U_ij|^2-sum_{i in I_(k-1)}|U_ij|^2
    =|U_i_k,j|^2.
This holds for every order, so mixing different flags also preserves all
singleton marginals. The exact existing singleton obstruction with
  a=(0,1,-2+2i,2-2i), b=(2+i,-2-2i,1+i,2)
excludes determinant reproduction by every such flag law: its separating
gap is 6392/3481. The present verifier rechecks that certificate and every
inclusion-marginal identity for Q.

Lyons' stochastic-domination theorem gives nested couplings of projection
DPPs for nested subspaces. Its hypotheses are satisfied by these row flags.
The failure is the additional requirement to reproduce this complex
determinant. Primary source: Russell Lyons, Determinantal probability measures,
arXiv:math/0204325v4, Theorem 6.2 and Section 10,
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0204325v4
Full text inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/math/0204325v4

Walsh4 and Fourier4 do admit common laws: uniform weights 1/8 on indices
  Walsh: 1,5,6,9,14,17,18,22;
  Fourier: 2,4,6,11,13,15,20,22.
The verifier checks all 70 coordinates. These are benchmark certificates;
no new Hadamard-family theorem is claimed.

5. New exact obstruction to preserving only the small Schur block

Let I_0=12,I_1=13,I_2=14, representing the three partitions of four indices
into two unordered pairs. Set
  S_rs(U)=rho(I_r,I_s)+rho(I_r,I_s^c).
This is a 3 by 3 doubly stochastic matrix. A permutation induces the usual
permutation matrix of its action on the three partitions. Preserving S fixes
the two-dimensional Schur block and leaves the standard block free.

For the Q above,
  S(Q)=(1/3481) [2465  116  900
                  116 2385  980
                  900  980 1601].
Take
  a=(0,1,i,-7/10+i/4),
  b=(1/20-i,-1/20+9i/5,-1/20,7/10-3i/10),
  eta=1+11i.
Define the rational matrix
  Lambda=[ -34523/40000       1433/8000       -868223/40000
            3473367/160000    219847/10000     0
            0                0                0 ].
For every one of the 24 permutations,
  Re(eta z_pi) <= sum_rs Lambda_rs S_rs(P_pi).                (4)
Every slack in (4) is rational and nonnegative. But
  Re(eta d)-sum_rs Lambda_rs S_rs(Q)
    =107258179/556960000 > 0.                                (5)
Thus no positive permutation weights can preserve S(Q) and reproduce d,
even if the weights depend arbitrarily on these spectra.

For comparison, MOC itself holds by the exact positive law supported on
indices 7,8,18, with weights
  15936663345562/34637710428725,
  536875203309/1385508417149,
  5279167000438/34637710428725.
The determinant is
  -86255171/139240000 - 105125017 i/111392000.
Formula (2) also supplies a positive certificate. The complete certificate
is quotient_exact.json; --quotient-exact reconstructs it without a stored
numerical search. The LP proposes a support and dual basis, after which all
inequalities, weights and identities are verified in rational arithmetic.

This obstruction complements the existing singleton-preserving obstruction.
A universal spectral transport proof cannot freeze either nontrivial Schur
block separately. Both blocks must be permitted to move, while retaining
the one complex observable d.

6. Numerical tests and surviving questions

The source was build/moc_extensive_20261031/all_moc_extensive_data.jsonl.
The n4 complete-product noncontrol subset has 4167 records. We excluded 72
whose planar covariance axis ratio was below 1e-8, leaving 4095. These are
numerical tests of the same records, not independent draws or a proof.

Positive-amplitude support: all 4095 targets were in the numerical hull of
{z_pi:c_pi>0}, after covariance whitening at tolerance 2e-7. Support sizes
ranged from 14 to 22. The nonnegative-factor test for formula (2) passed
4081/4095 at weight tolerance 1e-9. There are five failures below -1e-6,
including generic Haar examples; thus (2) is not globally positive.
The initial unwhitened covariance computation gave 4079 passes; only the
whitened final count is used here. No exact counterexample to the support
restriction was found. The formula's failure does not refute that restriction.

An exact small failure of formula (2) occurs at Walsh4 with
  a=(-2,-1+3i,-1+3i,-2), b=(-1,2-i,3+i,-2i).
Its smallest weight is -2529/589540. The verifier checks the rational value;
the universal eight-permutation Walsh law still proves MOC for these spectra.

A separate fresh test of 10000 real orthogonal/Gaussian-spectrum draws also
found no positive-support failure. Differential evolution at Q, Walsh and
eight fresh real orthogonals, using 1000 generations each, found none.
The two largest optimized normalized gaps were still negative, roughly
-2.05e-8 and -1.65e-7. The sample and optimization cannot certify a universal
inequality. Exact raw seeds/settings for the optimization are in
moc_moonshot_fermionic_signed.py and positive_amplitude_search.json.

The unproved candidate is
  sum_pi c_pi Re(eta z_pi) <= max_{c_pi>0} Re(eta z_pi)        (6)
for every unitary, actual spectra and phase eta. Equation (6) would prove
MOC. It is an additional assertion, not a consequence of the signed identity.
Testing actual spectral constraints is essential: arbitrary costs on the
permutations can plainly violate this restriction.

We also tested whether a law preserving just one chosen two-row DPP marginal
could reproduce d. The three choices are I=12,13,14; complements give the
same constraint. The feasible determinant set for I is exactly
  sum_{|J|=2} rho(I,J) conv{z_pi:pi(I)=J}.                    (7)
Its support function is sum_J rho(I,J) max_{pi(I)=J} Re(eta z_pi), so (7)
has a finite polygon test independent of generic LP success flags.

At least one pair passed LP in 4089/4095 records, and all three passed in
4024. The six all-pair LP failures were near-permutation cases with direct
support gaps between roughly -3e-14 and +3e-15, so they are numerical
ambiguities, not evidence of a mathematical failure. A fixed choice can
fail robustly (record small_n04_04018 and the Schur-block obstruction above).
For the latter rational example, preserving I=14 and using eta=1+18i gives
an exact support-bound excess of 78528447/278480000. The verifier checks
this inequality by maximizing separately over each of its six four-point
fibers, so this fixed-pair obstruction does not rely on a floating LP.
All 203 added mixers for the known nonordinary spectral tuple passed some
pair. Three 1500-generation optimizations at Q did not find a violation of
all three choices simultaneously. Adaptive pair selection remains unproved.

Primary literature checked also includes Grigori Olshanski, Determinantal
point processes and fermion quasifree states, arXiv:2002.10723v2,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10723v2 . Its CAR interpretation supplies context;
it does not assert the needed permutation law. The publisher abstract of
Drury and Cload's 1992 paper was accessible, but full text returned 403, so
no new assertion about its proof or precise counterexample is based on it:
https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3795(92)90319-6 .
