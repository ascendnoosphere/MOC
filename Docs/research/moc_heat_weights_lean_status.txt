The module proves three statements about the explicit weights

 w_i = [1-3r^2+(3r^2-2r^3)s1_i+2r^3 s2_i]/24.

For 0<=r<=1 and 3r^2<=1, nonnegative s1_i,s2_i give nonnegative weights.
If each of the two sums over Fin24 is 24, the weights sum to one.
Consequently sum_i w_i z_i belongs to the real convex hull of the arbitrary
complex points z_i. These are fully checked universal statements, with no
sorry, admitted declaration or custom axiom. Their axiom reports contain only
propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

The RMT moonshot identifies s1_i and s2_i with sums of squared ordinary and
two-by-two minors, and the weighted value with a heat-averaged determinant.
That matrix identity and stochastic interpretation have a separate written
proof; this module does not formalize them. In particular, it does not prove
the original unsmoothed MOC inclusion or the unrestricted four-dimensional
conjecture. The accepted convexity result verifies the positive-weight step
without assuming the desired hull membership.
