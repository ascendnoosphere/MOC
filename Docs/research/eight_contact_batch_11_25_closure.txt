Imported actual-spectrum facts
------------------------------
Write Q=D_1F, He=H_F(1), p(t)=det(H_F(g)-t He). An actual gap at
height h satisfies F(g)=h Q(g), with H_F(g-h1) singular and rank at most12.
The proved actual-pencil theorem gives

  [z^2]det(H_F(g-h1)+z He)<=0.

See docs/research/actual_pencil_sign.txt and its independent review. In both
families F<0, so actual Q!=0 and h!=0. The checker proves p is cubic,
p(0)=0, and

  det H_F(g-F(g)/Q(g)*1)=-4 N^2/Q(g)^2.

Thus actuality forces N=0. An actual nonzero h is a double root of p;
consequently p(t)=k t(t-h)^2 and the prohibited quadratic coefficient
is p'(0)/h. Therefore N=0 together with p'(0)>0 excludes positive heights.
The established order-four external-half-plane theorem handles h<0.
These statements do not require generic rank or distinct spectral values.

Orbit25: positive projected nonic

  F=-(a+b+1)(d+1)(e+1),
  Q=e(c-b-d-1)-b-d-1,
  N=(d+1)^2(e+1)(a+b+1)^2 P,

Thus no actual gap with positive mixed coefficient exists at any height.
This case needs neither the negative-height theorem nor weighted recovery.

Orbit11: a positive resultant on the triangle-unsafe region
----------------------------------------------------------
Put A=a+c+1 and B=b+1. Then

  F=-AB,  Q=e(c-d)-c-1,
  N=AB P,  P=c U+V,
  W=(a+1)B(d+e+1)+(a+b+1)de>0,
  V=((a+1)B-de)W,
  X=e[B(a+d+1)+ad]-Bd(d+1),
  Y=ed[B(a+d+1)+ad]+(a+1)Bd(d+1),
  R=W+B(a+d+1)c>0,
  S=cX-Y.

The checker reconstructs U as the coefficient of c in the exact P, then
verifies these two polynomial identities coefficientwise:

  VX+UY=e B^2(a+d+1)^2 W,
  p'(0)=4ABe R S.

If de>=1, then d>0 and the established weighted-minor inequality
f+d r23+d^(-1)r28>=0 recovers g>=0: its remaining r28 coefficient is
e-d^(-1)>=0 and all other coefficients are ordinary nonnegative terms.

It remains to exclude actual positive-height gaps for de<1. Here V>0.
If N=0, then P=0, so c>0 and U<0. Also e>0 because the checker proves

  P|e=0=(a+1)B^2(d+1)(a+c+1)>0.

The positive resultant identity now gives

  S=-(VX+UY)/U>0,
  p'(0)=4ABe R S>0.

The actual-pencil sign theorem excludes this candidate branch. Heights0
are impossible because F<0, and negative heights are covered as above.
Together with weighted recovery on de>=1 this proves the full family.

The fraction-field pivots are merely a way to compute polynomial identities.
Exact denominator clearing proves the identities on the whole coefficient
space, including every vanishing-pivot chart and ordinary boundary.
