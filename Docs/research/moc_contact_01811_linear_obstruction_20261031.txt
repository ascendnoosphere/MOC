Why the fixed-linear kernel shortcut does not extend to C=(0,1,8,11)
31 October 2026

Status. This is an exact obstruction to a proof mechanism. It does not
refute full contact-face SOS containment, a nonlinear positivity argument,
or MOC. The original contact tuple is C=(0,1,8,11), using lexicographic
permutation labels0..23 throughout.

1. Exact setup

Let B14 be the ordinary matching basis used by UniversalPencil, and let

 N be a rational basis of ker(B14[C,:]),  B=B14 N.

Then B is a24-by10 full-column-rank cost table for W_C. Put

 G_j=32 M(B[:,j]), j=0,...,9,
 M0=sum_(j=0)^9 (j+1) G_j.

The exact determinant is

                   det M0=221702534725632 !=0.

2. No fixed rank-one column, over the complex numbers

Suppose some nonzero constant vector u in C9 satisfies
rank[G_0u ... G_9u]<=1. Since M0 is invertible, M0u!=0. Every G_ju is
therefore a scalar multiple of M0u, so u is a common eigenvector of

                         A_j=M0^(-1)G_j.

It must lie in every common-commutator kernel. Exact rational elimination
gives successive intersection dimensions

 ker[A0,A1]:3,
 ker[A0,A1] intersect ker[A0,A2]:1,
 ker[A0,A1] intersect ker[A0,A2] intersect ker[A0,A3]:0.

Rank over Q equals rank over C here. Thus NO nonzero constant u can give
a rank-one column identity M_g u=b(g)v on this contact space, even with
complex u,v. This rules out the whole fixed-column mechanism, rather than
only a selected list of candidate vectors.

For comparison, all sixteen tetrahedral outer-product basepoints
u=vec(l_i l_j^T) have image rank3 or4. The eight entire common isotropic
three-planes have cost-image ranks8 or9; the simple low-dimensional
operator variant also does not immediately force extra contacts.

3. No universally forced additional linear score

For a real invertible3-by3 H let h=vec(H) and define the9-by10 matrix

                         A(H)=[G_0h ... G_9h].

At ten recorded small integer invertible H anchors, A(H) has rank9.
Its primitive integer nullvector a gives a contact cost g=B a satisfying

                         M(g)h=0

exactly, with rank M(g)=8. The ten coefficient vectors a form an
invertible10-by10 matrix. Hence their cost vectors span all of W_C.

Any fixed linear score that vanished identically on this rational
conormal family would vanish on every one of these regular anchors and
thus on all W_C. There is no additional fixed permutation contact, or
other fixed linear equality, available by this mechanism.

The anchors may have signed costs. They do not disprove the possibility
that NONNEGATIVITY forces extra contacts or an SOS decomposition through
a nonlinear inequality. That is the remaining potential route here.
