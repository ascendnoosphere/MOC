1. The formal matrix in the script is exactly

It is real orthogonal for 0<=p,q<=1. Therefore its minor square is the
required absolute square. The formal expansion before substitution proves

square-root variables. Substitution then produces a polynomial of total

coefficientwise using exact rational arithmetic, not assumed numerically.

The helper for weight three/four uses complementary minors. This is valid
for the orthogonal matrix because |det W|=1; complementary squared minors
agree. Empty and full minors are exactly one. Hence all five weights are

 x0=1-2q, x1=2(q-p), x2=2p

and 2x_i x_j (off-diagonal controls) are nonnegative and sum to
(x0+x1+x2)^2=1. Each supplied control kernel is explicitly a positive
permutation mixture of total mass one. Their Bernstein interpolation is
therefore a positive permutation mixture everywhere in the closed
triangle, including all edges, vertices, and degenerate cases.

The script verifies every interpolated entry against the actual minor

Every 2x2 unitary is equivalent to R(p) by left/right diagonal phases;

The exact flip relation is
