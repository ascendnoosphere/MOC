# Attribution and source status

Literature status checked as of 17 October 2026.

## Status of the unrestricted conjecture

The indexed publisher abstract of Juan Ignacio Mulero-Martínez, *A variational framework for determinantal inequalities of normal matrices: Successes and obstructions*, Linear Algebra and its Applications 740 (1 July 2026), 19–38, explicitly describes the conjecture for general normal matrices as open. Only the abstract and bibliographic metadata were checked; no independent audit of that paper's full proofs is claimed.

- [Publisher record and indexed abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0024379526001266)
- [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2026.03.019)
- [Author institution's publication metadata](https://portalinvestigacion.upct.es/documentos/69db0124226b77548f678650)

Alexander Kovačec's April 2026 preprint also treats the unrestricted order-four problem as open:

- [DMUC preprint 26-11](https://www.mat.uc.pt/preprints/ps/p2611.pdf)

These are bounded literature checks. They support the description of the status in those sources, and do not establish that no later proof exists.

## Eight-point invariant geometry: established source

The identification of \(M_8\) as the singular locus of an \(S_8\)-skew cubic, the cubic's matching description, and the secant/Hessian divisor results come from:

Benjamin Howard, John Millson, Andrew Snowden, and Ravi Vakil, *The geometry of eight points in projective space: representation theory, Lie theory, dualities*.

- [arXiv:1103.5255](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.5255)
- [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1103.5255)

The present record supplies explicit coordinates in the MOC permutation-product basis and investigates the real support-cost projection, Hessian fibers, exclusions, and their consequences for attempted MOC proofs. The original \(M_8\) cubic and its known singular-locus theorem are not claimed as discoveries of this campaign.

## Earlier report overlapping the sharp orthogonal bound

A public registry records Sidney Holden's *A fourteen-term identity and a sharp orthogonal obstruction bound*, posted to GitHub on 13 September 2026. Its abstract reports a sharp bound for the same family of forty-eight orthogonal common-minor obstructions. Accordingly, this campaign makes no priority claim for its independently derived sharp orthogonal bound.

- [Registry record](https://clopen.solutions/submissions/openproblemsinnla-holden-mi05-2026-09-13-report/v1/)
- [Recorded source PDF](https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/eb7c9d64f91757a82fbe9167e80ab9c1b7f36c22/references/holden-mi05-2026-09-13/report.pdf)

The registry labels the report's fidelity as not yet established. Only the abstract overlap has been verified. None of its unread lemmas is used as a premise of the proofs in this campaign. The angle-elimination proof and the extension to complex profiles in the current record were independently checked.

## Other established ingredients

The all-dimensional rank-one/Householder MOC case is established work of Stephen W. Drury. Our finite-support argument and coordinate-containing-plane reduction are recorded with that priority retained.

The equality between the copositive cone and the sum of the positive semidefinite and entrywise nonnegative cones through order four is the classical Diananda theorem. Our certificate class uses it; the theorem itself is not new.

- [Drury's discussion of the low-order copositive cone](https://www.math.mcgill.ca/drury/research/spn/index.html)
- [Original Diananda DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100036185)

The isotropic positive-matrix decomposition used for the two-phase and \(U(2)\) lifts has established connections to the real two-rebit separability criterion. Our application to the simultaneous squared-minor orbit is distinguished from that known cone fact.

- Caves, Fuchs, Rungta, [*Entanglement of formation of an arbitrary state of two rebits*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0009063)

The report is a private, proof-bearing working record. Exact calculations, independently reviewed arguments, numerical observations, and remaining conjectures are separately labeled. No external publication or peer-review claim is made.
