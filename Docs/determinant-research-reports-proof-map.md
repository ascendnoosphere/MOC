# Determinant research report proof map

For complex normal matrices $A$ and $B$ of the same order, the
Marcus–de Oliveira conjecture asks whether $\det(A+B)$ lies in the
real convex hull of the products obtained by matching their eigenvalues.
The unrestricted conjecture remains open in this library.
[Conjecture](../Lean/MOC/Core.lean#L33) · [Proof tree](../README.md#proof-tree)

This map identifies the mathematical claims in seven text reports and six
companion files. The file names identify the local research inputs.
The section numbers below refer to those inputs. A Lean link gives the
scope of the named declaration. A conditional declaration requires its
stated premise. An exact experiment concerns only its specified inputs.
Kernel verification for the new report modules is pending.
[Other research routes](research-attempts-proof-map.md)

The strategic sections in Reports 1, 2, 3, 5, and 7 compare routes that
still have open premises. For example, the normal compression disk
requires an interior comparison. The exact algebraic decision problem
requires a certificate for the given order. The spectral difference
variety requires exclusion of a saturated strict violation. Continuity
of an input family by itself gives no exact continuous choice of its
weights. The reported bibliography and final summary sections add no
further determinant implication.
