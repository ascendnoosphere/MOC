1. Correct geometric object and quantifiers.
Let V_4 be the 14-dimensional real common squared-minor cost space, identified
with its injective evaluations on S_4. For each unitary U, evaluation at U is
a linear functional q_U on V_4. Let P=conv{q_Ppi: pi in S_4} and
K=conv{q_U: U in U(4)}. These are correctly typed subsets of V_4^*; P is a
13-dimensional polytope. K is compact convex and P is a subset of K.
One must not assume K=P: the common permutation law is stronger than MOC.

For an active permutation set S, the appropriate cone is
 C_S={g in V_4: g(pi)>=0 for every pi, and g(s)=0 for every s in S}.
The spectral section consists of actual costs
 g(pi)=h-Re(eta product_i(a_i+b_pi(i))).
MOC on this section asks that g(q_U)>=0 for every actual U. The section is
not silently replaced by the whole polyhedral cone C_S.

classification of the full permutation-nonnegative cone: 34 ordinary minor

This note imports that already-certified classification; it does not assert

2. A support-line restriction from the toric matching relation.
Four products corresponding to independent disjoint swaps obey
 z00 z11=z10 z01. Put w=eta z; the same homogeneous identity holds for w.
Suppose three corners are active, w00=h+i y0, w10=h+i y1,
w01=h+i y2, with h!=0. Then
 h-Re w11 = h (y1-y0)(y2-y0)/(h^2+y0^2).
This follows by dividing w10 w01 by w00 and expanding real parts.
If all four corners were active, (y1-y0)(y2-y0)=0. Distinct products
exclude this. Thus a distinct-contact set on a line missing the origin
contains no disjoint-swap rectangle. The h=0 exception is essential.
For three corners, h>0 forces y0 outside the interval between y1,y2;
h<0 forces y0 inside it. These restrictions alone are not enough to prove MOC.

3. PROVED POSITIVE THEOREM: twelve contacts.
For n=4, suppose ALL24 complex matching products are pairwise distinct.
An actual spectral support gap with at least12 active permutation labels
and h!=0 belongs to the ordinary squared-minor cone. Consequently
its support inequality holds for every U in U(4).

Proof. Every mixed ray has exactly13 zeros. For the representative f above,
its zeros in one-line notation are
 1243,2134,2143,2314,2341,3124,3214,3241,3421,4123,4213,4312,4321.
Among these are the three disjoint-swap rectangles
 R1={2314,2341,3214,3241},
 R2={3124,3214,4123,4213},
 R3={2143,2341,4123,4321}.
Their common intersection is empty. Removing at most one point from the
13-element zero set therefore leaves at least one entire rectangle.
Thus a rectangle-free subset of a mixed-ray zero set has size at most11.
This is invariant under independent row and column permutations.

Decompose g into the complete82 rays with nonnegative coefficients. If any
mixed coefficient were positive, that ray would vanish at every active
label. The active set would then be a rectangle-free subset of its13 zeros
of size>=12, a contradiction. Only ordinary rays remain. Replacing each
ordinary ray by its squared minor gives an explicit nonnegative expression
on every unitary. QED.

This uses actual unitary constraints through nonnegativity of squared
minors, rather than treating a signed outer profile as unitary. It excludes
a class of possible terminal counterexamples, but does not reach the
six-contact threshold of the high-contact transfer theorem. It also does
not apply to a support line through the origin.
