THEOREM A: ZERO-HEIGHT EIGHT-CONTACT POSITIVITY
Under the setup above, if |S|>=8, then g is a nonnegative combination of
ordinary squared-minor rays. Hence for EVERY unitary U,
  -Re(eta det(diag(a)+U diag(b) U*))>=0.
This theorem uses only the82-ray classification, the exact matching-cubic
identity, and elementary generalized-circle geometry. It does not use
Shitov's sufficient condition or extend EVC by continuity.

Proof, with finite certificates:
1. Any three active corners in a disjoint-swap rectangle force the fourth
active. The identity z00*z11=z01*z10 and nonzero pure-imaginary active
values give a pure-imaginary fourth value. Thus no rectangle meets S
in exactly3 labels.

2. If g has a mixed coefficient in an82-ray representation, each ray with
positive coefficient vanishes on S. Let C(S) be all82 rays vanishing on S.
Then the intersection of their zero sets equals S: it contains S, and is
contained in the zero set of their positive subcombination g. Therefore
S is an exactly closed ray-zero face. zero_height_face_census.py enumerates
all such faces with>=6 labels, at least one allowed mixed ray, and no
3-corner rectangle. It gives27024 faces and46 row/column/inversion orbits:
  contacts     6      7      8     9    10
  faces     17280   7512   1728   312   192
  orbits      28     13      2     2     1
There are no such mixed faces with>=11 labels. The census was independently
regenerated and audited by the metaproof agent, including disjoint orbit
coverage and exact canonical rays.

3. Let F be the exact homogeneous cubic from closing_moc4_model_certificate.
For actual matching vectors, Re(eta z) satisfies F=0, hence F(g)=0 at h=0.
zero_height_cubic_screen.py expands F(sum_j t_j r_j) on all46 faces using
exact integer polarization. On22 faces all nonzero coefficients have one
sign, and the pure cube of every mixed ray has coefficient+1 or-1 of that
same sign. A positive mixed coefficient would therefore force F(g)!=0.
All these faces are ordinary-minor positive. The independent audit also
checked every mixed pure cube, not merely absence of opposite signs.

4. For two active matchings differing by one transposition, their ratio is
real and nonzero. Cancellation shows that the cross ratio of the two
corresponding a points and two beta points is real. The four distinct
points therefore lie on one generalized circle (a circle or a line).
Two such circles sharing>=3 distinct points coincide. This gives exact
circle-block merging.

5. Circle phase saturation: suppose a set C of spectral points is on one
generalized circle, and pi is active. Choose any rows I such that both a_i
and beta_pi(i) belong to C. Every matching obtained by permuting pi(I)
inside those rows is active. To prove this, choose a common Mobius map
m sending C to the real line, with its pole outside all8 points. Write
m(x)-m(y)=Delta*(x-y)/((gamma*x+delta)*(gamma*y+delta)). Every full matching
product is multiplied by the SAME nonzero factor
  Delta^4 / product_i(gamma*a_i+delta)
              product_j(gamma*beta_j+delta).
The quotient of the internally rematched transformed products is a
nonzero real number, because all their changed factors are real. Thus the
original full-product ratio is real; multiplying an active pure-imaginary
value by that ratio stays pure imaginary. This proves the assertion.

6. zero_height_circle_saturation.py iterates steps4--5 to a fixed point,
with explicit witnesses for each circle, merge, and new active label.

All other cubic-indefinite representatives have<=7 contacts. Thus every

This proves Theorem A. The metaproof agent independently replayed every pair-circle, merge, and
